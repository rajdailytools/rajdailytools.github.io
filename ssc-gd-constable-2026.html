Frequently Asked Questions

Top questions about SSC GD Constable 2026, answered from the official notification

What is SSC GD Constable 2026?+
SSC GD Constable 2026 is a recruitment notice (F. No. HQ-C-3007/10/2025-C-3) issued by the Staff Selection Commission, as per the Recruitment Scheme of the Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA), for the posts of Constable (General Duty) in BSF, CISF, CRPF, ITBP, SSB and SSF, and Rifleman (General Duty) in Assam Rifles.
What is the last date to apply for SSC GD Constable 2026?+
The last date and time for receipt of the online application is 31 December 2025 (23:00 hours). Online fee payment is accepted till 1 January 2026 (23:00 hours).
What is the application fee for SSC GD Constable 2026?+
The application fee is ₹100 (Rupees One Hundred only), payable online via BHIM UPI, Net Banking, or Visa/Master/Maestro/RuPay Debit Card (Para 9.1, 9.3).
Who is exempted from paying the application fee?+
Women candidates and candidates belonging to Scheduled Castes (SC), Scheduled Tribes (ST) and Ex-Servicemen (ESM) eligible for reservation are exempted from the fee (Para 9.2).
How many vacancies are there in SSC GD Constable 2026?+
There are approx. 25,487 tentative vacancies (Male: 23,467; Female: 2,020) across BSF, CISF, CRPF, SSB, ITBP, Assam Rifles and SSF. Updated vacancy details will be made available on ssc.gov.in (Para 3.1).
When will the SSC GD Constable 2026 Computer Based Examination be conducted?+
The Computer Based Examination (CBE) is tentatively scheduled for February–April 2026. Exact dates will be informed to candidates only through the SSC website.
What is the selection process for SSC GD Constable 2026?+
Selection happens in stages: Computer Based Examination (CBE) → Physical Efficiency Test (PET) & Physical Standard Test (PST) → Detailed Medical Examination (DME)/Review Medical Examination (RME) & Document Verification (DV) by the CAPFs → Final Result with Force allocation based on merit and Force preference.
What is the age limit for SSC GD Constable 2026?+
The age of the candidate must be 18–23 years as on 01-01-2026 (i.e. born not before 02-01-2003 and not later than 01-01-2008), with additional relaxation for reserved categories (Para 5).
Is there negative marking in SSC GD Constable 2026?+
Yes. There is a negative marking of 0.25 marks for each wrong answer in the Computer Based Examination (Para 12.1.2).
Do I need to upload a photograph while applying?+
No pre-existing photo upload is required. The Online Application Module captures a real-time photograph via camera while filling the form — without cap, mask, or spectacles, in good light with a plain background (Para 8.3–8.6).
What are the signature upload specifications?+
Signature must be scanned in JPEG format, sized 10–20 KB, with image dimensions of approximately 6.0 cm (Width) × 2.0 cm (Height) (Para 8.7).
What is the minimum qualifying mark in the CBE?+
Minimum qualifying marks (without NCC bonus marks) are: UR — 30%, OBC/EWS — 25%, All other categories — 20% (Para 14.2).
Are Persons with Disabilities (PwD) eligible to apply?+
No. As per Important Instruction No. 8 of the official notification, "Persons with Disabilities (PwD) are not eligible to apply for this Examination."
