📖 Complete Guide

Army Eye Test — Complete Guide 2026

Army Eye Test Kaise Hota Hai?

Indian Army medical board mein eye examination ek critical part hota hai. Medical test generally 3 parts mein hoti hai: Distance Vision Test (Snellen chart se 6 meter par), Near Vision Test (Jaeger/N-type chart se 33cm par), aur Color Perception Test (Ishihara plates se). Army Medical Officer (AMO) in teeno tests ko carefully evaluate karta hai. Kisi bhi test mein failure — medical rejection ho sakta hai.

Important: Army GD ke liye glasses/spectacles allowed nahi hain. Unaided (naked eye) vision 6/6 hona zaroori hai. Technical aur Clerk ke liye glasses with limited power (±3.5 Dioptre) allowed hain.

Color Blindness Kya Hoti Hai? Army Mein Kyun Reject Hote Hain?

Color blindness (Color Vision Deficiency) ek condition hai jisme person certain colors clearly distinguish nahi kar pata — most commonly red aur green. Army mein yeh isliye disqualifying hai kyunki soldiers ko battlefield par colored signals, maps, wire identification, aur target recognition tasks karne hote hain jisme color differentiation critical hoti hai. Kisi bhi Army post ke liye Color Perception Grade I (severe deficiency) barring condition hai — koi exception nahi.

  • Red-green color blindness sabse common hai (8% men affected)
  • Blue-yellow color blindness rare hai but equally disqualifying
  • Complete color blindness (achromatopsia) extremely rare hai
  • Color blindness hereditary hoti hai — genetics se aati hai
  • Ishihara test 38 plates se hota hai — Army mein selected plates use hoti hain

Army Eye Test Mein Common Rejection Reasons

Eye test mein rejection ke top reasons hain: (1) Color Blindness — kisi bhi degree ka color vision deficiency Army GD ke liye disqualifying. (2) Refractive errors beyond limit — -3.5D ya +3.5D se zyada power wale glasses wale GD ke liye rejected. (3) Night blindness — low light mein poor vision. (4) Squint (Strabismus) — eyes jo straight align nahi hoti. (5) Amblyopia (lazy eye) — ek eye permanently weak. (6) Corneal scars — previous injury ya infection ke marks.

Army GD Candidates ke liye Warning: GD mein glasses bilkul allowed nahi hain — na unaided test ke time par, na after selection. Agar aapko glasses ki zarurat hai — GD ke bajay Technical, Clerk, ya Officer track consider karein.

Eyesight Kaise Improve Kare Army Ke Liye?

Refractive errors (myopia/hyperopia) naturally cure nahi hoti lekin lifestyle changes se progression roka ja sakta hai. Helpful habits: Screen time reduce karo (har 20 min mein 20-second break, 20 feet door dekho — "20-20-20 rule"). Adequate lighting mein padho. Green vegetables, carrots, amla, fish oil — vitamin A rich diet rakho. Outdoor time badhao — natural light myopia progression rokti hai. Medical options: Laser eye surgery (LASIK) kaafi log karte hain — Army LASIK ke baad limited time mein apply karne ki restriction hai (6–12 months), official notification verify karein.

Kya Chashma Wale Army Mein Ja Sakte Hain?

Haan — kuch posts ke liye. Army GD ke liye spectacles allowed nahi hain. Lekin Technical, Clerk, SKT, Officer (SSC, CDS), aur various Corps (AMC, ASC, Ordnance, Education) ke liye glasses with limited power (generally ±3.5 Dioptre tak) allowed hain. NDA ke liye myopia -3.5D tak allowed hai. Important: spectacles ka power, squint, amblyopia, aur color vision sab separately check hota hai. Agar glasses power ±3.5D ke andar hai aur color vision normal hai — bahut saare posts ke liye eligible ho sakte hain.

Pro Tip: Color blindness ka pata lagane ke liye kisi bhi optician ya eye doctor ke paas free ya minimal cost Ishihara test hoti hai. Recruitment se pehle yeh test zaroor karwa lein — taaki sahi post ke liye apply kar sako.
❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Army me eyesight kitni honi chahiye — GD ke liye?
+
Army GD ke liye vision standards sabse strict hain: Unaided (bina glasses ke) distance vision 6/6 dono eyes mein hona zaroori hai. Glasses/contact lenses bilkul allowed nahi hain. Near vision N6 each eye. Color Perception CP-III (normal color vision required). Night blindness, squint ya amblyopia kisi bhi eye mein — immediate rejection. GD ke liye sabse strict vision criteria hoti hai kyunki direct combat role hota hai.
Kya chashma wale Army Technical ya Clerk mein ja sakte hain?
+
Haan! Army Technical aur Clerk ke liye spectacles allowed hain — with conditions: (1) Power ±3.5 Dioptre se zyada nahi honi chahiye (Sphere + half of Cylinder). (2) With correction, vision 6/6 achievable hona chahiye. (3) Color vision normal hona chahiye (CP-III). (4) No squint, amblyopia, or retinal disease. Officer posts (SSC, CDS, NDA) ke liye bhi glasses allowed hain with similar power limits. GD ke liye absolutely no glasses allowed.
Army me color blindness allowed hai kya kisi bhi post mein?
+
Nahi — Color blindness (Color Perception Grade I = severe deficiency) kisi bhi Indian Army post ke liye disqualifying condition hai. Koi exception nahi hai. Ishihara test se color vision check hoti hai. CP-II (moderate deficiency) bhi most posts ke liye reject kar deta hai. Sirf CP-III (normal/near-normal color vision) required hai. Agar color blindness hai — Indian Army ke kisi bhi post ke liye apply karna possible nahi hai. Alternative: paramilitary forces jaise CISF, BSF mein kuch relaxation hoti hai — unka notification check karein.
LASIK ke baad Army mein apply kar sakte hain?
+
LASIK surgery ke baad Army mein apply karne ke rules hain: GD ke liye LASIK-operated candidates generally not acceptable in recent notifications. Technical/Clerk ke liye LASIK operated candidates — minimum 6-12 months baad apply kar sakte hain, full stability documented honi chahiye. Officers (NDA, CDS, SSC) ke liye LASIK acceptable hai with specific conditions — at least 12 months post-surgery, stable refraction, no complications. Always verify the specific notification as rules can change.
Eye test simulator ka result kitna accurate hai?
+
Yeh simulator awareness ke liye hai — medical examination ka replacement nahi hai. Accuracy screen calibration, lighting conditions, aur device size par depend karti hai. Agar simulator mein color blindness ya vision issues aaye — please ek certified ophthalmologist ya optometrist se proper Ishihara test aur Snellen chart test karwayein. Army medical mein professional trained doctors proper equipment se test karte hain jo home screen se possible nahi. Is tool ko apni preparation aur awareness ke liye use karein.
NDA ke liye eyesight kitni honi chahiye?
+
NDA ke liye eyesight standards: Unaided distance vision: 6/6 in one eye and 6/9 in the other (OR with correction). Myopia: Not exceeding -3.5D (including astigmatism). Hypermetropia: Not exceeding +3.5D including astigmatism. Manifest myopia: Should not exceed 3.5D. Color vision: CP-II required (stricter than most Army posts). No squint, no amblyopia. LASIK: Acceptable after 12 months with stable results for Air Force wing — check latest UPSC notification for NDA.
Army eye test mein kya leke jaana chahiye?
+
Army medical mein eye examination ke liye: (1) Agar glasses use karte ho — original prescription leke jaao (ophthalmologist se). (2) Glasses khud leke jaao — AMO check karega. (3) Koi bhi previous eye surgery ka record (operation notes). (4) Agar contact lenses use karte ho — medical ke time pe remove karo aur glasses leke jao. (5) Aankh mein kisi bhi baar pehle hue injury ya disease ka history. Raat ko neend lena, fresh aankhen se test dena better results deta hai — travel se thake hue mat jaana.
