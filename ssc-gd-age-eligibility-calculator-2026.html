SSC GD Complete Guide 2026
Age limit, qualification, selection process, physical eligibility — sab kuch ek jagah.
📅
1. SSC GD Age Limit Explained — Poori Jankari

SSC GD (General Duty) recruitment me age eligibility sabse pehla aur sabse important criteria hai. Bina age eligibility ke koi bhi candidate selection process me aage nahi badh sakta. SSC GD ke liye general category candidates ko minimum 18 years aur maximum 23 years ki age chahiye hoti hai — yeh age official cutoff date par calculate hoti hai jo recruitment notification me mention hoti hai.

Age calculation ka formula simple hai: Aapki age = Cutoff date minus your date of birth. Agar cutoff date 01 January 2026 hai aur aapka DOB 15 March 2003 hai, toh aapki calculated age 22 years hogi. Yeh same formula sabhi categories ke liye apply hota hai, sirf maximum age limit mein category-wise relaxation ka difference hota hai.

Important: Age limit aur cutoff date har notification me alag ho sakti hai. Always official SSC notification ssc.gov.in par padhna zaroori hai before applying.
🏷️
2. Category-wise Age Relaxation — Kisko Kitni Milti Hai?

SSC GD me reserved category candidates ko upper age limit me relaxation milti hai. Yeh relaxation central government rules ke according fixed hoti hai:

  • General / EWS: Koi age relaxation nahi — maximum age 23 years
  • OBC (Non-Creamy Layer): 3 years relaxation — maximum age 26 years
  • SC / ST: 5 years relaxation — maximum age 28 years
  • Ex-Servicemen: Special rules apply — generally military service period + 3 years
  • PwBD (Divyangjan): Additional relaxation — as per notification
  • J&K Domicile candidates (certain periods): 5 years additional relaxation may apply

Ek important baat — OBC relaxation sirf Non-Creamy Layer ke liye applicable hai. Creamy Layer OBC candidates ko General category ke rules follow karne padte hain. Isliye apna valid OBC NCL certificate check karo before applying.

Tip: Category certificate current financial year ka hona chahiye aur competent authority se issued hona chahiye. Purana certificate reject ho sakta hai.
🎓
3. SSC GD Qualification Criteria 2026

SSC GD Constable post ke liye education qualification requirement relatively simple hai. Minimum 10th pass (Matriculation) kisi recognized board se hona zaroori hai. Yeh nahi hai ki higher education wale apply nahi kar sakte — 12th, graduation, postgraduate sab eligible hain, sirf minimum 10th pass chahiye.

  • Recognized Board/University se 10th pass compulsory
  • Private school candidates: school recognized board se affiliated hona chahiye
  • Open board candidates: board ka government recognition verify karo
  • Final year students: generally apply kar sakte hain but qualification before joining required
  • Foreign degree holders: equivalent certificate from recognized authority needed

Ek common confusion yeh hai ki kuch candidates sochte hain ki graduation hone se zyada weightage milegi — SSC GD me written exam merit based hoti hai, education level se extra marks nahi milte eligibility ke stage par. Lekin higher education wale written exam better crack kar sakte hain obviously.

🏃
4. SSC GD PET / PST Overview — Physical Test Kya Hota Hai?

SSC GD selection process me written exam clear karne ke baad PET (Physical Efficiency Test) aur PST (Physical Standard Test) hota hai. Dono alag cheezein hain:

PST — Physical Standard Test: Is test me height aur chest (male ke liye) check hoti hai. SSC GD ke liye general male height standard approximately 170 cm hai (state/region wise variation). Female candidates ke liye approximately 157 cm. Chest male ke liye minimum 80 cm with 5 cm expansion.

PET — Physical Efficiency Test: Is test me running test hota hai:

  • Male: 5 km in 24 minutes
  • Female: 1.6 km in 8.30 minutes
  • Ladakh region candidates: relaxed standards apply
PET/PST qualifying nature ka test hai — marks nahi milte. Fail hone par selection process se bahar ho jaate hain. Isliye physical preparation written exam ke saath saath chalu rakhni chahiye. Verify from ssc.gov.in for latest standards.
⚠️
5. Common Form Rejection Reasons — Yeh Galtiyan Mat Karo

SSC GD form rejection ke most common reasons jo candidates experience karte hain:

  • Age calculation error: Cutoff date galat assume karna sabse bada reason hai — notification carefully padhna zaroori hai
  • Wrong DOB entry: Form me date format DD/MM/YYYY hota hai — galti mat karo
  • Category certificate not valid: Purana ya wrong authority ka certificate — fresh current year certificate zaroori
  • Qualification mismatch: Form me galat qualification claim karna
  • Photo specifications: Wrong size, background, or recent photo nahi hona
  • Signature issues: Digital signature nahi milna ya guidelines follow na karna
  • OBC Creamy Layer: Creamy layer candidates ka OBC me apply karna — automatically rejected
  • Multiple forms: Ek se zyada applications submit karna

Hamara SSC GD Age Eligibility Calculator exactly age calculation confusion solve karta hai. Lekin other eligibility factors ke liye official notification zaroor padhe — ssc.gov.in par verify kare.

📋
6. SSC GD Selection Process — Step by Step
1
Online Application (Form Filling)
SSC official website par registration, form filling, photo upload, aur fee payment. Age, qualification, category sab verify karo before submitting.
2
CBT — Computer Based Test (Written Exam)
100 marks ka objective MCQ paper — General Intelligence, General Knowledge, Elementary Mathematics, English/Hindi. Negative marking hoti hai (0.25 marks).
3
PST — Physical Standard Test
CBT merit par shortlisted candidates ka height aur chest measurement. Qualifying in nature — no marks.
4
PET — Physical Efficiency Test
Running test — 5 km (male) ya 1.6 km (female). Qualifying in nature — no marks but must pass.
5
Medical Examination
Detailed medical fitness check — eyesight, hearing, general health, flat foot, knock knee etc. as per standards.
6
Document Verification & Final Merit
All original documents verified. Final merit based on CBT marks. Joining letter issued to selected candidates.
