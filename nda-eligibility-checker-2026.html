NDA 2026 Complete Eligibility Guide

Everything you need to know before filling the NDA 2026 form — age, qualification, selection process, common mistakes, and myths busted.

NDA Kya Hai aur Kaise Apply Karen?

NDA yaani National Defence Academy India ki sabse prestigious defence entrance examination hai. Ye exam UPSC (Union Public Service Commission) conduct karta hai, aur iske through candidates Army, Navy aur Air Force wings mein join karte hain. NDA exam saal mein do baar hoti hai — NDA I (April mein) aur NDA II (September mein). Written exam ke baad SSB (Services Selection Board) interview aur medical fitness test hota hai. Successfully selected candidates Pune near Khadakwasla Academy mein 3 saal ki training attend karte hain.

Key Fact: NDA 2026 exam approximately April aur September 2026 mein expected hai. DOB cutoff officially notification release hone par confirm hogi — yahan jo dates hain wo 2025 ke pattern se estimate ki gayi hain.

NDA 2026 Age Limit — Exact Calculation Guide

NDA ka age calculation thoda confusing lagta hai, isliye clearly samajhte hain. Age calculate hoti hai exam ke year ki 1st July ke basis par. NDA 2026 ke liye:

  • Minimum age: 16 years 6 months (16.5 years) on 1 July 2026
  • Maximum age: 19 years 6 months (19.5 years) on 1 July 2026
  • Expected DOB range: January 2, 2007 to July 1, 2010 (approximately)

Example: Agar aapki DOB 15 March 2008 hai, toh 1 July 2026 par aapki age hogi 18 years 3 months 16 days — jo ki 16.5 se 19.5 ke range mein hai. Aap eligible hain.

Important: Exact DOB cutoff official notification mein declare hota hai. This tool gives an estimated check based on the 2025 NDA pattern. Always verify with the official UPSC notification before applying.

NDA Qualification Criteria — Kaun Sa Stream Chahiye?

Ek common confusion jo bahut aspirants mein hoti hai wo hai qualification ka. Actually NDA wing-wise alag qualification maangta hai:

  • Army Wing: 12th pass kisi bhi stream se. Science, Arts, Commerce — koi bhi chalega. PCM ki zaroorat nahi.
  • Air Force & Naval Wings: 12th mein Physics aur Mathematics (PCM) compulsory hai. Bina PCM ke in wings ke liye apply nahi kar sakte.
  • 12th Appearing: Jo students abhi 12th mein padhte hain ya 12th ke result ka wait kar rahe hain, wo bhi apply kar sakte hain — provided wo 12th appearing certificate provide kar sakein.
Pro Tip: Agar aapne PCM nahi li hai toh sirf Army Wing ke liye apply kar sakte hain. Ye bhi bahut achha option hai — Army mein career ke kai avenues khulte hain NDA ke through.

Female Candidates — NDA 2026 Mein Kya Hai Niyam?

2022 se pehle NDA sirf male candidates ke liye tha. Supreme Court ke aadesh ke baad NDA 2022 (II) se female candidates bhi apply kar sakti hain. Ab dono genders ke liye eligibility criteria same hai — same age limit, same qualification requirement. Ye ek historic change hai jo lakho female aspirants ke sapno ko open karta hai.

Female candidates Army, Air Force aur Naval wings sabke liye eligible hain — qualification criteria same rahega (Air Force aur Navy ke liye PCM required).

NDA Selection Process — Step by Step

NDA selection ek multi-stage process hai. Ek aspirant ko sab stages successfully complete karni padti hain:

  • Stage 1 – Written Exam: UPSC conduct karta hai. Mathematics (300 marks) aur General Ability Test (600 marks) — total 900 marks. Objective type questions.
  • Stage 2 – SSB Interview: Services Selection Board 5-day assessment — Psychological tests, Group Testing Officer tasks, Personal Interview. Total 900 marks.
  • Stage 3 – Medical Examination: Comprehensive physical aur medical examination. Vision, hearing, structural fitness — sab check hota hai.
  • Stage 4 – Merit List: Written + SSB marks se final merit list banti hai. Document verification ke baad joining letter milta hai.

Common Mistakes Jo Aspirants Karte Hain

Bahut saare students eligibility mein confusion ki wajah se ya toh galat form bharte hain ya eligible hone ke bawajood form nahi bharte. Kuch common mistakes:

  • Age ko birthday se calculate karna — actually 1 July 2026 se calculate hoti hai
  • Non-PCM students ka Air Force/Navy wing ke liye apply karne ki koshish — ye directly reject hoga
  • 12th appearing status ka pata na hona — appearing students bhi apply kar sakte hain
  • Female candidates ka ye sochna ki wo eligible nahi hain — 2022 se eligibility open hai
  • Exact DOB boundary confirm na karna — borderline cases mein official notification zaroor check karein

NDA Ke Liye Physical Standards — Kya Expect Karen?

Written aur SSB ke baad medical examination hoti hai. Physical standards mein broadly ye check hota hai:

  • Height aur weight proportion — underweight ya overweight na ho
  • Vision — distant vision 6/6 ya 6/9 correctable, near vision N5 correctable in one eye aur N10 in other
  • No flat foot, knock knee, ya bow leg
  • No major surgery history or chronic illness
  • Hearing normal hona chahiye
Medical standards ke baare mein detailed info official NDA notification ya joining instructions mein milti hai. Pre-SSB medical check kisi registered doctor se karwa lena beneficial hota hai.

NDA Eligibility Myths — Busted!

Social media aur coaching centers pe kai galat information circulate hoti rehti hai. Kuch common myths clear karte hain:

  • Myth: "Arts stream wale NDA nahi de sakte" — Fact: Army Wing ke liye koi bhi stream eligible hai
  • Myth: "Females abhi bhi NDA nahi de sakti" — Fact: 2022 se females bhi fully eligible hain
  • Myth: "NDA ke liye minimum 60% marks chahiye" — Fact: Koi minimum percentage nahi hai — simply 12th pass/appearing hona chahiye
  • Myth: "Borderline age wale apply nahi kar sakte" — Fact: Exact boundary ke andar hone par apply kar sakte hain — official dates verify karein
Frequently Asked Questions

NDA 2026 eligibility ke baare mein sabse common questions ke clear answers.

NDA 2026 ke liye age limit kya hai? +
NDA 2026 ke liye candidates ki age 1 July 2026 ko 16.5 years se 19.5 years ke beech honi chahiye. Expected DOB range approximately 2 January 2007 to 1 July 2010 hai. Exact dates official UPSC notification mein confirm hongi.
Kya female candidates NDA 2026 ke liye eligible hain? +
Haan! Supreme Court ke 2021 ke decision ke baad NDA 2022 (II) se female candidates bhi eligible hain. Ab male aur female dono candidates ke liye eligibility criteria same hai — same age limit, same qualification requirement, same selection process.
12th Appearing students NDA ke liye apply kar sakte hain? +
Haan, 12th Appearing students bhi apply kar sakte hain. Unhe application ke time 12th appearing certificate submit karna hoga. SSB ke time ya joining ke time 12th pass certificate submit karna zaroori hoga. Agar 12th result mein fail hue toh candidature cancel ho sakta hai.
Air Force aur Navy wing ke liye PCM kyun zaroori hai? +
Air Force aur Naval wings mein technical aur scientific aspects bahut important hain — avionics, navigation, engineering — isliye 12th mein Physics aur Mathematics background zaroori hai. Army wing mein yeh requirement nahi hai kyunki Army wings mein non-technical roles bhi hain.
NDA mein 12th mein minimum kitne percentage chahiye? +
NDA eligibility ke liye 12th mein koi minimum percentage criteria nahi hai. Sirf 12th pass (ya appearing) hona kaafi hai. Lekin competition mein achha performance ke liye 12th mein achhe marks aur special subjects ka strong foundation helpful hota hai SSB preparation ke liye.
NDA ke liye physical standards kya hain? +
Medical examination mein height-weight ratio, eyesight (distant vision 6/6 ya 6/9 correctable), hearing, no structural deformities (flat foot, knock knee), no chronic illness assessed hota hai. Detailed physical standards official notification aur joining instructions mein milte hain. Medical ke liye pre-SSB doctor checkup beneficial hota hai.
Agar meri age borderline hai toh kya karein? +
Agar aapki age eligibility boundary ke bahut paas hai toh official UPSC NDA notification zaroor check karein — exact cutoff dates wahan clearly mentioned hoti hain. Is tool ki dates estimated hain previous year's pattern ke basis par. Official website: upsc.gov.in
NDA selection process mein kitne stages hote hain? +
NDA selection teen main stages mein hoti hai: (1) Written Exam — UPSC conduct karta hai, Maths + GAT, 900 marks; (2) SSB Interview — 5-day psychological aur group assessment, 900 marks; (3) Medical Examination — comprehensive physical fitness check. Sab stages clear karne ke baad merit list se final selection hoti hai.
