Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is RajDailyTools?+
RajDailyTools is an educational platform that helps students and aspirants prepare for competitive exams in India through detailed guides, eligibility checkers, recruitment updates, and smart calculators — all in one place.
Which exams are covered?+
We currently cover SSC (CGL and GD), Indian Army, Police recruitment, and Railway (RRB) exams, with more categories like UPSC, Banking, Teaching, State PSC, NDA, Air Force, and Navy being added over time.
Are calculators free?+
Yes. Every calculator and guide on RajDailyTools is completely free to use. There are no hidden charges or premium tiers.
Is registration required?+
No. You can access all guides, articles, and calculators without creating an account or logging in.
How often is content updated?+
We regularly review and update our guides to reflect the latest official notifications, eligibility criteria, and exam patterns.
Can I suggest a guide or calculator?+
Absolutely! We welcome suggestions. Email us at rajdailytools@gmail.com with your idea, and if it helps many aspirants, we'll consider building it.
Is my data safe when I use these calculators?+
Yes. Most of our calculators run entirely in your browser — your data never leaves your device. We don't collect, store, or sell personal data.
