BPSC Exam Calendar 2026 – Revised Schedule. The Bihar Public Service Commission has published its revised exam calendar dated 14 August 2026, covering Prelims/MCQ, Mains/Written, Interview and Final Result schedules for various ongoing and upcoming recruitment examinations.

The calendar is divided into five categories based on the examination pattern of each post: 3 Phase Exams (PT + Mains + Interview), 2 Phase Exams (Written + Interview), 2 Phase Exams (PT + Mains), 1 Phase Exams (Only Interview), and 1 Phase Exams (Only Written).

Where the official calendar shows TBD (To Be Decided), NA, or a blank cell, the same has been reproduced exactly as published — no dates have been assumed or added.
