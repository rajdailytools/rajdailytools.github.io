❓ FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Kundli and this free tool.

What is the difference between North Indian and South Indian Kundli?+
Both charts contain the exact same astrological information — the difference is only the visual layout. The North Indian chart uses a diamond-shaped layout where houses are fixed in position and signs rotate. The South Indian chart uses a square grid where signs are fixed and houses rotate. South Indian charts are more popular in Tamil Nadu, Andhra, and Karnataka, while North Indian charts are used across North India. Most astrologers today use both for reference.
What is Vimshottari Dasha?+
Vimshottari Dasha is the most widely used planetary period system in Vedic astrology. It is a 120-year cycle divided among the 9 planets (grahas). Each planet rules a specific period: Sun (6 years), Moon (10 years), Mars (7 years), Rahu (18 years), Jupiter (16 years), Saturn (19 years), Mercury (17 years), Ketu (7 years), Venus (20 years). The period you are born into is determined by your Janma Nakshatra. During each planet's Dasha period, its energy dominates your life events and experiences.
What is Mangal Dosha and is it really dangerous?+
Mangal Dosha (also called Kuja Dosha or Manglik Dosha) occurs when Mars (Mangal) is placed in the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th, or 12th house of the Kundli. It is traditionally associated with challenges in marriage, including conflicts, delays, or health issues for the partner. However, many astrologers consider its impact to be cancelled or reduced in various circumstances — such as when both partners have Mangal Dosha, or when certain planetary combinations are present. It is not considered "dangerous" by modern astrologers but is a factor to be checked before marriage.
What are Yogas in Kundli?+
Yogas are auspicious or inauspicious planetary combinations in a Kundli. There are hundreds of named Yogas in Vedic astrology. Key examples include: Gajakesari Yoga (Jupiter + Moon combination, associated with intelligence and fame), Raj Yoga (ruler of a kendra house in a trikona, associated with power and success), Budhaditya Yoga (Sun + Mercury, associated with sharp intellect), and Hamsa Yoga (Jupiter in specific positions, associated with wisdom and virtue). The presence of powerful Yogas is considered very auspicious.
