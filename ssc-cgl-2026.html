Vacancy Details

The Commission has notified approx. 12,256 tentative vacancies under SSC CGL 2026, across various Group "B" and Group "C" posts in different Ministries/Departments/Organizations of the Government of India and Constitutional/Statutory Bodies/Tribunals. Vacancies are tentative and updated separately on the Commission's website.

Level-7₹44,900 – ₹1,42,40018–30 / 20–30 yearsAssistant Section Officer (CSS/IB/Railways/MEA/AFHQ), Inspector (Income Tax, Central Excise, Preventive Officer, Examiner, Posts)

  • Vacancies are collected and updated by the Commission and published under "For Candidates → Tentative Vacancy" on ssc.gov.in.
  • Reservation for SC, ST, OBC, EWS, ESM and PwBD candidates applies as determined by the respective Indenting Ministries/Departments/Cadres.
  • ESM vacancies are reserved for Group "C" posts only.

Full post-wise & category-wise vacancy break-up is published separately on ssc.gov.in. State-wise/Zone-wise vacancies are not collected by the Commission — candidates should approach the concerned User Department for that information.

Eligibility Criteria

Before applying for SSC CGL 2026, candidates must satisfy the following eligibility conditions as laid down in the official notice:

  • Nationality/Citizenship: As specified in Section 5 below.
  • Age: Varies by post — 18–27, 20–30, 18–30 or 18–32 years as on 01.08.2026 (see Age Limit section).
  • Educational Qualification: Minimum a Bachelor's Degree from a recognized University/Institute, with specific additional requirements for certain posts (see Educational Qualification section).
  • Final-year candidates: Candidates appearing in their final year of graduation can also apply, but must possess the essential qualification as on 01.08.2026.
The Commission makes final allotment of posts on the basis of merit-cum-preference. Once a post is allotted, no change is made due to non-fulfilment of any post-specific physical/medical/educational requirement — candidature is rejected instead. Read post-specific requirements carefully before giving preferences.

Nationality / Citizenship

A candidate must be either:

  • A citizen of India, or
  • A subject of Nepal, or
  • A subject of Bhutan, or
  • A person of Indian origin who has migrated from Pakistan, Burma, Sri Lanka, East African countries of Kenya, Uganda, the United Republic of Tanzania (formerly Tanganyika and Zanzibar), Zambia, Malawi, Zaire, Ethiopia and Vietnam with the intention of permanently settling in India.

A candidate belonging to categories (b), (c) or (d) above must possess a certificate of eligibility issued by the Government of India.

Only male candidates are eligible for any vacancy(ies) reported in the Border Roads Organization (BRO).

Educational Qualification (as on 01.08.2026)

Assistant Audit Officer (Central Cadre)

  • Essential: Bachelor's Degree from a recognized University or Institute.
  • Desirable: CA / Cost & Management Accountant / Company Secretary / M.Com / MBA (Finance) / Masters in Business Economics.

Assistant Audit Officer / Assistant Accounts Officer (State Cadre)

  • Essential: Bachelor's Degree, plus proficiency (reading, writing, speaking & understanding at matriculation level) in the Regional/official language of the concerned State, backed by a certificate from a recognized Board.
  • Desirable: Same as Central Cadre AAO above.

Junior Statistical Officer (JSO)

  • Bachelor's Degree in any subject with at least 60% marks in Mathematics at 12th standard level, OR
  • Bachelor's Degree in any subject with Statistics as one of the subjects at degree level.

Statistical Investigator Grade-II

  • Bachelor's Degree in Statistics, Mathematics, Economics, Demography, Population Studies, Operation Research, IT, Computer Science/Engineering/Technology/Application, Data Science or Artificial Intelligence from a recognized University/Institute.

All Other Posts

  • Bachelor's Degree from a recognized University or equivalent.
Degrees via Open & Distance Learning (ODL) are accepted only if approved by the UGC's Distance Education Bureau for the relevant period. At Document Verification, the result of the qualifying exam must have been declared by 01.08.2026 — mere processing of results is not sufficient.

Attempts

The official notice does not place any restriction on the number of times a candidate may attempt SSC CGL.
  • Candidates may apply every year the exam is conducted, as many times as they wish.
  • The only binding limits are the age limit and educational qualification eligibility for the relevant post/year — once a candidate crosses the upper age limit for a post (accounting for any applicable relaxation), they become ineligible for that post in future cycles.
  • Only one Online Application Form is allowed per candidate per cycle; multiple forms for the same cycle lead to cancellation and debarment.

  • Candidates are shortlisted, category-wise, for Tier-II based on Tier-I marks.
  • In Tier-II, all candidates appear in all four sections of Paper-I; only candidates shortlisted for JSO/Statistical Investigator Grade-II also appear in Paper-II, and only those shortlisted for AAO/Assistant Accounts Officer also appear in Paper-III.
  • It is mandatory to qualify all sections of Paper-I separately.
  • Merit list (FRTA/Final Selection) is prepared on the basis of overall performance in Tier-II Examination only.
  • Posts with specific Physical Standards/Physical Tests/Medical Standards (Inspector CBIC variants, Inspector/Sub-Inspector CBN, SI/JIO NCB, SI CBI, SI NIA, UDC/SSA in BRO etc.) undergo those tests via the User Department after final selection.
  • There is no provision for re-evaluation/re-checking of scores at any Tier of the examination.

Tier-I is a single-session Objective Type, Multiple Choice Computer Based Examination, set in English & Hindi (except English Comprehension).

  • Duration is 1 hour, with a sectional timer of 15 minutes for each section.
  • Candidates eligible for scribe (Para 7.1, 7.2, 7.3) get an extended duration of 1 hour 20 minutes, with sectional timers of 15+5=20 minutes per section.
  • Negative marking: 0.50 marks deducted for each wrong answer.
  • Questions in Parts A, B & C are at Graduation level; Part-D questions are commensurate with Graduation level too, per the official Indicative Syllabus note.

Complete Syllabus (Indicative)

Tier-I: General Intelligence & Reasoning

  • Analogies (semantic, symbolic/number, figural), classification, series, coding-decoding
  • Space visualization & orientation, Venn diagrams, drawing inferences
  • Punched hole/pattern-folding & unfolding, embedded figures, indexing, address matching
  • Problem solving, word building, numerical & symbolic operations, trends, critical thinking, emotional & social intelligence

Tier-I: General Awareness

  • Current events of national & international importance
  • History, Culture, Geography, Economic Scene, General Policy & Scientific Research
  • Everyday scientific observation as expected of any educated person

Tier-I: Quantitative Aptitude

  • Number systems, decimals, fractions, percentages, ratio & proportion, averages
  • Interest, profit & loss, discount, partnership, mixture & alligation, time & distance, time & work
  • Basic algebra, graphs of linear equations, geometry (triangles, circles, tangents), mensuration
  • Trigonometric ratios, heights & distances, histogram, frequency polygon, bar diagram, pie chart

Tier-I: English Comprehension

  • Ability to understand correct English, basic comprehension & writing ability

Tier-II Paper-I, Section-I: Mathematical Abilities

  • Number systems, fundamental arithmetical operations, algebra, geometry, mensuration
  • Trigonometry (ratios, complementary angles, heights & distances, standard identities)
  • Statistics & probability — tables, graphs, measures of central tendency, simple probability

Tier-II Paper-I, Section-I: Reasoning & General Intelligence

  • Semantic/symbolic/number analogy & classification, trends, space orientation, Venn diagrams
  • Pattern folding/unfolding, embedded figures, critical thinking, problem solving, coding-decoding

Tier-II Paper-I, Section-II: English Language & Comprehension

  • Vocabulary, grammar, sentence structure, synonyms/antonyms, spot the error, fill in the blanks
  • Idioms & phrases, one word substitution, active/passive voice, direct/indirect narration
  • Sentence/paragraph shuffling, cloze passage, comprehension passages (at least one book/story-based, two current-affairs based)

Tier-II Paper-I, Section-II: General Awareness

  • Same broad areas as Tier-I General Awareness — History, Culture, Geography, Economic Scene, Polity, Science

Tier-II Paper-I, Section-III: Computer Knowledge / Proficiency

  • Computer basics — CPU, I/O devices, memory, back-up devices, ports, Windows Explorer, keyboard shortcuts
  • MS Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint), internet & email, web browsing, e-banking
  • Networking basics & cyber security — devices, protocols, threats (hacking, virus, worms, trojans), preventive measures

Tier-II Paper-II: Statistics (JSO & Statistical Investigator Grade-II)

  • Collection, classification & presentation of statistical data; measures of central tendency & dispersion
  • Moments, skewness & kurtosis; correlation & regression; probability theory & random variables
  • Sampling theory, statistical inference, analysis of variance, time series analysis, index numbers

Tier-II Paper-III: General Studies — Finance & Economics (AAO/AAO Accounts)

  • Part A — Finance & Accounts: Financial accounting nature/scope, GAAP, single/double entry, bank reconciliation, journal/ledger/trial balance, trading & P&L accounts, balance sheet, depreciation, inventory valuation
  • Part B — Economics & Governance: C&AG constitutional role, Finance Commission, micro-economics basics, demand & supply, production & cost, market forms & price determination, Indian economy (national income, population, poverty & unemployment, infrastructure), economic reforms since 1991, money & banking (RBI, fiscal policy, FRBM Act 2003), role of IT in governance
This is the "Indicative Syllabus" exactly as published in the official notice (Para 13.10 & 13.11). It is intentionally broad — SSC does not prescribe a chapter-wise breakdown beyond these topic areas.

Allowances

The official notice does not itemize allowances. Central Government Group "B"/"C" employees generally receive the standard allowance structure applicable to their cadre, summarized below for general awareness only.
  • Dearness Allowance (DA): Revised twice a year as per Government of India rates.
  • House Rent Allowance (HRA): Slab depends on city classification (X/Y/Z) where quarters are not provided.
  • Transport Allowance (TA): As per pay level and city classification.
  • Other allowances: Special duty allowance, deputation allowance or department-specific allowances, where applicable, as per respective cadre rules.

Job Profile

Detailed job profiles are not described in the recruitment notice — duties are governed by each Ministry/Department/Organization's own service rules. The summary below is general/indicative, based on the nature of each post category.
  • Assistant Audit Officer / Assistant Accounts Officer: Gazetted supervisory audit/accounts role under C&AG offices.
  • Assistant Section Officer / Assistant: Administrative & secretarial functions in Central Secretariat, IB, Railways, MEA, AFHQ and other Ministries.
  • Inspector (Income Tax / Central Excise / Preventive Officer / Examiner): Tax assessment, enforcement, and anti-evasion field/desk duties under CBDT/CBIC.
  • Sub-Inspector (CBI/NIA/CBN/NCB): Investigation and enforcement duties in specialized central agencies.
  • Auditor / Accountant / Tax Assistant: Accounts maintenance, audit support and tax record-keeping in respective offices.
  • Postal/Sorting Assistant: Counter, sorting and administrative operations in Department of Posts.

Promotion

Promotion policy is not detailed in the recruitment notice — it is governed separately by each Ministry/Department's Recruitment Rules and cadre-review policy.

In general, Group "B" & "C" central government employees progress through their department's departmental promotion hierarchy based on seniority, vacancy availability, departmental exams and service record, as per the rules of the respective cadre. Direct recruits to Assistant Audit Officer/Assistant Accounts Officer posts must additionally qualify the "Subordinate Audit/Accounts Service Examination" during probation for confirmation. Candidates should refer to their allotted department's official promotion/cadre rules for exact timelines and eligibility.

Post Preference Guide

  • Preference for various posts and departments is taken through an Online Option Form on the Commission's website, before declaration of the First Round of Tentative Allocation (FRTA)/final result.
  • A candidate will not be considered for a post/department if they have not indicated a preference for it.
  • Options once submitted are final and cannot be changed subsequently under any circumstances.
  • Candidates who do not submit post preferences within the stipulated time will not be considered for any post in the FRTA/result, with no further opportunity granted.
  • Posts with specific Physical Standards/Physical Tests/Medical Standards must be preferenced carefully — candidates must ensure they meet those requirements before opting in, since a post cannot be changed later if they fail post-selection tests.
  • Final allotment is made strictly on the basis of merit-cum-preference.
Study the Department List and post-specific requirements (physical/medical/educational) carefully before the Online Option Form window opens, since preferences cannot be revised once submitted.

Required Documents (at Document Verification)

  • Matriculation / Secondary Certificate.
  • Educational Qualification Certificate (mark sheets for all years/semesters + Provisional/Degree Certificate in original).
  • Caste/Category Certificate, if belonging to a reserved category.
  • Persons with Benchmark Disabilities Certificate, in the prescribed format, if applicable.
  • For Ex-Servicemen: Serving Defence Personnel Certificate (Annexure-VII), Undertaking (Annexure-VIII), and Discharge Certificate if discharged.
  • Relevant certificate if claiming any age relaxation.
  • Certificate for Central Government Civilian Employees, where applicable (Annexure-VI).
  • No Objection Certificate, if already employed in Government/Government undertakings.
  • Change-of-name supporting documents (marriage/divorce/Gazette notification), if applicable.
  • Two recent passport-size colour photographs and one original Photo ID Proof, unless Aadhaar Based Authentication was completed during OTR.
Verification of documents is done against original records — if any information furnished in the application is found wrong at this stage, candidature is rejected forthwith.

Photo Guidelines

You do not need a pre-existing photograph — the Online Application Module captures a live photo via webcam/camera while you fill the form.

  • Find a place with good light and a plain background.
  • Ensure the camera is at eye level before taking the photo.
  • Position yourself directly in front of the camera and look straight ahead.
  • Face must be fully inside the delineated frame — not too close, not too far.
  • No cap, mask or glasses/spectacles while capturing the photo.
  • Never capture a photo of a pre-existing printed photograph — such applications are summarily rejected.
  • Appearance on exam day must match the application photograph — mismatches can lead to being marked "suspicious" and denied entry.
Candidates who opt for Aadhaar-Based Authentication during OTR are exempted from photo-format rejection and don't need to carry printed photos/Photo-ID to the exam venue.

How to Apply — Step by Step

  • Step 1 — One-Time Registration (OTR): Register on the new SSC website (ssc.gov.in). OTR generated on the old ssc.nic.in site will not work — you must re-register.
  • Step 2 — Complete Personal Details: Name, DoB, Father's/Mother's name — exactly as per Matriculation Certificate.
  • Step 3 — Aadhaar or Document-based verification: Opt for Aadhaar-Based Authentication (recommended) or upload Proof of Name/DoB/Address/Gender documents.
  • Step 4 — Create Password & complete Additional Details: Category, nationality, address, disability status (if any).
  • Step 5 — Declare & submit OTR — once generated, it remains valid for all future SSC exams on the new website.
  • Step 6 — Login & fill the Examination Application Form: Educational qualification, examination centre preferences, medium of exam, PwBD/scribe declarations if applicable.
  • Step 7 — Capture live photo & upload signature as per the specifications above.
  • Step 8 — Preview, agree to declaration & submit the form — no edits possible after final submission (except during the correction window).
  • Step 9 — Pay the application fee online (if applicable) before 23 June 2026.
  • Step 10 — Take a printout of the submitted form for your records.

Admit Card

The exact admit-card release date is not specified in the notice — SSC typically releases the Admission Certificate a few days before each Tier, on its Regional Office websites.
  • The Admission Certificate (admit card) is issued separately for Tier-I and Tier-II, downloadable from the relevant SSC Regional Office website.
  • It carries the candidate's photograph, roll number, exam centre, date & shift/session timing.
  • Carry the admit card along with a valid original Photo ID Proof (unless Aadhaar Based Authentication was used) to the exam venue.
  • Report at the exam centre well before the reporting time mentioned on the admit card — late entry is not permitted.

Answer Key

  • Tentative Answer Keys of each Tier are placed on the Commission's website after the respective examination.
  • Candidates may go through the Answer Keys and submit online representations within a stipulated time limit, on payment of ₹100 per question (non-refundable).
  • Representations through any other mode (letter, application, email, etc.) are not entertained.
  • Representations are scrutinized before finalizing the Answer Keys; the Commission's decision is final.
  • The Final Answer Key is used for processing the result and is uploaded after the result of that Tier is declared.
  • The Commission's "Guidelines regarding Challenge/Objection Management System" (Notice dated 10.04.2026) apply to this examination.

Result

  • Tier-I result: category-wise shortlist of candidates qualifying for Tier-II, based on minimum qualifying marks/cut-off.
  • Tier-II result: candidates who qualify all mandatory sections of Paper-I (and Paper-II/III where applicable) are ranked for the Final Result.
  • Marks scored in Computer Based Examinations conducted in multiple shifts are normalized using the Commission's published procedure (Notice dated 02.06.2025), and normalized scores determine final merit and cut-off.
  • Final Result (First Round of Tentative Allocation / FRTA) is prepared on the basis of overall performance in Tier-II Examination only, combined with post preferences submitted via the Online Option Form.
  • No waiting/reserve list is maintained by the Commission after the final result is declared.

Previous Year Papers

Previous year question papers are not part of the official recruitment notice. SSC provides a free Mock Test facility in the Candidate's Corner on ssc.gov.in, based on the current exam pattern.
  • Practising previous years' Tier-I & Tier-II papers helps build familiarity with question difficulty and time management, even though the notified pattern/marks may change year to year — always cross-check against the current official pattern above.
  • Use the free official Mock Test on ssc.gov.in for a pattern-accurate practice experience.
  • For Paper-II (Statistics) and Paper-III (Finance & Economics), focus practice on standard graduate-level textbooks in those subjects alongside past papers, since these papers apply only to JSO/Statistical Investigator Grade-II and AAO/AAO Accounts respectively.

Important Instructions

  • Apply well before the closing date — heavy last-day website load can prevent submission, and the Commission is not responsible for such failures.
  • Only one Online Application Form is allowed per candidate; multiple forms lead to cancellation and debarment.
  • Name, DoB, Father's & Mother's name must exactly match the Matriculation Certificate throughout OTR and application.
  • Keep your application form and other exam-related documents (score/marks, answer response sheet, etc.) for at least 3 years, as per DoPT guidelines.
  • There is no provision for re-evaluation or re-checking of scores at any Tier — no correspondence on this will be entertained.
  • The schedule of examinations in this notice is tentative; any change is communicated only through the Commission's official website.
  • Metric systems of weights and measures are used in the question papers wherever necessary.
  • Toll-Free Helpline for application difficulties: 1800 309 3063.
Frequently Asked Questions

Top questions about SSC CGL 2026, answered from the official notice

What is SSC CGL 2026?+
It is the Staff Selection Commission's notice for the Combined Graduate Level Examination, 2026, for filling various Group "B" and Group "C" posts across Ministries/Departments/Organizations of the Government of India and Constitutional/Statutory Bodies.
What was the last date to apply for SSC CGL 2026?+
Online applications are accepted from 21.05.2026 to 22.06.2026 (23:00 Hrs). Fee payment is allowed till 23.06.2026 (23:00 Hrs).
What is the application fee for SSC CGL 2026?+
₹100, payable online via BHIM UPI, Net Banking, or Visa/Master/Maestro/RuPay Debit Card. Women, SC, ST, PwBD and eligible ESM candidates are exempted.
What is the age limit for SSC CGL 2026?+
It varies by post: 18–27, 20–30, 18–30 or 18–32 years as on 01.08.2026, with age relaxation of 5 years for SC/ST, 3 years for OBC, 10 years for PwBD (higher for PwBD-OBC/SC-ST), and further relaxation for ESM and other specified categories.
How many vacancies are there in SSC CGL 2026?+
Approx. 12,256 tentative vacancies across various Pay Level-4 to Level-8 posts. Updated figures are published separately on ssc.gov.in.
What is the exam pattern for SSC CGL Tier-I?+
100 objective questions across 4 sections (Reasoning, General Awareness, Quantitative Aptitude, English Comprehension), 200 total marks, in 1 hour, with 0.50 negative marking per wrong answer.
What is the exam pattern for SSC CGL Tier-II?+
Paper-I (compulsory for all) has 4 sections — Mathematical Abilities & Reasoning, English & General Awareness, Computer Knowledge Test, and Data Entry Speed Test — totalling 150 questions/450 marks. Paper-II (Statistics) applies only to JSO/Statistical Investigator Grade-II, and Paper-III (Finance & Economics) applies only to AAO/AAO Accounts shortlisted candidates.
What is the salary of an SSC CGL selected candidate?+
Pay Level-4 (₹25,500–₹81,100) to Level-8 (₹47,600–₹1,51,100) of the 7th CPC Pay Matrix, depending on the post allotted. Exact in-hand pay (after DA/HRA/allowances) is not specified in the notice.
What is the selection process for SSC CGL 2026?+
Tier-I (Computer Based Examination) → Tier-II (Paper-I mandatory for all, Paper-II/III for specific posts) → Document Verification / Physical & Medical Standards for specific posts → Final Result (FRTA) based on Tier-II performance and post preference.
Is there a limit on the number of attempts for SSC CGL?+
No. The official notice places no restriction on the number of attempts — only the age limit and educational qualification eligibility apply each year.
Do I need to upload a photograph while applying?+
No pre-existing photo is needed — the application module captures a live photograph via webcam during form-filling, without cap, mask, or spectacles, in good light against a plain background.
What are the signature upload specifications?+
JPEG/JPG format, 10–20 KB file size, approximately 6.0 cm (Width) × 2.0 cm (Height).
What is the minimum qualifying percentage in SSC CGL?+
30% for Unreserved (UR), 25% for OBC/EWS, and 20% for all other categories, in Tier-I and in Sections I–III of Paper-I, Paper-II & Paper-III of Tier-II.
