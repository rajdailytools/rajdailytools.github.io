📖 Complete Guide
Understanding Your Health & Fitness Numbers

🏥 Why Track BMI, TDEE & Daily Habits

Understanding your body's basic numbers — BMI, daily calorie needs (TDEE), and lifestyle impact factors like screen time — is the foundation of any health or fitness goal, whether that's losing weight, building muscle, or simply staying healthy. Most people either skip this step or rely on inconsistent online calculators. RajDailyTools' health hub uses standard medical formulas so your numbers are reliable and comparable to what a clinic would calculate.

Weight loss ho ya muscle gain, sabse pehla step hai apna current BMI aur daily calorie requirement samajhna — tabhi sahi diet aur exercise plan bana sakte ho.

⚖️ BMI & TDEE — The Starting Point

The BMI Calculator gives you your Body Mass Index and health category (underweight, normal, overweight, obese) based on WHO standards. The TDEE Calculator goes a step further — it estimates your Total Daily Energy Expenditure based on your activity level, which is the actual number you should use to plan a calorie deficit (for weight loss) or surplus (for weight gain).

Key insight: BMI alone doesn't account for muscle mass or body composition — use it alongside TDEE and your fitness goals rather than as a standalone health verdict.

🚶 Calorie, Weight Gain & Fat-Burn Tools

The Calories to Weight Gain Converter helps you understand exactly how a calorie surplus translates into expected weight gain over time — useful for anyone on a structured bulking plan. The Steps to Fat Burn Calculator converts your daily step count into an estimated calorie burn, helping walkers and joggers track progress without a fitness band.

📱 Lifestyle & Vision Health

Beyond weight, this hub includes a Screen Time Brain Impact Calculator to understand how daily screen exposure may affect focus and mental fatigue, and a free Color Blindness Test using standard Ishihara-style plates for a quick self-check of color vision.

❓ FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
Is the BMI Calculator accurate for all age groups?
+
The BMI Calculator uses the standard WHO formula suitable for adults. BMI interpretation can differ for children, teenagers, and elderly individuals — consult a doctor for age-specific health assessment.
What formula does the TDEE Calculator use?
+
It uses the widely accepted Mifflin-St Jeor equation combined with your activity level multiplier to estimate your Total Daily Energy Expenditure.
Can I use the Fat Burn Calculator to plan weight loss?
+
Yes — the Steps to Fat Burn Calculator estimates calories burned from walking, which you can combine with the TDEE Calculator to plan a realistic calorie deficit for weight loss.
Is the Color Blindness Test a medical diagnosis?
+
No — it's a quick self-screening tool using standard color plates. For an official diagnosis, please consult an ophthalmologist.
Is my health data stored when I use these calculators?
+
No — all calculations happen entirely in your browser. No height, weight, or health data is sent to any server or stored.
Are these health tools free to use?
+
Yes, 100% free with no login, subscription, or usage limits — use any health calculator as many times as you need.
