Complete Guide

What is a PTE to IELTS Converter?

The PTE to IELTS Converter is a free tool that helps students, immigration applicants, and professionals quickly understand how their PTE Academic score compares to an IELTS band score. Many universities and immigration authorities publish language requirements using IELTS bands — this tool bridges the gap instantly.

If you have a PTE score and want to know whether you meet an IELTS-based requirement, this converter gives you an estimated equivalent in seconds. It is also useful for those evaluating which test to take, or comparing scores across test attempts.

Important: Conversion values are based on widely used equivalency references across the education and immigration sector. They are not officially published by Pearson or British Council / IDP and should be used as an estimate, not a definitive conversion. Always confirm requirements directly with the institution or authority.

Why Convert PTE to IELTS?

Many universities and visa authorities publish their language entry requirements using IELTS bands. If you have taken PTE Academic, you may need to understand your approximate IELTS equivalent to:

  • Check if your PTE score meets a university's IELTS requirement
  • Compare your performance across both tests
  • Plan your English language preparation strategy
  • Understand your CEFR proficiency level
  • Support immigration or skilled migration applications
  • Advise students as a study abroad consultant

Understanding CEFR Levels

The Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR) is a globally recognised standard for describing language proficiency. Both PTE Academic and IELTS align with CEFR levels:

Is the Conversion Exact?

No. PTE Academic and IELTS are different tests measuring English proficiency in different ways. A direct mathematical equivalency does not officially exist. The conversion values in this tool follow commonly accepted score comparison references used across the education and immigration sectors — they are reliable estimates but not guaranteed to be accepted by all institutions.

Final acceptance of a score — whether PTE or IELTS — is always determined by the institution, employer, or immigration authority reviewing your application. Use this tool to plan and compare, then verify requirements directly with your target institution.

PTE Academic vs IELTS: Key Differences

  • PTE Academic is fully computer-based and AI-scored; IELTS includes a face-to-face Speaking component (paper-based format)
  • PTE results are typically available within 48 hours; IELTS results take 3–5 days (paper) or 3–5 days (computer-delivered)
  • Both tests are accepted by thousands of institutions and immigration authorities worldwide
  • PTE is often preferred by candidates who feel more comfortable with technology-based testing; IELTS suits those who prefer human-to-human Speaking interaction
  • PTE Academic is particularly popular in Australia for migration; IELTS is more widely used in the UK and Canada
Frequently Asked Questions

PTE to IELTS Converter FAQs

Common questions about converting PTE scores to IELTS equivalents.

What is a PTE to IELTS Converter?+
A PTE to IELTS Converter is a tool that estimates the equivalent IELTS overall band score from a given PTE Academic overall score. It uses widely accepted score comparison references to help students, professionals, and immigration applicants quickly understand how their PTE performance maps to the IELTS band scale.
Is the PTE to IELTS conversion exact?+
No. The conversion is an estimated equivalency based on commonly referenced comparison tables used in the education and immigration sectors. PTE Academic and IELTS are different tests with different scoring systems. There is no single official conversion published by Pearson or the British Council / IDP. Always verify score requirements with the institution or authority you are applying to.
Can universities accept a PTE-to-IELTS conversion?+
No. Universities that accept both PTE Academic and IELTS will require you to submit an actual score report from the respective test — they do not accept converted scores. This converter is for planning and comparison purposes only. If a university lists an IELTS band requirement and you have taken PTE, check whether they also directly accept PTE and what the minimum PTE score they require.
Does this tool convert section scores?+
No. This tool converts PTE Academic overall scores to estimated IELTS overall band equivalents. It does not perform section-by-section conversions (e.g. PTE Speaking to IELTS Speaking). Section score conversions are not standardised and would require a separate analysis.
Which countries accept PTE for visa applications?+
PTE Academic is accepted by immigration authorities in Australia (Department of Home Affairs), Canada (IRCC), New Zealand (Immigration New Zealand), the United Kingdom (UKVI), and the United States. Many other countries also accept PTE Academic for study, work, and residency applications. Always check the specific score requirement for your visa subclass and destination country.
Is PTE easier than IELTS?+
Neither test is inherently easier — it depends on individual strengths and test preferences. PTE Academic is fully computer-based with AI scoring, which some candidates find more objective and consistent. IELTS includes a human-to-human Speaking interview, which others find more natural. Candidates who type well and prefer technology-driven testing often perform better on PTE; those who prefer face-to-face interaction may do better on IELTS.
What is the equivalent of IELTS 7.0 in PTE?+
IELTS 7.0 is approximately equivalent to a PTE Academic score of 55–62 on the Global Scale of English. This places the candidate at CEFR level C1 — Upper Intermediate to Advanced proficiency. This range meets the entry threshold for many undergraduate and postgraduate programs, as well as most standard visa applications.
What is the equivalent of IELTS 6.5 in PTE?+
IELTS 6.5 is approximately equivalent to a PTE Academic score of 47–54. This corresponds to CEFR level B2 (Upper Intermediate). IELTS 6.5 / PTE 47–54 is commonly required for undergraduate admission and standard visa applications in Australia, Canada, and the UK.
