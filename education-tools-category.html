The Complete Guide to IELTS & PTE Preparation Tools

🎓 Why Score Calculators Matter for IELTS & PTE

IELTS and PTE Academic are the two most trusted English proficiency tests accepted by universities and immigration authorities worldwide. But raw test scores are rarely simple — IELTS averages four section bands with specific rounding rules, while PTE uses a 10-90 scale mapped from multiple skill sub-scores. Most students either miscalculate their own score or rely on rough online guesses. RajDailyTools' education hub gives you official-formula calculators for every section, so you know exactly where you stand before and after the test.

Chahe aap IELTS ka target band calculate kar rahe ho ya PTE ka expected score predict kar rahe ho — har tool actual scoring pattern follow karta hai, taaki aapki study strategy sahi direction mein rahe.

📊 IELTS Tools — Section-Wise & Overall

The IELTS section includes dedicated calculators for Listening, Reading, Writing Task 1 & 2, and Speaking, plus an Overall Band Score Calculator that combines all four correctly. Beyond scoring, there are tools to check university eligibility, visa score requirements, scholarship eligibility, and even a study planner generator that builds a week-by-week plan based on your target band.

Key insight: A common mistake is assuming IELTS overall band is a simple average — it actually follows specific 0.25 rounding rules. Our calculator applies the exact official formula so your self-assessed score matches what IELTS will report.

🗣️ PTE Academic Tools — Score, Readiness & Timeline

PTE Academic candidates get a Score Calculator, Score Predictor, Readiness Checker, and Result Date Calculator to know exactly when to expect results after the test. The PTE to IELTS Converter and IELTS to PTE Converter tools are especially useful for students deciding which test to take, or converting an existing score for a university that only lists one test's requirement.

✅ Who Should Use These Tools

  • IELTS & PTE Test-Takers: Calculate your exact band or score from section-wise results instantly.
  • Study Abroad Aspirants: Check university and visa eligibility before applying to save application fees.
  • Students Choosing Between Tests: Use the IELTS ↔ PTE converters to compare score equivalence.
  • Scholarship Applicants: Verify whether your score meets the minimum requirement for merit scholarships.
  • University Applicants: Use the CGPA to GPA Converter to present your academic record correctly on international applications.

❓ FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
How accurate is the IELTS Overall Band Score Calculator?
+
It uses the exact official IELTS averaging and 0.25 rounding formula across all four sections (Listening, Reading, Writing, Speaking), so your calculated band should match your official IELTS Test Report Form.
Can I convert my IELTS score to PTE or vice versa?
+
Yes — the IELTS to PTE Converter and PTE to IELTS Converter tools use published score-equivalence tables to give you an estimated equivalent score, useful when comparing university requirements listed in only one test format.
Are these education tools free for international students?
+
Yes, 100% free with no login or payment required — usable by students anywhere in the world preparing for IELTS, PTE, or international university applications.
How do I know if I'm ready to book my IELTS or PTE exam?
+
Use the IELTS Exam Readiness Checker or PTE Readiness Checker — enter your current practice scores and target band, and the tool gives an instant readiness assessment along with a suggested preparation timeline.
Does the CGPA to GPA Converter work for all countries?
+
It uses the standard 4.0/10.0 scale conversion widely accepted by US, Canadian and other international universities. Always double-check against your specific university's official conversion policy if it differs.
How long does it take to get an IELTS or PTE result after the test?
+
Use the PTE Result Date Calculator for an estimated PTE result date. IELTS results are generally available within 3-13 days depending on whether you took the paper-based or computer-delivered test.
