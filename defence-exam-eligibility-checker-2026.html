Defence Exam Complete Guide 2026

What is Defence Exam Eligibility and Why It Confuses Everyone

India has some of the most respected armed forces in the world — the Indian Army, Navy, and Airforce — along with powerful paramilitary forces like CRPF, BSF, CISF, and ITBP. Every year, lakhs of young Indians dream of serving the nation in uniform. But the first hurdle everyone faces is the same: am I even eligible to apply?

The confusion is real. India's defence recruitment system has more than 15 different entry schemes, each with its own age limit, height requirement, education qualification, and marital status rule. NDA eligibility is different from CDS eligibility. Agniveer rules are different from SSC GD rules. And then there are gender-wise differences on top of all that.

This is exactly why the defence exam eligibility checker india tool was needed — to put all the criteria in one place and give you a clear answer in seconds.

Always verify final eligibility from official defence recruitment websites — joinindianarmy.nic.in, joinindiannavy.gov.in, airmenselection.cdac.in, and ssc.gov.in — before submitting any application.

NDA — National Defence Academy

The NDA is the most prestigious entry for defence aspirants. Conducted by UPSC twice a year, it leads to a commission as an officer in the Indian Army, Navy, or Airforce after 3 years of training at the National Defence Academy in Khadakwasla, Pune.

NDA eligibility key points: You must be between 16.5 and 19.5 years of age. You must be unmarried. For Army wing, 12th pass in any stream is acceptable, but for Navy and Airforce wings, you must have Physics and Mathematics in 12th. The minimum height is 157 cm. This exam is only for male candidates as of 2026 (Supreme Court has allowed female candidates, implementation ongoing).

The biggest beginner mistake is checking for NDA after turning 19.5 — the window is narrow, so you must start preparing in class 11 itself. Always verify nda cds afcat eligibility criteria 2026 from official website par before applying.

CDS — Combined Defence Services

CDS is the officer-level entry for graduates. Also conducted by UPSC twice a year, it leads to selection for IMA (Army), INA (Navy), AFA (Airforce), and OTA (Officers Training Academy for SSC commission).

CDS eligibility key points: Age range is 19–25 years (varies by wing). Minimum qualification is graduation in any stream — but for Airforce and Navy, technical/science background is preferred. The OTA entry is open to graduates of any stream. Marital status restriction applies for Airforce and Navy entries only.

CDS is often overlooked by commerce and arts graduates who think defence is only for science students — but OTA entry at CDS is open to all graduates. This is one of the most important facts for army navy airforce eligibility kaise check kare purposes.

AFCAT — Air Force Common Admission Test

AFCAT is the Indian Airforce's own entry scheme for Flying Branch, Ground Duty (Technical), and Ground Duty (Non-Technical) branches. It is conducted twice a year.

AFCAT eligibility key points: Age is 20–26 years. Minimum 60% marks in graduation is required. For Flying Branch, Physics and Mathematics in 12th is mandatory. Height minimum is 162.5 cm for male candidates. Correctable vision is acceptable for non-flying branches.

SSC GD — Staff Selection Commission General Duty

SSC GD is the entry for Constable General Duty post in paramilitary forces — CRPF, BSF, CISF, ITBP, SSB, NIA, SSF, and ASSAM Rifles. It is one of the most applied defence exams in India.

SSC GD eligibility key points: Age range is 18–23 years (relaxation for reserved categories). Minimum education is only 10th pass — making it accessible to the widest pool of candidates. Height requirement is 170 cm for male (General), 157 cm for female. No marital restriction applies here. This makes SSC GD the most inclusive defence exam in India.

Agniveer — Army, Navy, Airforce

The Agniveer scheme, introduced in 2022 under the Agnipath scheme, allows youth to serve in the armed forces for four years as Agniveers, with 25% being retained for regular service. It covers Army (5 posts), Navy (2 posts), and Airforce (1 post).

  • Agniveer GD: 10th pass (45% aggregate, 33% each subject), age 17.5–21 years, height 170 cm
  • Agniveer Clerk/Store Keeper Technical (SKT): 12th pass (60% overall, 50% each subject), age 17.5–21, height 162 cm
  • Agniveer Technical: 12th Science with Physics, Chemistry, Maths (50% marks), age 17.5–21
  • Agniveer Nursing Assistant (NA): 12th Science with Physics, Chemistry, Biology (50% marks)
  • Agniveer Toposurvey: 12th with Maths and Science subjects

The Agniveer scheme has brought defence services closer to youth from rural and semi-urban areas. Agniveer eligibility kya hai is one of the most searched questions in India. Always verify age cut-off dates from official website par before applying.

CAPF AC — Central Armed Police Forces Assistant Commandant

CAPF AC is an officer-level entry conducted by UPSC for BSF, CRPF, CISF, SSB, and ITBP in the Assistant Commandant rank. Age range is 20–25 years. Graduation in any stream is required. Height minimum is 165 cm for male.

JCO — Junior Commissioned Officer (Religious Teacher / Pandit)

JCO Religious Teacher entry is unique — it is for candidates with religious qualifications (Sanskrit/Urdu/Gurbani etc.) who can serve as Pandits, Maulvis, Granthi, Padri, etc. in the Army. Age range is 27–34 years, and graduation along with relevant religious qualifications is required. Height minimum is 157 cm.

Havaldar — Education and Surveyor

Army Havaldar Education posts require graduation (B.Ed preferred) and are for candidates aged 20–25 years. Army Havaldar Surveyor requires 12th with Mathematics and Science. These are technical support roles within the Army structure.

Common Beginner Mistakes in Defence Exam Eligibility

  • Applying for NDA after age 19.5 — the window is strict
  • Not knowing that CDS OTA is open to all graduates including Arts/Commerce
  • Assuming AFCAT requires engineering — 60% graduation in any stream qualifies for Ground Duty
  • Forgetting that SSC GD has a very strict height requirement of 170 cm for male
  • Thinking Agniveer is only one post — there are 5+ posts with different education requirements
  • Not checking the medical eligibility (eye standards especially for AFCAT Flying)

How to Choose the Best Defence Exam for You

The best defence exam for you depends on three things: your age, your education, and your physical fitness. Here is a simple decision guide:

  • If you are in 11th–12th (Science with Maths): Start NDA preparation immediately — it is the earliest and most prestigious entry
  • If you just passed 10th: Aim for SSC GD and Agniveer GD simultaneously
  • If you are a graduate in any stream: CDS (OTA) and CAPF are your primary targets
  • If you are a graduate in Science/Engineering: CDS (Army/Navy/AF), AFCAT, and technical Agniveer posts all apply
  • If you are 25–34 years old: JCO Religious Teacher is a unique opportunity if you have religious qualifications

Physical Preparation is Just as Important as Written Exam

Many candidates crack the written exam but fail at the physical fitness test, medical board, or SSB interview. Simultaneously preparing your physical fitness — running, push-ups, pull-ups, swimming — is absolutely essential for any defence exam. Running timing for SSC GD is 1.6 km in 5.45 minutes for male candidates aged 18–23. Prepare from day one.

Frequently Asked Questions
Kya main NDA ke liye eligible hoon agar mera subject Arts hai?+
NDA ke liye Army wing mein Arts/Commerce 12th wale eligible hain. Lekin Navy aur Airforce wing ke liye 12th mein Physics aur Mathematics hona compulsory hai. Arts stream candidates sirf Army NDA ke liye apply kar sakte hain. Verify karo official website par before applying.
SSC GD aur Agniveer GD mein kya difference hai eligibility ka?+
SSC GD paramilitary forces (CRPF, BSF, CISF etc.) ke liye hai — age 18–23 years, 10th pass required, height 170 cm (male). Agniveer GD Indian Army ke liye hai — age 17.5–21 years, 10th with 45% marks required, height 170 cm. Dono alag organizations hain, alag selection process hai, aur Agniveer sirf 4 year contract hai while SSC GD is a permanent career.
CDS ke liye kya sirf science graduates apply kar sakte hain?+
Nahi! CDS ke OTA (Officers Training Academy) post ke liye kisi bhi stream ke graduates — Arts, Commerce, Science — apply kar sakte hain. Army wing bhi any degree accept karta hai. Sirf Navy aur Airforce wings ke liye technical/science background preferred hoti hai. Isliye CDS arts aur commerce graduates ke liye bhi ek excellent opportunity hai.
AFCAT mein height kitni chahiye aur kya glasses wale apply kar sakte hain?+
AFCAT ke liye male candidates ki minimum height 162.5 cm aur female ke liye 152 cm honi chahiye. Flying branch ke liye strict eye standards hain (6/6 normal vision required). Lekin Ground Duty Technical aur Ground Duty Non-Technical posts ke liye correctable vision acceptable hai (spectacles allowed up to certain power). Official website par current medical standards verify karo.
Agniveer scheme mein kitne posts hain aur sabke alag criteria hain kya?+
Haan, Agniveer mein multiple posts hain with alag eligibility: (1) Agniveer GD — 10th pass, height 170 cm; (2) Agniveer Clerk/SKT — 12th 60% any stream; (3) Agniveer Technical — 12th Science with Phy+Chem+Maths 50%; (4) Agniveer Nursing Assistant — 12th with Bio+Phy+Chem; (5) Agniveer Toposurvey — 12th Maths+Science. Navy aur Airforce mein bhi Agniveer posts hain alag criteria ke saath.
Kya married candidates defence exams ke liye apply kar sakte hain?+
Depends on the exam: NDA, Agniveer — strictly unmarried only. CDS Airforce/Navy wings — unmarried preferred. SSC GD, CAPF, JCO, Havaldar — married candidates eligible hain. JCO Religious Teacher ke liye actually 27–34 age range hai to married hona quite likely hota hai. Har exam ki specific notification se verify karo.
Agar main height mein thoda short hoon to kya koi relaxation milti hai?+
Haan! Kuch regions ke candidates ko height relaxation milti hai — jaise North Eastern states, Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, J&K, Sikkim, Ladakh etc. ke candidates ko 2–5 cm ki relaxation typically milti hai. SC/ST candidates ko bhi kuch exams mein relaxation milti hai. Exact relaxation percentage official notification se verify karo official website par. Physically training karo — proper posture bhi height measurement mein help karta hai.
