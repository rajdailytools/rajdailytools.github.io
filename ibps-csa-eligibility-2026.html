Nationality / Citizenship

As per the Official IBPS CRP CSA-XVI Notification

IBPS Customer Service Associate (CSA) Recruitment 2026 ke liye candidate ko notification me diye gaye nationality/citizenship criteria ko fulfil karna zaroori hai. Candidate nimnlikhit categories me se kisi ek category ka hona chahiye:

  • Citizen of India.
  • Subject of Nepal.
  • Subject of Bhutan.
  • Tibetan Refugee jo 01 January 1962 se pehle India aaye hon aur permanently India me settle hone ka intention rakhte hon.
  • Person of Indian Origin (PIO) jo Pakistan, Burma (Myanmar), Sri Lanka, Kenya, Uganda, United Republic of Tanzania, Zambia, Malawi, Zaire (Democratic Republic of Congo), Ethiopia ya Vietnam se permanently India me settle hone ke intention se migrate hue hon.

Important: Nepal, Bhutan, Tibetan Refugee aur Person of Indian Origin categories ke candidates ke paas Government of India dwara issue ki gayi Certificate of Eligibility hona chahiye.

Age Limit (As on 01 August 2026)

As per the Official IBPS CRP CSA-XVI Notification

The age of the candidate will be calculated as on 01 August 2026. Candidates must satisfy the prescribed minimum and maximum age limits mentioned in the official IBPS notification.

  • Minimum Age: 20 Years
  • Maximum Age: 28 Years
  • Candidates must have been born not earlier than 02 August 1998 and not later than 01 August 2006 (both dates inclusive).

The Date of Birth mentioned in the online application and supported by valid documentary proof will be considered for determining eligibility. Candidates must ensure that all documents match the details entered in the application form.

Important Note: Always calculate your age using the official cut-off date 01 August 2026. Category-wise age relaxation is available only for eligible candidates as specified in the official IBPS notification.

Educational Qualification

As per the Official IBPS CRP CSA-XVI Notification

Candidates applying for IBPS Customer Service Associate (CSA) Recruitment 2026 must possess a Bachelor's Degree (Graduation) in any discipline from a University recognized by the Government of India or any equivalent qualification recognized by the Central Government.

The candidate must possess the required educational qualification on or before the date of online registration mentioned in the official notification. Candidates should have the valid mark sheet or degree certificate while filling out the online application form.

During online registration, candidates will be required to enter the percentage of marks obtained in Graduation and provide details of the qualifying examination. All educational information entered in the application form must exactly match the supporting documents.

Original educational certificates, mark sheets and other supporting documents must be produced during document verification or at any stage of the recruitment process whenever required by IBPS or the Participating Bank.

Reservation Rules

Reservation benefits under IBPS Customer Service Associate (CSA) Recruitment 2026 are available for eligible candidates belonging to Scheduled Castes (SC), Scheduled Tribes (ST), Other Backward Classes (OBC - Non Creamy Layer), Economically Weaker Sections (EWS), Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD), Ex-Servicemen (ESM) and Disabled Ex-Servicemen (DESM) in accordance with the Government of India guidelines and the official IBPS CRP CSA-XVI Notification.

Candidates claiming reservation or age relaxation must possess valid category certificates issued by the competent authority in the prescribed Government of India format. Original certificates must be produced whenever required during document verification, joining or any subsequent stage of the recruitment process.

Candidates belonging to the OBC category must possess a valid Non-Creamy Layer (NCL) certificate. OBC candidates falling under the Creamy Layer are not eligible for OBC reservation and must apply under the General category.

Application Fee

The online application fee for IBPS CSA 2026 is:

  • ₹175/- (Inclusive of GST) for SC, ST, PwBD, Ex-Servicemen (ESM) and Disabled Ex-Servicemen (DESM) candidates.
  • ₹850/- (Inclusive of GST) for General, OBC and EWS candidates.

The application fee must be paid online during the registration process. Once paid, the fee is generally non-refundable and cannot be adjusted against any other recruitment process.

Important Official Eligibility Rules

  • Candidates must satisfy all the prescribed eligibility conditions on the dates specified in the Official IBPS CRP CSA-XVI Notification before submitting the online application.
  • Merely applying for IBPS CSA, qualifying the Preliminary/Main Examination or receiving provisional allotment does not guarantee appointment. The participating bank has the final authority to verify eligibility before recruitment.
  • Candidates must produce original documents supporting their age, educational qualification, nationality, category and other eligibility claims during document verification, joining or whenever required by IBPS or the Participating Bank.
  • No change in category or other eligibility details will be permitted after the online application is finally submitted and the Edit Window closes.
  • Candidates claiming reservation or age relaxation must possess valid certificates in the prescribed Government of India format. OBC candidates must also produce a valid Non-Creamy Layer certificate wherever applicable.
  • Incorrect information, false claims or failure to produce the required documents may result in cancellation of candidature at any stage of the recruitment process.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common IBPS CSA Eligibility 2026 questions answered as per the Official IBPS CRP CSA-XVI Notification

What is the minimum educational qualification for IBPS CSA 2026?+
Candidates must possess a Bachelor's Degree (Graduation) in any discipline from a University recognized by the Government of India or an equivalent qualification recognized by the Central Government. The candidate must possess the qualification on the date specified in the official notification.
What is the age limit for IBPS CSA 2026?+
Candidates must be between 20 and 28 years of age as on 01 August 2026. They should have been born between 02 August 1998 and 01 August 2006 (both dates inclusive).
Is age relaxation available for reserved category candidates?+
Yes. Age relaxation is available for SC, ST, OBC (Non-Creamy Layer), PwBD, Ex-Servicemen and other eligible categories as per Government of India and IBPS rules.
Can final year students apply for IBPS CSA 2026?+
No. Candidates must possess the required Graduation qualification on or before the date specified in the official notification. Candidates awaiting their final result are not eligible.
Is computer knowledge mandatory for IBPS CSA 2026?+
Yes. Candidates should possess working knowledge of computer systems as specified in the official notification.
Is local language proficiency compulsory?+
Yes. Candidates must satisfy the local language proficiency requirement of the State or Union Territory for which they apply, as mentioned in the official notification.
Can candidates apply for more than one State?+
No. A candidate can apply for only one State or Union Territory under the IBPS CSA recruitment process.
Who can apply under the nationality criteria?+
Indian Citizens, subjects of Nepal or Bhutan, eligible Tibetan Refugees and certain Persons of Indian Origin, subject to the conditions specified in the official notification, are eligible.
What documents are required to prove eligibility?+
Candidates must produce valid documents supporting age, educational qualification, nationality, category, reservation and any other eligibility claim during document verification.
Can candidates with a distance education degree apply?+
Yes, provided the degree is recognized by the Government of India or the competent authority as specified in the official notification.
What happens if a candidate is found ineligible later?+
If a candidate does not satisfy the prescribed eligibility conditions at any stage, the candidature may be cancelled by IBPS or the participating bank.
Is there any limit on the number of attempts?+
The official notification does not prescribe any limit on the number of attempts. Candidates may apply as long as they satisfy all eligibility conditions.
What is the cut-off date for determining eligibility?+
Eligibility conditions such as age and educational qualification are determined according to the cut-off dates mentioned in the official IBPS CSA notification.
Can foreign nationals apply for IBPS CSA?+
Only candidates covered under the nationality conditions prescribed in the official notification are eligible to apply.
Where can I download the official IBPS CSA notification?+
The official IBPS CSA notification can be downloaded from the official IBPS website.
