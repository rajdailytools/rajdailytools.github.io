Introduction

The Government Institute of Medical Sciences (GIMS), Greater Noida has invited online applications for direct recruitment to the Non-Teaching post of Staff Nurse vide Advertisement No. GIMS/2026/Manpower/NT-03/10 dated 09.08.2026. Selection to this post will be made through a Computer Based Test (CBT) Examination – 2026.

This page covers only the official Exam Pattern and Selection Process for GIMS Noida Staff Nurse Recruitment 2026, extracted strictly from the official information brochure. For eligibility, age limit, application fee, important dates and other topics, please use the linked pages below.

GIMS Noida Staff Nurse Exam Pattern 2026

Official Computer Based Test (CBT) pattern, as per the information brochure

As per the official brochure, candidates for the post of Staff Nurse will be selected through an examination conducted by a designated examination body (Examining Body), as decided by the Chairman. No candidate will be permitted to enter the examination without an admit card issued by the Examining Body.

The examination will be of objective type, containing questions related to Nursing, of which 80% will be based on theoretical knowledge and 20% on practical knowledge.

Important Examination Rules

Key CBT rules stated in the official brochure

GIMS Noida Staff Nurse Selection Process 2026

Official stages of the recruitment process, as per the information brochure

01
Provisional Admission to CBT
Candidates are given provisional admission to the Computer Based Test (CBT) on the basis of information furnished by them in the online application.
02
Computer Based Test (CBT)
An objective-type CBT is conducted by the designated Examining Body — 100 questions, 100 marks, with negative marking of 0.25 for each wrong answer.
03
Merit List Preparation
The Examining Body prepares a list of candidates in order of merit based on marks obtained in the CBT, keeping in view due representation of SC/ST and other categories as per Rule 6, and forwards it to the appointing authority.
04
Biometric & Document Verification
Shortlisted candidates undergo biometric verification and document verification from the concerned authorities. No TA/DA is admissible for attending this stage.
05
Medical Examination
Candidates selected for appointment are required to undergo the medical examination prescribed under the rules.
06
Final Selection
The final process of selection is based on Merit in CBT, Biometric & Document Verification, and Medical Examination.

Final Selection & Merit

As per the official brochure, the designated Examining Body prepares a list of candidates in order of merit based on the marks obtained by them in the CBT, keeping in view the need to ensure due representation of Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes and other categories as per Rule 6. As many candidates as deemed appropriate for appointment are recommended, and the list is forwarded to the appointing authority.

  • Marks Considered: Merit is based on marks obtained in the Computer Based Test (CBT).
  • Qualifying Requirement: Candidates must secure at least 40% (UR/EWS/OBC) or 35% (SC/ST) marks.
  • Tie-Breaking: If two or more candidates obtain equal marks, the candidate older in age (date of birth) is placed higher in the merit list.
  • Document Verification: Biometric and document verification is carried out from the concerned authorities.
  • Medical Examination: Selected candidates must undergo the required medical examination before final appointment.

The Institute grants provisional admission to the CBT based on information furnished by candidates; if a candidate is later found ineligible or their application was not fit to be accepted, the candidature (or even an appointment decision already taken) is liable to be cancelled.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers based on the official GIMS Noida Staff Nurse information brochure

GIMS Noida Staff Nurse exam kis mode me hoti hai?+
Selection Computer Based Test (CBT) ke madhyam se, online mode me hota hai, jaisa ki official brochure me diya gaya hai.
CBT me kitne questions aur kitne marks honge?+
Official brochure ke anusaar question paper me 100 questions honge, har question 1 mark ka hoga, aur total marks 100 honge.
Exam duration kitni hai?+
Official brochure ke exam-pattern section me exam duration specify nahi ki gayi hai; detailed schedule ke liye official website www.gims.ac.in dekhein.
Kya negative marking hai?+
Haan, har galat jawab ke liye us prashn ke liye nirdharit kul anko ka 1/4 (0.25 mark) kaata jayega. Agar koi prashn hal nahi kiya jata ya unanswered chhoda jata hai, to uske liye koi negative marking nahi hogi.
Minimum qualifying marks kya hain?+
General, EWS aur OBC candidates ke liye 40% aur SC/ST candidates ke liye 35% minimum qualifying marks nirdharit hain.
Exam paper kis language me hoga?+
Exam paper Hindi aur English dono bhashaon me uplabdh hoga. Kisi bhi vivad ki sthiti me English version ko final mana jayega.
Selection process ke stages kya hain?+
Final selection CBT me merit, Biometric & Document Verification, aur Medical Examination — in teen aadharon par hoga.
Barabar marks hone par merit list kaise banegi?+
Agar do ya adhik candidates barabar marks prapt karte hain, to age me bade (janm tithi ke aadhar par) candidate ko list me upar rakha jayega.
"Mark for Review" option kya hai?+
CBT ke dauran candidates kisi bhi prashn ko dobara dekhne ke liye "Mark for Review" option ka upyog kar sakte hain, lekin sirf usi active section ke nirdharit samay ke bhitar. Section submit hone ke baad review sambhav nahi hai.
Admit card ke bina exam me baith sakte hain?+
Nahi, kisi bhi candidate ko exam me pravesh nahi diya jayega jab tak unke paas nirdharit Examining Body dwara jaari admit card na ho.
