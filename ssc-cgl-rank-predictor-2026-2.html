Key Information

Important Notes – SSC CGL 2026

📅
Key Dates
Application: 21 May – 22 June 2026. Fee payment deadline: 23 June 2026. Tier-I tentatively in August–September 2026. Tier-II in December 2026.
💰
Application Fee
Fee is ₹100 only. SC, ST, PwBD, ESM (eligible for reservation), and Women candidates are fully exempted. Payment via BHIM UPI, Net Banking, or debit card.
🎓
Qualification
Essential qualification is a Bachelor's Degree from a recognised university as on 01-08-2026. For AAO/AAcO posts, desirable qualification is CA/CMA/CS/M.Com.
⚠️
Negative Marking
Tier-I: 0.50 marks deducted per wrong answer. Tier-II Paper-I Section-I & II: 1 mark deducted per wrong answer. Paper-II & III: 0.50 marks deducted per wrong answer.
📋
Vacancy Information
Final post-wise and category-wise vacancies will be available on ssc.gov.in under "For Candidates → Tentative Vacancy". SSC does not decide vacancy count.
🔄
Form Correction Window
Application form correction window available from 29 June – 1 July 2026 (23:00 hours). Correction charges apply. No change allowed after this window closes.
