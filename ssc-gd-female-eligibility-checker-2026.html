SSC GD Female Eligibility — Complete Guide 2026

SSC GD Female Eligibility Kya Hoti Hai?

SSC GD (General Duty) Constable recruitment mein female candidates ke liye alag eligibility criteria set kiya gaya hai. Male candidates se comparison mein female candidates ke liye height standard kam hai, running distance kam hai, aur chest measurement requirement nahi hai. Lekin age limit, qualification aur medical standards similar structure follow karte hain — category-wise relaxation ke saath.

Key Point: SSC GD female candidates ke liye chest measurement completely waived hai — ye ek common confusion hai. Bahut female candidates ye galat samajhti hain ki chest measurement compulsory hai — bilkul nahi. Running mein sirf 1.6 km — 8 min 30 sec mein complete karni hai.

SSC GD Female Age Limit Explained

SSC GD female age limit 18 to 23 years hai (General category). Age upper limit per notification date calculated hoti hai — DOB se, not application date. Category relaxation:

  • OBC (Non-Creamy Layer): 3 extra years → upper limit 26 years
  • SC/ST: 5 extra years → upper limit 28 years
  • EWS: Same as General (no additional relaxation beyond category-wise norms)
  • Ex-Servicemen/Dependents: Additional relaxation as per service rules
  • J&K residents: Additional 5 years in some recruitments — notification check karo

SSC GD Female Height Requirement — Region Wise

Female height standards alag-alag regions ke candidates ke liye different hain:

  • General/OBC/EWS (most states): 157 cm minimum
  • SC/ST from specified states: 150 cm (5 cm relaxation)
  • NE states (Assam, Meghalaya, Arunachal, Manipur, Mizoram, Nagaland, Tripura, Sikkim): 150 cm
  • Gorkha/Garhwali/Kumaoni and similar hill communities: 155 cm
  • Scheduled / Tribal areas: Notification-specific relaxation
✅ Good News for NE Candidates
Northeast state female candidates ke liye height 150 cm tak relaxed hai — 7 cm ka significant relaxation hai general standard se. Category ne independent hai ye relaxation — state se milti hai.
⚠️ Running Preparation Alert
1.6 km in 8:30 min seem simple lagti hai — lekin bina practice ke PET clear karna mushkil ho sakta hai. Roz practice shuru karo 2-3 months pehle se — ek bhi second zyada = fail.
👩 Medical Preparation Tip
Eyesight aur color vision advance mein check karwao. Color blindness SSC GD mein disqualifying condition hai. Advance mein test karwao — surprises avoid karo medical mein.
📋 Documents Ready Rakhna
10th/12th certificate, DOB proof, category certificate (if applicable), domicile certificate, aadhar card, passport photo — sab advance mein prepare karo. Verification mein problem na aaye.

SSC GD Female Physical Test (PET/PST) Details

Physical test two parts mein hota hai — PET (Physical Efficiency Test) aur PST (Physical Standard Test):

  • PET (Running): 1.6 km in 8 minutes 30 seconds. Qualifying mandatory — one chance only. No retake if fail.
  • PST (Height): Minimum height as per category and region. Barefoot measurement — shoes not allowed.
  • PST (Weight): Proportional to height — medical board decides. No fixed number given officially.
  • No Chest Measurement: Completely waived for female candidates — no expansion test required.

SSC GD Female Medical Standards

Medical examination mein female candidates ke liye ye checks hote hain:

  • Vision: 6/6 each eye without glasses (or correctable to standard with glasses for some posts)
  • Color Vision: Must be normal — Ishihara plate test se check hota hai
  • Hearing: Normal — audiometric test possible
  • Flat foot check: Grade 1 often acceptable, higher grades may disqualify
  • No varicose veins, no major chronic diseases, no physical deformities

SSC GD Female Preparation Tips — Practical Guide

  • Running Preparation: Roz subah 30-45 min running practice karo 2-3 months pehle se. Week 1-2: 800m jog. Week 3-4: 1.2 km. Week 5-6: Full 1.6 km timed. Week 7+: Consistency + speed build karo.
  • Written Exam: GK daily, Maths basics (percentage, ratio, SI/CI), Reasoning practice, English/Hindi grammar — daily 2-3 hours minimum.
  • Medical Preparation: Eyesight check karo advance mein, healthy diet maintain karo, weight proportional rakhna, hydration proper.
  • Documents: Sab documents advance mein ready karo — attestation karwao where needed. Missing document = rejection risk at verification.

SSC GD mein female ke liye chest measurement hoti hai?+

Female candidates ko sirf height, weight (proportional), aur running test clear karna hota hai PST/PET mein. Ye ek common misconception hai — chest test female ke liye nahi hota.

SSC GD female selection process: (1) Online application form fill karo — eligibility verify karo pehle (is tool se). (2) CBT (Computer Based Test) — objective written exam, 160 marks, same paper for male and female. (3) PET — 1.6 km in 8:30 min (female), qualifying. (4) PST — height and weight verification. (5) Medical examination — eyesight, color vision, overall health. (6) Document verification. (7) Final merit list based on CBT marks. Har stage qualifying hai — ek fail = process over.
