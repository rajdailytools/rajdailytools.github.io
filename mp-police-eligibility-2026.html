Complete Guide

MP Police Kya Hai & Eligibility Explained 2026

MP Police Constable ki eligibility, selection process aur common mistakes — sab kuch detail mein.

MP Police Constable Kya Hota Hai?

MP Police Constable — Madhya Pradesh Police Constable recruitment — Madhya Pradesh ke police department mein constable level posts ke liye state-level recruitment hai. Ye Madhya Pradesh Employee Selection Board (MPESB) dwara conduct ki jaati hai.

Selection process mein Written Test, Physical Standard Test (PST), Physical Efficiency Test (PET), aur Medical Examination shamil hote hain. MP Police constable ek statewide recruitment hai jisme lakhs candidates participate karte hain.

Official MP Police notifications ke liye visit karein: mpesb.mp.gov.in — latest aur accurate information wahan milegi.

Age Eligibility — Category-wise Relaxation

MP Police Constable ke liye age requirements kuch is tarah hain:

  • General/EWS candidates: 18–25 years (Male and Female)
  • OBC candidates: 18–28 years (+3 years relaxation)
  • SC/ST candidates: 18–30 years (+5 years relaxation)
  • MP domicile candidates ko additional relaxation milti hai — notification check karein

Height & Chest Criteria (Category + Region)

  • General/OBC/EWS male: 167 cm | female: 152 cm
  • SC/ST male: 162 cm (5 cm relaxation) | female: 147 cm (5 cm relaxation)
  • ST Area/Tribal area: Same as SC/ST (162/147 cm)
  • Chest (male only): 81 cm unexpanded + 5 cm expansion (General/OBC)
  • Chest (SC/ST/ST Area male): 79 cm unexpanded + 5 cm expansion
Important: ST Area / Tribal area ke candidates ko height aur chest mein relaxation milti hai — lekin valid domicile ya tribal area certificate required hai. Without certificate, General category rules apply honge.

Selection Process — 4 Stages

📝
Written Test
Online CBT — General Knowledge, Hindi, Maths, Reasoning
📏
Physical Standard Test
Height, Chest (Male), Weight verification at venue
🏃
Physical Efficiency Test
800m race (Male), 400m race (Female), Long jump, Shot put
🏥
Medical Examination
Vision, hearing, color blindness, overall fitness check

Common Mistakes Candidates Karte Hain

❌ Galat Region Select Karna
ST area ke candidates agar "General" region select karte hain toh relaxation lose ho jaati hai — as happened with many candidates.
❌ Education Check Na Karna
MP Police ke liye 12th Pass required hai — 10th Pass candidates directly apply nahi kar sakte.
❌ Chest Prep Ignore Karna
Male candidates chest expansion practice nahi karte — 81 cm unexpanded + 5 cm expansion dono required hain.
❌ Old Notification Follow Karna
Criteria har notification mein thoda change ho sakta hai — always latest MPESB notification verify karein.
Pro Tip: Written exam ke marks matter karte hain — higher marks better document verification mein advantage dete hain. Prepare well for both written and physical simultaneously.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

MP Police eligibility ke baare mein common sawaal.

MP Police ke liye age limit kitni hai 2026 mein?+
MP Police Constable 2026 ke liye minimum age 18 years aur maximum age category ke hisaab se: General/EWS: 25 years, OBC: 28 years (+3), SC/ST: 30 years (+5). MP domicile candidates ko additional relaxation milti hai. Age calculation official notification date par hoti hai — cut-off date dekh ke calculate karein.
MP Police mein height kitni honi chahiye?+
MP Police height criteria: General/OBC/EWS Male: 167 cm, Female: 152 cm. SC/ST Male: 162 cm (5 cm relaxation), Female: 147 cm (5 cm relaxation). ST Area/Tribal area candidates ko bhi same 5 cm relaxation milti hai — valid tribal area certificate required hai is relaxation ke liye.
Girls ke liye MP Police criteria kya hai?+
Female candidates ke liye MP Police criteria: Age: 18–25 (GEN), 18–28 (OBC), 18–30 (SC/ST). Height: 152 cm (GEN/OBC/EWS), 147 cm (SC/ST/ST area). Chest measurement females ke liye required nahi hoti. Physical test (PET) mein female candidates ke liye 400m race, long jump, aur shot put hote hain — race time aur distance male se different hoti hai.
MP Police mein chest kitna hona chahiye?+
Chest measurement sirf male candidates ke liye required hai. General/OBC/EWS: 81 cm unexpanded + minimum 5 cm expansion = 86 cm expanded. SC/ST/ST area: 79 cm unexpanded + 5 cm expansion. Daily breathing exercises se chest expansion improve ki ja sakti hai. Medical test mein officer measure karta hai — dono measurements (unexpanded and expanded) noted hoti hain.
ST area ke candidates ko MP Police mein kya relaxation milti hai?+
ST Area / Tribal Area ke candidates ko height mein 5 cm aur chest mein 2 cm ki relaxation milti hai — same as SC/ST category. Height: 162 cm (male), 147 cm (female). Chest: 79 cm unexpanded. Lekin age relaxation ST category SE ALAG hai — ST area candidates ko age mein koi extra relaxation nahi milti unless woh SC/ST category mein bhi hain. Valid tribal area domicile certificate required hai.
MP Police ke liye minimum education kya chahiye?+
MP Police Constable ke liye minimum qualification 12th Pass (Higher Secondary) hai — MP Board ya any recognized board se. 10th Pass candidates directly apply nahi kar sakte. Graduation hone se additional benefit nahi milta (constable level recruitment hai). Valid 12th marksheet aur certificate PST/document verification mein required hota hai.
MP Police physical test mein kya hota hai?+
MP Police PET (Physical Efficiency Test) mein: Male: 800m race (specific time limit), Long Jump (minimum distance), Shot Put throw. Female: 400m race (specific time), Long Jump, Shot Put (lighter ball). PET qualifying nature ka hota hai — written exam marks final merit mein count hote hain. PET pass karna mandatory hai selection ke liye.
OBC candidates ko height mein relaxation milti hai?+
Nahi — OBC candidates ko height mein koi relaxation nahi milti. OBC ke liye height criteria General category ke barabar hai — Male 167 cm, Female 152 cm. OBC ko sirf age mein 3 years ki relaxation milti hai (maximum 28 years). Chest mein bhi OBC ke liye koi relaxation nahi. Only SC, ST, aur ST area candidates ko height/chest relaxation milti hai.
