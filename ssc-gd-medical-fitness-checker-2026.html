SSC GD Medical — Complete Guide 2026

SSC GD Medical Test Mein Exactly Kya Hota Hai?

SSC GD selection process mein medical examination ek comprehensive physical aur systemic test hai. Ye examination trained medical officers conduct karte hain jo CAPF (Central Armed Police Forces) ke liye authorized hote hain. Medical test mein following check hota hai: vision (distant, near, color), ear-nose-throat condition, flat foot test, knock knee assessment, varicose veins, skin condition, musculoskeletal fitness, general health status, aur weight-height ratio.

Medical kab hota hai? SSC GD process mein PET (Physical Efficiency Test) aur PST (Physical Standard Test) clear karne ke baad medical examination hoti hai. Ye generally state-wise medical boards pe conducted hoti hai. Candidates ko original documents ke saath medical center report karna hota hai.

SSC GD Eye Vision Standard — Puri Detail

Vision SSC GD medical mein sabse critical parameter hai. SSC GD ke liye eye vision standard kuch is tarah hai:

  • Distant Vision: Dono aankhon mein 6/6 required hai without spectacles ya contact lenses. Kuch cases mein ek aankh 6/6 aur doosri 6/9 acceptable ho sakti hai, lekin ye medical board ke discretion pe depend karta hai.
  • Near Vision: N/6 better eye mein aur N/9 worse eye mein minimum requirement hai. Ye near vision without any correction test ki jaati hai.
  • Color Vision: CP-1 (Color Perception Grade 1) standard required hai. CP-2 ya CP-3 candidates generally disqualified hote hain. Ishihara color plates test kiya jaata hai.
  • Squint aur Night Blindness: Manifest squint ya night blindness disqualifying conditions hain. Latent squint kuch cases mein acceptable ho sakti hai.

Bahut se aspirants sochte hain ki agar unki vision 6/6 hai chashme ke saath toh kaam chalega — ye galat hai. Medical mein chashma utarwa ke test hota hai. Agar bina chashme ke 6/6 nahi hai toh rejection probable hai.

Flat Foot — SSC GD Ki Sabse Missed Problem

Flat foot (pes planus) wo condition hai jahan paon ka arch completely ya significantly absent hota hai — paon ka poora tala zameen ko touch karta hai. Ye SSC GD medical mein disqualifying condition hai kyunki long distance patrol aur operational duty ke liye paon ka proper arch zaroori hai.

Test kaise hota hai? Ek traditional tarika hai — candidate ko wet foot se plain surface pe khada karaya jaata hai. Print mein arch ka absence ya presence dekha jaata hai. Medical officer physical inspection bhi karte hain. Kuch centers pe orthopedic assessment bhi hoti hai.

Kya flat foot improve ho sakta hai? Mild cases mein specific exercises se arch strength improve ho sakti hai over time. Arch support exercises, towel curls, marble pickup exercises regularly karne se improvement possible hai. Lekin severe flat foot mein medical treatment se permanent fix possible nahi hai. Early detection aur orthopedic consultation zaruri hai.

Tattoo Rules — Sahi Jankari Kitni Zaroori Hai

SSC GD tattoo policy ke baare mein bahut confusion hai. Official position kuch is tarah hai (based on published CAPF recruitment norms):

  • Allowed: Small tattoo on inner forearm (elbow ke andar side). Traditional tribal tattoos on any part of body if it is a custom of the community.
  • Not Allowed: Tattoos on face, neck, dorsal (back) side of hands, fingers. Tattoos with content that is obscene, communal, or disrespectful in nature.
  • Grey Area: Large tattoos on chest, upper arm, back — ye cases medical board le dekhta hai. Some are permitted, some are not.

Tattoo removal option: Agar disqualifying location pe tattoo hai aur medical exam kuch time baad hai, toh laser tattoo removal consider kiya ja sakta hai. Ye expensive aur painful ho sakta hai lekin career ke liye worth it hai. Doctor se consult karo procedure ke baare mein.

SSC GD Medical Mein Reject Hone Ke Common Reasons

  • Eyesight below standard: Sabse common rejection — without glasses vision 6/6 nahi hoti
  • Flat foot: Candidates unaware hote hain pehle se — medical mein pata chalta hai
  • Knock knee: Significant genu valgum rejection cause karta hai
  • Color blindness: CP-3 ya complete color blindness — reject
  • Tattoo issues: Wrong location ya too large — disqualifying
  • Weight mismatch: Height se excessive difference in either direction
  • Varicose veins: Often undetected by candidates — medical mein catch hota hai
  • Active medical conditions: Controlled BP, diabetes bhi kuch cases mein issue create kar sakta hai

Medical Stage Preparation — 3 Months Pehle Se Start Karo

Medical stage ke liye strategic preparation karna chahiye written exam ke saath-saath. Ye ek common mistake hai ki candidates sirf written aur physical pe focus karte hain aur medical ka pata hi nahi rehta. Ye approach galat hai kyunki ek medical condition sab kuch zero kar sakti hai.

  • Eye check-up: Minimum 3 months pehle ophthalmologist se full eye check karwao. Agar weak hai toh natural vision improvement exercises try karo.
  • Flat foot self-test: Wet foot test ghar pe kar sakte ho. Ek glass of water se paon bhigo aur paper pe khade ho jaao. Print mein arch check karo. Agar arch nahi dikh raha toh orthopedic doctor se milna chahiye.
  • Weight management: Height ke hisaab se weight balance karo. Naat extreme exercise se weight suddenly drop karo — gradual healthy approach use karo.
  • Tattoo decision: Agar tattoo hai wrong location pe toh removal plan karo — adequate healing time chahiye.
  • Honest medical form: Medical history form mein sab kuch honestly fill karo. Hiding creates legal issues later.
