Low Expansion Warning — PST Failure Risk
Agar chest expansion 5 cm se kam hai, PST me disqualification ka high risk hai. General candidates ke liye minimum 5 cm expansion mandatory hai. Breathing exercises abhi se start karo — ssc.gov.in se exact standard verify karo.
⚠️
Incorrect Posture Warning
PST ke waqt slouched ya bent posture chest measurement galat de sakti hai. Straight khade raho measurement ke waqt. Practice karo ghar par bhi same posture se — yeh 1-2 cm ka difference create kar sakta hai.
ℹ️
Category Relaxation Notice
SC/ST aur hill area candidates ko chest me kuch relaxation milti hai as per official SSC rules. Apni category carefully select karo — wrong category declare karne par disqualification hoti hai. Official notification se verify karo exact relaxation values.

SSC GD Chest Criteria 2026 — Poori Jankari

SSC GD Physical Standard Test (PST) me male candidates ke liye chest measurement ek mandatory aur critical parameter hai. Standard requirement hai minimum 80 cm unexpanded aur 85 cm expanded — matlab 5 cm ka chest expansion zaroori hai. Yeh measurement tape se official PST officials dwara ki jaati hai.

Chest measurement failure ka matlab hai selection process ka end — aage koi chance nahi milta us cycle me. Isiliye physical test preparation me running ke saath chest preparation bhi equally important hai. Bahut se candidates running mein perfect hote hain lekin chest fail hone ke kaaran disqualify ho jaate hain.

  • Minimum unexpanded chest: 80 cm (normal area, General/OBC/EWS)
  • Minimum expanded chest: 85 cm (after deep inhalation)
  • Minimum expansion required: 5 cm (unexpanded se expanded me difference)
  • Hill/tribal area candidates: relaxed standards as per official notification
  • Measurement tape standard anthropometric instrument se ki jaati hai
  • Measurement generally underwear ya thin clothing me ki jaati hai — heavy clothes mat pehno
Always verify: Exact chest standards SSC GD official recruitment notification me mention hote hain. Standards cycle to cycle thodi variation ho sakti hai — ssc.gov.in se verify karo.
🫁
Chest Expansion — Kaise Improve Kare?

Chest expansion directly lung capacity aur chest muscle flexibility se related hai. Consistent training se expansion improve ho sakti hai — yeh ek achievable goal hai with 6-8 weeks of dedicated practice.

  • Deep Breathing Exercises: Daily 15-20 minutes — inhale fully, hold 4 seconds, slowly exhale. Yeh lung capacity directly increase karta hai
  • Swimming: Chest expansion ke liye swimming sabse effective exercise hai — weekly 3-4 sessions recommended
  • Pull-ups aur Chin-ups: Chest aur back muscles develop karte hain — daily 3 sets minimum
  • Chest Stretch: Door frame stretch, arm circle, shoulder blade squeeze — daily 10 minutes
  • Pranayama: Anulom Vilom aur Kapalbhati — lung expansion ke liye proven effective
  • Posture Improvement: Straight back maintain karna measurement me 1-2 cm ka difference deta hai
Pro Tip: PST se exactly 8 weeks pehle consistent breathing exercises shuru karo. Week 1-2 me foundation, week 3-6 me intensity build, week 7-8 me maintenance. Progress track karo weekly self-measurement se.
📐
PST Me Chest Measurement — Exactly Kaise Hoti Hai?

PST (Physical Standard Test) me chest measurement ka process candidates ke liye janna zaroori hai taaki nervous na ho aur sahi reading aaye:

  • Stance: Candidate seedha khada hota hai — relaxed position, arms slightly away from body
  • Tape placement: Measurement tape ko nipple line par rakha jaata hai — neither too high nor too low
  • Unexpanded reading: Normal breathing state me reading li jaati hai — na baahut andar kheenchi saansh, na bahar nikali
  • Expanded reading: Candidate ko deep breath lene ke liye kaha jaata hai — maximum inhalation pe reading
  • Expansion calculated: Expanded minus Unexpanded = Expansion value — minimum 5 cm required
  • Re-measurement: Generally ek baar measurement hoti hai — kuch cases me re-measurement request ki ja sakti hai
PST me ghbarane ki zaroorat nahi. Pehle se practice karo at home — same method se self-check karo. Practice se confidence aata hai aur actual measurement better aati hai.
⚠️
Common Chest Test Mistakes — Yeh Galtiyan Mat Karo

PST me candidates jo common mistakes karte hain unhe jaanna aur avoid karna zaroori hai:

  • Heavy clothing pehenna: Tight ya layered clothing se measurement galat hoti hai — light clothing pe measurement karo
  • Shoulders age ki taraf jhukaana: Slouched posture se chest measurement 2-3 cm kam aati hai — straight raho
  • Partial inhalation during expanded measurement: Jab expanded reading ho toh poori saansh andar lo — half breath se measurement kam aayegi
  • Nervous hona: Anxiety se breathing tight hoti hai aur expansion kam hoti hai — relax karo, deep breath from belly
  • Tape positioning error allow karna: Tape placement verify karo — agar galat lag rahi hai politely request karo
  • No prior preparation: Yeh sabse badi galti hai — chest ko physical test se pehle ready karo, last minute pe nahi
