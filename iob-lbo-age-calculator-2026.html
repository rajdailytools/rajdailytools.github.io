Complete Guide

IOB LBO Age Calculation Complete Guide 2026-27

Age calculation logic, examples, aur relaxation rules — sab kuch official notification ke hisaab se.

Age Kaise Calculate Hoti Hai?

IOB LBO 2026-27 recruitment mein age exact cut-off date — 01.08.2026 — ke hisaab se calculate hoti hai, na ki simply "current year minus birth year" se. Iska matlab hai ki agar aapka birthday cut-off date ke baad aata hai, to aapki calculated age ek saal kam hogi us saal ke mukable jisme aap turn karte. Ye calculator din, mahine aur saal — teeno ko exactly count karta hai, including leap years aur 29 February wale DOB ko bhi.

Base Age Limit

Official notification ke anusaar, minimum age 20 saal aur maximum age 30 saal honi chahiye — 01.08.2026 ko. Iska matlab candidate ka janm 02.08.1996 se 01.08.2006 ke beech (dono dates included) hona chahiye, General/EWS category ke liye. Agar aap kisi reserved category se hain to upar diya gaya age relaxation table check karein.

  • Cut-off date fix hai: 01.08.2026
  • Minimum age sabhi categories ke liye same hai — 20 saal
  • Maximum age General/EWS ke liye 30 saal hai
  • SC/ST/OBC/PwBD/Ex-servicemen/1984-riot ko relaxation milta hai

Key Insight: Relaxation only extends the maximum age limit — the minimum age of 20 years applies to every category, with no relaxation mentioned in the official notification.

Cumulative Relaxation Rule

Agar koi candidate ek se zyada relaxation category ke liye eligible hai (jaise SC + PwBD, ya OBC + Ex-servicemen), to official notification ke anusaar relaxation cumulative basis par milta hai — lekin sirf SC/ST/OBC candidates ke liye, aur total upper age limit 50 years se zyada nahi ho sakti.

Important: Relaxation apply karne ke liye Personal Interview ke time par relevant certificate (caste certificate, disability certificate, etc.) submit karna zaroori hai — jaisa official notification mein bataya gaya hai.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

IOB LBO 2026-27 age eligibility ke baare mein common sawaal.

IOB LBO 2026-27 ke liye age limit kya hai?+
Official notification (Advt No. HRDD/RECT/02/2026-27) ke anusaar minimum age 20 saal aur maximum age 30 saal hai, cut-off date 01.08.2026 ke hisaab se.
IOB LBO age calculation ka cut-off date kya hai?+
Cut-off date online registration shuru hone waale mahine ki 1 tareekh hai, yani 01.08.2026. Age aur educational qualification, dono is date ke hisaab se check kiye jaate hain.
Meri DOB range kya honi chahiye General category mein?+
General/EWS candidates ka janm 02.08.1996 se 01.08.2006 ke beech (dono dates included) hona chahiye, jaisa official notification mein diya gaya hai.
IOB LBO me age relaxation kis category ko milti hai?+
SC/ST ko 5 saal, OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) ko 3 saal, PwBD ko 10 saal, 1984 riot-affected ko 5 saal, aur eligible Ex-servicemen ko 5 saal ki relaxation official notification me di gayi hai.
Kya do relaxation categories ek saath mil sakti hain?+
Haan, agar candidate SC/ST/OBC hai aur ek aur category (PwBD/1984-riot/Ex-servicemen) ke liye bhi eligible hai, to relaxation cumulative basis par milta hai — lekin total upper age limit 50 years se zyada nahi ho sakti.
Kya minimum age (20 years) mein bhi relaxation milti hai?+
Nahi. Official notification ke anusaar relaxation sirf upper (maximum) age limit par lagu hoti hai. Minimum age 20 years sabhi categories ke liye same rehti hai.
Kya ye calculator official IOB result deta hai?+
Nahi. Ye tool sirf age eligibility ka preliminary estimate deta hai. Final eligibility aur selection Indian Overseas Bank ke official process se hi decide hoti hai.
