🎓 Eligibility

Educational Eligibility Criteria

UPTET has different eligibility criteria for Primary (Class 1–5) and Upper Primary (Class 6–8) teachers.

🏫
TET Primary Teacher
Class 1–5 (Paper I)
Candidate must have a Bachelor's Degree from an NCTE-Recognized Institution
Valid Teacher Training Qualification such as BTC, B.El.Ed, or Equivalent
Minimum 50% marks in Graduation or relevant training program required
Final Year Candidates of above courses are also eligible to apply
Graduation from any stream (Arts, Science, Commerce) is acceptable
🏛️
TET Upper Primary Teacher
Class 6–8 (Paper II)
Bachelor's Degree with minimum 50% (45% in some SC/ST cases) from an NCTE-Recognized Institution
Teacher Training Qualification such as B.Ed, BTC, B.El.Ed, or Equivalent
Final-year candidates of qualifying training courses are also eligible
Candidates with Specialized Education Qualifications from RCI-Recognized Institutes are eligible
In some cases, candidates with 50% marks in Intermediate + 4-Year Integrated Teaching Program can also apply

❓ FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

UPTET 2026 ke baare mein common sawaal aur unke jawaab.

UPTET Online Form 2026 ki last date kya hai?+
UPTET Online Form 2026 ki last date 26 April 2026 hai. Fee payment bhi isi date tak complete karni hogi. Form correction window 01 May 2026 tak available rahegi. Late fee ka provision nahi hai — time par apply karein.
UPTET 2026 exam kab hoga?+
UPTET 2026 exam 02, 03, aur 04 July 2026 ko conduct hoga. Admit card 30 June 2026 ke aas paas available hoga. Exam city details 22 June 2026 ko announce ki jayengi.
UPTET ke liye educational qualification kya chahiye?+
Primary (Class 1-5): Bachelor's degree from NCTE-recognized institute + BTC/B.El.Ed with minimum 50% marks. Upper Primary (Class 6-8): Bachelor's degree with 50% marks (45% for SC/ST) + B.Ed/BTC/B.El.Ed equivalent. Final year candidates bhi apply kar sakte hain.
UPTET application fee kitni hai?+
UPTET 2026 application fee: General/OBC/EWS: ₹1000, SC/ST: ₹500, PH Candidates: ₹300. Payment online mode mein hoti hai — Debit Card, Credit Card, Net Banking, IMPS, Mobile Wallet se.
UPTET 2026 ka official website kya hai?+
UPTET ke liye official websites hain: updeled.gov.in aur upessc.up.gov.in. Application form, official notification, admit card — sab official website se hi check karein. Third-party websites par depend na karein.
UPTET ke liye age limit kya hai?+
UPTET ke liye minimum age generally 18 years hai. Maximum age UPESSC rules ke according hoti hai — generally TET exam mein koi upper age limit nahi hoti. Exact information official UPTET notification se verify karein kyunki ye change ho sakti hai.
UPTET 2026 mein kaunse dono paper hote hain?+
UPTET mein Paper I (Primary Teacher, Class 1-5) aur Paper II (Upper Primary Teacher, Class 6-8) hote hain. Jo dono levels par teach karna chahta hai, woh dono papers de sakta hai. Mode of selection: Written Examination only.
UPTET certificate ki validity kitni hoti hai?+
UPTET certificate ki validity 7 years hoti hai (latest NCTE guidelines ke according). Ek baar TET clear karne ke baad aap 7 saal tak is certificate ka use kar sakte hain teacher recruitment ke liye. TET certificate lifetime valid bhi ho sakta hai — latest notification check karein.
