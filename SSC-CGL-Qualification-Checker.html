Graduation Rules

SSC CGL Graduation Requirement 2026

Basic Graduation Requirement

SSC CGL 2026 ke almost sabhi posts ke liye Bachelor's Degree from a recognized University or equivalent mandatory hai. Yeh cutoff date 01 August 2026 tak honi chahiye. Iske bina koi bhi candidate eligible nahi maana jaayega.

Official notification (Para 8.4.1) clearly kehta hai: "Bachelor's Degree from a recognized University or equivalent." Yani degree completed honi chahiye aur university recognized honi chahiye — dono conditions zaroori hain.

  • Bachelor's degree in any stream (BA, BSc, BCom, BBA, BCA, BTech, etc.) — sabhi valid hain
  • University ya institute UGC/Government recognized hona chahiye
  • Degree ki equivalence verify ki jaayegi document verification mein
  • Sirf degree ka naam kafi nahi — marks sheets aur provisional/final degree certificate dono required hain
Key Point: Agar aapka degree kisi equivalent qualification se hai, toh document verification mein Equivalence Certificate produce karna hoga concerned authorities se. Final decision User Department ka hoga.

Konsi Degrees Valid Hain?

SSC CGL ke liye bahut variety of degrees valid hain. Regular mode se leke distance education tak — sab acceptable hain provided recognition conditions poori hon.

  • Regular Mode: BA, BSc, BCom, BBA, BCA, BTech, BE, LLB, BHM — sab valid
  • Postgraduate Degrees: MSc, MCom, MBA, MCA, MA — bhi graduation ke barabar count hote hain
  • Open/Distance Learning: UGC ke DEB se approved hone par valid (Para 8.6)
  • IGNOU B.Tech: 2009-10 tak enrolled students ke liye Supreme Court order ke basis par valid
  • Equivalent Qualifications: Recognized hone par valid — verification required

JSO Qualification

SSC CGL JSO Qualification Criteria 2026

Junior Statistical Officer (JSO) — Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation — ek special post hai jiske liye special qualification criteria hai. Sirf graduation kafi nahi. Official notification (Para 8.2) ke anusar:

Official Rule (Para 8.2.1): "Bachelor's Degree in any subject from a recognized University or Institute with at least 60% Marks in Mathematics at 12th standard level; OR Bachelor's Degree in any subject with Statistics as one of the subjects at degree level."

JSO ke liye Do Raaste Hain:

  • Option A: Kisi bhi subject mein graduation + 12th mein Maths 60% ya zyada marks
  • Option B: Kisi bhi subject mein graduation + degree level mein Statistics ek subject ke roop mein
  • Dono mein se koi ek hona zaroori hai — sirf regular graduation se JSO nahi milega
  • 12th maths 60% below waale candidates bhi eligible hain agar degree mein Statistics tha

Common Confusion Clear Karein:

Bahut students sochte hain ki "BSc Math se graduation hai toh JSO mil jaayega" — yeh poori tarah sahi nahi hai. BSc Mathematics mein — agar aapne statistics bhi pada hai degree level pe toh Option B se eligible ho. Agar nahi pada, toh 12th mein 60%+ Maths chahiye tha. 12th Maths 60%+ means total marks in Maths paper 60% ya above.

Statistical Investigator

Statistical Investigator Grade-II Qualification 2026

Statistical Investigator Grade-II — Ministry of Home Affairs / RGI — ke liye ek specific stream mein graduation mandatory hai. General graduation degree sufficient nahi hai is post ke liye. Official notification (Para 8.3.1) kehta hai:

Official Rule (Para 8.3.1): "Bachelor degree in Statistics or Mathematics or Economics or Demography or Population Studies or Operation Research or Information Technology or Computer Science or Computer Engineering or Computer Technology or Computer Application or Data Science or Artificial Intelligence from a recognized University or recognized Institute."

Eligible Degree Subjects for Statistical Investigator Grade-II:

  • 📊 Statistics
  • ➗ Mathematics
  • 💹 Economics
  • 👥 Demography / Population Studies
  • 🔄 Operation Research
  • 💻 Information Technology
  • 🖥️ Computer Science
  • ⚙️ Computer Engineering / Computer Technology
  • 📱 Computer Application
  • 📈 Data Science
  • 🤖 Artificial Intelligence
Important: BA English, BCom, BBA, General BA/BSc (without these specific subjects as main degree) — in degrees se Statistical Investigator Grade-II ke liye apply karna valid nahi hoga. Subject clearly match karna chahiye.

Degree Recognition

Degree Recognition & Verification Rules

University Recognition Kyon Zaroori Hai?

SSC CGL mein sirf graduation hona kafi nahi — university ya institute recognized hona mandatory hai. Non-recognized university ya fake university se degree invalid hai. Document verification mein agar degree invalid nikli, candidature cancel ho jaati hai.

Open & Distance Learning (ODL) Degrees

Open and Distance Learning mode se degree bhi valid hai — provided conditions poori hon (Para 8.6):

  • University Parliament ya State Legislature ke Act se established honi chahiye
  • Ya Deemed University under UGC Act Section 3 honi chahiye
  • Degree Distance Education Bureau (DEB), UGC se approved honi chahiye relevant period ke liye
  • Document verification mein DEB approval certificate produce karna mandatory hai

Engineering/Medical Degrees via Distance Mode

UGC (ODL) Regulations 2017 ke under: Engineering, Medicine, Dental, Nursing, Pharmacy, Architecture — ye programmes ODL mode mein permitted nahi hain. Exception: IGNOU B.Tech — 2009-10 tak enrolled students ke liye Supreme Court order ke basis par valid hai.

Qualification Verification Process

SSC CGL exam ke time detailed scrutiny nahi hoti — candidature provisionally accepted hoti hai. Document Verification (DV) User Departments/Organizations conduct karte hain final result ke baad. Isliye sabhi original documents ready rakhein:

  • Mark sheets for all years/semesters of graduation
  • Provisional Certificate ya Degree Certificate
  • Equivalence Certificate (agar applicable ho)
  • DEB approval letter (agar ODL degree ho)
  • JSO ke liye 12th marksheet (Maths marks verify hone ke liye)

Common Qualification Mistakes — Bachein Inse

❌

Diploma ko Degree Samajhna

ITI, Polytechnic Diploma, PG Diploma — ye bachelor's degree nahi hain. Inse SSC CGL eligible nahi hain.

❌

Non-Recognized University Degree

Fake ya non-recognized university se degree valid nahi. Document verification mein reject ho jaayegi.

❌

JSO ke liye Sirf Graduation

General graduation se JSO apply nahi kar sakte. 12th Maths 60%+ ya degree mein Statistics mandatory hai.

❌

Result Process Hona = Declare Hona

University mein result process ho raha hai — yeh kafi nahi. Result officially DECLARE hona chahiye 01.08.2026 tak.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

SSC CGL 2026 qualification ke baare mein 50 common sawaal aur official answers.

1. SSC CGL 2026 ke liye minimum educational qualification kya hai?+
Almost saare SSC CGL 2026 posts ke liye Bachelor's Degree from a recognized University or equivalent mandatory hai. Yeh qualification 01.08.2026 tak honi chahiye. Sirf 12th pass ya diploma holders eligible nahi hain.
2. Kya BA, BSc, BCom — sabhi streams eligible hain?+
Haan! Most SSC CGL posts ke liye kisi bhi stream mein graduation valid hai — BA, BSc, BCom, BBA, BCA, BTech, BE, sab theek hain. Bas university recognized honi chahiye. Exception: JSO aur Statistical Investigator Grade-II ke liye additional/specific requirements hain.
3. Kya postgraduate degree (MSc, MCom, MBA) bhi valid hai?+
Bilkul! Agar aapne postgraduate degree complete ki hai (MSc, MCom, MBA, MCA, MA), toh aap automatically graduation criteria meet karte hain. PG degree degree se higher hoti hai, isliye qualification condition poori hoti hai.
4. Kya SSC CGL ke liye minimum percentage koi hai graduation mein?+
Most posts ke liye graduation mein koi minimum percentage nahi hai. Sirf pass hona kafi hai. Exception: JSO ke liye 12th Mathematics mein 60%+ marks required hain. Statistical Investigator ke liye specific subject required hai, percentage nahi.
5. Diploma se SSC CGL eligible ho sakte hain?+
Nahi. ITI, Polytechnic Diploma, PG Diploma — ye bachelor's degree ka substitute nahi hain. SSC CGL ke liye full 3–4 year bachelor's degree mandatory hai. Diploma holders eligible nahi hain.
6. Kya final year students SSC CGL 2026 ke liye apply kar sakte hain?+
Haan, apply kar sakte hain! Official notification (Para 8.5) ke anusar final year students eligible hain — lekin unhe 01.08.2026 tak graduation complete karni hogi. Agar us date tak result declare nahi hua, candidature cancel ho sakti hai.
7. "Appearing" students ka kya matlab hai eligibility mein?+
Jo students abhi final year exams de rahe hain ya result ka wait kar rahe hain — wo "appearing" category mein aate hain. Ye bhi apply kar sakte hain. Condition same hai: 01.08.2026 tak result officially declare hona chahiye.
8. Mera result 01.08.2026 ke baad aane ki umeed hai — kya main apply karun?+
Agar aapko pura confidence hai ki result 01.08.2026 ke baad aayega, toh technically document verification mein dikkat aa sakti hai. Apply toh kar sakte hain, exam de sakte hain — lekin final selection ke waqt degree required hogi. Risk apna aap assess karein.
9. "Result process ho raha hai university mein" — kya yeh kafi hai?+
Bilkul nahi! Official notification clearly kehta hai: "Mere processing of the result by the University/Institute by the cut-off date does not fulfill the EQ requirement." Result officially DECLARED hona chahiye — sirf processing kafi nahi.
10. Agar backlog hai toh kya main apply kar sakta hoon?+
Agar aapka backlog 01.08.2026 tak clear ho jaata hai aur result declare ho jaata hai — toh aap eligible honge. Backlog cleared hone ki proof document verification pe produce karni hogi. Agar backlog cutoff date tak clear nahi hua, eligible nahi maane jaayenge.
11. JSO (Junior Statistical Officer) ke liye kya qualification chahiye?+
Official notification (Para 8.2.1) ke anusar: Option A: Bachelor's Degree in any subject + at least 60% Marks in Mathematics at 12th standard level. Option B: Bachelor's Degree in any subject with Statistics as one of the subjects at degree level. Dono mein se ek condition poori honi chahiye.
12. 12th mein Maths tha lekin 58% aaye — kya JSO eligible hoon?+
Option A (60% Maths in 12th) se eligible nahi hain. Lekin agar aapke graduation mein Statistics ek subject tha, toh Option B se eligible ho sakte hain. Degree ka marksheet check karein — Statistics subject listed hai ya nahi.
13. BSc Mathematics se kya JSO eligible hoon?+
BSc Mathematics se graduation — agar Statistics bhi ek subject tha (BSc Maths-Statistics combination), toh Option B se eligible ho sakte hain. Sirf Mathematics degree mein agar Statistics nahi tha, toh 12th mein 60%+ Maths chahiye Option A ke liye. Marksheet verify karein.
14. BA ya BCom graduates kya JSO ke liye eligible hain?+
Haan — agar 12th mein Mathematics 60%+ marks ke saath tha, toh BA ya BCom graduates bhi JSO ke liye eligible hain (Option A). Ya agar BA/BCom mein Statistics ek subject tha degree level pe (Option B). General BA/BCom without these conditions eligible nahi hain.
15. JSO ke liye "Statistics as one of the subjects at degree level" ka kya matlab hai?+
Iska matlab hai aapke graduation course mein Statistics ek formal subject/paper ke roop mein padha gaya ho. Sirf basic statistics theory ek chapter mein nahi — balki Statistics ek full paper/course ke roop mein hona chahiye. Degree marksheet mein clearly "Statistics" subject listed hona chahiye.
16. Statistical Investigator Grade-II ke liye kya degree chahiye?+
Official notification (Para 8.3.1) ke anusar: Bachelor degree specifically in — Statistics, Mathematics, Economics, Demography, Population Studies, Operation Research, Information Technology, Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Computer Technology, Computer Application, Data Science, or Artificial Intelligence — from a recognized University or Institute.
17. BCA aur BCA graduates Statistical Investigator ke liye eligible hain?+
BCA (Bachelor of Computer Applications) — "Computer Application" category mein aata hai jo eligible subjects ki list mein hai. Isliye BCA graduates Statistical Investigator Grade-II ke liye eligible hain. Official notification ka Para 8.3.1 check karein confirmation ke liye.
18. BCom ya BA graduates Statistical Investigator ke liye eligible hain?+
General BCom ya BA graduates eligible nahi hain Statistical Investigator Grade-II ke liye — unless their specific degree subject matches the list (like BA Economics ya BCom with Statistics). Sirf degree ka naam match karna kafi hai — main subject match karna chahiye.
19. BTech Computer Science se Statistical Investigator eligible hoon?+
Haan! BTech in Computer Science ya Computer Engineering — dono Statistical Investigator Grade-II ke eligible subject list mein hain. Computer Engineering aur Computer Technology dono covered hain. Aap eligible hain.
20. Data Science ya Artificial Intelligence mein degree — kya Statistical Investigator eligible hoon?+
Haan! SSC CGL 2026 notification mein Data Science aur Artificial Intelligence dono explicitly mentioned hain Statistical Investigator Grade-II ke eligible subjects mein (Para 8.3.1). Yeh nayi addition hai jo bahut relevant hai modern graduates ke liye.
21. Recognized University ya Institute ka kya matlab hai SSC CGL mein?+
Recognized university wo hai jo Parliament ya State Legislature ke Act se established ho, ya UGC ke under Deemed University ho, ya National Importance ki Institution declared ho. Simple way to check: UGC ki recognized universities list dekho — ugc.gov.in par available hai.
22. Open University ya Distance Education degree valid hai SSC CGL mein?+
Haan, valid hai — provided degree Distance Education Bureau (DEB), UGC se approved ho relevant period ke liye (Para 8.6). Candidate ko DEB approval certificate document verification mein produce karna hoga. IGNOU, SOL Delhi University, etc. ki degrees generally valid hoti hain.
23. IGNOU se degree kya valid hai?+
IGNOU (Indira Gandhi National Open University) Parliament ke Act se established hai aur UGC recognized hai. IGNOU degree generally valid hai SSC CGL ke liye. Exception: IGNOU se engineering degrees (except B.Tech for students enrolled up to 2009-10 via Supreme Court order) valid nahi hain ODL mode mein.
24. Private university se degree valid hai?+
Private university degrees valid hain — agar university recognized hai. State Government ya UGC se recognized private universities ki degrees acceptable hain. Non-recognized ya fake university se degree invalid hai. Apni university ka status UGC website par verify karein.
25. Foreign university se degree valid hai SSC CGL mein?+
Foreign university degree technically valid ho sakti hai — provided equivalent honi chahiye Indian degrees ke saath aur relevant authority se equivalence certificate hona chahiye. Document verification mein Equivalence Certificate produce karna mandatory hoga. User Department final decision lega.
26. Kya Economics graduation se JSO eligible hoon?+
BA Economics ya BSc Economics se — JSO ke liye eligible hone ke liye ya toh 12th mein Maths 60%+ chahiye (Option A), ya degree mein Statistics ek formal subject hona chahiye (Option B). Sirf Economics degree se directly JSO eligible nahi hain — additional condition check karein.
27. BA Economics se Statistical Investigator Grade-II eligible hoon?+
Haan! BA Economics ya BSc Economics — "Economics" explicitly eligible subject list mein hai Para 8.3.1 mein Statistical Investigator Grade-II ke liye. Aap eligible hain — degree recognized university se honi chahiye.
28. BBA se SSC CGL ke liye eligible hoon?+
Most posts ke liye haan! BBA (Bachelor of Business Administration) ek graduation degree hai. Most SSC CGL posts (ASO, Inspector, UDC, Tax Assistant, etc.) ke liye BBA valid hai. Lekin Statistical Investigator Grade-II ke liye BBA directly listed nahi hai unless specific subject match kare.
29. LLB (Law degree) se SSC CGL eligible hoon?+
5-year integrated LLB (BA LLB, BSc LLB, BCom LLB) — graduation equivalent hai aur most SSC CGL posts ke liye valid hai. 3-year LLB (after graduation) — aap already graduate ho toh bhi eligible hain. Subject aur recognition confirm karein.
30. Computer Application degree mein operation research tha — kya Statistical Investigator eligible hoon?+
Agar aapki main degree "Computer Application" (like BCA) ya "Operation Research" mein hai, toh aap eligible hain. Notification mein dono subjects listed hain. Subject name degree title/marksheet mein clearly hona chahiye.
31. Qualification cutoff date kya hai SSC CGL 2026 mein?+
SSC CGL 2026 ke liye qualification cutoff date 01 August 2026 (01.08.2026) hai. Iska matlab hai ki is date tak candidate ke paas required educational qualification honi chahiye. Final year students ke liye bhi yahi date apply hoti hai.
32. Kya provisonal certificate se kaam chalega document verification mein?+
Haan, provisional certificate acceptable hai — agar final degree certificate abhi issue nahi hua. Official notification (Para 8.8) kehta hai: mark sheets for all years/semesters ya Provisional Certificate dono acceptable hain, provided result cutoff date tak declared ho.
33. Kya main exam de sakta hoon agar abhi graduation result pending hai?+
Haan, exam de sakte hain. SSC CGL candidature initially provisional hoti hai. Exam mein baithne ke liye result pending hone par bhi allowed hain. Lekin document verification tak result declare hona zaroori hai. Agar DV date tak result nahi aaya, candidature cancel ho sakti hai.
34. SSC CGL mein Equivalence Certificate kab chahiye?+
Agar aapke paas equivalent educational qualification hai (koi foreign degree ya non-standard Indian qualification), toh document verification mein Equivalence Certificate produce karna hoga — concerned authorities se issued. Final decision User Department ka hoga (Para 8.9).
35. Kya graduation ke saath work experience bhi required hai?+
Nahi. SSC CGL 2026 ke liye kisi bhi post mein work experience required nahi hai eligibility criteria mein. Sirf educational qualification aur age limit (aur kuch posts ke physical standards) matter karte hain. Fresh graduates bhi apply kar sakte hain.
36. Document Verification mein kaunse documents chahiye graduation ke liye?+
Document Verification pe lana hoga: (1) Mark sheets for all years/semesters, (2) Provisional Certificate ya final Degree Certificate, (3) Equivalence Certificate agar applicable ho, (4) DEB approval letter agar ODL degree ho, (5) JSO ke liye 12th marksheet bhi.
37. Kya SSC CGL mein educational qualification verification exam se pehle hoti hai?+
Nahi. SSC detailed scrutiny exam time pe nahi karta. Candidature provisionally accepted hoti hai. Educational qualification verification Document Verification (DV) mein hoti hai — jo User Departments/Organizations conduct karte hain final result ke baad (Para 14.2).
38. Agar DV mein documents nahi mile toh kya hoga?+
Agar document verification mein required educational qualification documents nahi hain ya invalid hain — candidature cancel ho jaayegi. Aur jo post allot ki gayi hai, us se bhi hath dhona padega. Isliye documents bilkul ready rakhein aur sab kuch genuine hona chahiye.
39. Kya wrong educational qualification claim karne pe penalty hai?+
Haan, serious penalty hai. Agar koi galat ya false educational qualification claim kare — candidature cancel hogi, future SSC exams se debar kiya ja sakta hai, aur criminal action bhi possible hai. Commission aur User Departments dono se action ho sakta hai.
40. Kya ek hi SSC CGL attempt mein JSO aur regular posts dono ke liye apply kar sakte hain?+
Haan! Application form mein candidate indicate karta hai ki wo JSO ke liye bhi apply karna chahta hai (S. No. 27.1 aur 27.2) aur Statistical Investigator ke liye bhi (S. No. 28.1 aur 28.2). Separate Paper-II JSO/Statistical Investigator waalon ke liye conduct hota hai Tier-II mein.
41. Kya correspondence degree se SSC CGL eligible hoon?+
Correspondence (Distance Education) degree valid hai — provided university recognized ho aur DEB, UGC se approved ho. Document verification mein DEB approval letter produce karna hoga. IGNOU, SOL DU jaisi established open universities ki degrees generally accepted hoti hain.
42. Kya B.Ed degree se SSC CGL mein eligible hoon?+
B.Ed (Bachelor of Education) ek graduation level degree hai. Most SSC CGL posts ke liye eligible hain agar university recognized hai. B.Ed typically 2-year post-graduation program hoti hai (BA/BSc ke baad), isliye aap already graduate ho. B.Ed alone se bhi apply kar sakte hain.
43. Kya Integrated courses (5-year BA-MA, BSc-MSc) se eligible hoon?+
5-year Integrated courses (BA-MA, BSc-MSc, etc.) — agar poori tarah complete ho gayi hai toh eligible hain. Agar abhi final years mein hain toh final year rule apply hoga (01.08.2026 cutoff). Generally ye degrees graduation se higher level ki hoti hain — eligible maani jaayengi.
44. Kya Army/Defence candidates ke liye qualification rules alag hain?+
Educational qualification rules saare candidates ke liye same hain. Matriculate Ex-Servicemen (15+ years service) Group C posts ke reserved vacancies ke liye eligible hain — lekin Group B posts (jahan graduation required hai) ke liye unhe bhi graduation chahiye hogi.
45. 3-year Graduation vs 4-year Graduation — kya dono valid hain?+
Dono valid hain. BSc, BCom, BA (3-year), BTech, BE (4-year) — sabhi graduation degrees hain. SSC CGL mein specifically years ki requirement mention nahi hai — sirf "Bachelor's Degree" requirement hai. Recognized university se honi chahiye.
46. Kya Operation Research mein degree Statistical Investigator ke liye eligible hai?+
Haan! "Operation Research" explicitly Para 8.3.1 mein listed hai Statistical Investigator Grade-II ke eligible subjects mein. Agar aapki bachelor degree Operation Research mein hai — aap eligible hain is post ke liye.
47. AAO (Assistant Audit Officer) ke liye koi special qualification hai?+
AAO ke liye Essential Qualification: Bachelor's Degree from a recognized University or Institute (Para 8.1.1.1). Desirable (not mandatory): CA, CMA, CS, MCom, MBA Finance, etc. Desirable qualification hone se preferably Commerce stream mein allocation milta hai — lekin mandatory nahi hai apply karne ke liye.
48. Kya BCA graduates JSO ke liye bhi eligible hain?+
BCA graduates JSO ke liye eligible hain — agar 12th mein Maths 60%+ tha (Option A), ya BCA course mein Statistics formally ek subject tha (Option B). Sirf BCA degree se JSO nahi milta — additional condition check karein. BCA curriculum vary karta hai — marksheet verify karein.
49. SSC CGL ke liye qualification verification kab aur kaun karta hai?+
Qualification verification Document Verification (DV) stage par hoti hai jo User Departments/Organizations conduct karte hain — SSC final result declare karne ke baad. SSC khud exam ke time detailed verification nahi karta. Candidature initially provisional accepted hoti hai (Para 14.2).
50. SSC CGL 2026 ka official notification kahan available hai?+
SSC CGL 2026 ka official notification SSC ki official website ssc.gov.in par available hai. Notification date: 21.05.2026. Educational qualification details Para 8 mein hain. Always official source se verify karein — hum sirf educational guidance provide karte hain.
