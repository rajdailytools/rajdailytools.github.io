About RajDailyTools

Helping Students & Aspirants Prepare Smarter Every Day.

RajDailyTools is an educational platform dedicated to helping students and government job aspirants across India through trusted educational resources, recruitment information, exam guides, eligibility checkers, and practical calculators.

We bring together everything an aspirant usually has to search for across multiple websites — eligibility rules, physical standards, syllabus, exam patterns, and preparation strategies — and present it in one clear, reliable place.

Our goal is simple: to simplify exam preparation through reliable information and easy-to-use resources, so you can focus on preparing instead of searching.

The Story Behind It All

From Scattered Information
to One Trusted Place

RajDailyTools started as a response to a problem many aspirants face — recruitment information spread across dozens of tabs, outdated PDFs, and confusing forum posts. We wanted a single place where students could find clear, dependable answers.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is RajDailyTools?+
RajDailyTools is an educational platform that helps students and government job aspirants prepare for competitive exams in India through detailed guides, eligibility checkers, recruitment information, and smart calculators — all in one place.
Which exams are covered?+
We currently cover SSC (CGL and GD), Indian Army, Police recruitment, and Railway (RRB) exams, with more categories such as UPSC, Banking, Teaching, State PSC, NDA, Air Force, and Navy being added over time.
Are the calculators free?+
Yes. Every calculator and guide on RajDailyTools is free to use. There are no subscriptions or premium tiers.
How often is information updated?+
We regularly review our guides to reflect the latest official notifications, eligibility criteria, and exam patterns as they change.
Can I rely on the eligibility guides?+
Our guides are researched carefully and reviewed for accuracy. That said, recruitment rules can change, so we always recommend cross-checking critical details against the official notification before applying.
How can I contact RajDailyTools?+
You can reach us anytime at rajdailytools@gmail.com or through our Contact page. We welcome questions, corrections, and suggestions for new guides.
