KV Admission Process 2026–27 Guide

Admission process se lekar documents verification tak — sab kuch detail mein samjhein.

KV Admission Process 2026–27

KV admission process do phases mein hota hai — Online Registration Phase (mainly for Class 1 and Balvatika) aur Offline Verification Phase (sabke liye mandatory). Dono phases ke liye alag documents ki zaroorat ho sakti hai.

Official KV admission notification aur latest updates ke liye visit karein: kvsangathan.nic.in

Online Registration vs Offline Admission

Online Registration (Class 1 aur Balvatika): Parents KVS portal par digital form fill karte hain. Is stage par scanned copies upload karni ho sakti hain.

Offline Verification (Sabke liye): Original documents school mein verify kiye jaate hain. Ye stage sabse important hai — ek bhi document missing hone par provisional seat cancel ho sakti hai.

Document Verification Process

  • Original documents school mein physical verification ke liye le jaayein
  • Har document ki 2 self-attested photocopies ready rakhein
  • Documents ko plastic folder ya file mein organized rakhein
  • Verification ke baad documents wapas mil jaate hain — school only photocopy rakhti hai
  • Agar koi document missing ho toh 7 days ka time diya jaata hai — lekin ye guaranteed nahi hai

Common Mistakes Parents Karte Hain

❌ Outdated Category Certificate
OBC/EWS certificates expire ho jaate hain. Hamesha current financial year ka certificate banwaein.
❌ Wrong Address Proof
Address proof mein same address hona chahiye jahan aap rahte hain. Mismatched address reject hota hai.
❌ Unofficial Birth Certificate
Hospital slip se kaam nahi chalta. Municipal Corporation ya Government issued birth certificate chahiye.
❌ Missing Affidavit for SGC
Single Girl Child ke liye notarized affidavit mandatory hai. Simple declaration accepted nahi hoti.
Pro Tip: KV admission ke liye ek dedicated folder banaayein aur sabse pehle sab documents check kar lein — verification date se at least 15 days pehle. Koi document missing ho toh replace karna kafi time leta hai.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

KV admission documents ke baare mein sabse common sawaal.

KV admission ke liye kaunse documents sabse important hain?+
Birth Certificate sabse important document hai — bina iske admission possible nahi. Iske baad Address Proof, Passport Photo, aur Aadhaar (if available) mandatory hain. Agar aap kisi special category ya quota mein hain toh us category ka certificate bhi essential hai.
Kya KV admission ke liye Aadhaar card mandatory hai?+
Aadhaar card mandatory nahi hai lekin "if available" category mein hai. Agar bachche ka Aadhaar available hai toh submit karna recommended hai kyunki ye strong address proof aur identity proof dono hota hai. Aadhaar nahi hone par alternative documents (birth certificate + separate address proof) submit karne honge.
Income certificate kab lagta hai KV admission mein?+
Income certificate EWS (Economically Weaker Section) category ke liye mandatory hai — annual family income ₹8 lakh se kam honi chahiye. BPL category ke liye BPL Ration Card ya BPL certificate lagta hai (income certificate separate nahi). General, OBC, SC, ST ke liye income certificate ki zaroorat nahi hoti (sirf caste certificate).
Original documents kab verify hote hain KV mein?+
Original documents offline verification stage par verify hote hain — ye online registration ke baad hota hai. School mein jab aap jaate hain toh original documents leke jaane mandatory hai. Officer original dekhkar photocopy rakhi jaati hai aur original wapas kar dete hain. Bina originals ke provisional admission cancel ho sakta hai.
RTE quota ke liye kya documents chahiye?+
RTE (Right to Education) Act ke tahat admission ke liye: EWS Certificate ya BPL Certificate (income/poverty proof), standard mandatory documents (birth certificate, address proof, photo), aur parents ki income/BPL status supporting documents. RTE quota 25% seats ke liye hota hai — ye quota regular priority se alag hai.
Single Girl Child affidavit kahan se banwaayen?+
Single Girl Child affidavit Notary Public ya Judicial Magistrate se banwana hota hai. Ye plain paper par nahi chalta — notarized stamp mandatory hai. Affidavit mein clearly mention hona chahiye ki aapki sirf ek beti hai (koi bhai ya aur behen nahi). Kisi bhi legal stationery shop par ya court ke bahar notary se ban sakta hai — typically ₹100-200 mein.
Defence employee ke liye kya special documents chahiye?+
Defence (serving) employees ke liye: Service Certificate (commanding officer/unit se), Posting Order (current posting showing transferable nature), aur Defence ID Card. Retired Defence (Ex-Serviceman) ke liye: Discharge Certificate, PPO (Pension Payment Order), aur Ex-Serviceman Certificate. Ye documents KV admission mein Priority 1 establish karte hain.
Class 2 aur above ke liye kaunsa extra document chahiye?+
Class 2 aur above (upto Class 12) mein admission ke liye Transfer Certificate (TC) previous school se mandatory hai. TC original hona chahiye aur school ke official seal ke saath. Iske bina Class 2+ mein admission nahi hota — isko pehle se arrange kar lein kyunki TC milne mein 7-15 din lag sakte hain.
