🟡 OBC Relaxation

SSC CGL OBC Age Relaxation 2026

SSC CGL 2026 me OBC (Other Backward Classes) candidates ko 3 saal ki age relaxation milti hai upper age limit se. Matlab agar post ki upper age limit 27 saal hai, to OBC candidate 30 saal tak apply kar sakta hai.

📋 OBC Age Relaxation — Key Points Code 02
01
OBC candidates ko 3 saal ki age relaxation milti hai (Code 02).
02
Non-Creamy Layer certificate mandatory hai — Creamy Layer OBC ko relaxation nahi milti.
03
OBC certificate closing date ke din valid hona chahiye. Purana certificate accepted nahi hoga.
04
OBC + PwBD candidates ko 13 saal ki relaxation milti hai (Code 04).
05
Application form me category code 02 select karna hoga OBC ke liye.

SSC CGL SC/ST Age Relaxation 2026

SSC CGL 2026 me SC (Scheduled Caste) aur ST (Scheduled Tribe) candidates dono ko 5 saal ki age relaxation milti hai. Ye relaxation Code 01 ke under aati hai aur document verification pe caste certificate zaroori hai.

📋 SC/ST Age Relaxation — Key Points Code 01
01
SC aur ST dono categories ko 5 saal ki age relaxation milti hai (Code 01).
02

04

♿ PwBD Relaxation

SSC CGL PwBD Age Relaxation 2026

Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD) candidates ko SSC CGL 2026 me 10 se 15 saal tak ki age relaxation milti hai — jo ki sabse zyada hai. Inka category code 03, 04, ya 05 hota hai depending on their social category.

📋 PwBD Age Relaxation — Complete Details Codes 03, 04, 05
03
PwBD (General/UR) — 10 Years relaxation (Code 03). Base limit 27 hai to max age 37 hogi.
04
PwBD + OBC — 13 Years relaxation (Code 04). OBC aur PwBD dono benefits combine hote hain.
05
PwBD + SC/ST — 15 Years relaxation (Code 05). SSC CGL me ye maximum age relaxation hai.
06
Valid disability certificate (minimum 40% disability) SADM/CMO/Civil Surgeon se required hai.
07
Notification me specifically mentioned post ke liye PwBD reservation check karna zaroori hai.
🎖️ Ex-Servicemen

SSC CGL Ex-Servicemen Age Relaxation 2026

Ex-Servicemen ke liye age relaxation calculation thodi alag hoti hai. Pehle actual age se military service deduct hoti hai, phir uske baad 3 saal ki additional relaxation milti hai. Calculation date 01-08-2026 hai.

📋 ESM Age Calculation Formula Code 06
📐
Formula: Effective Age = Actual Age (as on 01-08-2026) − Military Service Years + 3 Years
📌
Agar aapki actual age 35 saal hai aur military service 8 saal hai: Effective Age = 35 − 8 + 3 = 30 saal
⚡
Phir yeh effective age post ki upper age limit se compare hogi — agar kam ya barabar hai to eligible hain.
📄
Discharge certificate aur service record document verification par mandatory hai.
⚠️
ESM reservation aur age relaxation alag benefits hain — dono alag cheezein hain.
🏛️ Govt Employee

SSC CGL Central Government Employee Age Relaxation 2026

Central Government Civilian Employees jinke paas closing date tak minimum 3 saal ki regular aur continuous service hai, unhe SSC CGL Group 'C' posts ke liye special age relaxation milti hai — ye upper age limit nahi balki maximum allowed age hai.

📋 Central Govt Employee — Age Relaxation Codes 10 & 11
10
Central Govt Employee (General/OBC/EWS): Maximum age limit 40 saal tak allowed hai (Code 10).
11
Central Govt Employee (SC/ST): Maximum age limit 45 saal tak allowed hai (Code 11).
🗓️
Minimum 3 saal ki regular aur continuous service closing date tak honi chahiye — contract / adhoc service nahi chalegi.
📋
Service certificate apne Head of Department se lena hoga document verification ke liye.
⚠️
Ye relaxation sirf Group 'C' posts ke liye hai — Group 'B' posts ke liye applicable nahi.
👩 Widow / Divorced

SSC CGL Widow & Divorced Women Age Relaxation 2026

Widow, Divorced aur Judicially Separated women jinki remarriage nahi hui hai, unhe SSC CGL 2026 me special age relaxation milti hai. Ye Code 12 aur 13 ke under aata hai.

📋 Widow / Divorced Women — Age Relaxation Codes 12 & 13
12
Widow / Divorced Women (General/OBC/EWS): Maximum age 35 saal tak allowed hai (Code 12).
13
Widow / Divorced Women (SC/ST): Maximum age 40 saal tak allowed hai (Code 13).
♀️
Judicially separated women bhi eligible hain — agar woh remarried nahi hain.
📄
Death certificate (widow ke liye), divorce decree / judicial separation order document verification par present karna hoga.
⚠️ Common Mistakes

SSC CGL Age Relaxation — Common Mistakes

Candidates frequently in mistakes ki wajah se apna application reject karwa lete hain. In points ko dhyan se padhein:

❌ Wrong Category Code Select Karna
Bahut log OBC hote hue bhi General select karte hain ya fir wrong code choose karte hain. Notification ki table se apna correct code verify karein.
❌ Creamy Layer OBC Age Relaxation Claim Karna
OBC Creamy Layer candidates age relaxation ke liye eligible nahi hain. Sirf Non-Creamy Layer OBC certificate valid hai.
❌ Galat Cut-off Date Use Karna
Age calculation 01-08-2026 se hogi — koi aur date use karna galat result dega. Official notification ki cut-off date hamesha check karein.
❌ ESM Calculation Galat Karna
ESM ke liye sirf 3 saal add nahi karte — pehle military service minus karni hoti hai actual age se, phir 3 saal add hote hain.
❌ Certificate Nahi Rakhna
Relaxation claim karne par document verification me relevant certificate present karna hoga. Certificate nahi hai to candidature cancel ho sakti hai.
❌ EWS Ko Age Relaxation Expect Karna
SSC CGL 2026 notification me EWS category ke liye koi separate age relaxation nahi di gayi hai. Ye sirf reservation ke liye hai, age relaxation ke liye nahi.
