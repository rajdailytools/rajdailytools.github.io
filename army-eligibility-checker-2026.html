Agniveer Army Complete Guide 2026

Indian Army Agniveer scheme, entry types, aur region-based criteria sab kuch simple Hinglish mein.

🔹 Agniveer Scheme Kya Hai?

Agniveer Scheme — officially "Agnipath" scheme — Indian Army, Navy, aur Air Force mein 4-year short-service recruitment hai. Is scheme ke tahat selected candidates ko "Agniveer" kaha jaata hai. 4 saal ke baad 25% Agniveers ko regular service mein convert kiya jaata hai, baaki ko service fund aur experience ke saath release kiya jaata hai.

Official Army recruitment ke liye visit karein: joinindianarmy.nic.in — latest notification aur rally schedule wahan milega.

🔹 Region-Based Height Relaxation

🏔️ Gorkha
Gorkha candidates (Darjeeling, Sikkim, Nepal-origin) ke liye GD mein height 160 cm hoti hai. Special Gorkha Regiment bhi hoti hai Army mein. Certificate of Gorkha domicile required.
🌿 North East / Assam
Northeast states — Assam, Meghalaya, Manipur, Nagaland, Mizoram, Arunachal, Tripura — ke candidates ke liye height relaxation milti hai. GD: 160 cm. State certificate required.
🌳 ST / Tribal Area
Scheduled Tribe aur tribal area candidates ke liye GD height approximately 162 cm hoti hai. Valid ST certificate required. Recruitment rally mein verify hoti hai.
🏔️ Ladakh / J&K
Ladakh aur J&K region ke candidates ke liye bhi height mein slight variation hoti hai. State-specific norms apply hoti hain. Valid domicile certificate required.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Army eligibility aur Agniveer scheme ke baare mein common sawaal.

Army GD ke liye minimum height kya hai?+
Agniveer GD ke liye General category: 170 cm minimum. Gorkha / NE candidates: 160 cm. ST / Tribal area: approximately 162 cm. Assam region: 160 cm. Height measurement recruitment rally mein medical staff dwara ki jaati hai.
Gorkha candidates ke liye Army mein kya relaxation hai?+
Gorkha candidates ko significant height relaxation milti hai — GD ke liye 160 cm (vs. General 170 cm). Clerk/SKT ke liye 160 cm. Technical ke liye 162 cm. Gorkha candidates ke liye special Gorkha regiments (like 11 Gorkha Rifles) bhi hain. Valid Gorkha domicile/community certificate required hai.
Assam aur North East region ke liye height kitni hoti hai?+
Assam aur all Northeast states (Assam, Meghalaya, Manipur, Nagaland, Arunachal Pradesh, Mizoram, Tripura, Sikkim) ke candidates ke liye height relaxation milti hai — GD ke liye approximately 160 cm. State domicile certificate required hai. Exact criteria recruitment rally notification mein specify hoti hai.
Agniveer Clerk aur GD mein kya difference hai?+
GD (General Duty): 170 cm height, 10th Pass minimum, combat/field roles. Clerk/SKT: 162 cm height (lower!), 12th Pass required with minimum 60% marks, computer/typing skills required, office/administrative roles. Key difference: Clerk ke liye height kam chahiye lekin education zyada. Jo candidates GD ke liye height se thode miss ho jaate hain woh Clerk ke liye eligible ho sakte hain.
Agniveer kya hai aur iska future kya hai?+
Agniveer Scheme (Agnipath) ke tahat Indian Army, Navy, aur Air Force mein 4-year short-term service hoti hai. 4 saal ke baad: 25% Agniveers ko permanent service mein convert kiya jaata hai. Baaki 75% ko "Seva Nidhi" (approximately ₹11.71 lakh tax-free) milta hai plus skill certificates. Many state governments ne Agniveer ex-candidates ke liye police/government jobs mein preference announce ki hai.
Army ke liye age limit kitni hai?+
Agniveer (GD, Clerk, Technical, Tradesman, Nursing Assistant) ke liye age 17.5 years (17 years 6 months) minimum aur 21 years maximum hoti hai as per standard scheme. Government ne kuch cycle mein upper age limit 23 years tak bhi bhadha di thi — latest official notification check karein. Lower age (17.5 se 18 ke beech) ke candidates bhi apply kar sakte hain but joining date par minimum 17.5 complete honi chahiye.
ST / Tribal area ke candidates ko Army mein kya benefit milta hai?+
ST candidates ko height mein approximately 5–8 cm relaxation milti hai. GD ke liye ~162 cm (vs 170 general). Written exam mein marks aur cutoff mein relaxation bhi possible hai. Valid ST certificate state government dwara issued hona zaroori hai. Additional quota benefits recruitment rally mein apply hote hain.
Nursing Assistant ke liye kya eligibility chahiye?+
Nursing Assistant (NA) ke liye: 12th Pass with Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology — PCB) mandatory. Height: 167 cm (General), 162 cm (Gorkha/NE/ST). Minimum 50% in PCB aur English. Age: 17.5–21 years. NA role Army ke medical corps mein hoti hai — hospitals aur field medical units mein service hoti hai.
