SSC GD Eligibility — Complete Guide 2026

SSC GD Mein Attempt Limit Hoti Hai? — Sach Jaano

Ye sabse bada misconception hai SSC GD preparation mein — bahut candidates sochte hain ki 3 ya 4 attempts ke baad apply nahi kar sakte. Yeh bilkul galat hai. SSC GD mein koi fixed attempt limit nahi hoti. Jab tak aap age-eligible hain — chahe pehla attempt ho ya 10waan — apply kar sakte ho. Sirf age limit matter karti hai, attempt count nahi.

Important Clarification: "Attempt limit" wali baat CDS aur NDA jaisi exams pe apply hoti hai — wahan specific attempt limits hain. SSC GD (CAPF recruitment) mein aisi koi restriction nahi. Har saal notification aane par, agar age eligible hai, apply kar sakte ho.

SSC GD Age Limit Calculation — Exactly Kaise Hoti Hai

SSC GD age notification ke hisaab se calculate hoti hai — specific reference date diya jaata hai. Generally January 1 of notification year hota hai. Matlab agar notification mein likha hai "candidates born between 02-Jan-2001 to 01-Jan-2007" — toh sirf in dates ke beech wale eligible hain. DOB calculation mein:

  • Reference date se exact years aur months calculate hote hain
  • 18 years minimum — jab tak 18 nahi hote, eligible nahi
  • Maximum age category ke hisaab se — General 23, OBC 26, SC/ST 28
  • Actual notification mein exact dates clearly mentioned hoti hain
  • Is tool mein Jan 1, 2026 reference date use ki gayi hai estimate ke liye

SSC GD Category Relaxation — Practically Samjho

Category relaxation ka matlab hai ki reserved category candidates ko age upper limit mein extra years milte hain:

  • General/EWS: 18 to 23 years — koi extra relaxation nahi
  • OBC (NCL): 18 to 26 years — 3 extra years. Example: General candidate 24 mein overage, OBC candidate 24 mein still eligible.
  • SC/ST: 18 to 28 years — 5 extra years. Sabse badi relaxation. SC/ST candidates 27-28 mein bhi eligible rehte hain.
  • Ex-Servicemen: Service years ke hisaab se additional relaxation — notification check karo

Qualification Requirement — SSC GD 2026

SSC GD ke liye minimum qualification 12th class (Class XII) pass kisi bhi recognized board se — CBSE, State Board, NIOS, Open Board sab valid hain. Koi stream restriction nahi — Arts, Commerce, Science, Vocational sab. Appearing students bhi apply kar sakte hain lekin joining se pehle pass karna mandatory hai. Graduate ya higher qualification wale bhi eligible hain — no upper qualification restriction.

Future Attempt Planning — Smart Strategy

Agar abhi eligible nahi hain ya pehli attempt mein clear nahi hua, future planning important hai:

  • Age remaining years calculate karo — is tool se exactly pata chalega kitne saal bacha hai
  • Remaining years mein consistently prepare karo — written exam sabse zyada weight rakhta hai
  • Physical preparation parallel mein rakhna — running daily, height/weight maintain karo
  • Document preparation advance mein karo — category certificate validity check karo
  • Multiple government exams parallel mein attempt karo — SSC CHSL, MTS, Railway, Army
  • Har SSC GD notification pe apply karo — cost kam hai, opportunity zyada
Smart Planning Tip: Is tool se apna exact remaining eligible years count karo. Agar 3+ years bachein hain toh proper 6-month preparation cycles plan karo. Agar 1 year se kam bacha hai toh immediately apply karo aur maximum preparation intensity maintain karo. Age ek baar khatam ho jaata hai — use wisely.
50 Frequently Asked Questions

SSC GD eligibility aur attempts ke baare mein sabse common doubts — clearly answered

1. SSC GD Attempt Eligibility Tool kya hai?+
SSC GD Attempt Eligibility Tool ek free online calculator hai jo aapki Date of Birth aur category ke hisaab se exact age calculate karta hai, category relaxation apply karta hai, aur instantly batata hai ki aap SSC GD 2026 ke liye eligible hain ya nahi. 2. SSC GD mein attempt limit hoti hai kya?+
Nahi — SSC GD mein koi fixed attempt limit nahi hoti. Jab tak aap age-eligible hain, tab tak kitni bhi baar apply kar sakte ho. Ye sabse common misconception hai. CDS/NDA mein attempt limits hain — SSC GD (CAPF) mein nahi. Sirf age limit matter karti hai, attempt count nahi.
3. SSC GD age limit 2026 mein kya hai?+
SSC GD 2026 age limits: General/EWS — 18 to 23 years. OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) — 18 to 26 years (+3 relaxation). SC — 18 to 28 years (+5 relaxation). ST — 18 to 28 years (+5 relaxation). Ex-Servicemen — service-based relaxation. Age generally reference date Jan 1 of notification year se calculate hoti hai. Official notification se confirm karein exact dates.
4. OBC candidates ko SSC GD mein age relaxation milti hai?+
Haan — OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) candidates ko 3 saal ki age relaxation milti hai. General mein upper limit 23 hai, OBC mein 26. Matlab 24-25-26 saal ke OBC candidates bhi eligible hain jab General candidates overage ho chuke hote hain. NCL certificate valid hona zaroori hai — current/recent year ka hona preferred hai.
5. SC/ST candidates ko SSC GD mein kitni relaxation milti hai?+
SC aur ST candidates dono ko 5 saal ki age relaxation milti hai — sabse zyada kisi bhi category mein. Upper age limit 23 (General) ki jagah 28 ho jaati hai. Matlab SC/ST candidate 27-28 mein bhi SSC GD ke liye age-eligible hai. Caste certificate prescribed format mein valid hona zaroori hai.
6. SSC GD ke liye minimum age kya hai?+
SSC GD ke liye minimum age 18 years hai — sab categories ke liye same. 18 se neeche koi bhi apply nahi kar sakta regardless of category. Age reference date pe 18 complete hona chahiye. Is tool mein DOB enter karo — exactly pata chalega 18 kab complete ho rahe hain.
7. SSC GD ke liye kya qualification chahiye?+
SSC GD minimum qualification: 12th class pass (Class XII) kisi bhi recognized board se — CBSE, State Board, NIOS, Open Board sab valid. Koi stream restriction nahi — Arts/Commerce/Science/Vocational sab. Appearing students bhi apply kar sakte hain lekin joining tak 12th pass karna mandatory. Graduate ya higher qualification wale bhi eligible — koi upper qualification limit nahi.
8. SSC GD ke liye age kaise calculate hoti hai?+
SSC GD age notification mein diye gaye specific reference date se calculate hoti hai. Generally January 1 of notification year hota hai. Agar notification mein "born between 02-Jan-2001 to 01-Jan-2007" likha hai — toh sirf in dates wale eligible hain. Is tool mein Jan 1, 2026 reference date use ki gayi hai — actual notification se confirm karein.
9. 10th ke baad SSC GD de sakte hain?+
Nahi — SSC GD ke liye minimum 12th class pass hona zaroori hai. 10th pass candidates directly eligible nahi hain. Pehle 12th complete karo — phir SSC GD apply karo. Agar age-eligible ho (18-23/26/28 depending on category), toh 12th complete hone ke baad same recruitment cycle mein ya next mein apply kar sakte ho.
10. SSC GD mein EWS candidates ko kya relaxation milti hai?+
EWS (Economically Weaker Section) candidates ko age mein separately koi additional relaxation nahi milti — age limit General category ke same hai (18-23 years). Lekin EWS candidates ko 10% reservation benefit milta hai vacancies mein. EWS certificate current financial year ka valid hona zaroori hai — income ₹8 lakh se kam aur property criteria meet karna hoga.
11. Kya 12th appearing students SSC GD de sakte hain?+
Haan — 12th appearing students SSC GD apply kar sakte hain. Lekin joining se pehle 12th pass karna mandatory hoga — agar final result aate tak pass nahi kiye toh joining cancel ho sakti hai. This is standard practice — apply karo, appear karo, result aate time pass raho. Age eligible hona zaroori hai — appearing ka koi extra benefit nahi.
12. SSC GD mein Ex-Servicemen candidates ki age limit kya hai?+
Ex-Servicemen candidates ke liye age relaxation service period + 3 years ke hisaab se milti hai — notification mein exact formula given hota hai. Dependent candidates (ESM dependents) ke liye bhi specific relaxation milti hai. Official SSC GD notification pe Ex-SM specific age criteria clearly mentioned hoti hai — confirm karo. Is tool mein approximate check milta hai.
13. SSC GD ke liye physical eligibility kya hai?+
SSC GD physical eligibility: Height — Male General 170 cm, SC/ST/NE 162-165 cm. Female General 157 cm, SC/ST/NE 150 cm. Running — Male 5 km in 24 min, Female 1.6 km in 8:30 min. Chest (Male only) — 80 cm with 5 cm expansion. Weight — proportional to height. Medical — eyesight, color vision, flat foot, overall fitness. Ye sab age eligibility ke baad separate stages mein check hote hain.
14. SSC GD 2026 mein kya nayi eligibility rules hain?+
SSC GD 2026 mein basic eligibility rules previous recruitments ke similar hain — age limits, category relaxation, aur qualification requirements consistent rehte hain. Exact notifications mein minor updates ho sakte hain — official SSC website pe latest notification check karo. Is tool mein May 2026 tak updated information hai — latest notification release hone par official source confirm karo.
15. Agar pehle fail ho gaye toh SSC GD dobara de sakte hain?+
Bilkul — SSC GD mein fail hone ke baad ya non-selection ke baad aap next recruitment cycle mein apply kar sakte ho. Koi "fail ho gaye toh barred" rule nahi hai. Jab tak age eligible hai, har SSC GD notification par apply karo. Previous attempts ka koi negative impact nahi — haan, score improve karne ki koshish karo har attempt mein.
16. SSC GD ke liye nationality kya honi chahiye?+
SSC GD ke liye Indian citizen hona mandatory hai. Nepal aur Bhutan ke citizens bhi certain conditions mein eligible ho sakte hain — notification check karo. OCI (Overseas Citizen of India) ya PIO specific conditions pe eligible ho sakte hain. Foreign nationals generally eligible nahi hain. Nationality proof — Aadhar card, passport ya similar government document acceptable hoga.
17. Kya married candidates SSC GD ke liye eligible hain?+
Haan — marital status SSC GD eligibility affect nahi karta. Married candidates age-eligible hain toh apply kar sakte hain. Koi "unmarried only" restriction nahi hai SSC GD recruitment mein. NDA mein ye restriction hoti hai — SSC GD mein nahi. Age limit aur qualification meet karo — selection process mein participate karo.

SSC GD age eligibility notification mein diye gaye reference date se calculate hoti hai — generally January 1 of notification year. Application date ya exam date se nahi. Notification mein clearly likha hota hai "candidates born on/after [date] and on/before [date] are eligible." 21. Kya SSC GD mein caste certificate zaruri hai?+
Reserved category candidates (OBC/SC/ST) ke liye caste certificate mandatory hai category benefit ke liye. Agar general category mein apply karo toh zaroori nahi — lekin benefit bhi nahi milega. SC/ST certificate permanent hoti hai, OBC-NCL certificate time-limited hoti hai (generally 3 years). EWS certificate current financial year ka chahiye.
22. 23 saal ke General candidate ke liye SSC GD ka kya scope hai?+
23 saal ke General category candidate ke liye ye last or second last attempt ho sakta hai. Agar notification Jan 2026 se pehle hai aur aap 23 complete nahi hue hain — still eligible. 23. NE state candidates ke liye SSC GD mein koi special eligibility hai?+
Northeast state (Assam, Meghalaya, Arunachal, Manipur, Mizoram, Nagaland, Tripura, Sikkim) candidates ke liye height relaxation milti hai — male 162-165 cm, female 150 cm. Age relaxation NE candidates ke liye separately generally nahi hoti — category-wise normal relaxation applicable hai. NE candidates apni category select karein aur is tool se check karein.
24. SSC GD aur Army GD mein eligibility difference kya hai?+
Key differences: SSC GD (CAPF) — minimum 12th pass, age 18-23/26/28, no attempt limit. Army GD — minimum 10th pass, age 17.5-21, different physical standards. SSC GD mein CBT (written exam) zyada important hai merit mein — Army GD mein running marks directly merit mein count hote hain. Both are government jobs — SSC GD CRPF/BSF/CISF mein, Army GD Indian Army mein.
