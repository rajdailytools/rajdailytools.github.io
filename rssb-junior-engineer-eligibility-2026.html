Introduction

The Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB), Jaipur has released the notification for the Junior Engineer Combined Direct Recruitment 2026 (Advt No. 10/2026), inviting online applications for 874 Junior Engineer posts. These posts are spread across the Public Works Department, the Water Resources Department and the Public Health Engineering Department, and include both degree-holder and diploma-holder categories.

Before applying, candidates should be clear on whether they meet the eligibility conditions set out in the official notification — age limit, educational qualification, nationality and other requirements. This page puts together everything that decides "Am I eligible for RSSB Junior Engineer Recruitment 2026?", based strictly on the official notification.

🎓 Educational Qualification

The required qualification depends on whether a post is for degree holders or diploma holders, and on the engineering branch. Post-wise qualification is given below.

Post NameRequired Qualification
Junior Engineer (Civil) – Degree HolderDegree in Civil Engineering from a university established by law in India, or from a recognized institution, or an equivalent qualification declared by the Government
Junior Engineer (Civil) – Diploma HolderDiploma in Civil Engineering from a recognized institution, or a Diploma in Civil Engineering recognized by the Institution of Engineers for the purpose of exemption from its associate membership examination
Junior Engineer (Mechanical/Electrical) – Diploma HolderDiploma in Mechanical/Electrical Engineering from a recognized institution, or an equivalent diploma recognized by the Institution of Engineers for the purpose of exemption from its associate membership examination
For all three categories, candidates must also have a practical working knowledge of Hindi written in the Devanagari script, along with knowledge of Rajasthani culture.

🎂 Age Limit

As per the departmental service rules, a candidate's age is calculated as on the first day of January following the last date of the online application. For this recruitment:

  • Minimum Age: 18 years
  • Maximum Age: 40 years
  • Age Cut-Off Date: 01 January 2027

A candidate must have completed 18 years of age and must not have completed 40 years of age as on 01 January 2027.

The notification also clarifies that since no recruitment took place in the year for which age was earlier calculated as on 01 January 2025 for this Junior Engineer cadre, all applicants have been given an additional one-year relaxation in the upper age limit for this recruitment.
Age Relaxation

Category-wise upper age limit relaxation as per the official notification

CategoryUpper Age Relaxation
SC / ST / OBC / MBC / EWS male candidates (Rajasthan domicile)5 years
General category women candidates5 years
SC / ST / OBC / MBC / EWS women candidates (Rajasthan domicile)10 years

Note: As per the notification, these relaxation provisions are non-cumulative — a candidate can avail the benefit of only one applicable relaxation, and multiple relaxations cannot be added together.

📝 Final Year / Appearing Candidates

Candidates who are appearing in, or awaiting the result of, the final year examination of the qualifying course for the notified posts are permitted to apply for this recruitment.

  • Such candidates must submit proof of having acquired the required educational qualification before appearing in the main written examination (or before the interview, wherever applicable).
  • The required qualification must be obtained on or before the date of the recruitment examination.
  • Candidates who acquire the qualification after the examination date will not be treated as eligible for this recruitment.

🌐 Nationality

As per the official notification, a candidate must be one of the following:

  • A citizen of India, or
  • A subject of Nepal, or
  • A subject of Bhutan, or
  • A Tibetan refugee who came to India before 1 January 1962 with the intention of permanently settling in India, or
  • A person of Indian origin who migrated from Pakistan, Myanmar, Sri Lanka, or the East African countries of Kenya, Uganda, the United Republic of Tanzania, Zambia, Malawi, Zaire and Ethiopia, with the intention of permanently settling in India.
Candidates belonging to categories (b), (c), (d) and (e) above are required to submit an eligibility certificate issued by the Government of India in this regard.

📋 Other Eligibility Conditions

  • Candidates must be of sound mental and physical health, free from any defect that could interfere with the efficient performance of their duties. A fitness certificate from the Chief Medical & Health Officer, or a medical jurist of the district of residence, is required upon selection.
  • A candidate's character must be such that they are suitable for appointment to the post. A character certificate from the last educational institution attended is required, along with certificates from two responsible persons not related to the candidate.
  • A male candidate with more than one living spouse, or a woman candidate who has married a man who already has a living spouse, is not eligible for selection or appointment, except where the Government is satisfied that there are special grounds for granting an exemption.
  • A married candidate who has accepted dowry at the time of marriage is not eligible for appointment to this service.
  • As per the applicable Personnel Department notification, a candidate with more than two living children on or after 1 June 2002 is generally not eligible, subject to the specific exceptions listed in the notification.
  • Marriage registration is mandatory for appointment to government service, as per the applicable circular of the Home Department.

📄 Documents Related to Eligibility

  • Educational qualification certificates and mark sheets (degree/diploma as applicable)
  • Date of birth proof
  • Category/caste certificate (SC/ST/OBC/MBC), where applicable
  • EWS certificate, where applicable
  • Domicile/original resident certificate of Rajasthan, where required for category or Scheduled Area benefits
  • Valid UDID (Unique Disability Identification) card for candidates applying under the Divyangjan/PwD category
  • Discharge certificate / No Objection Certificate for ex-servicemen candidates
  • Sports certificate issued by the competent authority, for candidates applying under the Outstanding Sportsperson category
  • Character certificate from the last educational institution attended
  • Death certificate of husband (for widow candidates) or divorce decree/order (for divorced women candidates), where applicable
  • No Objection Certificate from the current employer, for candidates already in government service

✅ Selection Process

  • Written Examination (Computer Based Test / Tablet Based Test / OMR based, as decided by the Board)
  • Document Verification
  • Medical Examination

The written examination consists of a single objective-type paper of 120 marks in 2 hours, covering General Knowledge (Rajasthan's history, art, culture, geography and political & administrative setup) and the relevant engineering subject. A minimum of 40% marks is required to qualify, and one-third of the marks allotted to a question is deducted for each wrong answer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers based on the RSSB Junior Engineer 2026 official notification

What is the age limit for RSSB Junior Engineer Recruitment 2026?+
Candidates must be at least 18 years old and must not have crossed 40 years of age as on 01 January 2027. Age relaxation is available for reserved categories.
What qualification is required for RSSB Junior Engineer (Civil)?+
A degree in Civil Engineering is required for the degree-holder posts, and a recognized Diploma in Civil Engineering is required for the diploma-holder posts, along with working knowledge of Hindi in Devanagari script.
Can diploma holders apply for RSSB Junior Engineer 2026?+
Yes, dedicated posts are notified for diploma holders in Civil Engineering as well as Mechanical/Electrical Engineering, across all three departments.
Can final-year students apply for RSSB Junior Engineer 2026?+
Yes, candidates in the final year of their qualifying course can apply, but they must acquire the required qualification by the date of the examination.
Is there any age relaxation in RSSB Junior Engineer Recruitment 2026?+
Yes. Reserved category male candidates from Rajasthan get 5 years, reserved category women get 10 years, general category women get 5 years, and ex-servicemen and persons with benchmark disabilities get further relaxation. These relaxations are non-cumulative.
What is the age calculation date for this recruitment?+
Age is calculated as on 01 January 2027, which is the first day of January following the last date of the online application.
How many total posts are there in RSSB JE Recruitment 2026?+
A total of 874 Junior Engineer posts have been notified across the Public Works Department, Water Resources Department and Public Health Engineering Department.
Is domicile of Rajasthan mandatory to apply?+
Domicile of Rajasthan is not required to apply for the non-scheduled area posts in general, but reservation benefits under SC/ST/OBC/MBC/EWS categories are available only to candidates who are original residents of Rajasthan. Posts notified for the Scheduled Area can be applied for only by local residents of Rajasthan's Scheduled Area.
