Complete Guide

IOB LBO Eligibility Complete Guide 2026-27

Har eligibility criteria ka detailed explanation — sab kuch official notification ke hisaab se.

Nationality / Citizenship

Official notification ke anusaar candidate ko in mein se koi ek category ka hona chahiye:

  • Citizen of India
  • Subject of Nepal
  • Subject of Bhutan
  • Tibetan refugee jo 1 January 1962 se pehle permanently settle hone ki niyat se India aaya ho
  • Pakistan, Burma, Sri Lanka, East African countries (Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Zambia, Malawi, Zaire, Ethiopia) ya Vietnam se migrate hua Indian-origin person, permanently settle hone ki niyat se
Important: Categories 2-5 ke candidates ke paas notification se pehle Government of India dwara issued eligibility certificate hona zaroori hai.

Age Limit

Minimum age 20 saal aur maximum age 30 saal honi chahiye, cut-off date 01.08.2026 ke hisaab se. SC/ST/OBC/PwBD/Ex-servicemen/1984-riot affected candidates ko upar diye gaye table ke anusaar relaxation milti hai. Detailed age breakdown ke liye hamara IOB LBO Age Calculator bhi use kar sakte hain.

Educational Qualification

Candidate ke paas kisi bhi discipline mein Degree (Graduation) honi chahiye, Government of India dwara recognized University se, ya Central Government dwara recognized equivalent qualification. Candidate ke paas valid Mark-sheet / Degree Certificate hona zaroori hai jo ye prove kare ki wo online registration ke din graduate hai, aur registration ke time percentage of marks bhi indicate karna hoga.

Local Language Proficiency

Candidate ko apply kiye gaye state ki local language mein reading, writing aur speaking proficient hona chahiye — jaisa vacancy table mein diya gaya hai (Tamil Nadu-Tamil, Karnataka-Kannada, Maharashtra-Marathi, Gujarat-Gujarati). Agar candidate ne 10th ya 12th standard mein ye subject nahi padha, to Personal Interview se pehle Local Language Proficiency Test (LPT) clear karni hogi. LPT clear na karne par further selection process ke liye consider nahi kiya jayega.

Note: Candidate ek hi state ke liye apply kar sakta hai. Registration ke baad state change karne ki permission nahi hai.

Worked Examples

✅ Example 1 — All Criteria Met
Citizen of India, DOB 15 June 2000 (age 26 as on 01.08.2026), Graduate, Tamil Nadu applicant who studied Tamil in 12th → Basic Eligibility Criteria Met
✅ Example 2 — Language Conditional
Citizen of India, SC category, DOB 10 March 1995 (age 31, within relaxed max 35), Graduate, Gujarat applicant, did not study Gujarati in 10th/12th → Basic Criteria Met, LPT Required
❌ Example 3 — Not a Graduate
Citizen of India, DOB 1 January 2000, age criteria met, but does not yet hold a valid graduation degree/marksheet → Educational Qualification Not Met
❌ Example 4 — Age Not Met
Citizen of India, General category, DOB 1 January 1990 (age 36), Graduate → Age Criteria Not Met (max permissible age is 30 for General)

Document Verification

Documents like Nationality proof, Date of Birth proof, Educational certificates, Language proficiency proof (10th/12th marksheet) aur Category certificates (as applicable) must be produced in original with photocopies at the time of Personal Interview. This eligibility checker cannot verify documents — final eligibility depends on document verification by Indian Overseas Bank.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

IOB LBO 2026-27 eligibility ke baare mein common sawaal.

IOB LBO 2026-27 ke liye basic eligibility criteria kya hain?+
Official notification (Advt No. HRDD/RECT/02/2026-27) ke anusaar candidate ka Citizen of India (ya eligible nationality category), age 20-30 years (relaxation applicable), any discipline me graduation degree, aur applied state ki local language proficiency honi chahiye.
Kya ye tool final eligibility confirm karta hai?+
Nahi. Ye tool sirf ek preliminary basic eligibility check deta hai jo aapke diye gaye inputs par based hai. Final eligibility Indian Overseas Bank dwara document verification ke baad hi decide hoti hai.
Agar maine local language 10th/12th me nahi padhi to kya karna hoga?+
Aapko Personal Interview se pehle Local Language Proficiency Test (LPT) clear karni hogi, jaisa official notification me bataya gaya hai. Isse aapki basic eligibility disqualify nahi hoti, lekin LPT clear karna zaroori hai.
Kya main ek se zyada state ke liye apply kar sakta hoon?+
Nahi. Official notification ke anusaar candidate sirf ek state ke liye apply kar sakta hai, aur registration ke baad state change karne ki permission nahi hai.
Educational qualification ke liye kya specific degree chahiye?+
Nahi, koi specific discipline required nahi hai. Kisi bhi discipline mein Degree (Graduation), Government of India dwara recognized University se, ya equivalent qualification chahiye — aur registration ke din valid marksheet/degree certificate hona zaroori hai.
Kya experience IOB LBO 2026-27 ke liye mandatory hai?+
Nahi, experience eligibility criteria ka hissa nahi hai. Halaanki, official notification ke anusaar Scheduled Commercial Bank mein prior Officer service ke liye advance increment mil sakta hai (max 2 increments), agar job profile match kare — lekin ye eligibility ke liye required nahi hai.
Kya ye calculator official IOB result deta hai?+
Nahi. Ye tool sirf eligibility ka preliminary estimate deta hai. Final eligibility aur selection Indian Overseas Bank ke official process se hi decide hoti hai.
