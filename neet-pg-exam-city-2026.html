How to Check NEET PG Exam City Details 2026

1Visit the official NBEMS website — natboard.edu.in.
2Go to the Examinations section and select NEET-PG.
3Click on the candidate login / application link for the NEET PG 2026 session.
4Enter your registered User ID and Password to log in.
5Open the City Intimation Slip link on your candidate dashboard.
6Your allotted exam city will appear on the screen — check it carefully.
7Download and save the city intimation slip for future reference.

What You'll Need

🔑 Details Required to Check Exam City

  • Registered User ID
  • Password set at the time of NEET PG 2026 registration
  • RajDailyTools only links you to the official login — we never ask for or store your credentials.

📋 Exam City Slip vs Admit Card

  • The city intimation slip only tells you the allotted exam city, so you can plan travel and stay.
  • It is not the admit card and does not carry your exact centre address or reporting time.
  • The admit card with full centre details will be released separately by NBEMS before the exam.

Important Instructions

Use only natboard.edu.in. NBEMS has not authorised any other website to host the NEET PG city intimation slip or admit card — avoid third-party "direct download" links. Check the city details carefully, keep your login credentials secure, download the slip for your records, and rely only on the official website for further updates rather than unofficial admit-card claims.
