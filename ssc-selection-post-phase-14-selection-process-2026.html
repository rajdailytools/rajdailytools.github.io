Single CBE stage: Recruitment is through one Computer Based Examination (CBE) of Objective Multiple Choice Questions — conducted separately for Matriculation, Higher Secondary (10+2) and Graduation & above levels.

SSC Selection Post Phase XIV/2026 — Selection Process Overview

As per Para 19.1 of the official notification (Advertisement No. Phase-XIV/2026/Selection Posts), recruitment to Selection Posts is made through an examination conducted in Computer Based Mode, consisting of Objective Type Multiple Choice Questions.

The overall selection procedure, as laid down across Para 15 (Scheme of Examination), Para 17 (Document Verification) and Para 19 (Selection Procedure) of the notification, follows this sequence:

  • Online Application submission and provisional acceptance by the Commission.
  • Computer Based Examination (CBE) — one of three separate CBEs depending on the Educational Qualification level of the post applied for.
  • Shortlisting for Scrutiny of Documents, based on CBE score and merit, in a prescribed vacancy-based ratio.
  • Upload of self-attested supporting documents (Educational Qualification, Experience, Category, Age, Age-relaxation, etc.) for Scrutiny.
  • Skill Test (Typing/Data Entry/Computer Proficiency Test, etc.), wherever prescribed as an Essential Qualification for the post.
  • Document Verification (DV), conducted by the concerned User Department/Organization.
  • Preparation of the Final Merit List and nomination of selected candidates to the User Departments.

Candidature remains purely provisional at every stage of this process, as stated in Para 16.2 and Para 19.19 of the notification, and is subject to final scrutiny of eligibility conditions.

Computer Based Examination (CBE)

As per Para 15 (Scheme of Examination) of the official notification

PartSubjectQuestionsMax. MarksDuration
AGeneral Intelligence255060 Minutes total (sectional timer of 15 minutes per part); 80 minutes (20 min/part) for candidates eligible for scribes
BGeneral Awareness2550
CQuantitative Aptitude (Basic Arithmetic Skill)2550
DEnglish Language (Basic Knowledge)2550
Total100200—
Three separate CBEs (Para 15.1): One for posts with minimum Educational Qualification of Matriculation Level, one for Higher Secondary (10+2) Level, and one for Graduation & above Level. Common candidates applying across levels must appear separately for each level.
Negative Marking (Para 15.2): 0.50 marks will be deducted for each wrong answer.
Normalization (Para 15.3): If CBE is conducted in multiple shifts, marks will be normalized as per the procedure published vide Notice No. HQ-PP001/6/2024-PP dated 02.06.2025, and the normalized scores will be used for final merit and cut-off.
Medium of Examination (Para 15.4): CBE will be conducted in Hindi and English only. Candidates select their medium at the application stage (Sr. No. 23) and responses are recorded only in the selected language.
Differential Qualifying Standards (Para 15.6): The Commission has discretion to fix different minimum qualifying standards for each CBE component, considering category-wise vacancies and number of candidates.
Answer Keys (Para 15.7–15.8): Tentative Answer Keys are placed on the Commission's website; representations can be submitted online at Rs. 50 per question within the given time limit. The Final Answer Key is then used for processing results.

Skill Test / Typing Test

As per Para 15.5 and Para 19.11 of the official notification, a Skill Test such as Typing Test, Data Entry Test or Computer Proficiency Test is conducted only for those categories of posts where it is prescribed as part of the Essential Qualification.

  • The Skill Test is qualifying in nature, unless specified otherwise in the Post Details and any Addendum/Corrigendum issued by the Commission.
  • The Skill Test is conducted from amongst candidates who have uploaded the relevant documents at the Scrutiny stage and are found eligible, by the concerned User Department/Regional Office.
  • Candidates should refer to the Post Details (Annexure-III) for the specific post they are applying to, to check whether a Skill Test applies to that post.

Information not specified in the official notification: The main notification PDF does not specify exact typing speed, duration, software, or qualifying standards for the Skill Test — these details, wherever applicable, are given in the Post Details of Annexure-III for the specific post.

Document Verification (DV)

As per Para 17 of the official notification, Document Verification (DV) is conducted by the concerned User Department/Organization after shortlisting of candidates based on the CBE result and Scrutiny of the documents submitted by them.

What to Carry for DV (Para 17.3–17.4)

  • Two passport size recent colour photographs and one Original Photo ID Proof.
  • Matriculation/Secondary Certificate.
  • Educational Qualification Certificate as per the requirement of the post applied for.
  • Order/letter for equivalent Educational Qualification, if claiming equivalence.
  • Experience Certificate, if required for the post.
  • Caste/Category Certificate, if belonging to a reserved category.
  • Persons with Disabilities (PwD) Certificate in prescribed format, if applicable.
  • Requisite Ex-Servicemen (ESM) certificates — Serving Defence Personnel Certificate (Annexure-IX), Undertaking (Annexure-IX(A)) or Discharge Certificate.
  • Relevant certificate if seeking age relaxation, and a No Objection Certificate for CGCE candidates.
  • Name-change documents (marriage/re-marriage/divorce/other), where applicable.
  • Any other document specified in the Admission Certificate for DV.

No request for change of DV venue is entertained by the Commission or User Department (Para 17.1). If any information furnished in the application is found wrong or incomplete during DV, the candidature is rejected forthwith, and no appeal against such rejection is entertained (Para 19.13).

Medical Standards

As per Para 28 ("Good Mental and Bodily Health of the Candidate") of the official notification: a candidate must be in good mental and bodily health and free from any physical defect likely to interfere with efficient discharge of duties as an officer of the service.

  • A candidate who, after medical examination prescribed by the Competent Authority, is found not to satisfy these requirements, will not be appointed.
  • Only candidates who are likely to be considered for appointment will be medically examined.
  • For disabled Ex-Defence Services Personnel, a certificate of fitness granted by the Demobilisation Medical Board of the Defence Services is considered adequate for the purpose of appointment.

Information not specified in the official notification: Post-specific physical/medical standards (e.g. height, chest, vision standards for individual posts) are not detailed in the main notification PDF — these, wherever applicable, are given in the Post Details of Annexure-III.

Final Merit Process

As per Para 19.14–19.21 of the official notification, the Final Merit List and selection process follows these principles:

  • Scores/marks of selected candidates are disclosed on the website of the concerned Regional Office at the time of declaration of the Final Result for that category of post (Para 19.14).
  • SC, ST, OBC, ESM, EWS & PwBD candidates selected on their own merit without relaxed standards are not adjusted against the reserved share of vacancies — they are accommodated against unreserved vacancies or their category's earmarked vacancies, whichever is more advantageous to them (Para 19.15).
  • Candidates of these categories who qualify on the basis of relaxed standards (age limit, experience, qualification, permitted chances, extended zone of consideration, etc.) are counted against reserved vacancies, irrespective of merit position (Para 19.16).
  • Deduction of military service period for Ex-Servicemen, and the 10-year age relaxation for PwBD candidates, are not treated as "relaxed standards" for this purpose (Para 19.16).
  • A benchmark disability candidate selected on own merit can be appointed against an unreserved vacancy if the post is identified suitable for that disability category (Para 19.17).
  • Success in the examination confers no automatic right to appointment unless the Government is satisfied, after due enquiry, that the candidate is suitable in all respects (Para 19.18).
  • Selected candidates carry an All India Service Liability (AISL) and are liable to serve anywhere in India (Para 19.20).
  • Candidates may be required to acquire proficiency in the local language of their allotted State/UT/Zone for confirmation to the post (Para 19.21).
Resolution of Tie-Cases

As per Para 20 of the official notification — applied one after another, till the tie is resolved

PriorityTie-Breaking Criterion
1Total Normalized Marks in Computer Based Examination
2Marks in Part-A of Computer Based Examination (General Intelligence)
3Marks in Part-B of Computer Based Examination (General Awareness)
4Marks in Part-C of Computer Based Examination (Quantitative Aptitude)
5Date of Birth — older candidate placed higher
6Alphabetical order in which the names of the candidates appear

Important Instructions

Based on Para 16, Para 19 and Para 21 of the official notification, candidates should note the following:

  • Admission at all stages is purely provisional, subject to satisfying eligibility conditions — candidature can be cancelled at any stage without notice if conditions are not met (Para 16.2, 19.19).
  • Admission Certificates for CBE are available only on the Commission's website — not sent by post or email (Para 16.3).
  • Candidates must bring a printout of the Admission Certificate to the exam hall along with a valid original Photo ID (Para 16.7–16.8).
  • Applications with blurred/miniature/no signature, or photographs not matching the acceptable specimen, are liable to be rejected summarily (Para 19.3).
  • 'Common candidates' applying across different post levels must appear separately for each level's CBE, or their candidature will be cancelled (Para 19.4–19.6).
  • Appearing twice in the CBE of the same Educational Qualification level leads to cancellation of candidature (Para 21.1.14).
  • Non-uploading of self-attested documents when called for, or non-production of original certificates at DV, leads to cancellation (Para 21.1.7, 21.1.12).
  • The Commission undertakes post-examination analysis including log analysis, photograph/biometric verification and CCTV review; malpractice leads to cancellation and debarment ranging from 1 to 7 years depending on the offence (Para 22).
  • The Commission's decision on all matters — eligibility, mode of selection, merit list preparation and post allocation — is final and binding (Para 23).

Common Mistakes

Frequent errors candidates make during the Selection Process

❌ Assuming Skill Test applies to every post
Skill Test is conducted only where prescribed in the Essential Qualification of the specific post — always check the Post Details in Annexure-III.
❌ Skipping the Scrutiny document upload
Failing to upload self-attested documents for Scrutiny after the CBE result leads to summary cancellation of candidature.
❌ Not bringing original documents to DV
Non-production of original certificates at Document Verification, as per Para 21.1.12, is a direct ground for rejection.
❌ Appearing twice for the same CBE level
Common candidates appearing more than once in the CBE of the same Educational Qualification level get their candidature cancelled (Para 19.6, 21.1.14).
❌ Requesting a change of DV venue
No request for change of Document Verification venue is entertained by the Commission or User Department (Para 17.1).
❌ Mismatched information in the application
Any information found wrong or incomplete during DV leads to forthwith rejection, with no appeal entertained (Para 19.13).
Tips to Qualify

Manage negative marking wisely

🪪
Carry valid ID & correct documents to DV
Bring all original documents exactly as listed in Para 17.4, along with two passport photos and a valid original photo ID.
Stage-wise Preparation Strategy

How to prepare for each stage of the Selection Process

1
Before the CBE
Revise all four subject areas — General Intelligence, General Awareness, Quantitative Aptitude and English Language — as per the Indicative Syllabus (Para 15.9) for your applicable Educational Qualification level. Practice full-length mock tests within the sectional time limits.
2
After CBE, before Scrutiny
Watch the Commission's website for your result and shortlisting status. Organize self-attested copies of all documents relevant to your Educational Qualification, Category, Age-relaxation and Experience.
3
If a Skill Test applies to your post
Check the Post Details in Annexure-III for whether a Typing/Data Entry/Computer Proficiency Test is prescribed, and begin practicing well ahead of the scheduled test.
4
Before Document Verification
Prepare the complete original document set listed in Para 17.4 — Matriculation certificate, EQ certificate, category/age-relaxation certificates and any post-specific documents mentioned in your Admission Certificate.
5
After Document Verification
Regularly check the concerned Regional Office/User Department website for updates on the Final Merit List and nomination status.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers based on the official SSC Selection Post Phase-XIV/2026 notification

SSC Selection Post Phase XIV/2026 ka selection process kya hai?+
Selection ek Computer Based Examination (CBE) ke through hota hai, uske baad Scrutiny of Documents, Skill Test (jahan prescribed ho), aur Document Verification hoti hai. Final Merit List CBE marks ke aadhar par banti hai (Para 19.1, 19.11, 19.14).
Kitne CBE hote hain?+
Teen alag CBE hote hain — Matriculation Level, Higher Secondary (10+2) Level aur Graduation & above Level ke posts ke liye (Para 15.1).
CBE me kitne questions aur marks hote hain?+
4 Parts (A-D) me 25-25 questions, har part 50 marks — total 100 questions aur 200 marks (Para 15.1).
Negative marking kitni hai?+
Har galat jawab ke liye 0.50 marks kaate jaate hain (Para 15.2).
Skill Test kis-kis post ke liye hota hai?+
Sirf un posts ke liye jahan Essential Qualification me Typing/Data Entry/Computer Proficiency Test prescribed ho. Ye Skill Test qualifying nature ka hota hai, jab tak Post Details/Addendum me alag se na bataya gaya ho (Para 15.5, 19.11).
Document Verification kaun conduct karta hai?+
DV concerned User Department/Organization dwara conduct ki jaati hai, CBE result aur Scrutiny ke baad (Para 17.1).
Cut-off marks kitne hain?+
UR ke liye 30%, OBC/EWS ke liye 25%, aur baaki categories ke liye 20% minimum qualifying cut-off hai (Para 19.7).
Scrutiny ke liye kitne candidates shortlist hote hain?+
5 tak vacancies ke liye 1:30 ratio, 5 se zyada vacancies ke liye 1:15 ratio (minimum 150) me candidates shortlist hote hain. PwBD/ESM category ke sabhi qualifying candidates shortlist hote hain (Para 19.8).
Tie hone par kaise resolve hota hai?+
6-step criteria follow hoti hai — Normalized CBE marks, phir Part-A, Part-B, Part-C marks, phir Date of Birth (bade candidate ko priority), aur last me alphabetical order (Para 20).
Kya exam pass karne se appointment guarantee hota hai?+
Nahi, success in examination confers no right of appointment — Government ki satisfaction ke baad hi appointment hoti hai (Para 19.18).
Medical standard kya hai?+
Candidate ko good mental aur bodily health me hona chahiye, aur efficient duty discharge me baadha daalne wale physical defect se free hona chahiye. Competent Authority ke medical examination ke baad hi confirm hota hai (Para 28).
Post-wise exact selection stages kahan milenge?+
Post-wise Skill Test applicability aur medical standards Annexure-III (Post Details) me diye gaye hain, jo ssc.gov.in/rhq-selection-post/rhq-post-details par dekhe ja sakte hain — main notification PDF me ye directly nahi diya gaya.
