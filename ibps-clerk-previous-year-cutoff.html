IBPS Clerk Previous Year Cut Off

State-wise and category-wise IBPS Clerk Prelims, Mains and Reserve List cut off marks for 2025, 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021 and 2020 — compiled in one place for quick reference.

IBPS Clerk previous year cut off refers to the minimum marks a candidate needed to clear a particular stage of the IBPS Clerk recruitment exam in earlier years. Reviewing these numbers helps candidates gauge how competitive the exam has been and set a realistic score target while preparing.

The cut off is not the same for everyone — it varies by State/UT, by category (SC, ST, OBC, EWS, General/UR) and by exam stage. A candidate's applicable cut off depends on the state they applied for and the category they belong to.

Prelims and Mains cut off are different stages with separate qualifying marks. Clearing the Prelims cut off only qualifies a candidate for the Mains exam; the final selection depends on the Mains (and, in some years, Reserve List) cut off.

Note: previous-year cut off is a useful reference for preparation but does not guarantee or predict the cut off for any upcoming exam, since it depends on factors like vacancies, number of applicants and difficulty level each year.
