🚂 Railway RRB ALP Exam Kya Hai?

RRB ALP (Assistant Loco Pilot) Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) dwara conduct kiya jaata hai. Assistant Loco Pilot ki job Indian Railways mein ek Grade C post hai — train driving team ka important member. ALP ka kaam locomotive pilot (LP) ko assist karna, instruments monitor karna aur safety ensure karna hota hai.

2026 mein CEN-01/2026 ke through approximately 11,127 ALP posts bharti ki ja rahi hain — puri country ke different railway zones mein. Ye ek prestigious, permanent government job hai jisme job security, good salary, aur railway perks milte hain.

👤 Age Eligibility — Simple Language Mein

RRB ALP 2026 ke liye age 01 July 2026 ko reference date maan kar calculate hogi. Matlab aapki age us date par kya hogi — wahi count karegi.

  • Minimum Age: 18 years (as on 01 July 2026) — sabhi categories ke liye same
  • General / EWS: Maximum 30 years
  • OBC (Non-Creamy Layer): Maximum 33 years (+3 years relaxation)
  • SC / ST: Maximum 35 years (+5 years relaxation)
  • PwD / Ex-Serviceman: As per Railway board regulations (additional relaxation)
Example: DOB 05 August 1996 → Age on 01 July 2026 = 29 years 10 months = ✅ Eligible for General. DOB 20 March 1994 → Age = 32 years 3 months = ❌ NOT eligible for General, but ✅ eligible for OBC (max 33).

🎓 Qualification Required for RRB ALP 2026

RRB ALP ke liye teen types ki qualification accept hoti hai:

  • 10th Pass + ITI Certificate: ITI must be from a NCVT/SCVT recognized institute in a relevant trade (Electrician, Fitter, Mechanic, etc.)
  • Diploma in Engineering: From a recognized institution in Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics, or Automobile Engineering
  • B.E. / B.Tech Degree: Engineering degree in relevant discipline from a recognized university
ITI Students Note: Haan — ITI certificate holders ALP ke liye apply kar sakte hain. Ye RRB ALP ki ek badi khasiyat hai — 10th ke baad ITI karne wale directly eligible hote hain. Trade relevant hona zaroori hai — check notification for complete trade list.

🔢 Selection Process — RRB ALP 2026

  • CBT 1 (Computer-Based Test): 75 questions, 60 minutes — Mathematics, General Intelligence, General Science, General Awareness
  • CBT 2 (Computer-Based Test): Part A (100 questions) + Part B (75 questions) — Trade specific syllabus
  • CBAT (Computer-Based Aptitude Test): For shortlisted candidates — tests spatial reasoning, memory, etc.
  • Document Verification: All original documents verified
  • Medical Examination: Vision and physical fitness standards must be met

❌ Common Mistakes Jo Students Karte Hain

❌ Galat Cutoff Date Use Karna
Age aaj ki date se count karna — WRONG. RRB ALP 2026 ke liye sirf 01 July 2026 use karein. Ye official cutoff date hai.
❌ Category Relaxation Bhoolna
General ki 30 saal limit dekh ke OBC/SC/ST candidates discourage ho jaate hain — apni category ke liye sahi limit check karein.
❌ ITI Trade Verify Na Karna
Sirf ITI certificate hone se kaam nahi — trade relevant hona zaroori hai. Official notification mein approved trades ki list zaroor dekhe.
❌ Last Date Miss Karna
14 June 2026 ke baad application accept nahi hogi. Last minute mein technical issues aa sakte hain — 5-7 din pehle apply karein.

RRB ALP 2026 eligibility ke baare mein common sawaal.

RRB ALP 2026 mein age limit kya hai?+
RRB ALP 2026 ke liye minimum age 18 years aur maximum age 30 years hai (General/EWS) — as on 01 July 2026. OBC (NCL) ke liye max 33 years (+3 relaxation), SC/ST ke liye max 35 years (+5 relaxation). PwD aur Ex-Servicemen ke liye Railway board ke rules ke hisaab se additional relaxation milti hai. Always official notification verify karein.
RRB ALP ke liye qualification kya chahiye?+
RRB ALP 2026 ke liye teen options hain: (1) 10th Pass + ITI Certificate (relevant trade, NCVT/SCVT recognized); (2) Diploma in Engineering (Mechanical/Electrical/Electronics/Automobile or equivalent); (3) B.E./B.Tech Degree in relevant Engineering discipline. Sirf koi ek qualification sufficient hai. Trade/specialization relevant honi chahiye — complete list official notification mein check karein.
Kya ITI students RRB ALP ke liye apply kar sakte hain?+
Haan — bilkul! ITI certificate holders RRB ALP ke liye fully eligible hain provided they have 10th pass certificate along with ITI from a recognized institute. Ye RRB ALP ki ek major advantage hai — graduation ki zaroorat nahi. ITI trade relevant honi chahiye (Electrician, Fitter, Welder, Mechanic etc.) — official notification mein approved trades ki complete list di hoti hai.
RRB ALP 2026 mein age cutoff date kya hai?+
RRB ALP 2026 ke liye official age cutoff date 01 July 2026 hai. Matlab aapki age 01 July 2026 ko jo hogi — wohi eligibility decide karegi. Form fill karne ki date ya exam date se koi farak nahi padta. Is tool mein apni DOB enter karo — automatically 01 July 2026 ko exact age calculate ho jaati hai.
Kya ye eligibility calculator accurate hai?+
Ye tool official RRB ALP 2026 short notice (CEN-01/2026) ke mutabiq published age norms aur qualification criteria par based hai. Age calculations mathematically accurate hain. Lekin short notice ke baad final notification mein details change ho sakti hain. Isliye hamesha indianrailways.gov.in se official notification se final confirmation zaroor lein before applying. Ye tool quick reference ke liye hai.
RRB ALP 2026 ke liye officially apply kahan karein?+
RRB ALP 2026 ke liye officially apply karne ka link indianrailways.gov.in par 15 May 2026 se activate hoga. Alternatively, apne zone ka RRB website visit karein (e.g., rrbahmedabad.gov.in, rrbbhopal.gov.in etc.). Last date hai 14 June 2026 (tentative). RRB ALP mein total kitne posts hain 2026 mein?+

RRB ALP ka selection process kya hai?+
RRB ALP 2026 selection 4 stages mein hota hai: (1) CBT 1 — Computer-Based Test (75 questions, 60 min); (2) CBT 2 — Part A general + Part B trade-specific (175 questions total); (3) CBAT — Computer-Based Aptitude Test for shortlisted candidates; (4) Document Verification & Medical Examination. Medical standards ke liye vision requirements strict hoti hain — official notification check karein.
