Introduction

GIMS Noida Staff Nurse Recruitment 2026 ke liye apply karne se pehle candidates ko eligibility criteria aur educational qualification ko dhyan se samajhna chahiye. Is page par diya gaya har fact seedha official GIMS notification (Advt. No. GIMS/2026/Manpower/NT-03/10, dated 09-08-2026) ke Point 2 "Educational and Experience Qualifications" se liya gaya hai; jahan notification me information nahi hai, wahan koi anuman nahi lagaya gaya hai.

Government Institute of Medical Sciences (GIMS), Greater Noida ne Staff Nurse ke Non-Teaching posts par sidhi bharti ke liye Computer Based Test (CBT) Examination-2026 ke madhyam se online applications aamantrit ki hain. Is page par sirf eligibility aur qualification requirements cover ki gayi hain — age limit, exam pattern, selection process, application fee aur anya vishay is page ka hissa nahi hain.

Educational Qualification

As per Point 2 "Educational and Experience Qualifications" of the official GIMS notification, a candidate must possess ONE of the following two qualification paths for the post of Staff Nurse

Path A
B.Sc Nursing Route
1
B.Sc Nursing (4 years course) from an Indian Nursing Council / State Medical Faculty recognized Institute or University OR B.Sc. Nursing (Post Basic) (2 Years Course) from an Indian Nursing Council recognized institute/university.
2
Registered as Nurse & Midwife in State / Indian Nursing Council.
OR
Path B
GNM Diploma Route
1
Diploma in General Nursing Midwifery (G.N.M.) from an Indian Nursing Council recognized Institute / Board or Council.
2
Registered as Nurse & Midwife in State / Indian Nursing Council.
3
Two Years' experience in a minimum 50-bedded hospital, after acquiring the above educational qualification.
Important: These are two separate, independent qualification routes as stated in the official notification. Candidates satisfying either Path A or Path B are considered eligible on the qualification front — the notification does not state that one qualification is equivalent to the other.
Nursing Qualification Details

Note: The official notification does not mention any other qualification (such as B.Sc Nursing Post Basic Distance Mode, ANM, or M.Sc Nursing) as eligible for this post. Only the qualifications listed above are accepted.

Registration Requirement

As per the official notification, irrespective of which qualification path a candidate holds (B.Sc Nursing or GNM Diploma), the candidate must be registered as Nurse & Midwife in the State Nursing Council or the Indian Nursing Council.

  • Registration is a mandatory eligibility requirement under both qualification paths.
  • The registration must be held by the candidate as a Nurse & Midwife specifically, as stated in the official notification.
  • The official notification does not specify further procedural details about the registration certificate beyond this requirement.

Experience Requirement

The official notification specifies an experience requirement only for candidates applying through the GNM Diploma path (Path B):

  • Required Experience: Two (2) years
  • Type of Experience: Clinical/hospital experience, after acquiring the GNM educational qualification
  • Institution Requirement: The experience must be in a hospital with a minimum of 50 beds
For B.Sc Nursing candidates (Path A): The official notification does not specify any separate experience requirement. Only the qualification and registration mentioned under Path A are stated as mandatory.
Eligibility Checklist

Every item below is directly supported by the official GIMS notification

Required Qualification: B.Sc Nursing (4-year) / B.Sc Nursing Post Basic (2-year) OR GNM Diploma, from an Indian Nursing Council / State Medical Faculty recognized Institute, Board, Council or University.
Required Nursing Registration: Registered as Nurse & Midwife in State / Indian Nursing Council (mandatory for both qualification paths).
Required Experience (GNM path only): 2 years of experience in a minimum 50-bedded hospital, after acquiring the GNM qualification.
Qualification Deadline: All mandatory qualifications (educational and other) must be held by the candidate up to the last date of application.
Who Can Apply?

A simple, candidate-friendly summary based only on the official qualification criteria

You may be eligible if:
  • ✅ You hold a B.Sc Nursing (4-year) or B.Sc Nursing Post Basic (2-year) degree from an Indian Nursing Council / State Medical Faculty recognized Institute or University, and are registered as Nurse & Midwife.
  • ✅ OR you hold a GNM Diploma from an Indian Nursing Council recognized Institute/Board/Council, are registered as Nurse & Midwife, and have 2 years' experience in a minimum 50-bedded hospital after acquiring the GNM qualification.
You may not be eligible if:
  • ❌ You do not hold either B.Sc Nursing (4-year/Post Basic) or a GNM Diploma from an Indian Nursing Council / State Medical Faculty recognized source.
  • ❌ You are not registered as Nurse & Midwife in a State or Indian Nursing Council.
  • ❌ You hold a GNM Diploma but do not have 2 years' experience in a minimum 50-bedded hospital after acquiring the qualification.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers based on the official GIMS Noida Staff Nurse notification (Point 2)

GIMS Noida Staff Nurse 2026 ke liye educational qualification kya hai?+
Candidate ke paas B.Sc Nursing (4 years) ya B.Sc Nursing Post Basic (2 years) degree ho aur State/Indian Nursing Council mein Nurse & Midwife ke roop mein registered ho, YA GNM Diploma ho, registration ho aur ek minimum 50-bedded hospital mein 2 saal ka experience ho.
Kya B.Sc Nursing zaroori hai?+
Nahi, B.Sc Nursing (4-year ya Post Basic 2-year) ek qualifying option hai. GNM Diploma bhi wikalpik roop mein (registration aur experience ke saath) sweekar kiya jata hai.
Kya GNM qualification accepted hai?+
Haan, Indian Nursing Council se manyata prapt sanstha se GNM Diploma sweekar kiya jata hai, basharte candidate registered ho aur qualification prapt karne ke baad minimum 50-bedded hospital mein 2 saal ka anubhav ho.
Kya Nursing Council registration zaroori hai?+
Haan, dono qualification paths (B.Sc Nursing aur GNM) mein candidate ka State ya Indian Nursing Council mein Nurse & Midwife ke roop mein registered hona anivarya hai.
Kya GIMS Noida Staff Nurse 2026 ke liye experience zaroori hai?+
Experience sirf GNM Diploma wale path ke liye zaroori hai — qualification prapt karne ke baad minimum 50-bedded hospital mein 2 saal ka anubhav. B.Sc Nursing path ke liye official notification mein alag se experience zaroori nahi bataya gaya hai.
Kya B.Sc Nursing aur GNM candidates ke liye alag-alag eligibility hai?+
Haan, dono do alag qualification paths hain jinme se koi ek sanshtust karna hota hai. Registration dono paths ke liye anivarya hai, lekin experience sirf GNM path ke liye zaroori hai.
Qualification kis tithi tak dharan karni hogi?+
Official notification ke anusaar, saari anivarya yogyataen (shaikshik evam anya) aavedan ki antim tithi tak dharan karni anivarya hain.
Kya B.Sc Nursing aur GNM qualification ko samaan (equivalent) mana jata hai?+
Official notification mein dono qualifications ko alag-alag, swatantra eligibility paths ke roop mein diya gaya hai; unhe ek doosre ke barabar (equivalent) ghoshit nahi kiya gaya hai.
