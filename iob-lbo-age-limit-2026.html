IOB LBO Age Limit 2026 – Overview

Indian Overseas Bank (IOB) has released the Official Notification for the Recruitment of Local Bank Officer (LBO) 2026-27 (Advt. No. HRDD/RECT/02/2026-27, dated 07.08.2026) inviting online applications against 250 vacancies in JMGS-I across Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Maharashtra and Gujarat. Before applying, candidates must carefully verify the age eligibility criteria prescribed in the official notification.

This article explains the IOB LBO minimum age, maximum age, age cut-off date, date of birth eligibility and category-wise age relaxation for SC, ST, OBC, PwBD, Ex-Servicemen and other eligible categories, based entirely on the Official IOB LBO Recruitment Notification 2026-27. Wherever the official notification does not specify a particular detail, this article does not assume or add such information.

Age Calculation & Cut-off Date

As per the Official IOB LBO Recruitment Notification 2026-27

As per the official notification, "The cut-off date for the purpose of eligibility in age and educational qualification shall be the 1st day of the month in which online registration commences i.e. 01.08.2026." This means every candidate's age is calculated with reference to 01.08.2026, regardless of the exact date on which the online application is submitted.

  • Minimum Age (as on 01.08.2026): 20 Years
  • Maximum Age (as on 01.08.2026): 30 Years
  • The above age limits apply before any category-wise age relaxation is added.
Important Note: Always calculate age using the official cut-off date 01.08.2026, exactly as prescribed in the Official IOB LBO Notification. Category-wise age relaxation applies only to candidates who fulfil the specified conditions and produce valid supporting certificates.

Date of Birth Eligibility

Directly stated in the Official IOB LBO Recruitment Notification 2026-27

The official notification directly states: "A candidate must have been born not later than 01.08.2006 and not earlier than 02.08.1996 (both days inclusive)." This Date of Birth range corresponds to the basic minimum age of 20 years and maximum age of 30 years as on the cut-off date 01.08.2026, before any category-wise age relaxation.

Important Note: *The Ex-Servicemen relaxation also applies to those whose assignment is due to be completed within 12 months from the closing date of online registration, subject to the ceiling prescribed under Government guidelines. Maximum age limit (30 years) is applicable to General category and EWS candidates only. All relaxations are subject to the candidate fulfilling the conditions prescribed by the Government of India and producing the requisite certificate at the time of Personal Interview.

Important Rules Regarding Age Relaxation

As per the Official IOB LBO Recruitment Notification 2026-27, the following rules apply to age relaxation:

  • Maximum age limit specified (30 years) is applicable to General Category candidates and Economically Weaker Section (EWS) category candidates.
  • If a candidate is eligible for relaxation under more than one category, the age relaxation is available on a cumulative basis with any one of the remaining categories for which age relaxation is permitted, subject to a maximum upper age limit of 50 years. This cumulative relaxation is available to SC/ST/OBC candidates.
  • The above relaxations are available only if candidates fulfil the various conditions prescribed in the Government of India orders and instructions in this regard.
  • To claim age relaxation, candidates should submit a copy of the necessary/requisite certificate(s) at the time of Personal Interview.
  • OBC candidates coming under the creamy layer, and/or whose caste does not find place in the Central List, are not entitled to OBC reservation or the associated age relaxation; such candidates must indicate their category as General in the online application form.
  • An Ex-Serviceman who has once joined a Government job on the civil side after availing the benefits given to him as an Ex-Serviceman for re-employment (including a job in a Public Sector Undertaking) ceases to enjoy Ex-Serviceman status for further employment.
  • There is no reservation for Ex-Servicemen in the Officers' Cadre; however, age relaxation is applicable as per extant Government guidelines.
  • Candidates eligible for age relaxation under "Persons affected by 1984 riots" must produce a certificate from the District Magistrate confirming eligibility under the Rehabilitation Package for 1984 Riot Affected Persons.

Important Age Eligibility Guidelines

Key points every IOB LBO 2026 applicant should keep in mind

📅
Use the Correct Cut-off Date
Always calculate age as on 01.08.2026, exactly as prescribed in the official notification — not the date of application submission.
📄
Keep Certificates Ready
Candidates claiming any age relaxation must be ready to submit the necessary category/relaxation certificate at the time of Personal Interview.
🧮
Understand Cumulative Relaxation
SC/ST/OBC candidates eligible under more than one category can combine relaxation with only one other eligible category, capped at 50 years.
🚫
No Change After Registration
No change of category or state applied for is permitted after registration, which also affects the age relaxation category considered.
⚖️
Creamy Layer OBC Candidates
OBC candidates under the creamy layer are not entitled to OBC age relaxation and must apply under the General category.
🏦
Bank's Decision is Final
The Bank reserves the right to reject any application at any stage if the candidate is found ineligible on age or any other ground.
Frequently Asked Questions

Common IOB LBO Age Limit & Age Relaxation 2026 questions, answered as per the Official IOB LBO Notification

What is the minimum age for IOB LBO 2026?+
As per the Official IOB LBO Recruitment Notification 2026-27, the minimum age is 20 years as on the cut-off date 01.08.2026.
What is the maximum age for IOB LBO 2026?+
The maximum age is 30 years as on 01.08.2026. This maximum age limit is applicable to General category and EWS category candidates.
What is the age cut-off date for IOB LBO 2026?+
The cut-off date is 01.08.2026 — the 1st day of the month in which online registration commences, as stated in the official notification.
What is the SC/ST age relaxation for IOB LBO 2026?+
SC and ST candidates are eligible for an age relaxation of 5 years as per the official notification.
What is the OBC age relaxation for IOB LBO 2026?+
OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) candidates are eligible for an age relaxation of 3 years as per the official notification.
What is the PwBD age relaxation for IOB LBO 2026?+
Persons with Benchmark Disabilities are eligible for an age relaxation of 10 years as per the official notification.
Is Ex-Servicemen age relaxation available for IOB LBO 2026?+
Yes. Ex-Servicemen and Commissioned Officers, including ECOs/SSCOs, who have rendered at least 5 years of military service and were released otherwise than by dismissal, discharge on misconduct/inefficiency, or disability attributable to military service, are eligible for a 5-year age relaxation, subject to the ceiling prescribed under Government guidelines.
How is age calculated for IOB LBO 2026?+
Age is calculated as on 01.08.2026. As per the notification, a candidate must have been born not later than 01.08.2006 and not earlier than 02.08.1996 (both days inclusive), before any applicable relaxation.
Can age relaxation benefits be combined for IOB LBO 2026?+
Yes, in a limited manner. If a candidate is eligible for relaxation under more than one category, the relaxation is available cumulatively with only one of the remaining eligible categories, subject to a maximum upper age limit of 50 years for SC/ST/OBC candidates.
What happens if a candidate exceeds the prescribed maximum age?+
The official notification states that candidates must fulfil the stipulated eligibility criteria, including age, and the Bank reserves the right to reject any application at any stage of the recruitment process if a candidate is found ineligible.
Is age relaxation available for persons affected by the 1984 riots?+
Yes. Persons affected by the 1984 riots are eligible for a 5-year age relaxation, subject to production of the requisite District Magistrate certificate as per the official notification.
Do OBC creamy layer candidates get age relaxation?+
No. Candidates belonging to OBC category but coming under the creamy layer, or whose caste does not find place in the Central List, are not entitled to OBC reservation or the related age relaxation. They must indicate their category as General in the online application.
Where can I download the official IOB LBO notification?+
The Official IOB LBO Recruitment Notification 2026-27 can be downloaded from the official IOB website, www.iob.bank.in, under the Careers page.
