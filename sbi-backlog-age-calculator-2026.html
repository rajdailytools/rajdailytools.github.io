Complete Guide

SBI Backlog Age Calculation Complete Guide 2026

Age calculation logic, examples, aur relaxation rules — sab kuch official notification ke hisaab se.

Age Kaise Calculate Hoti Hai?

SBI Backlog Junior Associate 2026 recruitment mein age exact cut-off date — 01.04.2026 — ke hisaab se calculate hoti hai, na ki simply "current year minus birth year" se. Iska matlab hai ki agar aapka birthday cut-off date ke baad aata hai, to aapki calculated age ek saal kam hogi us saal ke mukable jisme aap turn karte. Ye calculator din, mahine aur saal — teeno ko exactly count karta hai, including leap years aur 29 February wale DOB ko bhi.

Base Age Limit

Official notification ke anusaar, minimum age 20 saal aur maximum age 28 saal honi chahiye — 01.04.2026 ko. Iska matlab candidate ka janm 02.04.1998 se 01.04.2006 ke beech (dono dates included) hona chahiye, bina kisi relaxation ke. Ye recruitment sirf SC/ST/OBC Backlog vacancies ke liye hai, isliye General/EWS category applicable nahi hai.

  • Cut-off date fix hai: 01.04.2026
  • Minimum age sabhi eligible categories ke liye same hai — 20 saal (koi relaxation nahi)
  • Maximum age bina relaxation ke 28 saal hai
  • OBC/SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-servicemen/Widows/Trained Apprentices ko upper age mein relaxation milta hai
  • Cumulative relaxation available NAHI hai — sirf ek hi category ka relaxation lagu hota hai

Worked Examples

DOB: 15 June 2000
Age as on 01.04.2026: 25 years, 9 months, 17 days
Within base 20–28 range → Age Criteria Met

Key Insight: Relaxation only extends the maximum age limit — the minimum age of 20 years applies to every category, with no relaxation mentioned in the official notification.

No Cumulative Relaxation Rule

SBI Backlog notification explicitly states: "Cumulative age relaxation will not be available either under the above items or in combination with any other items." Iska matlab agar koi candidate ek se zyada relaxation category ke liye eligible hai (jaise SC + PwBD), to bhi relaxation add nahi hoti — sirf wahi ek specific combined category (jaise "PwBD (SC/ST)") ka fixed relaxation lagu hota hai, jo already official table mein diya gaya hai.

Important: Relaxation apply karne ke liye joining ke time par relevant certificate (caste certificate, disability certificate, ex-servicemen proforma, etc.) submit karna zaroori hai — jaisa official notification mein bataya gaya hai. Category change registration ke baad allowed nahi hai.

SBI Backlog Junior Associate recruitment 2026 ke liye age limit kya hai?+
Official notification (Advt No. CRPD/CR/SPLDRIVE/2026-27/16) ke anusaar minimum age 20 saal aur maximum age 28 saal hai, cut-off date 01.04.2026 ke hisaab se.
SBI Backlog age calculation ka cut-off date kya hai?+
Cut-off date 01.04.2026 hai, jaisa official notification ke "Age Limit (As on 01.04.2026)" clause mein clearly diya gaya hai.
Eligible DOB range kya hai?+
Candidate ka janm 02.04.1998 se pehle nahi aur 01.04.2006 ke baad nahi (dono dates included) hona chahiye, bina relaxation ke, jaisa official notification mein diya gaya hai.
SBI Backlog me age relaxation kis category ko milti hai?+
OBC ko 3 saal, SC/ST ko 5 saal, PwBD(OBC) ko 13 saal, PwBD(SC/ST) ko 15 saal, eligible Ex-Servicemen ko actual service period + 3 (ya disabled SC/ST ke liye +8) saal, Widows/Divorced/Separated women ko 7 saal, aur Trained Apprentices of SBI ko 4/6/14/16 saal ki relaxation official notification me di gayi hai.
Kya do relaxation categories ek saath mil sakti hain (cumulative)?+
Nahi. Official notification clearly kehta hai — "Cumulative age relaxation will not be available either under the above items or in combination with any other items." Sirf apni sabse applicable category ka fixed relaxation hi lagu hota hai.
Kya General category candidates is drive ke liye apply kar sakte hain?+
Nahi. Ye ek Special Recruitment Drive for SC/ST/OBC Backlog Vacancies hai, jaisa official notification ke title mein hi mentioned hai. General/EWS category is drive ke liye applicable nahi hai.
Kya age criteria meet karne ka matlab hai ki main fully eligible hoon?+
Nahi. Ye tool sirf age eligibility ka preliminary estimate deta hai. Educational qualification, category certificate, nationality aur baaki conditions bhi verify karna zaroori hai. Final eligibility SBI ke official process se hi decide hoti hai.
