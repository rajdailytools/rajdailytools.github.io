📅 Age Cutoff Date — 01 January 2026 Kyun?

Official Patna High Court TA 2026 notification ke mutabiq, candidates ki age 01 January 2026 ko reference date maankar calculate ki jaati hai. Matlab aapki exact age us date par kya thi — wohi eligibility determine karegi.

Example: DOB: 15 March 1988 → Age on 01 Jan 2026 = 37 years 9 months 17 days. UR Male ke liye max 37 years — NOT eligible (exceeded). BC Male ke liye max 40 — ELIGIBLE. SC/ST ke liye max 42 — ELIGIBLE.

📋 Category-Wise Age Limits — Explained

Patna High Court TA 2026 mein age limits category aur gender dono par depend karti hain:

  • UR/EWS Male: Minimum 18 — Maximum 37 years as on 01 Jan 2026
  • UR/EWS Female: Minimum 18 — Maximum 40 years (females ko 3 year extra relaxation)
  • BC Male: Minimum 18 — Maximum 40 years (BC category ko 3 year relaxation)
  • BC Female: Minimum 18 — Maximum 40 years
  • EBC Male: Minimum 18 — Maximum 40 years (EBC ko bhi same relaxation)
  • EBC Female: Minimum 18 — Maximum 40 years
  • SC/ST (Male & Female): Minimum 18 — Maximum 42 years (highest relaxation)
Key Insight: SC/ST candidates ko sabse zyada relaxation milti hai — 5 extra years compared to UR Male. Female candidates ko bhi extra benefit milta hai. Isliye apna correct category aur gender select karna bahut important hai.
