1. SSC CGL Document Verification Overview

SSC CGL 2026 notification ke mutabik, ab Document Verification regional offices ki jagah User Departments dwara conduct kiya jayega. Yeh tab hota hai jab Final Merit List jaari ho jati hai. Aapko apne pure original documents ke saath self-attested photocopies ka ek set lekar jaana hota hai. Document Verification mein fail hone ka matlab hai ki aapki candidature turant cancel kar di jayegi, chahe aapne exam mein kitne hi ache marks kyun na liye hon.

⚠️ Crucial Date Reminder: SSC CGL 2026 ke liye certificates aur educational eligibility ki crucial date 22.06.2026 hai. Is date ke baad ke certificates aksar issue create karte hain.

2. SSC CGL Required Documents List

Aapko niche diye gaye documents original form mein lekar jaane honge:

3. Educational Qualification Documents

Aapke paas valid degree honi chahiye jo kisi recognized university se ho. Agar aapne Open & Distance Learning (ODL) se padhai ki hai, toh ensure karein ki us course ko UGC/DEB ka approval ho (Notification Clause 8.7).

Pro Tip: Agar aapki degree original nahi aayi hai, toh aap Provisional Degree Certificate dikha sakte hain, lekin woh valid university format mein hona chahiye.

4. Identity Proof Requirements

Document verification ke time aapko kam se kam 2 Passport size photographs aur ek original valid Photo ID proof dikhana hoga. Inmein se koi bhi chalega:

  • Aadhaar Card / Printout of E-Aadhaar
  • Voter ID Card
  • PAN Card
  • Driving License
  • Passport
  • University/College ID Card

5. Category Certificate Requirements (OBC/SC/ST/EWS)

Agar aap reservation ka fayda uthana chahte hain, toh certificate ka format SSC dwara diye gaye annexures ke hisaab se hona chahiye.

CategoryCertificate Type Required
OBCOBC Certificate with Non-Creamy Layer (NCL) status.
EWSIncome & Asset Certificate for 2025-26/2026-27.
SC / STStandard Caste Certificate as per Annexure-IX.
PwBDDisability Certificate (Clause 6.3) as per Annexure-XII/XIII.
Ex-ServicemanDischarge Certificate and Undertaking (Annexure-VIII).

6. Common DV Mistakes to Avoid

  • Name Mismatch: 10th certificate aur identity proof mein naam alag hona.
  • Invalid Format: State level certificate dikhana jabki Central format required ho.
  • Expired EWS: EWS certificate current financial year ka na hona.
  • Missing NOC: Govt job holders ka bina proper approval ke aana.
DV FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions (50)

Kya SSC CGL 2026 mein DV SSC khud karega?
Nahi, official notification (Clause 15.1) ke mutabik DV ab final result ke baad User Department dwara kiya jayega.
Educational qualification ki crucial date kya hai?
Crucial date 01.08.2026 hai degree complete hone ke liye, jabki documents checking application closing date 22.06.2026 par nirbhar karti hai.
Agar mere paas original degree nahi hai toh?
Aap provisional degree dikha sakte hain jo university se issue ki gayi ho.
Caste certificate kab tak ka hona chahiye?
Caste certificate application ki closing date 22.06.2026 se pehle ka hona chahiye.
Kya Digilocker se documents valid hain?
Physical verification ke liye original hard copies dikhana mandatory hai.
Name mismatch hone par kya karein?
Agar spelling minor alag hai toh Affidavit lekar jayein, lekin 10th certificate final mana jayega.
EWS certificate kab ka hona chahiye?
Financial year 2025-26 ki income ke basis par notification year 2026-27 ka.
NOC zaroori hai kya?
Haan, agar aap already kisi central ya state govt department mein permanently employed hain.
PwBD candidates ke liye kya rule hai?
Unhe Annexure-XII ya XIII ke format mein medical certificate dikhana hoga.
Kya matriculation certificate se DOB verify hoti hai?
Haan, SSC sirf 10th standard certificate ko hi DOB proof maanti hai.
OBC Non-creamy layer certificate validity?
Ye generally 3 saal purana nahi hona chahiye crucial date se.
ID proof mein Aadhaar mandatory hai?
Nahi, Voter ID, PAN, ya DL bhi chalega agar Aadhaar nahi hai.
Ex-Serviceman ke liye discharge date?
Unhe application closing date se 1 saal ke andar discharge hona chahiye (Clause 5.6).
Distance education ki degree valid hai?
Haan, agar woh UGC/DEB approved university se hai.
Scribe use karne walon ko kya dikhana hoga?
Scribe ke details aur ID proof Document Verification ke samay dena hoga.
Annexure format change ho sakta hai?
Nahi, hamesha notification mein diye gaye format (Annexure-V etc.) ka use karein.
Final year students apply kar sakte hain?
Haan, lekin unka result 01.08.2026 tak declare ho jana chahiye.
Address change hone par kya karein?
DV ke samay latest address proof dikha sakte hain, halanki ID proof primary hai.
Identity proof ki kitni copies chahiye?
At least 2 sets self-attested photocopies ke saath rakhein.
Post preference kab deni hoti hai?
DV se pehle option form online bharna hota hai, jo bad mein change nahi hota.
ESM certificate format?
Annexure-VII (Serving) aur Annexure-VIII (Undertaking).
SC/ST certificate validity?
Inki koi expiry nahi hoti, lekin Central format hona chahiye.
Central format vs State format?
SSC hamesha Central Government format hi mangta hai.
Marriage ke baad name change case?
Marriage certificate ya Gazette notification dikhana hoga.
Application form gum gaya toh?
Website se duplicate login karke print nikalne ki koshish karein.
Degree certificate English mein hona chahiye?
Hindi ya English dono valid hain. Regional language hone par translation zaroori hai.
Final marksheet mandatory hai?
Haan, aggregate marks verify karne ke liye ye zaroori hai.
Central Govt Employees age relaxation docs?
Unhe Annexure-VI dikhana hoga (Clause 15.11.7).
Photos kitni purani honi chahiye?
Official rule ke mutabik photographs current status se match honi chahiye.
Disability certificate validity?
Permanent disability ke liye lifelong, temporary ke liye renewed.
Verification fail hone par kya hota hai?
Direct rejection (Clause 16.9).
DEST error relaxation docs?
Clause 13.9.10.6 ke mutabik OH candidates ko DEST exemption ke liye medical certificate chahiye.
NCL certificate update frequency?
Har 3 saal mein update karwana advisable hota hai.
University name change certificate?
Agar college ki university badal gayi hai toh official circular dikhayein.
Marksheet mein back paper case?
Result 01.08.2026 tak final pass status hona chahiye.
Father's name mistake?
Affidavit aur notification ka gap verify karein.
Aadhaar card address problem?
Address verification second priority hai, naam aur photo primary hai.
NOC format?
Apne head of department (HOD) se standard Govt NOC.
Equivalence certificate?
Agar degree generic nahi hai toh Clause 8.9 ke mutabik authority se equivalence layein.
Crucial date after lockdown extension?
22.06.2026 hi final crucial date hai 2026 notification ke liye.
Photo size for DV?
Standard 3.5cm x 4.5cm passport size.
Multiple degrees valid?
Haan, lekin main degree wahi dikhayein jo form mein bhari hai.
ID signature issue?
Signature ID proof se match hona chahiye.
CGL DV location?
Jiss department mein aapka selection hua hai unke head office mein.
Waiting list documents?
SSC CGL mein koi waiting list nahi hoti (Clause 15.3).
Annexure printing?
Annexures hamesha official notification se hi print karein.
Correction window documents?
Application form mein galti theek karne par uski updated copy zaroori hai.
DV process delay recovery?
Contact user department immediately agar bulawa nahi aaya.
Missing document appeal?
Generally Department koi second chance nahi deta stage par.
Is this checklist final?
Haan, ye SSC CGL 2026 official notification guidelines par based hai.
