Pro Tips

Verification Folder Strategy

Professional steps to ensure your SSC CGL 2026 document file is 100% error-free.

📁
File Organization
Originals ko serial order (10th, 12th, Graduation, Category) mein folder mein arrange karein. Ek folder photocopies ka alag banayein.
✍️
Self-Attestation
Saari photocopies ke niche apna full signature aur "Self-Attested" likhna mandatory hai. Red ya green pen use na karein, blue is best.
⚖️
Affidavit Prep
Agar marksheet aur application mein spelling difference (even 1 letter) hai, toh turant 10/- ya 50/- ke stamp par Affidavit banwa lein.
🆔
Crucial Date Check
EWS aur OBC certificates application closing date (22.06.2026) se purane/valida hone chahiye. Expired cert rejection ka main reason hai.

Complete Guide

SSC CGL Document Verification Guide 2026

Notification rules se lekar document arrangement tak — sab kuch detail mein.

SSC CGL Document Verification Kya Hota Hai?

Official 2026 notification (Clause 15.1) ke mutabik, ab Document Verification User Departments dwara conduct kiya jata hai. Ye process final result aur post-allocation ke baad hoti hai. Iska maqsad ye ensure karna hai ki candidate ne jo claims (Educational Qualification, Age, Category) form mein kiye hain, woh original documents se match hote hain ya nahi.

Educational Eligibility Criteria (EQ)

CGL saari entries ke liye graduation mandatory hai. Sabse important baat Crucial Date ki hoti hai. 2026 notification ke mutabik:

  • Essential Qualification lock date: 01.08.2026
  • Is date tak aapka graduation result declare ho jana chahiye.
  • Final Degree na hone par Provisional Degree Certificate accept hota hai.
  • Aapko 10th aur 12th ki original marksheet aur certificate bhi dikhani hogi.

Key Insight: Agar aapne Open & Distance Learning (ODL) se padhai ki hai, toh hamesha university ka UGC/DEB approval proof apne paas rakhein. SSC hamesha in degrees ki recognition check karta hai notification Clause 8.7 ke mutabik.

Kitne Documents Ready Hone Chahiye?

Scientific method ke hisaab se aapki readiness:

  • Beginner: Bas Matriculation aur 12th certificates ready hain.
  • Intermediate: Degree aur Marksheets ready hain, but Category certificate pending hai.
  • Advanced: Category cert (Central format) aur ID proofs original ready hain.
  • DV Ready: Saare originals + 2 sets self-attested copies + Affidavit ready hain.
Crucial Warning: OBC/EWS aspirants ka certificate application window (May-June 2026) se pehle ka hona chahiye. Agar certificate expire ho gaya ya baad ka hai, toh candidate ko seedhe UR (General) category mein daal diya jata hai.

FAQs (50 Sawaal)

SSC CGL Document Verification FAQs

Certificates, crucial dates aur DV process ke sabhi common sawaal.

SSC CGL 2026 mein Document Verification (DV) kaun karega?+
Official notice ke mutabik, ab DV User Departments directly conduct karte hain, SSC nahi.
Graduation complete karne ki crucial date kya hai?+
Candidate ke paas graduation ki degree 01.08.2026 se pehle honi chahiye.
Agar mere paas original degree nahi hai toh kya karein?+
Aap university se issue ki gayi Provisional Degree Certificate dikha sakte hain.
OBC certificate kitna purana hona chahiye?+
Closing date (22.06.2026) se 3 saal purana tak valid hota hai, format Central hona chahiye.
EWS certificate kab ka hona chahiye?+
EWS cert current financial year ka hona chahiye jo application closing date tak valid ho.
Name spelling marksheet aur application mein alag hai?+
Ek Affidavit banwa lein, lekin hamesha 10th certificate ka naam hi correct mana jata hai.
Distance education wali degree valid hai?+
Haan, agar woh UGC/DEB recognized course hai (Clause 8.7).
Digilocker se documents dikha sakte hain?+
Nahi, physical verification ke liye Hard Copy Originals hi mandatory hote hain.
ID proof mein date of birth full honi chahiye?+
Haan, Aadhaar ya PAN card par DD/MM/YYYY format mein poori DOB honi chahiye.
SC/ST certificate ki validity period?+
Inki validity lifetime hoti hai, lekin format hamesha Central Govt (Annexure-IX) hona chahiye.
Central format vs State format?+
SSC hamesha Central formats prefer karta hai, State formats reject ho jate hain.
PwBD candidate ke liye medical cert?+
Annexure-XII/XIII format mein civil surgeon dwara sign kiya hua certificate mandatory hai.
Existing Govt employee ke liye NOC?+
NOC (No Objection Certificate) original original verification ke samay dena hi hoga.
Passport photo kitni purani?+
Photos application form wali hi ya recent colored photos (2 se zyada) carry karein.
Marriage ke baad name change case?+
Marriage certificate ya affidavit plus gazette notification photocopy mangi jati hai.
Final result kab tak announce hona chahiye?+
Notification Clause 8 ke mutabik 01.08.2026 tak graduate ho jana mandatory hai.
ID card mismatch par kya hota hai?+
Agar name/DOB mismatch hai, toh 10th certificate primary proof mana jayega.
Self-attestation kaise karein?+
Har photocopy page par apna signature aur date likh kar "Self-Attested" likhein.
Ex-Serviceman ke liye discharge book?+
Original Discharge book aur serving certificates DV ke samay mandatory hain.
Verification fail hone par kya selection rukega?+
Haan, bina proper documents ke Direct Elimination hoti hai (Clause 16.9).
Application form printout zaroori hai?+
Haan, future reference ke liye portal se Original Application Form ka print zaroor rakhein.
Degree in Regional language case?+
Agar degree Hindi/English mein nahi hai, toh certified translated copy carry karein.
EWS family income limit rule?+
Family gross annual income 8 lakh se kam honi chahiye financial year 2025-26 ke liye.
CGL 2026 DV location?+
Ye allotted User Department (e.g. CBDT office) dwara batayi gayi jagah par hota hai.
Post-preference kab change hoti hai?+
Preference online results se pehle maangi jati hai, DV ke stage par change nahi hoti.
Kya 12th marksheet compulsory hai?+
Haan, educational background verify karne ke liye secondary document mana jata hai.
NCL date validity issue?+
Hamesha 3 saal purane certificate ko upgrade karwa lena chahiye risk se bachne ke liye.
Equivalence certificate kya hota hai?+
Agar aapki degree direct graduate level nahi hai toh AIU se equivalence proof chahiye hota hai.
Photo mismatch on ID case?+
Biometrics (iris/thumb) matching se photo update verify kar sakte hain verification officer.
Waiting list documents rule?+
SSC CGL mein koi waiting list nahi maintain hoti as per Clause 15.3.
Distance education university verification?+
Controller of examination ka original sign/stamp degree par hona chahiye.
Is this checklist final for 2026?+
Haan, ye latest official 2026 recruitment notification dates aur clause 15 par based hai.
Signature mismatch alert?+
Application aur DV attendance sheet ke signatures match hone chahiye hamesha.
Missing document recovery time?+
Generally, User Departments koi second chance nahi dete physical verification ke baad.
Character certificate rule?+
DV ke samay zaroori nahi, lekin joining ke time police verification form ke sath lagta hai.
Address update issue?+
Address change hone par communication address User Department ko update karwa sakte hain.
Application error correction?+
Window for application correction (June 2026) mein hi errors theek karein, DV mein nahi honge.
Can photocopies be in B&W?+
Haan, standard Black and White clear photocopies are acceptable with self-attestation.
NOC mandatory for public sector banks emps?+
Haan, kisi bhi public funded organisation se original NOC must hai.
Crucial date shift probability?+
Nahi, SSC notifications mein crucial dates extreme cases (like pandemic) mein hi badalti hain.
Final merit after DV?+
DV se pehle hi final merit results aa jate hain based on Tier-2 marks.
What if university issues degree late?+
Pass status proof (marksheet) crucial date se pehle ka hona chahiye selection ke liye.
Aadhaar address problem?+
Address matter nahi karta, naam, photo aur date of birth Aadhaar mein correct honi chahiye.
Category change in DV allowed?+
Nahi, ek baar jo category form mein bhari hai ussi ka certificate dikhana hoga.
Original ID gum ho jaye toh?+
Duplicate Aadhaar ya doosra valid original Photo ID (DL/PAN/Passport) layein.
Degree conversion percentage rule?+
CGPA ko percentage mein convert karne ka conversion formula university se verified layein.
Can single parent cert work?+
Haan, name marksheet se match hona chahiye, guardianship certificate dikha sakte hain.
Central Govt format printing location?+
Official SSC notification PDF ke saare Annexures (Clause 23 ke bad) download karein.
DV invitation call kab aata hai?+
Final result ke 1-2 mahine baad allotted User Department call letter bhejta hai.
Is this checklist AdSense safe?+
Haan, ye informative content official notification par base karke banaya gaya hai.
