Introduction

The Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB), Jaipur has notified the exam pattern and syllabus for the Junior Engineer Combined Direct Recruitment 2026 (Advt No. 10/2026) along with the recruitment notification. This RSSB Junior Engineer Syllabus 2026 page organizes that officially notified syllabus — Part A General Knowledge and Part B technical subject — so candidates can see exactly what to study before the exam.

The technical syllabus in Part B is not identical for every post — it depends on whether a candidate is applying for the Civil Engineering (Degree), Civil Engineering (Diploma), or Mechanical/Electrical Engineering (Diploma) category. Each is covered separately below, exactly as given in the official notification.

RSSB JE Exam Pattern 2026

The same exam pattern applies to all three technical categories

PartSubjectMarksDuration
Part AGeneral Knowledge (Rajasthan History, Art & Culture, Heritage & Geography, Political & Administrative System)402 Hours
Part BTechnical Subject (Civil / Mechanical / Electrical Engineering, as applicable)80
Total120
The question paper is objective type (multiple-choice) and all questions carry equal marks. A candidate must score a minimum of 40% marks to qualify. One-third (1/3) of the marks allotted to a question is deducted for each wrong answer.

This section is common to all three technical categories: Junior Engineer (Civil) Degree, Junior Engineer (Civil) Diploma, and Junior Engineer (Mechanical/Electrical) Diploma.

Rajasthan History, Art & Culture, Traditions & Heritage

  • Main sources of Rajasthan's history
  • Major prehistoric civilizations of Rajasthan
  • Major dynasties of Rajasthan and their achievements
  • Mughal-Rajput relations
  • Key features of architecture
  • Important forts, monuments and structures
  • Religious movements of Rajasthan and folk deities
  • Major paintings, styles and handicrafts of Rajasthan
  • Major works of Rajasthani language and literature, regional dialects
  • Fairs, festivals, folk music, folk dance, musical instruments and ornaments
  • Rajasthani culture, tradition and heritage
  • Important historical tourist places
  • Major personalities of Rajasthan
  • Princely states of Rajasthan and British treaties, the 1857 people's movement
  • Peasant and tribal movements, Praja Mandal movement
  • Unification of Rajasthan
  • Political awakening and development of Rajasthan, with special reference to women

Geography of Rajasthan

  • Location and extent
  • Main physical divisions: desert region, Aravalli hill region, plain region, plateau region
  • Drainage system
  • Climate
  • Soil
  • Natural vegetation
  • Forest and wildlife conservation
  • Environmental and ecological issues
  • Desertification
  • Agro-climatic regions and major crops
  • Livestock
  • Multipurpose projects
  • Irrigation projects
  • Water conservation
  • Transport
  • Mineral resources

Political and Administrative System of Rajasthan

  • Local urban self-government in Rajasthan
  • 74th Constitutional Amendment Bill

  • State Information Commission
  • State Election Commission
  • Rajasthan Right to Public Service Guarantee Act, 2011

🏗️ Part B – Junior Engineer (Civil) Degree Syllabus

This technical syllabus applies to the degree-holder Civil Engineering posts under the Public Works, Water Resources and Public Health Engineering Departments.

1. Building Technology and Construction Management

  • Building materials: stones, bricks, steel, timber, lime, cement, sand, aggregates for cement concrete, paints, distempers, use of pozzolana, manufacturing of lime concrete, cement concrete for plain, reinforced and pre-stressed concrete work
  • Road materials: coarse aggregate, screenings and binding materials for WBM, bricks for soling, coarse and fine aggregate for bituminous roads, IRC standard size aggregates, tars and asphalt, asphaltic concrete, asphaltic emulsions, mastic asphalt and mineral fillers
  • Construction management: plants and equipment, planning for construction using network analysis, CPM and PERT techniques

2. Fluid Mechanics

  • Properties of fluids, hydrostatics, buoyancy, equilibrium of fluid particles and flow
  • Hydro-kinematics: types of flows, continuity equation, rotational and irrotational motion, free and forced vortex
  • Euler's and Bernoulli's equations and their applications: Pitot tube, Venturi meter, orifice meter, notches, weirs
  • Momentum equation and its applications; flow through pipes, laminar and turbulent flow, losses of head, power transmission through pipes

3. Surveying, Estimating, Costing & Field Engineering

  • Measurement of distances, angles and directions; chain and compass traversing
  • Traversing methods and adjustment; leveling and contouring
  • Plane table surveying; trigonometric leveling; field astronomy
  • Introduction to Remote Sensing and GIS; estimation of quantities, rate analysis, tender and contract documents, centre-line diagram, building layout

4. Irrigation & Water Resources

  • Types and methods of irrigation; hydrology, rainfall measurement, run-off, losses from precipitation
  • Water requirement of crops, duty, delta, command area, irrigation efficiencies
  • Canals and canal lining; wells; weirs and barrages; Kennedy's and Lacey's theories
  • Flood control; water logging; land reclamation; major irrigation projects in India

5. Theory of Structures and Strength of Materials

  • Elasticity constants; determinate and indeterminate beams; bending moment and shear force diagrams
  • Moment of area and moment of inertia; bending and shear stress for various sections
  • Eccentric loads; slope deflection; critical load and columns; torsion of circular sections; springs and vibration

6. Structural Analysis

  • Indeterminate structures; degrees of freedom, static and kinematic indeterminacy
  • Slope-deflection and moment-distribution methods for beams and portal frames
  • Unit load method; analysis of determinate and redundant frames

7. Soil Mechanics and Foundation Engineering

  • Origin of soil, phase diagram, index properties, grain size distribution
  • Permeability, consolidation, effective stress; shear strength of soils
  • Soil compaction; earth pressure theories; bearing capacity of soils, plate load and standard penetration tests

8. Design of R.C. Concrete and Masonry Structures

  • RCC beams: flexural, shear and bond strength; singly and doubly reinforced beams
  • T-beams, lintels, one-way and two-way slabs, isolated footings, columns, staircases, retaining walls, water tanks
  • Concrete technology: properties, mix design, batching, mixing, curing and quality control of concrete

9. Design of Steel Structures

  • Design and construction of steel columns, beams, roof trusses and plate girders

10. Construction Technology

  • Stone and brick masonry; lintels, plastering, pointing, flooring; centering and shuttering
  • Roofing types; flooring types; damp proof course; anti-termite treatment

11. Auto-CAD Civil Engineering Drawing

12. Public Health Engineering

  • Per capita water requirement, population forecasting, water sources and purification
  • House drainage system; sewerage layout; treatment units; urban water supply and sanitation

13. Highway and Bridges

  • Principles of highway planning; classification of roads; camber, gradient, sight distance, curves and super elevation
  • Flexible pavements; sub-base, base course; bituminous construction; traffic engineering and intersection control

🏗️ Part B – Junior Engineer (Civil) Diploma Syllabus

This technical syllabus applies to the diploma-holder Civil Engineering posts under the Public Works, Water Resources and Public Health Engineering Departments.

1. Building Technology and Construction Management

  • Physical and chemical properties, classification, standard tests, uses and manufacture/quarrying of materials — building stones, silicate-based materials, cement (Portland), asbestos products, timber and wood-based products, laminates, bituminous materials, paints, varnishes

2. Surveying, Estimating & Costing

  • Chain surveying, compass traversing, plane table surveying, theodolite traversing
  • Leveling, contouring, curvature and refraction corrections, tacheometric survey, curve setting
  • Estimating: analysis of rates, items of work — earthwork, brickwork, RCC work, shuttering, timber work, painting, flooring, plastering
  • Cost estimate of septic tank, flexible pavements, tube well, footings, steel truss, piles and pile caps; valuation methods

3. Strength of Materials

  • Elasticity constants; determinate and indeterminate beams; bending moment and shear force diagrams
  • Moment of inertia; bending and shear stress; eccentric loads; slope deflection; critical load and columns; torsion of circular sections

4. Reinforced Concrete Design

  • RCC beams — flexural, shear and bond strength; singly and doubly reinforced beams; T-beams; lintels
  • One-way and two-way slabs, isolated footings, reinforced brick works, columns, staircases, retaining walls, water tanks

5. Irrigation & Water Resources

  • Types and methods of irrigation; hydrology, rainfall measurement, run-off, water requirement of crops
  • Canals and canal lining; wells; weirs and barrages; Kennedy's and Lacey's theories
  • Flood control; water logging; land reclamation; major irrigation projects in India

6. Soil Engineering

  • Origin of soil, phase diagram, index properties, grain size distribution
  • Permeability, consolidation, effective stress; shear strength of soils; soil compaction
  • Earth pressure theories; bearing capacity of soils, plate load and standard penetration tests

7. Auto-CAD Civil Engineering Drawing

⚙️ Part B – Junior Engineer (Mechanical/Electrical) Diploma Syllabus

This post is notified jointly for Mechanical and Electrical Engineering diploma holders under the Public Health Engineering Department. The official notification lists a separate technical syllabus for each branch, given below.

Electrical Engineering Diploma Syllabus

  • Basic Concepts: Resistance, inductance, capacitance and factors affecting them; current, voltage, power, energy and their units
  • Circuit Laws: Kirchhoff's laws, simple circuit solutions using network theorems
  • Magnetic Circuits: Flux, mmf, reluctance, magnetic materials, electromagnetic induction, self and mutual induction
  • AC Fundamentals: Instantaneous, peak, RMS and average values; series and parallel R-L-C circuits; resonance; 3-phase star and delta connections
  • Measurement & Measuring Instruments: Power and energy measurement, ammeters, voltmeters, wattmeters, multimeter, megger, CT, PT, earth fault detection
  • Electrical Machines: DC machines; single and 3-phase transformers; 3-phase induction motors; fractional kilowatt and single-phase induction motors
  • Synchronous Machines: Generation of 3-phase EMF, armature reaction, voltage regulation, parallel operation, synchronous motors
  • Generation, Transmission and Distribution: Types of power stations, load/diversity/demand factor, power factor improvement, tariffs, faults, switchgear, protection systems, cables
  • Estimation and Costing: Estimation of lighting schemes, electrical installation of machines, relevant IE rules, earthing practices
  • Utilization of Electrical Energy: Illumination, electric heating, welding, electroplating, electric drives and motors
  • Basic Electronics: P-N junction diodes, transistors (NPN and PNP), BJT and JFET, simple circuits using these devices

Mechanical Engineering Diploma Syllabus

  • Fluid Mechanics: Properties and classification of fluids, fluid statics, fluid kinematics, Bernoulli's equation and its applications
  • Fluid Machines: Hydraulic turbines, centrifugal and reciprocating pumps, hydraulic accumulator, intensifier, press, coupling and torque converter
  • Internal Combustion Engines: Classification, working principles, petrol and diesel engines, carburetion, fuel injection, cooling and lubrication, engine performance, gas turbines, air compressors
  • Strength of Materials: Simple stress and strain, compound stress, strain energy, bending moment and shear force, moment of inertia, deflection, columns and struts, torsion of shafts, springs, thin cylindrical shells
  • Theory of Machines: Simple mechanisms, velocity and acceleration in mechanisms, dynamics of reciprocating parts, friction, transmission of power (belt, chain, gear drives), balancing, vibration, governors, brakes and dynamometers, gyroscope
  • Auto-CAD – Mechanical Engineering Drawing: Machining symbols and tolerances, working drawings, assembly drawings, gear profiles, cam profiles, computer graphics
  • Thermodynamics: Basic concepts and gas laws, laws of thermodynamics, availability, formation of steam and its properties, steam generators and boiler performance, gas power cycles
  • Machine Drawings

Preparation Tips

A few practical pointers based on the official syllabus and exam pattern

📋 How to Approach the Syllabus
1
Start from the official syllabus units listed above rather than a generic list — the technical paper is specific to your branch (Civil Degree, Civil Diploma, or Mechanical/Electrical Diploma).
2
Divide each unit into smaller topics and track which ones you have covered.
3
Since the paper is objective type with negative marking, practice topic-wise multiple-choice questions rather than only long-form problems.

Revise weaker units closer to the exam date, and keep an eye on the official RSSB website for the confirmed exam date.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers based on the RSSB Junior Engineer 2026 official notification

What is the exam pattern for RSSB Junior Engineer 2026?+
The exam is a single objective-type paper of 120 marks to be completed in 2 hours, divided into Part A (General Knowledge, 40 marks) and Part B (Technical Subject, 80 marks).
How many subjects are included in the RSSB JE syllabus?+
The syllabus has two parts: Part A covers General Knowledge (Rajasthan History, Art & Culture, Geography, and Political & Administrative System), and Part B covers the technical engineering subject relevant to the post applied for.
Is the syllabus different for different posts in RSSB JE 2026?+
Yes. Part A (General Knowledge) is the same for all posts, but Part B (Technical Subject) differs — separate syllabus is notified for Junior Engineer (Civil) Degree holders, Junior Engineer (Civil) Diploma holders, and Junior Engineer (Mechanical/Electrical) Diploma holders.
Is there negative marking in the RSSB Junior Engineer exam?+
Yes. One-third of the marks allotted to a question is deducted for each wrong answer.
What is the minimum qualifying mark for RSSB JE 2026?+
Candidates must secure a minimum of 40% marks in the examination to be considered eligible for further selection stages.
Where can I check the official RSSB Junior Engineer syllabus?+
The complete official syllabus is part of the RSSB Junior Engineer Combined Direct Recruitment 2026 notification (Advt No. 10/2026), available on the official RSSB website.
