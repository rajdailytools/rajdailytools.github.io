IOB LBO Exam Pattern & Selection Process 2026 – Overview

Indian Overseas Bank (IOB) has released the Official Notification for the Recruitment of Local Bank Officer (LBO) 2026-27 (Advt. No. HRDD/RECT/02/2026-27, dated 07.08.2026) inviting online applications against 250 vacancies in JMGS-I. Before appearing for the exam, candidates must clearly understand the official exam pattern, marking scheme and complete selection process.

This article explains the IOB LBO Online Examination pattern, number of questions, maximum marks, exam duration, negative marking, qualifying marks, Language Proficiency Test, Personal Interview and final merit calculation, based entirely on the Official IOB LBO Recruitment Notification 2026-27. Wherever the official notification does not specify a particular detail, this article does not assume or add such information.

Online Examination Pattern

Structure of the Online Examination as per the Official IOB LBO Recruitment Notification 2026-27

As per the official notification, selection is made through an Online Examination consisting of 140 objective type questions under 4 sections, carrying a total of 200 marks. The examination is conducted in English and Hindi, except the English Knowledge section which is conducted in English only.

Section / Topic No. of Questions Maximum Marks Medium of Exam Duration

Important Note: The Bank reserves the right to modify or alter the structure of the online examination, which will be intimated through the Bank's website. Detailed information regarding the examination will be provided in an Information Handout, available for download along with the call letters from www.iob.bank.in.
IOB LBO Marking Scheme 2026

As per the Official IOB LBO Recruitment Notification 2026-27

  • Negative Marking: There will be a penalty for wrong answers. For each question with a wrong answer, 1/4th (0.25) of the marks assigned to that question will be deducted as penalty to arrive at the corrected score.
  • Unattempted Questions: There shall be no penalty for an unattended (unattempted) question.
  • Sectional Scoring: The official notification does not prescribe sectional qualifying marks — there is no sectional cut-off in the online examination.
  • Overall Scoring: Cumulative marks of all 4 topics — Reasoning and Computer Aptitude, General/Economy/Banking Awareness, Data Analysis and Interpretation, and English Knowledge — are reckoned together for drawing the merit list for shortlisting candidates for the further selection process.
Note: The official notification specifies negative marking of 0.25 marks per wrong answer. No other marking-related detail (such as bonus marks or normalisation formula) is mentioned in the notification, and this article does not assume any such detail.

Language Proficiency Test (LPT)

As per the official notification, the candidate must have proficiency (reading, writing and speaking) in the Local Language of the State applied for. Candidates shortlisted for the Personal Interview will be required to qualify the Language Proficiency Test, failing which they will not be allowed to appear for the Personal Interview, and their candidature will not be considered for further selection.

  • Candidates who have not studied the local language of the applied State in 10th or 12th standard must appear for the LPT as part of the selection process, prior to the Personal Interview.
  • Candidates who produce a 10th or 12th standard mark sheet / certificate evidencing that they studied the specified local language as one of the subjects are exempted from the LPT.
  • Candidates who fail to qualify the LPT will not be considered for further selection process.
Personal Interview

As per the Official IOB LBO Recruitment Notification 2026-27

A merit list of candidates, based on marks obtained in the online examination, is prepared in descending order for the respective categories, and candidates must have qualified the LPT (wherever applicable). Candidates securing the minimum qualifying marks in the online examination and LPT (wherever applicable) are called for the Personal Interview based on Bank requirements.

IOB LBO Selection Process – Step by Step

As per the Official IOB LBO Recruitment Notification 2026-27

1
Online Examination
140 objective type questions, 200 marks, 3 hours, across 4 sections.
2
Language Proficiency Test (LPT)
Required only for candidates who have not studied the local language of the applied State in 10th/12th standard.
3
Personal Interview
100 marks; conducted for candidates who qualify the Online Exam and LPT (wherever applicable).
4
Document Verification
Candidates must produce original and self-attested photocopies of prescribed documents at the time of Personal Interview.
5
Final Selection
Merit list prepared state-wise and category-wise based on combined score (80:20 weightage); appointment subject to medical fitness and character/antecedent verification.

Final Merit List Preparation

As per the official notification, the final selection is calculated as follows:

  • Weightage Ratio: The weightage ratio of Online Examination and Personal Interview is 80:20 respectively.
  • Minimum Aggregate Score: The minimum aggregate score of the online examination and interview combined will be 30% for reserved categories and 35% for unreserved category candidates.
  • Combined Final Score: The combined final score of candidates is arrived at on the basis of scores obtained in the Online Examination and Personal Interview.
  • Merit List: Selection is made from the top-ranked candidates in the merit list, which is prepared state-wise and category-wise.
  • Post-Selection Conditions: Appointment of selected candidates is subject to their being declared medically fit as per Bank requirements, and upon satisfactory verification of character, antecedents and caste certificates (wherever applicable). Till such verification, the appointment remains provisional.

The official notification does not disclose any normalisation formula or tie-breaking rule for candidates with equal scores; this article does not assume or invent such details.
