IBPS PO/MT XVI Recruitment 2026: Short Details

  • Recruitment: Common Recruitment Process for Probationary Officers / Management Trainees (CRP PO/MT-XVI)
  • Organization: Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS)
  • Post: Probationary Officer / Management Trainee (PO/MT)
  • Recruitment Cycle: 2027–28
  • Participating Banks: 11 Participating Public Sector Banks
  • Total Vacancies: 7,365 (indicative; revised from an initial 6,715 via the 20 July 2026 corrigendum)

Eligibility Criteria

  • Candidates must hold a Bachelor's Degree in any discipline from a University recognized by the Government of India, or an equivalent qualification recognized as such by the Central Government.
  • Age Limit: 20 to 30 years as on 01 July 2026 (candidates born between 02 July 1996 and 01 July 2006, both dates inclusive, are eligible). Age relaxation is applicable for reserved categories as per IBPS / Government of India rules.

Mode of Selection

  • Preliminary Examination
  • Main Examination
  • Personality Test
  • Common Interview
  • Provisional Allotment

Important Note

The PET Admit Card / call letter is meant only for candidates who opted for Pre-Examination Training and belong to the SC/ST, OBC or Minority Community. This is not the Preliminary Examination admit card.
PwBD category candidates should also check the separate important notice dated 7 August 2026 on the official IBPS website.
Vacancy figures are indicative and may be revised further by participating banks — always confirm the final number from the official notification or latest corrigendum.
Carry a valid original photo ID along with the downloaded PET Admit Card at the training venue.
The Preliminary and Main Examination admit cards will be released separately by IBPS closer to the respective exam dates.
