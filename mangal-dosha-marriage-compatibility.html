Mangal Dosha aur Vivah Compatibility — Poori Jaankari

Mangal Dosha Vivah Mein Kyon Important Hai?

Mangal Dosha — jise Kuja Dosha, Manglik Dosha ya Angaraka Dosha bhi kehte hain — Vedic jyotish mein ek important astrological condition hai. Jab Mangal (Mars) janm kundli mein 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th ya 12th ghar mein hota hai, tab Mangal Dosha maana jaata hai. Shaadi se pehle yeh check karna isliye zaroori maana jaata hai kyonki Mangal ki energy marital harmony ko affect kar sakti hai.

Lekin asli sawaal yeh hai — agar dono mein se sirf ek Manglik hai aur doosra nahi, toh kya hoga? Aur agar dono Manglik hain toh kya vivah zyada acha hoga? Yahi sawaalat is tool se turant solve ho jaate hain.

Ek important sach: 40–50% logon mein Mangal Dosha hota hai. Yeh itna common hai ki agar sirf is ek cheez par dhyan diya jaye toh bahut saare rishte toot sakte hain. Cancellation rules samajhna aur proper upay karna bahut zaroori hai.

Teen Scenarios — Dono Manglik, Ek Manglik, Koi Nahi

Mangal Dosha matching ke teen main scenarios hote hain. Inhe samajhna bahut zaroori hai before finalizing any marriage:

  • Scenario 1 — Dono Manglik (Double Manglik): Yeh sabse acha scenario hai. Dono ke dosha ek dusre ko neutralize kar dete hain. Shaadi mein passion, energy aur commitment dono taraf se bharpoor hoti hai. Saath hi koi special restriction nahi rehti. Minor upay kaafi hote hain.
  • Scenario 2 — Sirf Ek Manglik: Yeh caution zone hai. Yahan Mangal Dosha ki energy ek-sided hoti hai. Upay aur precautions zaroor lene chahiye. Kumbh Vivah ya Mangal Shanti puja strongly recommended hai. Lekin yeh koi hard prohibition nahi — cancellation rules check karna zaroori hai.
  • Scenario 3 — Koi Nahi Manglik: Yeh clean match hai. Mangal Dosha ki koi restriction nahi. Sirf overall Guna Milan aur Nadi Dosha check karein. Koi Mangal-specific upay ki zaroorat nahi.

Dono Manglik Hain Toh Kya Hoga — Poora Sach

Yeh question bahut logon ko confuse karta hai. Kuch log kehte hain "Double Manglik bahut bura hai" — yeh bilkul galat information hai. Vedic jyotish ke classical texts clearly kehte hain:

  • Jab dono partners Manglik hote hain — dosha ek doosre ko cancel karta hai
  • Dosha cancel hone ke baad vivah mein koi Mangal-related restriction nahi rehti
  • Dono ka Mars energy similar hoti hai — relationship mein balance aata hai
  • Dono mein passion, energy aur determination equal level par hoti hai — strong bond banta hai
  • Marriage delay ya mishap ki koi special concern nahi — normal match ke barabar hi hai
Modern aur classical astrologers dono agree karte hain: Double Manglik match actually ek very positive scenario hai — not a warning. Popular culture mein jo fear hai woh based on incomplete knowledge hai. Proper matching, remedies aur cancellation rules se koi bhi match successful ho sakta hai.

Sirf Ek Manglik Hone Par Kya Upay Karein?

Agar sirf ladka ya sirf ladki Manglik hai toh pareshan hone ki zaroorat nahi. Neeche diye upay kaafi effective hote hain:

  • Kumbh Vivah: Manglik vyakti ki pehle symbolic shaadi banana ya peepal ke ped ya Lord Vishnu ki murti se karwai jaati hai — isse dosha shaant ho jaata hai
  • Mangal Shanti Puja: Qualified pandit se Mangal Shanti puja karwani chahiye — especially shaadi se pehle
  • Mangal Mantra: "Om Kraam Kreem Kraum Sah Bhaumaya Namah" — 108 baar daily, especially Tuesday ko
  • Red Coral (Moonga): Manglik vyakti copper ring mein red coral pehne — astrologer ki salah se
  • Tuesday Fast: Shadi se pehle 21 Tuesdays ka vrat rakhen — bahut effective upay hai
  • Age 28 ke baad shaadi: Classical texts kehte hain 28 ke baad Mangal Dosha ki intensity kam ho jaati hai

Cancellation Rules — Kab Dosha Automatically Khatam Ho Jaata Hai?

Mangal Dosha ke bahut se well-established cancellation (parihara) rules hain. In rules ki wajah se dosha cancel ya significantly reduce ho jaata hai:

  • Dono partners Manglik hain — mutual cancellation
  • Mars apni own sign (Aries ya Scorpio) mein hai — dosha cancel
  • Mars exalted (Capricorn) mein hai — dosha nullified
  • Jupiter Mars ko aspect kar raha hai — dosha greatly reduced
  • 7th house lord strong aur well-placed hai — dosha effect kam
  • Leo lagna mein Mars 2nd house mein — auspicious maana jaata hai
  • Venus chart mein strong hai — marital effects mitigate hote hain
  • 28 saal ke baad shaadi — classical texts ke anusar dosha intensity kam hoti hai
❓ FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Mangal Dosha aur Vivah Compatibility ke baare mein sabse common sawaal.

Dono Manglik hain toh kya double problem hogi?+
Bilkul nahi — yeh ek bahut bada misconception hai. Vedic jyotish mein "Double Manglik" match actually best scenario maana jaata hai. Dono ke Mangal Dosha ek dusre ko neutralize kar dete hain — isi tarah jaise ek negative aur ek negative milkaar positive banta hai. Dono ki Mars energy similar level par hoti hai, relationship mein passion aur commitment dono taraf se equal hoti hai, aur koi special Mangal-related restriction nahi rehti. Zaroor ek minor puja karwayein lekin koi badi restriction nahi hai.
Sirf ek Manglik hai toh kya shaadi bilkul nahi karni chahiye?+
Aise koi absolute rule nahi hai. Sirf ek ke Manglik hone par extra precautions zaroor lene chahiye, lekin yeh koi hard prohibition nahi. Pehle cancellation rules check karein — dosha already cancel hua bhi ho sakta hai. Agar nahi toh Kumbh Vivah, Mangal Shanti Puja, Mangal Mantra aur proper remedies se dosha practically khatam ho jaata hai. Bahut saare Manglik-Non-Manglik couples perfectly happy hain. Complete Kundli Milan aur qualified astrologer se zaroor milein.
Match percentage kaise calculate hota hai is tool mein?+
Is tool mein match percentage calculate hota hai dono ke dosha status, intensity score aur applicable cancellation rules ke basis par. Double Manglik match mein high percentage milti hai kyunki mutual cancellation hoti hai. Non-Manglik clean match bhi high percentage deta hai. One-sided Manglik match mein medium se low percentage aati hai depending on dosha severity aur cancellations. Yeh ek educational tool hai — final decision ke liye qualified astrologer se milein.
Kumbh Vivah kya hota hai aur kab zaroori hota hai?+
Kumbh Vivah ek symbolic pre-wedding ceremony hai jisme Manglik vyakti ki pehle shaadi banana ya peepal ke ped ya Lord Vishnu ya Shaligram ki murti se karwai jaati hai. Isse Mangal Dosha shaant hone ki belief hai. Yeh mainly tab recommend kiya jaata hai jab sirf ek vyakti Manglik ho aur dosha severe ho (especially 7th ya 8th house Mars), aur koi major cancellation rule apply na ho. Ise qualified Vedic pandit ke guidance mein karwana chahiye.
Kya Mars ki position sirf 7th house mein hi problem karti hai?+
Nahi — Mangal Dosha cheh ghar se trigger hota hai: 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th, 12th. Lekin sabse zyada discussed 7th house (marriage) aur 8th house (longevity) hain kyunki yeh seedha marriage ko affect karte hain. 1st house ka Mars personality dominance se, 2nd ka family conflict se, 4th ka domestic peace se, 12th ka intimacy issues se related hai. 7th aur 8th mein Mars sabse severe maana jaata hai — yahan upay sabse zyada recommend kiye jaate hain.
28 saal ke baad Mangal Dosha automatically khatam ho jaata hai kya?+
Yeh ek classical Vedic astrology belief hai — bahut se shastriya texts aur traditional astrologers kehte hain ki 28 saal ke baad Mangal ki intense "youthful energy" calm ho jaati hai aur dosha ka impact significantly reduce ho jaata hai. Iska matlab yeh nahi ki dosha poori tarah khatam ho jaata hai, lekin iska intensity bahut kam ho jaati hai. Yeh rule ek cancellation factor ke roop mein consider kiya jaata hai. Lekin sirf is rule par depend karna advisable nahi — complete analysis karwayein.
