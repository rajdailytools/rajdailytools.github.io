SSC GD Exam Complete Guide 2026
Exam pattern, revision tips, stress management — sab kuch ek jagah.
📋
SSC GD CBT Exam Pattern — Poori Jankari

SSC GD Computer Based Test (CBT) ek objective multiple choice exam hai. Total 100 marks ka paper hota hai jisme 4 sections hote hain — aur har section se 25 marks aate hain. Exam duration 60 minutes hoti hai (PwBD candidates ko relaxation milti hai).

  • General Intelligence & Reasoning: 25 questions, 25 marks — pattern, analogy, coding-decoding, direction
  • General Knowledge & General Awareness: 25 questions, 25 marks — current affairs, science, history, geography, sports
  • Elementary Mathematics: 25 questions, 25 marks — percentage, average, ratio, time-work, profit-loss
  • English / Hindi: 25 questions, 25 marks — grammar, vocabulary, comprehension
Negative Marking: 0.25 marks per wrong answer. Guessing mat karo agar confident nahi ho. Verify exam pattern from official SSC notification at ssc.gov.in.
📅
SSC GD Admit Card — Download Kaise Kare?

SSC GD admit card download karne ke liye candidates ko official SSC website par jaana hota hai. Admit card generally exam se 7 to 10 days pehle available hoti hai. Kuch important steps:

  • ssc.gov.in par jaao — "Admit Card" section find karo
  • Registration number aur date of birth/password se login karo
  • Admit card download karo — PDF format me save karo
  • Print karo — black and white print bhi generally acceptable hai
  • Sare details verify karo — name, DOB, exam centre, reporting time
  • Koi galti ho toh turant SSC helpline contact karo
Important: Admit card screenshot exam centre par accept nahi hota — proper printout lena zaroori hai. Multiple copies print karo — ek safe raho.
🧠
SSC GD Last Week Revision Strategy — Jo Kaam Aati Hai

Last week me jo candidates smartly revise karte hain, wo generally better perform karte hain. Yeh tested strategy hai jo actually kaam aati hai:

  • Day 1-2: Math revision — formulas, short tricks, important question types
  • Day 3-4: GK marathon — last 6 months current affairs, static GK important facts
  • Day 5: Reasoning aur English quick revision — pattern recognition practice
  • Day 6: Full mock test — 60 minutes strict timing, analyze every mistake
  • Day 7 (exam se ek din pehle): Koi heavy study nahi — notes padhna, documents ready karna, rest
Pro Tip: Exam se pehle wali raat 10-11 baje so jao. Neend poori hone par exam me focus better rehta hai aur answer accuracy badhti hai.
😌
SSC GD Exam Stress Management — Anxiety Ko Defeat Karo

Exam ke kareeb aate hi anxiety hona normal hai — lekin ise manage karna zaroori hai. Yeh practical tips jo actually kaam karti hain:

  • Countdown tracker daily check karo — yeh remind karega ki time hai, panic mat karo
  • Daily preparation routine maintain karo — predictability stress reduce karti hai
  • Social media exam news se thodi duri rakho last 15 days me — focus disturb hoti hai
  • Exercise daily karo — morning walk ya run stress chemical naturally reduce karta hai
  • Deep breathing practice karo — 4-7-8 technique: 4 sec inhale, 7 hold, 8 exhale
  • Successful candidates ke interviews padhna — motivation boost hoti hai genuinely
  • Ek baar exam clear karo — yeh opportunity bahut mehnat ke baad aayi hai, celebrate the journey
❌
SSC GD Exam Me Common Mistakes — Bhool Se Bhi Mat Karo

Yeh woh galtiyan hain jo candidates exam me regularly karte hain aur regret karte hain baad me:

  • Admit card ghar pe bhool jaana — ek din pehle bag me rakh do
  • Reporting time ke baad pahunchna — late entry nahi milti, full stop
  • Random guessing karna — 0.25 negative marking hai, score damage hota hai
  • Easy sections me zyada time lagana — sabse pehle easy wale complete karo
  • GK me uncertain answers mark karna — GK me positive marking ke liye confident hona chahiye
  • Rough work ek jagah nahi karna — scattered rough work time waste karti hai
  • Phone ya smart watch carry karna — barred items le jaane par disqualification bhi ho sakti hai
Exam centre par prohibited items ki list official notification me mention hoti hai. Pehle se padhna zaroori hai — ssc.gov.in se verify karo.
