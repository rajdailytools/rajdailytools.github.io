SSC GD Selection — Complete Guide 2026

SSC GD Selection Process Kaise Hoti Hai?

SSC GD CAPFs selection ek multi-stage process hai — sirf CBT clear karna enough nahi hota. Poora process kuch is tarah hota hai: (1) Computer Based Test (CBT) — objective pattern, 160 marks, negative marking, (2) Physical Efficiency Test (PET) — running test qualifying, (3) Physical Standard Test (PST) — height/weight/chest verification, (4) Medical Examination — overall fitness check, (5) Document Verification, (6) Final Merit List — CBT performance ke basis par.

Key Point: SSC GD final merit generally CBT marks ke basis par banti hai — PET/PST/Medical qualifying nature ke hote hain. Iska matlab hai jitna zyada CBT score, utna better selection chance. Lekin physical stages fail hone par koi merit nahi — qualify karna mandatory hai.

Role of PET/PST in SSC GD Selection

PET (Physical Efficiency Test) aur PST (Physical Standard Test) dono qualifying stages hain. PET mein running test hota hai — male 5 km, female 1.6 km. PST mein height, weight, chest standards check hote hain. Koi bhi stage fail hone par candidate selection process se out ho jaata hai. Is predictor mein physical status input karne par probability accordingly adjust hoti hai — physical fail = significantly lower chances.

Category-Wise Competition aur Cutoff

SSC GD mein category-wise competition bahut alag hoti hai. General category mein competition highest hoti hai — expected cutoff bhi sabse upar. SC/ST candidates ke liye cutoff relatively lower hoti hai. Is tool mein category select karna accurate prediction ke liye zaroori hai kyunki ek hi CBT score general candidate ke liye "average" ho sakta hai lekin SC candidate ke liye "excellent" ho sakta hai.

  • General/EWS: Most competitive — 100+ score recommended for safe selection
  • OBC: Moderate-high competition — 95+ marks safe zone
  • SC: Lower cutoff — 85-90+ marks generally sufficient
  • ST: Lowest cutoff — 75-80+ marks can qualify

SSC GD Medical Standards — Kya Check Hota Hai

Medical examination mein SSC GD ke standards comprehensive hain:

  • Eye vision: Distance vision 6/6 (unaided generally preferred), color vision test, near vision check
  • Hearing: Both ears normal hearing required
  • General health: Blood pressure, diabetes, heart conditions checked
  • Musculoskeletal: Flat foot check, varicose veins, deformities
  • Color blindness: Fail karwa sakti hai specifically certain roles ke liye

Medical fail hona bahut frustrating hota hai — isliye advance mein apni health check karwao. Eye power correction through surgery (LASIK) possible hai before medical. Color blindness test advance mein karo — hmare website par free color blindness test available hai.

Common Mistakes Jo Selection Chances Reduce Karte Hain

  • Physical preparation ignore karna — written pe itna focus ki body prepare nahi hoti
  • Expected cutoff se 5-7 marks neeche score karke satisfy ho jaana — zyada competition mein dangerous hai
  • Medical standards ke baare mein advance mein aware na rehna — surprise medical fail bahut common problem hai
  • Document verification ke liye certificates timely nahi banwana — OBC certificate, domicile etc.
  • Wrong force preference — posting preference inform nahi karna application mein carefully

Frequently Asked Questions

SSC GD selection ke baare mein 50 most common doubts — clearly answered

1. What is SSC GD selection process?+
SSC GD selection process mein stages hain: CBT (Computer Based Test), PET (Physical Efficiency Test — running), PST (Physical Standard Test — height/weight/chest), Medical Examination, aur Document Verification. Final merit generally CBT marks ke basis par banti hai after all qualifying stages clear karne ke baad.
2. What is a safe score for SSC GD selection?+
Safe score category par depend karta hai. General/EWS: 100+ marks (out of 160) generally safe. OBC: 95+. SC: 88+. ST: 80+. Ye expected trends hain — actual safe score yearly cutoff ke hisaab se vary karta hai. Expected cutoff se 15-20 marks zyada score karo comfortable margin ke liye.
3. Can I qualify SSC GD with 50 marks?+
50 marks (out of 160) se selection chances bahut kam hain — ye expected cutoff se kaafi neeche hai almost all categories ke liye. Sirf extraordinary vacancy surge ya exceptional circumstances mein is score pe selection possible ho sakta hai. Aapko significantly improve karna hoga — 70+ marks ke bina selection extremely difficult hai.
4. Can I qualify SSC GD with 60 marks?+
60 marks pe selection chances ST candidates ke liye slightly possible hai (low probability), SC ke liye very low, aur General/OBC ke liye practically nil. 60 marks pe selection expectation mat rakho — cutoff typically 75-85+ hoti hai depending on category. Improvement zaroori hai.
5. Can I qualify SSC GD with 70 marks?+
70 marks pe ST candidates ke liye moderate chances, SC ke liye low-moderate, OBC ke liye low, aur General ke liye very low chances hain. 70 marks borderline territory hai — physical stages sabhi clear hone par ST mein selection possible ho sakti hai. Other categories ke liye 80+ target karo.
6. Does SSC GD PET affect final merit?+
SSC GD PET generally qualifying nature ka hota hai — pass/fail basis par. Directly merit marks mein add nahi hote. Lekin PET fail = automatic disqualification. Final merit primarily CBT marks se banti hai. So PET pass karna mandatory hai, lekin CBT marks hi selection determine karte hain ultimately.
7. Is SSC GD physical test difficult?+
Jo candidates regularly nahi daudte unke liye SSC GD physical initially challenging lag sakta hai. But 8-10 weeks systematic training se most healthy candidates qualify kar lete hain. Male ke liye 5 km 24 minutes mein, female ke liye 1.6 km 8:30 mein — daily running practice se achievable hai.
8. What is SSC GD cutoff?+
SSC GD cutoff har saal vacancies, candidate performance aur competition level ke hisaab se change hoti hai. Generally General: 85-105, OBC: 82-100, SC: 75-95, ST: 68-88 (approximate range, out of 160). Actual cutoff SSC officially declare karta hai PET/PST ke baad.
9. How many candidates clear SSC GD?+
Selected candidates ki number total vacancies par depend karti hai jo SSC GD notification mein mentioned hoti hai. Generally 40,000 to 1,00,000+ vacancies hoti hain different recruitments mein. CBT mein lakho candidates appear karte hain — competition level bohot high hota hai.
10. Is SSC GD easy to crack?+
SSC GD paper difficulty medium level ka hota hai — lekin competition high hai. Consistent 3-6 months ki preparation with daily practice can significantly improve success chances. Written exam aur physical preparation parallel chalte rehni chahiye. Consistency sabse important factor hai.
11. Can girls clear SSC GD easily?+
Female candidates CBT mein same questions face karte hain jaise male — same preparation needed. PET mein female ke liye 1.6 km running distance shorter hai compared to male ke 5 km. Height standard bhi alag hai. With proper preparation, female candidates SSC GD qualify kar sakti hain. Competition exist karta hai — "easy" nahi hai but achievable hai.
12. Is SSC GD a permanent government job?+
Haan — SSC GD through selected candidates central government ke armed police forces (BSF, CISF, CRPF, ITBP, SSB, NIA, SSF) mein permanent constable/rifleman/sepoy ke post par join karte hain. Ye central government job hai with pension, allowances aur other benefits under 7th Pay Commission.
13. Which force is best in SSC GD?+
BSF, CISF, CRPF aur ITBP sab ke apne benefits hain. CISF generally stable postings ke liye preferred hai — industrial/airport security. BSF border duty hai — challenging. CRPF varied duties. ITBP high altitude postings. Force preference personal choice aur lifestyle expectations par depend karta hai.
14. What is SSC GD merit list?+
SSC GD merit list physically qualified candidates ki final ranking list hai — jo CBT marks ke basis par prepare hoti hai. PET/PST/Medical/Document verification sab clear karne ke baad merit list banti hai. Isi list ke basis par final appointment hoti hai force aur posting allocation ke saath.
15. How is SSC GD final merit prepared?+
SSC GD final merit preparation: CBT marks (160 max) + Category (cutoff comparison) = Merit ranking. PET/PST/Medical qualifying stages hain — marks nahi milte in mein. Jo candidates sab stages clear karte hain unhe CBT score se rank kiya jaata hai. Highest scorers pehle select hote hain vacancies ke andar.
16. Can short candidates get selected in SSC GD?+
SSC GD mein minimum height requirement mandatory hai. General/OBC/EWS male: 170 cm, SC/ST male: 162.5 cm (some categories different). Female: 157 cm (General), 150 cm (relaxed categories). Height standard satisfy nahi karna = PST fail = selection not possible. This predictor mein "not eligible" select karne par probability significantly drops.
17. What is SSC GD medical test?+
SSC GD medical test mein comprehensive fitness check hota hai: Eye vision (distance + near), color vision test, hearing examination, dental check, heart/BP, musculoskeletal assessment (flat foot, varicose veins), general health status. Any significant deficiency can result in medical unfit marking — advance mein check karwao.
18. Is eyesight important in SSC GD?+
Haan — eyesight SSC GD medical mein important factor hai. Distance vision, near vision, aur color vision sab check hote hain. High myopia (very high power) reject kar sakta hai. Color blindness specific tests mein fail kar sakti hai. Advance mein eye check karwao — LASIK surgery option explore karo agar needed.
19. Can spectacles be used in SSC GD?+
SSC GD medical examination mein glasses pehne kar vision test generally nahi hota — unaided vision check kiya jaata hai. Prescribed limits ke andar vision honi chahiye. High power glasses wale candidates ko medical mein issue aa sakta hai. Official notification ke exact vision standards check karo — LASIK surgery before joining possible hai.
20. What is SSC GD running time?+
SSC GD PET running standards: Male — 5 km in 24 minutes. Female — 1.6 km in 8 minutes 30 seconds. Isse zyada time lene par candidate fail ho jaata hai. SSC GD Running Marks Calculator tool se apna exact time check karo — humara tool available hai.
21. Does SSC GD have negative marking?+
Haan — SSC GD CBT mein negative marking hoti hai. Generally 0.50 marks deduct hote hain har wrong answer ke liye. Isliye blind guessing avoid karo — sirf confident answers pe mark karo. 75-80% accuracy ke saath attempt karna better strategy hai compared to all questions attempt karna randomly.
22. How many marks are required for SSC GD?+
Required marks category aur year ke hisaab se vary karte hain. Approximate requirements (out of 160): General/EWS: 90-105+. OBC: 85-100+. SC: 80-95+. ST: 72-88+. Female candidates ke liye cutoff slightly lower hoti hai. Is predictor mein category-specific cutoff comparison se accurate estimate milta hai.
23. Can average students clear SSC GD?+
Haan — SSC GD CBT paper 10th/12th level ka hota hai — average academic background wale candidates bhi qualify kar sakte hain with proper focused preparation. Key hai consistent 3-4 months ki daily practice. Physical preparation simultaneously karo. Average students ne SSC GD qualify kiya hai — success mentality aur consistency se possible hai.

SSC GD aur Army GD dono alag recruitments hain. SSC GD written exam level thoda zyada structured hota hai multiple sections ke saath. Army GD mein physical test more rigorous ho sakta hai (beam, ditch, balance). Competition level dono mein high hai. Difficulty comparison individual preparation level par depend karta hai.

33. Is SSC GD exam online?+
Haan — SSC GD CBT (Computer Based Test) online conducted hota hai designated exam centers mein. Questions on screen aate hain, mouse se options select karne hote hain. Internet connection ya personal computer ki zarurat nahi — exam center computers use karte hain. Good typing aur computer basic knowledge helpful hoti hai.
34. What subjects come in SSC GD exam?+
SSC GD CBT mein 4 sections hain: (1) General Intelligence & Reasoning — 20 questions, 40 marks, (2) General Knowledge & Awareness — 20 questions, 40 marks, (3) Elementary Mathematics — 20 questions, 40 marks, (4) English/Hindi — 20 questions, 40 marks. Total: 80 questions, 160 marks. Duration: 60 minutes. Negative marking: 0.50 per wrong answer.
35. What is SSC GD exam duration?+
SSC GD CBT exam ki duration 60 minutes hoti hai — 80 questions, 160 marks ke liye. Average time per question approximately 45 seconds. Time management critical hai — GK/Language sections fast attempt karo, Mathematics mein time allocate karo. Practice tests se time management improve karo.
36. Can I clear SSC GD in 2 months?+
2 months mein SSC GD qualify karna possible hai if aap pehle se basic knowledge rakhte ho. Daily 4-5 hours focused study: GK current affairs (daily), maths basics daily practice, reasoning shortcuts, language basics. Physical preparation bhi parallel — daily running. 2 months sufficient hai starting point pe jo strong hai. Complete beginners ke liye 3-4 months better hai.
37. Is SSC GD preparation enough for Army?+
SSC GD aur Army GD preparation mein kafi overlap hai — GK, maths, reasoning common hain. Physical preparation bhi similar hai. But Army GD mein alag physical events hote hain (beam, 9-foot ditch etc.) jo SSC GD PET mein nahi hote. Dono ke liye prepare karna smart strategy hai — cross-preparation beneficial hai.
38. What is SSC GD posting?+
SSC GD selected candidates central armed police forces mein post hote hain: BSF (Border Security Force), CISF (Central Industrial Security Force), CRPF (Central Reserve Police Force), ITBP (Indo-Tibetan Border Police), SSB (Sashastra Seema Bal), NIA (National Investigation Agency), aur SSF (Special Security Force). Force allocation merit aur preference pe based hoti hai.
39. Which SSC GD force has best duty?+

BSF — border duty, challenging but good allowances. ITBP — high altitude postings, adventure but demanding. SSB — Nepal/Bhutan border. Each has unique advantages.

Field postings mein duty hours more demanding ho sakte hain. Headquarter aur urban postings mein more structured timings hote hain. Training period mein strict schedule follow karna hota hai.

HRA, DA, Transport Allowance, Medical benefits, Canteen facilities, Pension (NPS), Leave Travel Concession sab include hote hain.

CISF: approximately 9 months. Training physically demanding hoti hai — physical fitness maintain karना post-selection bhi zaroori hai.

CISF (industrial security) generally lower risk. BSF/CRPF active deployments mein higher risk. Ye service karne ki commitment hai — known risk ke saath.

SSC GD constable se promotion path: Constable → Head Constable → ASI → SI — departmental promotion exams ke through. Performance based promotions bhi hote hain. Service period aur departmental exams pass karne par rank advancement milti hai. Additional qualifications aur good service record promotion accelerate kar sakte hain.
