About NCERT LDC Result 2026

The National Council of Educational Research and Training (NCERT) has issued a notice dated 31 August 2026 (F. No. 2‑2/2025‑R‑II/(L.D.C)‑41/10062‑68) releasing the list of candidates provisionally shortlisted for the Computer Typing Test and Documents Verification for the post of Lower Division Clerk (LDC), in alphabetical order, attached as Annexure‑I. This recruitment was carried out under Advertisement No. 01/2025/Non‑Academic, published in the Employment News dated 27th December 2025 to 2nd January 2026.

  • Result Date: 31 August 2026
  • Shortlisted for: Computer Typing Test & Documents Verification
  • Typing Test / DV Dates: 19 & 20 September 2026 (Sat & Sun)
  • Selection Process: Written Exam → Typing Test & DV → Final Result
  • Official Website: ncert.nic.in

How to Check NCERT LDC Result 2026

  1. Visit the official website of NCERT – ncert.nic.in
  2. Click on "Vacancies / Recruitment" on the homepage.
  3. Find the link for the LDC shortlist notice and click on it.
  4. The Annexure‑I PDF will open. Press Ctrl+F and enter your Roll Number.
  5. If your Roll Number is in the list, you are shortlisted.
