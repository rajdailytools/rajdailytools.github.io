Important Notes

⚠️This checker covers only the APFC post under Advt No. 52/2026. No "Enforcement Officer / Accounts Officer" post appears in this official notification.
ℹ️The date for determining eligibility of all candidates in every respect is the closing date for submission of the online application, 11-09-2026 (1800 Hrs).
ℹ️The age limits shown (35/38/40 years) already include the standard category relaxation for OBC/SC/ST. Age relaxations are generally not cumulative — a candidate eligible on more than one ground gets only the applicable relaxation for that ground.
ℹ️PwBD candidates get up to 10 years age relaxation, subject to a maximum age of 56 years, only for posts identified suitable for their specific disability type.
✅Selection process: Written Recruitment Test (RT) → Interview → Document Verification → Medical Examination.
⚠️Application fee: ₹25 for General/OBC/EWS male candidates; exempt for SC/ST/PwBD/Women candidates of any community.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Does UPSC EPFO 2026 include an EO/AO post?+
No. Special Advertisement No. 52/2026 advertises only the Assistant Provident Fund Commissioner (APFC) post, 80 vacancies. No Enforcement Officer / Accounts Officer post appears in this notification.
What is the educational qualification for UPSC APFC 2026?+
A degree of a recognised university or equivalent. A diploma in Company Law, Labour Laws, or Public Administration is desirable but not mandatory.
What is the age limit for UPSC APFC 2026?+
35 years for UR/EWS, 38 years for OBC, and 40 years for SC/ST, as on the closing date 11-09-2026. PwBD candidates get up to 10 additional years, capped at 56 years.
Is work experience required for APFC?+
No experience requirement is prescribed as an essential qualification for this post in the notification.
Can PwBD candidates apply for the APFC post?+
Yes, for disability types identified suitable for this post as listed in the notification's category-wise PwBD table. Some sub-types (e.g. Autism, Intellectual Disability, Specific Learning Disability, stiff back/hips) are not identified suitable.
Does this tool guarantee eligibility or selection?+
No. This is an informational checker based on the official notification. Eligibility remains provisional until verified by UPSC at the time of Document Verification.
Where can I read the full official notification?+
On the official UPSC website, upsc.gov.in, or directly via the notification PDF linked above. Beware of unofficial third-party websites.
