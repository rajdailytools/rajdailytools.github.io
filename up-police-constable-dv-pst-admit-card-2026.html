Introduction

Uttar Pradesh Police Recruitment and Promotion Board (UPPRPB) ne UP Police Constable (Sipahi Civil Police) Recruitment 2025–26 (Advt No. 07/2025) ke likhit pariksha me qualify karne wale candidates ke liye Document Verification (DV) aur Physical Standard Test (PST) ka admit card apni official website par release kar diya hai. Is page par sirf official UPPRPB DV/PST admit card link diya gaya hai, taaki candidates apna admit card aasani se download kar sakein.

Likhit pariksha me total 76,184 candidates shortlist hue hain, jo 32,679 total vacancies ke liye DV/PST round me shaamil honge. DV/PST process 17 August 2026 se pradesh ke 75 districts me shuru hoga. Eligibility Criteria

  • Candidates must have passed Class 12th (Intermediate) from a board recognized by the Government of India, or an equivalent qualification.
  • Age Limit: Male candidates 18 to 22 years and Female candidates 18 to 25 years, as on 01 July 2025. Age relaxation is applicable for reserved categories, and the UP Government has also approved a one-time 3-year age relaxation for this recruitment.
  • Candidates must be Indian citizens; domicile requirements apply as per the official notification.

Mode of Selection

  • Written Examination
  • Document Verification (DV)
  • Physical Standard Test (PST)
  • Physical Efficiency Test (PET)
  • Medical Examination

Important Instructions

Verify all your candidate details printed on the DV/PST admit card carefully.
