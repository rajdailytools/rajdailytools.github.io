NDA Female Eligibility Complete Guide 2026

Kya Girls NDA De Sakti Hain? — Yes, Ab Allowed Hai!

Bahut saari ladkiyan aaj bhi ye sochti hain ki NDA sirf boys ke liye hai — aur ye ek bahut common misconception hai. 2021 tak ye partially sach tha. Lekin August 2021 mein Supreme Court of India ne ek landmark order diya — female candidates ko NDA examination mein allow kiya jaaye.

UPSC ne NDA 2/2021 notification se female candidates ko include karna shuru kiya. Iske baad se har NDA notification mein male aur female dono candidates apply kar sakte hain. Ye India ki defence forces ke liye ek historic shift hai — aur agar aap NDA join karna chahti hain, toh ye bilkul possible hai.

Historic Fact: Supreme Court of India ka 2021 ka order — "The exclusion of women from NDA examination is unconstitutional" — ye wo turning point tha jab India ki ladkiyon ke liye NDA ka darwaza officially khula. Agar koi aaj bhi bolta hai "girls NDA nahi de sakti" — wo galat hai.

NDA Female Eligibility — Age Limit Kya Hai?

NDA ke liye age limit male aur female dono ke liye same hai — 16.5 years se 19.5 years. Ye age exam date ke hisaab se calculate hoti hai, nahi ki application date ke hisaab se. Iska matlab:

  • Aapki age exam date par 16 years aur 6 months se kam nahi honi chahiye
  • Aapki age exam date par 19 years aur 6 months se zyada nahi honi chahiye
  • Ye calculation exact hoti hai — days bhi count hote hain
  • UPSC notification mein exact date range clearly mention hoti hai — wo hamesha official source maano

Ye age window bahut competitive hai — Class 12 ke final year ya just after 12th pass aayi ladkiyan is window mein typically fit hoti hain. Agar aap abhi Class 11 mein hain, toh apni age carefully calculate karo aur plan karo ki kaunsa attempt suitable hoga.

Height Requirement for NDA Female Candidates

NDA mein female candidates ke liye minimum height approximately 152 cm hai. Ye height standard Air Force, Navy aur Army — teeno wings ke liye applicable hai, though slight variations possible hain official notification ke hisaab se. Ye height measurement medical examination mein barefoot standing position mein hoti hai.

Ek common myth hai ki NDA ke liye bahut zyada height chahiye — ye galat hai. 152 cm ek achievable standard hai majority of Indian women ke liye. Agar height thodi borderline hai, toh:

  • Proper posture maintain karo — slouch height chhupaata hai
  • Standing straight during measurement — head level, shoulders relaxed
  • Morning measurement slightly taller hoti hai
  • Medical board standard dekh — exact cutoff official notification confirm karta hai

Qualification — 12th aur PCM Requirement

NDA female eligibility ke liye qualification requirement wing ke hisaab se alag hai:

  • Army Wing: 12th class pass (any recognized board) with any stream — PCM compulsory nahi. Arts, Commerce, Science — sab valid hain.
  • Naval Wing: 12th class with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM) compulsory. Non-PCM students Naval wing ke liye eligible nahi hain.
  • Air Force Wing: 12th class with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM) compulsory — same as Naval. Aviation requires technical foundation.
  • Appearing Students: Jo students 12th board exam appear kar rahe hain unhe bhi apply karne ki permission hoti hai — subject to qualification at the time of joining.
Practical Tip: Agar aap currently Arts ya Commerce mein hain aur NDA join karna chahti hain — Army wing choose karo. Agar Air Force ya Navy interest hai, toh ab bhi time hai — Class 12 mein PCM add karne ke options explore karo ya open board se PCM complete karo.

Girls ke liye NDA — Selection Process

NDA selection process do stages mein hota hai — written examination aur SSB (Service Selection Board) interview. Dono stages female candidates ke liye same hain, koi alag standard nahi hai:

  • Written Exam (UPSC): Mathematics paper (300 marks) + General Ability Test / GAT (600 marks). Total 900 marks. Objective type questions. Negative marking applicable.
  • SSB Interview (5 days): Day 1 — OIR test + PPDT. Day 2 — Psychological tests (TAT, WAT, SRT, SDT). Day 3–4 — GTO tasks (outdoor group tasks, lecturette, command task). Day 5 — Conference. SSB is primarily about officer-like qualities, not gender.
  • Medical Examination: After SSB recommendation, medical board check height, weight, vision, hearing, and overall physical fitness.
  • Merit List: Based on written + SSB — final merit list published by UPSC.

NDA Female Eligibility — Common Confusions Cleared

  • Confusion 1 — "Girls NDA nahi de sakti": Ye purani information hai. 2021 ke baad from NDA 2/2021, girls officially apply kar sakti hain. Supreme Court order — constitutional right hai.
  • Confusion 2 — "PCM compulsory hai girls ke liye bhi": Nahi — sirf Naval aur Air Force wing ke liye. Army wing ke liye any stream valid hai.
  • Confusion 3 — "Height bahut zyada chahiye": Nahi — 152 cm minimum standard hai female candidates ke liye. Ye achievable hai majority of candidates ke liye.
  • Confusion 4 — "Appearing students apply nahi kar sakti": Galat — appearing candidates (12th mein) bhi apply kar sakti hain subject to final qualifying at joining time.
  • Confusion 5 — "Girls ke liye alag NDA exam hota hai": Nahi — same UPSC NDA exam male aur female dono ke liye. Same paper, same standards.

NDA Joining After Selection — Wings aur Training

NDA selected female candidates Joint Services Wing mein train karti hain — Khadakwasla, Pune mein. Training 3 years ki hoti hai — academics + physical + military training. After NDA, respective service academies mein further training hoti hai — IMA (Army), INA (Navy), AFA (Air Force).

Female officers Indian Army, Navy, aur Air Force mein various roles mein serve kar sakti hain — though frontline combat positions ka framework evolving hai. Most technical, administrative, medical, signals, intelligence, logistics roles open hain female officers ke liye.

Nahi — SSB process female candidates ke liye exactly same hai jaise male candidates ke liye. Same tests, same standards, same evaluation. SSB 5 days ki process hai — OIR + PPDT (Day 1), Psychological Tests (Day 2), GTO Tasks (Day 3–4), Conference (Day 5). SSB primarily officer-like qualities test karta hai — leadership, communication, teamwork, mental agility. Gender-specific koi alag round nahi hota. Female candidates recently SSB mein strong results de rahi hain — yahi batata hai ki preparation aur personality matter karte hain, gender nahi.

NDA selected female candidates Khadakwasla, Pune mein Joint Services Wing mein train karti hain — exactly same campus jahan male cadets train karte hain. Training 3 years ki hoti hai — academics (BSc / BA), physical training, military training, and wing-specific training. After NDA graduation — Army ke liye IMA (Dehradun), Navy ke liye INA (Ezhimala), Air Force ke liye AFA (Dundigal) mein further training hoti hai. NDA training internationally prestigious hai — duniya ke top military academies mein se ek.
