Complete Guide

What is an IELTS to PTE Converter?

The IELTS to PTE Converter is a free tool that helps students, immigration applicants, and professionals quickly understand how their IELTS band score maps to an estimated PTE Academic score range. It is useful for anyone comparing the two tests, evaluating whether to switch from IELTS to PTE, or checking whether their IELTS performance would translate to meeting a PTE-based requirement.

Unlike PTE, which produces a single overall score (10–90), IELTS uses a 0–9 band scale in 0.5 increments. Because the two tests measure English proficiency differently, the conversion produces a score range rather than a single number.

Important: Conversion values are estimates based on widely used equivalency references across the education and immigration sector. They are not officially published by British Council, IDP, or Pearson and should be used as planning guidance — not as a guarantee of acceptance. Always confirm requirements directly with the institution or authority.

Why Convert IELTS to PTE?

There are several common reasons someone needs to convert an IELTS band to an estimated PTE score:

  • Your target institution lists a PTE score requirement and you want to know if your IELTS result is equivalent
  • You are deciding which test to sit and want to understand the scoring systems side by side
  • You have a previous IELTS score and are considering taking PTE instead for a new application
  • You are advising others as a study abroad consultant or immigration agent
  • You want to understand your approximate CEFR level from your IELTS result

Who Should Use This Tool?

  • Working professionals switching between English proficiency tests
  • Study abroad consultants supporting clients with multiple English test results
  • English language learners tracking progress against internationally recognised benchmarks

Understanding the PTE Score Range

Because IELTS bands are discrete steps (4.5, 5.0, 5.5 … 9.0) while PTE Academic scores are a continuous scale, each IELTS band corresponds to a range of PTE scores rather than a single point. For example:

IELTS vs PTE: Which Test Should You Take?

Both IELTS and PTE Academic are accepted by thousands of institutions worldwide. The right choice depends on your strengths, comfort with technology, and timeline:

  • Choose IELTS if you prefer a face-to-face Speaking test and are more comfortable with paper-based testing (Academic or General Training format)
  • Choose PTE if you want faster results (typically within 48 hours), prefer AI-scored testing, and are comfortable with computer-based interfaces
  • Both tests are accepted in Australia, UK, Canada, USA, New Zealand, and Ireland for study, work, and immigration purposes
  • PTE Academic is particularly popular in Australia for skilled migration; IELTS remains more widely used in the UK and for some Canadian pathways
  • Always confirm which tests your target institution or immigration authority accepts before booking

Can You Use a Converted Score for Applications?

No. Universities, employers, and immigration authorities require official score reports from the respective testing organisation. A converted or estimated score cannot be submitted in place of an actual test result. Use this converter for planning and comparison purposes only — always book and sit the test required by your target institution.

Frequently Asked Questions

IELTS to PTE Converter FAQs

Common questions about converting IELTS band scores to PTE Academic equivalents.

What is an IELTS to PTE Converter?+
An IELTS to PTE Converter is a tool that estimates the equivalent PTE Academic score range from a given IELTS overall band score. It uses widely accepted score comparison references to help students and professionals quickly understand how their IELTS performance maps to the PTE Academic scoring scale (GSE 10–90).
Is the IELTS to PTE conversion exact?+
No. The conversion is an estimated equivalency. IELTS and PTE Academic are different tests with different scoring systems, and there is no single official conversion published by British Council, IDP, or Pearson. The values in this tool follow commonly referenced comparison tables used in the education and immigration sectors. Always verify requirements directly with the institution or authority you are applying to.
Why does IELTS give a band but PTE gives a range?+
IELTS overall band scores are a single number (e.g. 7.0) measured in 0.5 increments on a 0–9 scale. PTE Academic scores are a continuous scale from 10 to 90. Because each IELTS band covers a range of PTE performance, the conversion produces a score range (e.g. IELTS 7.0 ≈ PTE 55–62) rather than one fixed number. The actual PTE score a candidate achieves depends on their individual performance across all sections.
Can universities accept a converted IELTS-to-PTE score?+
No. Institutions require official score reports issued directly by the testing organisation — either IELTS (British Council / IDP) or PTE Academic (Pearson). A converted estimate cannot be submitted as evidence of language proficiency. This tool is for planning and comparison only. Check whether your target institution accepts PTE directly, and if so, what the minimum required score is.
What is the PTE equivalent of IELTS 6.5?+
IELTS 6.5 is approximately equivalent to a PTE Academic score of 47–54. This corresponds to CEFR level B2 (Upper Intermediate). IELTS 6.5 / PTE 47–54 is commonly required for undergraduate admission at universities in Australia, UK, and Canada, and for standard student visa applications.
What is the PTE equivalent of IELTS 7.0?+
IELTS 7.0 is approximately equivalent to a PTE Academic score of 55–62. This corresponds to CEFR level C1 (Effective Operational Proficiency). This range meets the entry requirements for many undergraduate and postgraduate programs, as well as most standard visa applications in Australia, UK, and Canada.
Is PTE harder or easier than IELTS?+
Neither test is inherently harder or easier — it depends on your individual strengths. PTE Academic is fully computer-based with AI scoring, which some candidates find more consistent and less subjective. IELTS has a human-to-human Speaking component, which others find more natural. Candidates who type well and prefer technology-driven environments often perform better on PTE; those comfortable with face-to-face interaction may prefer IELTS.
Can I use this tool before deciding which test to take?+
Yes — that is one of the most common uses of this converter. If you have a previous IELTS score and want to understand what PTE score you would need to meet a particular requirement, this tool gives you a useful starting estimate. Combine it with your research on the specific requirements of your target institution or visa subclass before booking either test.
