How To Check & Download RBI Grade B Result 2026

1 Visit the official Reserve Bank of India (RBI) website.
2 Open the RBI Opportunities / Results section.
3 Find the result for Officers in Grade ‘B’ (DR) – General Cadre – PY 2026.
4 Open the official result page or result PDF provided by RBI.
5 For the Phase-I result, enter your Roll Number and Date of Birth along with the required captcha, if prompted.
6 Check your result status and whether you have been shortlisted for the Phase-II Examination.
7 Download or save the result/score information for future reference.
