Everything you need to know about Indian Army height rules — region wise, post wise

Indian Army Height Rules — What You Need to Know

Indian Army mein height ek basic physical requirement hai — lekin jo cheez log nahi jaante wo ye hai ki ye requirement sabke liye ek jaise nahi hoti. Height criteria post ke hisaab se alag hoti hai, aur sath hi region aur state ke hisaab se bhi alag relaxation milti hai. Agar aap sirf ek generic figure 170 cm padhke discourage ho gaye hain, toh ye guide aapke liye hai.

Indian Army officially follows different height norms for different regions, posts and categories. The key document is the official Soldier Recruitment Notification published by ARO (Army Recruiting Office) for each rally.

GD (General Duty) Height Requirement

Army GD — yani General Duty — ke liye India ke general states mein minimum height 170 cm hai. Ye sabse common figure hai jo log quote karte hain. Lekin NE states ke candidates ke liye ye limit sirf 162 cm hai — matlab 8 cm ki chhoot. Gorkha region (Darjeeling, Sikkim, HP, Uttarakhand hills) ke liye 163 cm hai.

  • General states (UP, Bihar, Rajasthan, Haryana, etc.) — 170 cm minimum for GD
  • North East states (Assam, Arunachal, Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram, Meghalaya, Tripura) — 162 cm
  • Gorkha / Hill districts — 163 cm minimum
  • Scheduled Tribal candidates from tribal belts — 162 cm

Clerk / SKT Height Requirement

Army Clerk aur Stores Keeper Technical (SKT) ke liye general states mein minimum height 162 cm hai — jo GD se 8 cm kam hai. Ye post un candidates ke liye better option ban sakti hai jinki height 162–169 cm ke beech hai. NE states ke liye clerk height 157 cm hai.

Technical / Nursing Assistant Height Requirement

Army Technical (Sol Tech) aur Nursing Assistant (NA) ke liye general states mein 167 cm minimum height chahiye. Ye GD se thodi kam hai. NE candidates ke liye ye 162 cm hai. Technical posts mein education ka bhi bada role hota hai — PCM with 50% marks mandatory hai.

JCO (Junior Commissioned Officer) — Direct Entry & Pandit

JCO direct entry aur JCO Religious Teacher (Pandit/Maulvi/Padri) ke liye height requirement 160 cm hai — jo army ki sabse low civilian-entry height hai. In posts ke liye alag educational qualification bhi chahiye.

Education Havaldar Height Requirement

Army Education Corps ke Havaldar (Education) ke liye height criteria 162 cm hai general states mein. Graduation compulsory hai is post ke liye. Ye post educated candidates ke liye ek excellent opportunity hai jinki height GD ke liye sufficient nahi.

Female Candidates (Agniveer Military Police)

Mahila candidates ke liye Indian Army mein sirf Agniveer Military Police (MP) ki vacancy available hoti hai. Inke liye minimum height 152 cm hai — jo general male requirement se 18 cm kam hai. Weight bhi proportional honi chahiye.

North East Region — Why the Special Relaxation?

North East India ke log genetically thode shorter hote hain — ye ek biological reality hai, koi discrimination nahi. Army ne is fact ko acknowledge kiya hai aur NE states ke candidates ko significant height relaxation di hai. Iska matlab ye nahi ki NE candidates less fit hain — balki physical fitness standards alag tarike se measure kiye jaate hain region wise.

Agar aap Assam, Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram, Meghalaya, Arunachal Pradesh ya Tripura se hain — toh aapko automatically 8 cm ki height relaxation milti hai GD mein. Ye relaxation domicile/nativity certificate ke basis par milti hai.

Gorkha Region Relaxation

Darjeeling district (West Bengal), Sikkim, aur kuch hill districts of Uttarakhand, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu & Kashmir — ye Gorkha relaxation zone mein aate hain. Yahan ke candidates ko GD mein 163 cm minimum chahiye — general 170 cm se 7 cm less. Gorkha Regiment mein recruitment alag hoti hai aur criteria slightly different ho sakta hai.

GD vs Clerk vs Technical — Height Difference

Ek common confusion ye hai ki "agar GD eligible nahi hoon toh kya koi option hai?" — answer hai haan. Clerk aur Technical posts mein height requirement kam hai. GD ke liye 170 cm chahiye general states mein, Clerk ke liye 162 cm, aur JCO Pandit ke liye sirf 160 cm. Toh agar aap 164 cm hain aur UP se hain — GD nahi milega but Clerk aur JCO ke liye eligible hain.

Smart Strategy: Agar height borderline hai — pehle identify karo ki kis post ke liye eligible ho. Fir us post ki physical fitness aur written exam dono ki preparation karo. Ek sahi post choose karna half battle jeet jaata hai.

Height Measurement Process in Army Rally

Army rally mein height measurement bilkul standard process se hoti hai — barefoot standing on a flat board, using a calibrated stadiometer. Candidate ko seedha khada rehna hota hai, heels together, shoulders relaxed, eyes level. Hair ko measure se exclude kiya jaata hai. Morning mein measurement thodi zyada hoti hai kyunki spine compressed nahi hoti — isliye subah ki rally mein thoda advantage hota hai.

What to Do If Height is 1–2 cm Short?

Agar aap 1–2 cm se reh gaye hain, toh panic mat karo. Kuch practical steps hain. Pehli cheez — posture correct karo. Bahut log slouch karte hain jisse 1–2 cm natural height chhupta hai. Hanging exercises (pull-up bar pe 2–3 sets daily), swimming, aur skipping rope regularly karne se kuch months mein noticeable improvement aata hai — especially younger candidates mein. Sleep properly — 8 hours quality sleep growth hormone release karta hai.

Army Height Rule Common Confusions — Cleared

  • Confusion 1: "Kya relaxation sirf ST category ke liye hai?" — Nahi. NE states mein ALL categories ko relaxation milti hai, not just ST.
  • Confusion 2: "Kya 170 cm sabke liye compulsory hai?" — Nahi. Sirf general states mein GD ke liye. Other posts aur other regions mein less hai.
  • Confusion 3: "Female candidates ke liye height nahi hoti?" — Galat. Agniveer MP female ke liye 152 cm compulsory hai.
  • Confusion 4: "Shoes pehne reh sakte hain measurement mein?" — Bilkul nahi. Measurement strictly barefoot hoti hai.
  • Confusion 5: "Agar height fail ho toh directly reject?" — Haan, height requirement physical fitness test ka first filter hai. Height fail = rally mein aage nahi.

Conclusion — Height Check Karo, Sahi Post Choose Karo

Indian Army mein height ek important criterion hai — lekin har candidate ke liye region, post aur category ke hisaab se alag. NE states, Gorkha region aur tribal areas ke candidates ko significant relaxation milti hai. Agar aap sahi information se armed hain — toh aap sahi post choose kar sakte hain, sahi rally target kar sakte hain, aur apni preparation focus kar sakte hain. Frequently Asked Questions

Most common height-related doubts — answered clearly

Army mein GD ke liye minimum height kitni chahiye? +
General states (UP, Bihar, Rajasthan, Haryana, Punjab etc.) ke candidates ke liye Army GD minimum height 170 cm hai. North East states ke candidates ke liye ye 162 cm hai. Gorkha/hill region ke liye 163 cm hai. Tribal ST candidates ke liye bhi 162 cm applicable hoti hai. Height measurement barefoot hoti hai.
Kya North East states ke candidates ko height relaxation milti hai? +
Haan — aur ye relaxation substantial hai. Assam, Arunachal Pradesh, Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram, Meghalaya aur Tripura ke candidates ke liye GD mein minimum height 162 cm hai — general states ke 170 cm se poore 8 cm kam. Ye relaxation ALL categories ko milti hai — General, OBC, SC, ST sabko. Relaxation ke liye state domicile/nativity certificate required hota hai.
Army Clerk ke liye height kitni chahiye? +
Army Clerk / SKT (Stores Keeper Technical) ke liye general states mein minimum height 162 cm hai — jo GD se 8 cm less hai. North East candidates ke liye 157 cm. Female candidates ke liye 152 cm. Clerk post ke liye 60% marks in Class 12 with English aur Mathematics/Accounts/Book Keeping bhi chahiye.
Agar meri height 1-2 cm se reh jaaye toh kya kare? +
Sabse pehle — posture check karo. Bahut log natural slouch ki wajah se 1-2 cm lose karte hain. Seedha khade rehne ki practice karo. Hanging exercises (bar pe latakna), skipping rope, aur swimming regularly karo. Proper 8 hours sleep lo — growth hormone raat mein release hota hai. Healthy diet lo jisme calcium aur proteins hon. Younger candidates (under 21) mein ye methods zyada effective hain.
Army mein height measurement exactly kaise hoti hai? +
Army rally mein height measurement calibrated stadiometer se hoti hai. Candidate ko barefoot (bina joote ke) flat board par seedha khada rehna hota hai. Heels saath mein, shoulders relaxed, chin level. Hair ko measure mein count nahi kiya jaata. Measurement once hoti hai aur ARO officer ke saamne. Subah measurement karna slightly advantageous hota hai kyunki spine compressed nahi hoti — maximum height milti hai.
JCO Pandit aur Education Havaldar ke liye height kya hai? +
JCO Religious Teacher (Pandit/Maulvi/Padri/Pendanda) ke liye minimum height 160 cm hai — jo army recruitment mein sabse low requirement hai general categories mein. Education Havaldar ke liye 162 cm chahiye (general states). Ye posts educated candidates ke liye specially beneficial hain jinka height GD ke required 170 cm tak nahi pahuncha.
Female candidates ke liye Army height requirement kya hai? +
Female candidates ke liye Indian Army mein Agniveer Military Police (AMP) ki vacancy hoti hai jisme minimum height 152 cm required hai. Weight proportional hona chahiye (48 kg minimum). Female candidates ke liye alag physical fitness standards hote hain — running time aur other events mein different norms apply hote hain. Regularly ARO notifications check karte rahein kyunki female vacancy limited hoti hai.
