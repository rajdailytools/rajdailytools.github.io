Complete Guide

SSC GD Kya Hai & Eligibility Explained 2026

SSC GD ki taiyari shuru karne se pehle poori eligibility samajhna bahut zaroori hai. Yahan sab kuch detail mein hai.

SSC GD Kya Hota Hai?

SSC GD — Staff Selection Commission General Duty Constable — India ki Central Armed Police Forces (CAPF) mein constable posts ke liye central government ki recruitment exam hai. Isme CRPF, BSF, CISF, ITBP, SSB, NIA, SSF, aur Assam Rifles mein constables ka selection hota hai.

Ye ek four-stage selection process hai — Computer Based Test (CBT), Physical Efficiency Test (PET), Physical Standard Test (PST), aur Medical Examination. Lakhs of candidates apply karte hain har saal.

Official SSC GD notifications ke liye visit karein: ssc.nic.in — sabse accurate aur updated information wahan milegi.

Age Eligibility — Category-wise Relaxation

General category ke candidates ke liye age 18–23 years honi chahiye. Different categories ko relaxation milti hai:

  • OBC candidates: Maximum age 26 years (+3 years relaxation)
  • SC/ST candidates: Maximum age 28 years (+5 years relaxation)
  • Ex-Serviceman: Additional 3 years over their category limit
  • PH (Physically Handicapped) candidates: 10 years relaxation for GEN, 13 for OBC, 15 for SC/ST

Height Relaxation — Category & Region

SSC GD mein height criteria aur relaxation carefully apply karni chahiye:

  • General male candidates: 170 cm minimum required
  • SC/ST male candidates: 165 cm (5 cm relaxation)
  • Gorkha / Garhwali / Kumaoni / Dogra: 165 cm (5 cm relaxation)
  • North East / Assam region: 165 cm (5 cm relaxation)
  • ST Area/Tribal Belt: 165 cm (5 cm relaxation)
  • Women (General): 157 cm minimum
  • Women (SC/ST/Special Region): 152 cm (5 cm relaxation)
Important: Height relaxation ke liye relevant certificate (caste certificate, domicile certificate) ready rakhein. Certificate ke bina relaxation claim nahi kar sakte.

Common Mistakes Candidates Karte Hain

❌ Age Calculate Galat Karna
Age official notification date pe calculate hoti hai — cut-off date check karein. Calendar year se directly nahi.
❌ Category Relaxation Ignore Karna
OBC/SC/ST candidates age aur height mein apni relaxation claim karna bhool jaate hain — jo eligible bana sakti hai.
❌ Regional Relaxation Miss Karna
Gorkha/NE/Dogra area ke candidates height relaxation claim nahi karte — valid domicile certificate ready rakhein.
❌ Physical Prep Neglect Karna
Written exam ke baad physical test ke liye taiyar nahi rehna — 5 km race aur high/long jump ke liye regular practice zaroori hai.
Pro Tip: SSC GD ka written exam sirf qualifying hai — final selection mainly physical test performance par based hota hai. Physical preparation ko written exam ke saath hi shuru karein.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

SSC GD eligibility ke baare mein sabse common sawaal.

SSC GD ke liye age kitni chahiye 2026 mein?+
SSC GD 2026 ke liye minimum age 18 years aur maximum age 23 years honi chahiye (General/EWS). OBC ke liye maximum 26 years, SC/ST ke liye maximum 28 years. Ex-Serviceman ke liye additional 3 years relaxation milti hai. Age calculation official notification date par hoti hai — cut-off date se calculate karein.
SSC GD mein height kitni honi chahiye male ke liye?+
Male General/OBC/EWS: 170 cm minimum. SC/ST candidates: 165 cm (5 cm relaxation). Gorkha, Garhwali, Kumaoni, Dogra, North East candidates: 165 cm (5 cm relaxation). Chest measurement bhi required hai — 80 cm unexpanded with minimum 5 cm expansion. Hill region candidates ke liye chest criteria thodi different ho sakti hai.
Girls (Female) ke liye SSC GD height criteria kya hai?+
Female candidates ke liye SSC GD height criteria: General/OBC/EWS: 157 cm minimum. SC/ST female candidates: 152 cm (5 cm relaxation). Gorkha/NE/Special region female candidates: 152 cm (5 cm relaxation). Females ke liye chest measurement required nahi hoti. Weight proportionate to height as per medical standards.
OBC candidates ko SSC GD mein kitna relaxation milta hai?+
OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) candidates ko SSC GD mein age mein 3 years ki relaxation milti hai — maximum age 23+3 = 26 years ho jaati hai. Height mein OBC ke liye koi relaxation nahi milti — same as General category (Male 170 cm, Female 157 cm). NCL (Non-Creamy Layer) certificate mandatory hai — without it, General category apply hogi.
Gorkha / North East ke candidates ke liye SSC GD mein alag rules hain?+
Haan, Gorkha, Garhwali, Kumaoni, Dogra, North East (Assam, Meghalaya, Arunachal Pradesh, Manipur, etc.) aur ST Area candidates ko height mein 5 cm ki relaxation milti hai. Male ke liye 165 cm (instead of 170) aur Female ke liye 152 cm (instead of 157) sufficient hai. Age mein koi alag relaxation nahi milti in regions ke liye. Valid domicile ya relevant certificate required hai.
SSC GD ke liye minimum education qualification kya chahiye?+
SSC GD ke liye minimum qualification 10th Pass (Matriculation) hai — any recognized board se. Higher education (12th, Graduate) hone se koi extra benefit nahi milta kyunki SSC GD sirf constable level recruitment hai. Below 10th (aur 10th fail) candidates apply nahi kar sakte. 10th ka valid marksheet aur certificate hona zaroori hai.
SC/ST candidates ko SSC GD mein kya advantages milte hain?+
SC/ST candidates ko SSC GD mein multiple advantages milte hain: 1) Age relaxation: +5 years (maximum 28 years); 2) Height relaxation: −5 cm (Male 165 cm, Female 152 cm); 3) Fee exemption (application fee); 4) Written exam mein reserved seats. Valid caste certificate required hai — state government dwara issued.
SSC GD physical test mein kya hota hai?+
SSC GD Physical Efficiency Test (PET) mein: Male — 5 km Race in 24 minutes, 11 feet Long Jump, 3.5 feet High Jump. Female — 1.6 km Race in 8.5 minutes, 9 feet Long Jump, 3 feet High Jump. Physical Standard Test (PST) mein height, weight, aur chest measurement verified hoti hai. PET/PST sirf qualifying nature ka hota hai — marks nahi milte.
Kya graduated candidate ko SSC GD mein koi extra benefit milta hai?+
Nahi — SSC GD ke liye graduation ka koi extra benefit nahi milta. Minimum requirement 10th Pass hai aur higher education hone se koi relaxation ya preference nahi milti. Lekin agar same candidate SSC CHSL ya SSC CGL apply karna chahta hai, toh graduation useful hogi. SSC GD specifically constable-level recruitment hai jahan 10th base minimum hai.
SSC GD age cut-off date kya hoti hai?+
SSC GD ke liye age cut-off date official notification mein mention hoti hai — generally January 1st of the recruitment year ya notification release date. Age usi cut-off date par calculate ki jaati hai — aapka birthday uss date tak ke hisaab se count hota hai. Hamesha notification ki cut-off date dekh ke apni age calculate karein, not the current date.
