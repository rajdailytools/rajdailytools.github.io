📚 Complete Guide

SSC CGL Physical Eligibility 2026 — Full Guide

Official Annexure-XV ke aadhar par complete physical eligibility guide. Read carefully before filling post preference.

What is SSC CGL Physical Eligibility?

SSC CGL mein kuch specific posts ke liye candidates ko physical standards meet karne padte hain. Yeh standards height, chest (male only), weight (female), walking test, cycling test aur eyesight cover karte hain. Yeh standards official SSC CGL notification ke Annexure-XV mein diye hote hain.

Physical eligibility ek qualifying criteria hai — iska matlab hai ki agar aap physical standards meet nahi karte to aap us post ke liye select nahi honge, chahe written exam mein aapka score kitna bhi acha ho. Isliye post preference form bharne se pehle physical eligibility check karna bahut zaroori hai.

Physical test SSC conduct nahi karta. Concerned department (CBIC, CBI, NIA, NCB) nomination ke baad verify karta hai. Ek baar post miss ho gayi to SSC koi second chance nahi deta.

Which SSC CGL Posts Need Physical Standards?

SSC CGL 2026 notification ke anusaar neeche diye gaye posts mein physical standards required hain:

  • Inspector (Central Excise) — CBIC under Ministry of Finance
  • Inspector (Examiner) — CBIC
  • Inspector (Preventive Officer) — CBIC
  • Inspector CBN — Central Bureau of Narcotics
  • Sub Inspector CBN — Central Bureau of Narcotics
  • Sub Inspector CBI — Central Bureau of Investigation
  • Sub Inspector NIA — National Investigation Agency
  • Sub Inspector / JIO NCB — Narcotics Control Bureau

Baaki SSC CGL posts jaise Tax Assistant, Auditor, Accountant, UDC, MEA posts etc. ke liye koi physical standard required nahi hai.

Height Requirements Explained

SSC CGL mein alag alag posts ke liye alag height requirements hain. Sab se important baat — NIA SI ke liye male candidates ko 170 cm chahiye jo sabse zyada hai. CBI SI aur NCB SI ke liye 165 cm chahiye, jabki Excise Inspector aur CBN posts ke liye 157.5 cm (male) aur 152 cm (female) required hai.

  • NIA SI — 170 cm (Male) | 150 cm (Female) — Highest requirement
  • CBI SI — 165 cm (Male) | 150 cm (Female)
  • NCB SI/JIO — 165 cm (Male) | 152 cm (Female)
  • Excise / Examiner / Preventive / CBN Posts — 157.5 cm (Male) | 152 cm (Female)

Chest Requirements Explained

Chest requirement sirf male candidates ke liye hai. Female candidates ke liye koi chest measurement nahi hoti. Male candidates ke liye do standards hain:

  • Excise / Examiner / Preventive / CBN posts — 81 cm chest with 5 cm expansion required
  • CBI SI / NIA SI — 76 cm chest with expansion required
  • NCB SI/JIO — 76 cm chest with 5 cm expansion required

Chest measurement unexpanded hoti hai aur uske saath expansion bhi minimum required hoti hai. Agar aapka chest 81 cm hai par expansion sirf 3 cm hai, to aap fail ho sakte hain.

Chest expand karne ki practice karo. Daily deep breathing exercises aur swimming chest expansion improve karte hain. 3-4 mahine mein expansion improve ho sakti hai.

Walking Test Rules

Walking test Excise Inspector, Examiner, Preventive Officer aur CBN posts ke liye applicable hai. Yeh running test nahi hai — specifically walking test mention kiya gaya hai notification mein.

  • Male candidates — 1600 meters in 15 minutes
  • Female candidates — 1 kilometer in 20 minutes

Walking pace maintain karna important hai. Aap jogging nahi kar sakte — yeh brisk walking test hota hai. Time limit mein complete karna mandatory hai.

Cycling Test Rules

Cycling test bhi Excise/CBN related posts ke liye hai. Distance aur time standards notification mein clearly mentioned hain:

  • Male candidates — 8 kilometers in 30 minutes
  • Female candidates — 3 kilometers in 25 minutes

Cycling test ke liye cycle concerned department provide karta hai ya khud lani padti hai — yeh department-specific hota hai. Regular cycling practice se stamina build hoti hai jo test mein help karta hai.

Vision Standards Explained

Vision standards sabhi physical posts ke liye mandatory hain. CBI SI ke liye specifically mention hai:

  • Distant Vision — one eye 6/6, other eye 6/9
  • Near Vision — one eye 0.6, other eye 0.8

Baaki posts ke liye vision standards concerned department ke medical examination ke anusaar hote hain. Color blindness ke baare mein official notification mein specific mention nahi hai — department verify karta hai. Regular eye checkup karwa ke apni vision status confirm karo.

Height Relaxation Categories

Kuch special categories ke candidates ko height aur weight mein relaxation milti hai. Yeh relaxation specified posts ke liye hi applicable hai:

  • Garhwali candidates — 5 cm (male) / 2.5 cm (female) height relaxation
  • Assamese candidates — 5 cm (male) / 2.5 cm (female) height relaxation
  • Gorkha candidates — 5 cm (male) / 2.5 cm (female) height relaxation
  • Scheduled Tribe (ST) — 5 cm (male) / 2.5 cm (female) height relaxation
  • Female weight relaxation — 2 kg for above categories

Common Reasons for Physical Rejection

SSC CGL physical verification mein in wajahon se sabse zyada candidates reject hote hain:

  • Height measurement shortfall — especially NIA SI (170 cm) ke liye
  • Chest expansion insufficient — 81 cm hai par 5 cm expansion nahi
  • Eyesight standards meet nahi karna — corrected vision se bhi nahi
  • Female candidates mein weight 48 kg se kam hona
  • Walking test timing miss karna

How to Prepare for Physical Verification

Physical verification ke liye advance mein prepare karo. Kuch practical tips jo actually kaam aate hain:

  • Height: Subah nahane ke baad height measure karo — subah 1-2 cm zyada hoti hai
  • Chest expansion: Regular swimming, deep breathing, stretching se expand karo
  • Walking: Daily brisk walking practice karo — 15 min mein 1600m comfortable ho jaaye
  • Cycling: Regular cycling se stamina build karo — 30 min mein 8 km comfortable karo
  • Vision: Annual eye checkup karwao — deteriorating vision ke baare mein aware raho
  • Weight (female): Healthy diet maintain karo — 48 kg se kuch upar rehne ki koshish karo

Official SSC CGL Physical Rules

Kuch important official rules jo candidates ko yaad rakhne chahiye:

  • Physical test SSC conduct nahi karta — concerned department karta hai
  • Physical test qualifying nature ka hai — marks nahi milte
  • Fail hone par SSC koi alternate post nahi deta
  • Measurement shoes pehenkar nahi hoti
  • Category relaxation claim karne ke liye valid certificate present karna hoga
  • Latest official notification se hi final standards verify karo — yeh tool educational purpose ke liye hai
SSC CGL physical posts ke liye post preference bharte time apni physical eligibility confirm karke hi preference do. Wrong preference dene par aur physical fail hone par koi second chance nahi milta.
