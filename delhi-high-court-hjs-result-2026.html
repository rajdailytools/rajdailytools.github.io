How To Check & Download Delhi High Court HJS Result 2026

1Visit the official Delhi High Court website.
2Open the official Delhi Higher Judicial Service (HJS) Result 2026 notice/result.
3Open the official shortlisted candidates result PDF.
4Download or open the result PDF on your device.
5Use the PDF search option (Ctrl + F on a computer or the search icon on a mobile PDF viewer).
6Enter your Roll Number and check whether it appears in the shortlisted candidates list.
7Candidates whose Roll Numbers appear in the list are provisionally shortlisted for the Delhi Higher Judicial Service Mains Examination (Written) 2026.
8You can also download the complete marks PDF from the Important Links section for checking your marks.
