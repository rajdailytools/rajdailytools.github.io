Army Chest Test — Poori Jankari 2026

Army Chest Measurement Test Kya Hota Hai — Basics Samjho

Army recruitment process mein physical fitness test ke andar chest measurement ek mandatory component hai. Ye medical test ka hissa hai jahan doctor ya para-medical staff do cheezein measure karta hai — pehla, normal chest circumference (relaxed breathing state mein), aur doosra, expanded chest circumference (poora saas andar lete time).

Dono measurements ke beech ka fark "chest expansion" kehlata hai. Army ke liye minimum 5 cm expansion mandatory hai for most categories. Iska matlab ye nahi ki aapki chest bahut badi ho — iska matlab hai ki aapke lungs and ribcage itne flexible hain ki proper breath le sakein. Ye ek fitness indicator hai, sirf size indicator nahi.

Normal chest ka bhi ek minimum requirement hoti hai — GD aur most categories ke liye 77 cm. Ye approximately 30.3 inches hota hai. Iska matlab hai 77 cm se kam chest wale candidates ko seedha reject kiya ja sakta hai, expansion se pehle bhi.

Army Chest Test Kaise Hota Hai — Process Samjho

Recruitment rally ke medical test phase mein, candidates ko ek line mein khada kiya jaata hai. Har candidate ki baari aane par:

  • Candidate ko shirt utaarne ke liye kaha jaata hai ya upar uthane ke liye
  • Medical staff ek measuring tape nipple line ke around — horizontally, level — lagaate hain
  • Pehle normal state mein measurement li jaati hai — seedha khade ho, normal breathing, relaxed
  • Phir candidate ko poora saas lene ke liye kaha jaata hai aur us state mein measurement li jaati hai
  • Dono measurements note ki jaati hain aur expansion calculate hota hai
  • Agar normal chest required minimum se kam hai ya expansion 5 cm se kam hai, candidate ko fail kar diya jaata hai

Ye process usually 2–3 minutes ka hota hai. Koi bhi trick ya manipulation kaam nahi karta — measurement bahut straightforward hoti hai. Isliye pehle se preparation zaroori hai.

Chest Expansion Kitna Hona Chahiye — Category Wise

Army ke different trades ke liye chest requirements slightly alag ho sakti hain, lekin ek common thread hai — expansion ka minimum almost sab jagah 5 cm rahta hai. Ye sab categories mein standard requirement hai. Kuch specific points:

  • GD (General Duty): 77 cm normal chest, 5 cm expansion. Ye sabse common category hai aur yahan competition sabse zyada hoti hai.
  • Clerk/SKT: 77 cm normal, 5 cm expansion. Educational criteria zyada strict hai lekin physical requirements roughly same hain.
  • Technical: 77 cm normal, 5 cm expansion. Ye category specialized trade training ke liye hoti hai.
  • Tradesman: 76 cm normal chest acceptable hai kuch sub-categories mein, 5 cm expansion required.
  • Nursing Assistant: 77 cm normal, 5 cm expansion — medical field hone ke bawajood physical standard same hai.

Important: Ye numbers official recruitments mein vary kar sakte hain. Notification mein clearly mention hota hai — hamesha apni specific rally ka notification check karo.

Chest Expansion Kaise Improve Hoti Hai — Science Samjho

Chest expansion actually do factors pe depend karti hai — pehla, ribcage ki flexibility (intercostal muscles), aur doosra, lung capacity. Dono ko training se improve kiya ja sakta hai. Ye genetics nahi hai — ye trainable fitness parameter hai.

Intercostal muscles woh muscles hain jo ribs ke beech hoti hain. Jab ye muscles strong aur flexible hoti hain, ribcage zyada expand ho sakta hai. Deep breathing exercises directly inhi muscles pe kaam karti hain. Pushing exercises aur stretching chest ka overall size aur flexibility badhaate hain.

Studies suggest karte hain ki 4–8 weeks of consistent training se 1–3 cm chest expansion improvement possible hai for most individuals. Ye small lag sakta hai, lekin Army ke context mein ye difference between selection aur rejection ho sakta hai.

Chest Kam Hone Par Kya Kare — Recovery Plan

Agar aapki current measurement below required hai, ghabraao mat. Ye fixable hai. Ek practical 6-week plan:

  • Week 1–2: Focus on breathing. Daily 15 min deep breathing drills. Kapalbhati aur anulom-vilom bhi helpful hai. Koi intense training nahi — bas lungs ko open karo.
  • Week 3–4: Add push-ups and chest stretches. Daily 3 sets push-ups (start from what you can do, increase by 5 per week). Arm circles aur chest opener stretches add karo.
  • Week 5–6: Combine breathing + exercises. Subah breathing drills, evening chest workout. Swimming ya swim-stroke simulation agar possible ho — ye chest expansion ke liye sabse effective hai.
  • Diet: Protein-rich food chest muscle development ke liye zaruri hai. Eggs, dal, milk, paneer. Junk food se door raho — ye lung capacity ko affect karta hai.
  • Posture: Seedha khade rehna bhi chest measurement improve karta hai. Hunchback posture se chest measurement 1–2 cm kam aa sakti hai even if actual size bigger hai.

Chest Test Mein Fail Kyu Hote Hain — Common Reasons

Bahut saare candidates chest test mein fail hote hain — ye surprising hai kyunki ye apparently "easy" lagta hai. But reasons clear hain:

  • Awareness nahi thi: Running aur height pe focus kiya, chest pe nahi. Many candidates chest measurement check hi nahi karte during preparation.
  • Posture problem: Slouching ke karan measurement 1–2 cm kam aati hai. Seedha khada hona sikhna chahiye.
  • Nervous hona: Test ke time nervous hone se breathing shallow ho jaati hai aur expansion kam hota hai. Practice at home multiple times.
  • No expansion training: Body fit ho sakti hai — running acha ho — lekin ribcage flexibility specifically train nahi ki hoti. Ye alag exercise hai.
  • Wrong measurement technique: Ghar pe khud measure karte time galat position ya galat tape placement se measurements sahi nahi aati.
Frequently Asked Questions

Army chest test ke baare mein sabse common sawaal — seedha jawab

Army mein chest kitni chahiye — exact number kya hai?
+
GD aur most Army categories ke liye minimum normal chest 77 cm (approx 30.3 inches) chahiye. Tradesman mein 76 cm bhi acceptable ho sakti hai. Lekin sirf normal chest size kaafi nahi — expansion bhi minimum 5 cm honi chahiye. Matlab aapki chest relax se expand karke 5 cm ya zyada badhni chahiye.
Chest expansion kitna hona chahiye Army ke liye?
+
Minimum 5 cm expansion mandatory hai almost all Army categories ke liye. Matlab expanded chest minus normal chest = 5 cm ya zyada. Agar ye fark sirf 4 cm hai toh borderline aata hai. Agar 3 cm ya kam hai, toh clearly fail. 5 cm ya zyada hai toh pass consider hoga.
Kya chest badh sakti hai training se? Kitna time lagega?
+
Haan, bilkul badh sakti hai. Chest size aur expansion dono improve ho sakte hain focused training se. Normal chest size — push-ups, chest exercises se 4–6 weeks mein 1–3 cm increase possible hai. Chest expansion — deep breathing exercises, stretching, swimming se 3–4 weeks mein 1–2 cm improvement typical hai. Consistent training zaroori hai — ek din ki exercise se kuch nahi hota.
Army chest test kaise hota hai — process kya hai?
+
Rally ke medical phase mein, chest measurement nipple line ke around measuring tape se li jaati hai. Pehle normal relaxed state mein, phir fully expanded (saas bhar ke) state mein. Dono readings note karte hain — agar normal ≥77cm AND expansion ≥5cm, tab pass. Koi bhi ek criteria fail ho toh candidate fail. Measurement 2–3 minute ka process hai.
Army chest test mein fail hone par kya kare?
+
Fail hone par agle recruitment opportunity ka wait karo — Army rallies regularly hoti hain. Iss beech: identify karo ki fail kyu hua — normal chest size issue tha ya expansion. Phir uske according training shuru karo. 6–8 weeks ki dedicated practice se significant improvement possible hai. Ye calculator use karo regularly apni progress track karne ke liye.
Chest expansion kaise measure karein ghar par?
+
Ek measuring tape aur ek helper chahiye. Seedhe khade ho, shirt upar. Nipple level par tape horizontally wrap karo (back aur front dono mein same height). Pehle normal saans mein measurement lo. Phir poora deep saas lo aur usi position mein measurement lo. Dono ka fark = expansion. Ye calculator mein enter karo result dekhne ke liye.
Chest aur expansion — dono mein se zyada important kaun hai?
+
Dono equally important hain — dono criteria simultaneously pass karne hote hain. Lekin zyaatar candidates expansion mein fail hote hain kyunki isko specifically train nahi karte. Agar aapki normal chest 77 cm se upar hai lekin expansion sirf 4 cm hai, toh bhi fail. Isliye expansion training pe specific focus karo — especially deep breathing exercises aur chest flexibility drills.
