SSC MTS 2026 Signature Size & Upload Rules

While filling the online application for the SSC MTS & Havaldar (CBIC & CBN) Examination, 2025, candidates must upload a scanned signature that strictly matches the official specifications. Applications with a signature outside these limits are summarily rejected (this rejection ground does not apply to candidates who complete Aadhaar Based Authentication).

  • Signature must be uploaded in JPEG/JPG format only.
  • File size must be between 10 KB and 20 KB.
  • Image dimension should be about 6.0 cm (width) x 2.0 cm (height).
  • The signature should be horizontally aligned.
  • A blurred or miniature signature will be rejected summarily.

Why 708 x 236 Pixels?

The official notification specifies the signature dimension in centimetres (6.0 cm x 2.0 cm), not pixels. The application portal's own upload screen states this should be captured at a resolution of 300 DPI. Converting 6.0 cm x 2.0 cm to pixels at 300 DPI gives approximately 708 x 236 pixels — the exact target this tool uses in Official Mode, while preserving the required 3:1 width-to-height ratio.

Common Rejection Reasons & Tips

Avoid these common signature-upload mistakes

🌫️
Blurred Signature
Scan or photograph your signature in good lighting, hold the camera steady, and avoid shadows across the paper.
🔍
Miniature Signature
Don't upload a tiny cropped signature — use the crop tool here to fill the 6.0cm x 2.0cm frame properly.
📄
Wrong Format
Only JPEG/JPG is accepted. This tool always outputs a JPEG file regardless of what you upload.
⚖️
Wrong File Size
Files below 10 KB or above 20 KB may be rejected. This tool automatically compresses into the exact official range.
↔️
Not Horizontally Aligned
Rotate your source photo before uploading so the signature reads left-to-right, not tilted or vertical.
🖊️
Faint Ink
Use a dark blue or black pen and press firmly so the signature stays legible even after compression.
