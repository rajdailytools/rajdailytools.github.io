SSC GD PET — Complete Guide 2026

SSC GD Running Test Kya Hota Hai?

SSC GD CAPFs (Central Armed Police Forces) ki recruitment mein Physical Efficiency Test (PET) ek mandatory qualifying stage hai. Written exam clear karne ke baad candidates ko PET ke liye bulaya jaata hai jahan running test hota hai. Male candidates ke liye 5 km aur female candidates ke liye 1.6 km ki running hoti hai — prescribed time limit ke andar complete karni hoti hai. Agar PET fail hua toh selection process wahan khatam ho jaata hai.

Key Fact: SSC GD PET qualifying nature ka hota hai — iska matlab hai ki pass karna mandatory hai lekin marks merit mein count nahi hote (generally). Lekin agar fail ho gaye toh chahe written mein kitne bhi marks aaye ho — selection nahi hoga. Isliye PET preparation written exam se kam important nahi.

Male vs Female Running Standards

SSC GD PET mein male aur female ke standards bilkul alag hain:

  • Male Candidates: 5 km ki running 24 minutes ke andar complete karni hoti hai. Average pace approximately 4 min 48 sec per km. SC/ST candidates ke liye bhi generally same timing applicable hai.
  • Female Candidates: 1.6 km ki running 8 minutes 30 seconds ke andar complete karni hoti hai. Average pace approximately 5 min 19 sec per km. Ye distance relatively shorter hai lekin proportionally similar fitness level demand karta hai.

Male candidates ke liye 24 minute ka target comfortable lagta hai — lekin jo candidates regularly nahi daudte unke liye ye challenging hota hai. Deva bhaiya ki tarah bahut saare candidates written clear karke bhi PET mein fail ho jaate hain sirf proper preparation ki wajah se.

SSC GD PET Qualification Process

SSC GD PET ek multi-stage process hai — sirf running nahi:

  • PET (Physical Efficiency Test): Running — 5 km (male) / 1.6 km (female) within prescribed time. Qualifying nature.
  • PST (Physical Standard Test): Height, weight, chest measurement verification. Separate eligibility standards for different categories.
  • Medical Examination: Overall fitness, vision, hearing, color blindness check — after PET/PST clear.
  • Document Verification: All certificates checked — qualification, caste, domicile.

Is tool mein specifically PET running calculation ki jaati hai — aapko exactly pata chalega ki aap pass zone mein hain ya improve karna padega.

Running Performance Levels — Kya Aim Karna Chahiye

  • Excellent (Male under 20 min / Female under 7 min): Outstanding stamina. No tension — strong comfortable qualifier. Focus on maintaining fitness.
  • Good (Male 20-22 min / Female 7-7:30): Good performance. Safe qualifier with margin. Keep practicing to maintain or improve.
  • Average (Male 22-24 min / Female 7:30-8:30): Borderline zone — qualifying but no margin for error on PET day. 4-6 weeks improvement needed.
  • Weak (Male above 24 min / Female above 8:30): Fail zone. Serious training required immediately — 8-12 weeks systematic plan.

Common Mistakes Jo SSC GD Candidates Karte Hain PET Mein

  • Running practice nahi karna written exam ki tayari ke dauran — physical aur written parallel chalte hain
  • Exact timing track nahi karna — park mein daudna aur measured race mein daudna alag hota hai
  • PET se 1-2 din pehle hard training karna — body tired ho jaati hai race day pe
  • Pehli 1 km mein too fast bhaagna — baaki distance ke liye energy nahi bachti
  • Hydration ignore karna — dehydration se performance 15-20% down ho sakti hai
  • Improper footwear — running shoes vs canvas shoes significant timing difference create karte hain
Pro Tip: Is calculator mein apna current practice run time enter karo. Agar result "Average" ya "Weak" aaye, toh abhi se 8-10 weeks ka systematic running plan shuru karo. PET date announce hone ke baad normally 4-6 weeks milte hain — jo bahut kum hai serious improvement ke liye. Isliye pehle se preparation shuru karo.

Warm-Up Guide — PET Se Pehle Kya Karo

PET ke din warm-up skip mat karo — ye directly timing affect karta hai:

  • Race se 20-25 minutes pehle: Easy 400m-500m light jog — body temperature raise karo
  • Dynamic stretching: Leg swings, hip circles, high knees — 5 minutes
  • Short sprint: 2-3 quick 50m runs at 70% effort — muscle activation
  • Deep breathing exercises — calm karo, heart rate normalize karo
  • Starting position mein aao — confident posture, relaxed shoulders
  • Pace strategy mentally review karo — first km slow, last km fast

SSC GD PET running ke baare mein sab kuch — clearly answered

2. SSC GD mein running distance kitna hota hai?+
Male candidates ke liye 5 km ki running hoti hai aur female candidates ke liye 1.6 km. Dono ke qualifying times alag hain — male ke liye 24 minutes aur female ke liye 8 minutes 30 seconds (approximately, official notification se confirm karein).
3. SSC GD PET kya hota hai?+
SSC GD PET (Physical Efficiency Test) ek mandatory qualifying stage hai written exam ke baad. Isme running test hota hai jo candidates ko prescribed time limit mein complete karni hoti hai. PET fail hone par selection process khatam ho jaata hai chahe written mein kitne bhi marks aaye hon.
4. SSC GD running mein kitne marks milte hain?+
SSC GD PET generally qualifying nature ka hota hai — matlab marks directly merit mein count nahi hote (alag marks assigned nahi hote running ke liye). Lekin qualify karna mandatory hai. Final merit generally written exam marks aur physical measurement standards ke basis par banti hai. Official notification se confirm karein.
5. SSC GD male running ka qualifying time kya hai?+
Male candidates ke liye SSC GD PET running qualifying time 24 minutes (5 km ke liye) approximately hai. Is time ke andar 5 km complete karna mandatory hai. 24 minutes se zyada time lagane par candidate fail ho jaata hai. Target karo 22 minutes ya usse kam — better margin milti hai.
6. SSC GD female running ka qualifying time kya hai?+
Female candidates ke liye SSC GD PET mein 1.6 km ki running 8 minutes 30 seconds mein complete karni hoti hai. 8:30 se zyada time lagane par fail. Female candidates ke liye target hona chahiye 7:30 ya usse kam — comfortable qualifying margin ke liye.
7. SSC GD running mushkil hoti hai kya?+
Jo candidates regularly nahi daudte unke liye SSC GD running challenging ho sakti hai. But 8-10 weeks ki systematic training se most candidates qualifying time easily achieve kar lete hain. Key hai daily consistency — har din practice, timing track karna, aur gradual improvement.
8. SSC GD running ke bina selection ho sakti hai kya?+
Nahi — PET running qualification mandatory hai. Agar running fail ho gayi toh selection process wahin khatam ho jaata hai. Written exam mein kitne bhi marks hon, PET fail = selection nahi. Isliye physical preparation ko written exam jitna hi seriously lena chahiye.
9. SSC GD running fail hone par kya hota hai?+
SSC GD running fail hone par candidate us particular recruitment mein further stages ke liye disqualified ho jaata hai. Next recruitment cycle mein phir se apply karke PET attempt karne ka option hota hai. Isliye advance mein proper preparation karo — PET day pe koi second chance nahi milta.
10. SSC GD PET qualifying nature ki hoti hai kya?+
Haan, SSC GD PET generally qualifying nature ki hoti hai — pass/fail basis par. Generally merit marks mein directly add nahi hote. Lekin qualify karna mandatory hai. PST (Physical Standard Test) — height, weight, chest — bhi mandatory hai aur qualifying nature ka hota hai.
11. Male candidates ka SSC GD running distance kitna hota hai?+
Male candidates 5 km (5000 meters) ki running karte hain SSC GD PET mein. Qualifying time approximately 24 minutes hai. Is distance ke liye average pace 4:48 per km maintain karna hota hai. Regular training se 22 minutes ya usse kam mein 5 km complete karna comfortable ho jaata hai.
12. Female candidates ka SSC GD running distance kitna hota hai?+
Female candidates 1.6 km (1600 meters) ki running karti hain SSC GD PET mein. Qualifying time approximately 8 minutes 30 seconds hai. Is distance ke liye average pace 5:19 per km maintain karna hota hai. Regular practice se 7-7:30 minutes mein 1.6 km complete karna achievable hai.
13. SSC GD running speed kaise improve kare?+
Speed improve karne ke liye: (1) Daily running practice with stopwatch, (2) Interval training — fast 400m reps with rest, (3) Long slow runs — stamina build karta hai, (4) Proper breathing technique, (5) Correct pacing strategy — slow start, fast finish. Is calculator se apna current time track karo aur weekly improvement dekho.
14. SSC GD PET easy hoti hai kya?+
Jo candidates regularly 2-3 months pehle se running practice karte hain unke liye SSC GD PET manageable hoti hai. Jo candidates sirf written exam pe focus karte hain unhe PET tough lagti hai. "Easy" ya "hard" — dono relative hain — regular practice se anyone qualify kar sakta hai.
15. SSC GD physical eligibility kya hai?+
SSC GD physical eligibility mein shaamil hain: (1) PET — running qualifying test, (2) PST — height (male: 170cm general, female: 157cm general), chest measurement (male only: 80-85 cm expansion), weight proportional to height, (3) Medical examination. Sab stages qualify karne ke baad final merit list banti hai.
16. SSC GD running qualifying time kab decide hota hai?+
SSC GD running qualifying time official recruitment notification mein declare kiya jaata hai. Generally male ke liye 24 minutes (5 km) aur female ke liye 8:30 (1.6 km) hota hai. Specific recruitment ke exact timing ke liye hamesha official SSC notification check karo — ye slightly change ho sakta hai.
17. Female candidates SSC GD Running Calculator use kar sakti hain?+
Haan — ye calculator specifically male aur female dono ke liye designed hai. Gender select karne par automatically correct distance (male: 5 km, female: 1.6 km) aur qualifying time standards apply hote hain. Female candidates ke liye separate performance levels bhi set hain.
18. SSC GD running sab candidates ke liye mandatory hai?+
Haan — jo candidates PET ke liye qualify kiye hain (written exam cleared) unhe mandatory running test dena hota hai. Medical reasons ya temporary injury pe exemption nahi milti generally. Ex-servicemen ke liye relaxed standards ho sakte hain — official notification confirm karta hai.
19. SSC GD running test ki tayari kaise kare?+
SSC GD running tayari ke liye: (1) PET date se 3 months pehle running shuru karo, (2) Week 1-4: Base build — daily easy jog 2-3 km, (3) Week 5-8: Distance increase — 4-5 km, (4) Week 9-12: Timed runs — exact race simulation, (5) Last week: Taper. Is calculator mein har week apna time enter karo — progress track karo.
20. SSC GD PET selection process kya hai?+
SSC GD selection stages: (1) Written Exam (CBE) — online objective test, (2) PET — running qualifying test, (3) PST — height, weight, chest standards, (4) Medical Examination — fitness check, (5) Document Verification, (6) Final Merit List. Har stage qualifying hai — ek fail = process khatam.
21. SSC GD PET mein chest measurement hota hai kya?+
Haan — chest measurement PST (Physical Standard Test) ka hissa hai jo PET ke saath ya baad mein hota hai. Male candidates ke liye: unexpanded chest 80 cm minimum, expansion 5 cm minimum (80/85 cm). Female candidates ke liye chest measurement generally required nahi hota. Exact standards official notification se confirm karein.

Haan — ye calculator aapka running time official SSC GD PET standards se compare karta hai aur estimated qualification status dikhata hai. PASS/FAIL verdict, performance level (Excellent/Good/Average/Weak), time difference analysis, aur improvement needed — sab milta hai. Ye educational estimate hai — official result SSC decide karta hai.
23. SSC GD physical test kya hota hai?+
SSC GD physical test mein teen main stages hain: PET (running — 5km male / 1.6km female), PST (height minimum standards, weight, chest measurement for males), aur Medical Examination (overall fitness, eyesight, hearing, color vision). Teeno clear karne ke baad hi final selection hota hai.

SSC GD running criteria generally same rehta hai across states — centrally SSC decide karta hai. Lekin specific notifications mein minor variations possible hain. Hamesha apni specific recruitment notification (like SSC GD CAPFs 2024-25 notification) se exact timing standards confirm karein.
