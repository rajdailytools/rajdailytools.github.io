SSC CGL Attempts — Poori Jankari 2026

Attempts se lekar eligibility years tak — sab kuch ek jagah, simple aur clear language mein.

SSC CGL Attempt Calculator Overview

Bahut saare aspirants ek common sawaal ke saath confuse rehte hain — "SSC CGL mein kitne attempts milte hain?" Ye confusion aksar isliye hoti hai kyunki UPSC Civil Services mein fixed attempt limit hoti hai. Lekin SSC CGL ek bilkul alag exam hai. Yahan koi attempt counter nahi hota, koi attempt limit nahi hoti.

Ye tool usi confusion ko door karne ke liye banaya gaya hai. Aap apni DOB, category aur post select karein — tool batata hai ki aap abhi eligible hain ya nahi, aur kitne saal tak eligible rahenge.

Does SSC CGL Have Attempt Limit?

Nahi — bilkul nahi. SSC CGL 2026 ki official notification (published 21 May 2026, ssc.gov.in par) mein koi bhi jagah "attempt limit" ka zikr nahi hai. Notification sirf age limit, educational qualification, aur category conditions mention karti hai.

Official Confirmation: SSC CGL 2026 notification ka Para 5 (Age Limit) aur Para 8 (Educational Qualification) mein attempt limit ka koi mention nahi hai. Eligibility sirf age aur qualification par depend karti hai.

SSC CGL Unlimited Attempts Explained

SSC CGL "unlimited attempts" ka matlab yeh nahi ki aap umar bhar exam de sakte hain. Iska matlab hai ki jab tak aap age eligible hain, aap har saal SSC CGL de sakte hain — chahe aap pehle bhi apply kar chuke hon ya nahi.

  • Har saal SSC CGL notification aati hai — aap apply kar sakte hain
  • Previous years ke attempts count nahi hote
  • Form fill karna ya exam miss karna — attempt nahi count hota
  • Sirf age eligibility matter karti hai

How Age Limit Affects Attempts

Age limit hi aapke "remaining attempts" determine karti hai. SSC CGL 2026 ke reference date hai 01.08.2026. Is date par aapki age calculated hogi. Jo candidate is date par upper age limit cross kar chuke hain, wo eligible nahi honge.

For example: Agar post ka upper age limit 27 hai aur aap 01.08.2026 ko 24 saal ke hain, to aapke paas roughly 3 saal ki eligibility window hai — means 3 aur SSC CGL cycles mein apply kar sakte hain (agar har saal notification aati hai).

Category Relaxation and Attempts

Category relaxation indirectly aapke attempts badhata hai — kyunki relaxation se aapki maximum eligible age badhti hai, jisse eligibility window badi ho jaati hai.

  • OBC: +3 saal = 3 extra cycles mein apply karne ka chance
  • SC/ST: +5 saal = 5 extra cycles mein apply karne ka chance
  • PwBD: +10 saal = significantly more eligibility window
  • PwBD + SC/ST: +15 saal = maximum relaxation available

Common Attempt Myths — Busted

  • Myth: SSC CGL mein bhi UPSC jaisi attempt limit hoti hai — Fact: Bilkul galat.
  • Myth: Form fill karne se attempt count hota hai — Fact: Nahi hota.
  • Myth: Exam mein appear na hone par attempt waste hota hai — Fact: Nahi.
  • Myth: 5 attempts ke baad apply nahi kar sakte — Fact: Galat. Sirf age limit matter karti hai.
  • Myth: Tier 1 qualify na karne se ek attempt jaata hai — Fact: Attempt system hi nahi hai SSC CGL mein.
Key Takeaway: SSC CGL mein success ki key hai — qualification, preparation quality, aur age eligibility. Attempts count karne ki zarurat nahi — focus karein exam mein better performance par.

Official SSC CGL 2026 Rules

SSC CGL 2026 notification ke mutabiq:

  • Age calculated as on 01.08.2026
  • Essential qualification: Bachelor's Degree (as on 01.08.2026)
  • Notification date: 21.05.2026
  • Application window: 21.05.2026 to 22.06.2026
  • Total vacancies: Approximately 12,256
  • No mention of attempt limit anywhere in the 132-page notification

SSC CGL mein koi fixed attempt limit nahi hai. Jab tak aap age eligibility conditions fulfill karte hain, aap apply kar sakte hain. Official 2026 notification mein koi attempt limit ka zikr nahi hai.

SSC CGL attempt limit category-wise alag hai kya?+
Nahi, attempt limit kisi bhi category ke liye nahi hai. Haan, age relaxation category-wise alag hoti hai — OBC ko 3 saal, SC/ST ko 5 saal, PwBD ko 10 saal milte hain.
SSC CGL mein age limit important hai ya attempts?+
Sirf age limit important hai. Attempts ka koi role nahi hai SSC CGL eligibility mein. Age limit fulfill karte ho — apply kar sakte ho. Simple.
OBC candidates kitne attempts de sakte hain?+
OBC candidates age eligibility tak unlimited attempts de sakte hain. Unhe upper age limit mein 3 saal ki extra relaxation milti hai, jo effectively unki eligibility window badha deta hai.
SC candidates kitne attempts de sakte hain?+
SC candidates bhi age eligibility tak unlimited attempts de sakte hain. Unhe upper age limit mein 5 saal ki relaxation milti hai SSC CGL 2026 notification ke anusaar.
ST candidates kitne attempts de sakte hain?+

General category attempts limit kya hai?+
General category ke liye koi fixed attempt limit nahi hai. Jab tak aap upper age limit (post ke hisaab se 27/30/32 saal) ke andar hain, apply kar sakte hain.
Kya SSC CGL UPSC ki tarah attempt count karta hai?+
Bilkul nahi. UPSC Civil Services mein category-wise fixed attempt limit hoti hai (General: 6, OBC: 9, SC/ST: unlimited). SSC CGL mein aisa koi system nahi hai — attempts unlimited hain age limit tak.
Form bharne se attempt count hota hai?+
Officially attempt limit hi nahi hai, to form bharne se koi attempt count nahi hota. Aap chahe baar baar apply karein — eligibility sirf age par based hai.
Exam miss karne par attempt count hota hai?+
Nahi. SSC CGL mein attempt limit applicable hi nahi hai. Exam miss karna ya attend karna — kuch count nahi hota. Agali saal fir apply karein agar age eligible hai.
Tier 1 dene par attempt count hota hai?+
SSC CGL mein attempt counting system hi nahi hai. Tier 1 dein ya na dein, koi attempt count nahi hota. Eligibility next year bhi age limit ke hisaab se check hogi.
Tier 2 qualify na karne par next year apply kar sakte hain?+
Haan, bilkul. Tier 2 qualify na karna aapki eligibility affect nahi karta next year ke liye. Age eligible hain to apply kar sakte hain.
Har saal SSC CGL de sakte hain?+
Haan, age eligibility aur qualification tak har saal apply aur appear ho sakte hain. SSC CGL generally annual exam hota hai.
