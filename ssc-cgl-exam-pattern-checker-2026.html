📋 SSC CGL 2026 – Overview aur Do-Tier Exam Structure

SSC CGL yaani Staff Selection Commission Combined Graduate Level Examination 2026 mein Computer Based Examination do stages mein hogi — Tier-I aur Tier-II. Dono tiers online computer-based format mein conduct honge. Jo candidates Tier-I clear karenge, unhe Tier-II mein bulaya jaayega. Tier-II ke marks hi final merit list aur post allocation ke liye use honge.

Ek important baat — agar exam multiple shifts mein ho, to marks normalization procedure apply hoga, jo SSC ne 02-06-2025 ki notice mein clearly explain kiya hai. Matlab, tumhara actual score normalize hoga taaki different shifts ki difficulty mein fair comparison ho sake.

Jab pattern clear hota hai tabhi preparation smart hoti hai.

⏱️ Tier-I Session Structure aur Sectional Timer

Tier-I mein ek important feature hai — sectional timer of 15 minutes per subject. Iska matlab hai har subject ke liye 15 minute fix hain. Ek section khatam hote hi agle section ka timer start ho jaata hai aur purana section band ho jaata hai. Tum wapis nahi ja sakte.

  • General Intelligence & Reasoning — 15 minutes
  • General Awareness — 15 minutes
  • Quantitative Aptitude — 15 minutes
  • English Comprehension — 15 minutes
  • Total duration: 1 hour (scribe eligible candidates ko 1 hour 20 minutes)

⌨️ DEST – Data Entry Speed Test ke Baare Mein

DEST yaani Data Entry Speed Test Tier-II Paper-I ka Section-IV hai. Ye Session-II mein conduct hota hai aur iske liye 15 minute ka separate session hota hai. Exam ke din Session-I khatam hone ke baad candidates ko Session-II ke liye re-register karna hota hai.

  • Passage of approximately 2000 key depressions type karni hoti hai
  • Duration exactly 15 minutes hoti hai
  • DEST qualifying nature ka hai — marks final merit mein directly add nahi hote
  • Tax Assistant (CBIC/CBDT) aur UDC/SSA (CBN) ke liye higher DEST standards apply honge
  • PwBD-OH candidates certain conditions pe exemption le sakte hain
  • DEST fail karna final selection ko affect kar sakta hai

💻 Computer Knowledge Test (CKT) — Section-III

Section-III yaani Computer Knowledge Test mein 20 MCQ questions hote hain jo 60 marks (20×3) ke hain. Iska duration 15 minutes hota hai aur ye Session-I ke end mein conduct hota hai, Section-II ke turant baad. Ye bhi qualifying nature ka hai.

  • 20 questions × 3 marks = 60 marks total
  • Negative marking: 1 mark per wrong answer (Section-I/II/III uniform rule)
  • Sab candidates ke liye mandatory hai
  • Assistant Section Officer (CSS, MEA, AFHQ), Inspector (CGST), Postal Assistant jaise posts ke liye higher qualifying standards apply hoti hain

📈 JSO aur Statistical Investigator Grade-II — Paper-II Details

Jo candidates Junior Statistical Officer (JSO) ya Statistical Investigator Grade-II post ke liye apply karke Tier-I mein shortlist ho jaate hain, unhe Tier-II mein Paper-II bhi dena hota hai. Ye Statistics ka paper hota hai.

  • Subject: Statistics
  • Total Questions: 100 (Objective MCQ)
  • Maximum Marks: 200 (2 marks per question)
  • Duration: 2 hours
  • Negative Marking: 0.50 marks per wrong answer
  • Paper alag shift ya alag din conduct hota hai

💼 AAO – Assistant Audit Officer / Assistant Accounts Officer — Paper-III

AAO yaani Assistant Audit Officer ya Assistant Accounts Officer ke liye shortlist hue candidates ko Paper-III dena hota hai. Ye General Studies (Finance and Economics) ka paper hai.

  • Subject: General Studies (Finance and Economics)
  • Total Questions: 100 (Objective MCQ)
  • Maximum Marks: 200 (2 marks per question)
  • Duration: 2 hours
  • Negative Marking: 0.50 marks per wrong answer

📌 Important Official Exam Rules – Jo Sabko Pata Honi Chahiye

SSC ne exam conduct ke related kuch important rules clearly mention kiye hain. Inhe seriously lo:

  • Tier-II Paper-I ke sabhi sections ko separately qualify karna mandatory hai
  • Re-evaluation ya re-checking ki koi provision nahi hai kisi bhi tier mein
  • Answer key challenge ke liye ₹100 per question non-refundable fee lagti hai
  • Exam schedule change ki jaankari sirf SSC official website se milegi
  • Questions mein Metric system of weights and measures use hoga
  • Multiple shifts mein exam hone par normalization apply hoga
FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

SSC CGL 2026 exam pattern ke baare mein common sawaalon ke jawab

SSC CGL 2026 mein kitne Tier hote hain? ▼
SSC CGL 2026 mein total 2 Tier hain — Tier-I aur Tier-II. Dono Computer Based Examination (CBE) format mein online hote hain. Tier-I ek qualifying stage hai jabki Tier-II ke marks final merit list banane ke liye use hote hain.
Tier-I mein total kitne questions aur marks hain? ▼
Tier-I mein 4 subjects hain — General Intelligence & Reasoning, General Awareness, Quantitative Aptitude aur English Comprehension. Har subject mein 25 questions hote hain yaani total 100 questions. Har question 2 marks ka hai to total marks 200 hain.
Tier-I mein sectional time kya hota hai? ▼
Tier-I mein har subject ke liye 15 minute ka sectional timer fix hota hai. Ek section band hone ke baad tum wapis nahi ja sakte. Scribe-eligible candidates ko har section mein 5 extra minutes milte hain (total 15+5=20 min per section). Overall duration 1 ghanta hai — scribe walon ke liye 1 ghanta 20 minute.
Tier-I ki negative marking kitni hai? ▼
Tier-I mein har galat jawab ke liye 0.50 marks yaani half mark kaat liye jaate hain. Un-attempted questions pe koi penalty nahi hai. Isliye soach samajh ke attempt karo — agar question nahi aata to chhod do, guess mat karo blindly.
Tier-II Paper-I mein kaunse sections hain? ▼
Tier-II Paper-I mein 4 sections hain: Section-I (Mathematical Abilities + Reasoning — 60 questions, 180 marks, 1 hour), Section-II (English Language & Comprehension + General Awareness — 70 questions, 210 marks, 1 hour), Section-III (Computer Knowledge Test — 20 questions, 60 marks, 15 min) aur Section-IV (DEST — 15 min, qualifying). Ye sab Session-I aur Session-II mein conduct hote hain.
Tier-II mein negative marking kya hai? ▼
Tier-II mein negative marking is tarah hai: Paper-I ke Section-I, Section-II aur Section-III mein har galat jawab pe 1 mark katta hai. Paper-II (Statistics) aur Paper-III (Finance & Economics) mein 0.50 marks per wrong answer katta hai.
DEST kya hota hai aur iske liye kya prepare karna chahiye? ▼
DEST yaani Data Entry Speed Test ek typing skill test hai jo Tier-II Paper-I ka Section-IV hai. Isme 15 minutes mein approximately 2000 key depressions type karni hoti hain. Ye qualifying nature ka hai — sabhi posts ke liye mandatory hai. Kuch posts (Tax Assistant, UDC/SSA) ke liye higher DEST standards hain. Daily English typing practice aur accuracy improvement essential hai.
Computer Knowledge Test (CKT) qualifying hai ya merit mein count hota hai? ▼
Computer Knowledge Test (Section-III) qualifying nature ka hai. Iske marks directly final merit mein add nahi hote. Par ise seriously lena chahiye kyunki fail hone se candidature affected ho sakti hai. Certain posts (CSS/MEA Inspector, Postal Assistant etc.) ke liye higher qualifying standards apply hote hain.
JSO ke liye Tier-II mein kya extra dena hota hai? ▼
Junior Statistical Officer (JSO) ya Statistical Investigator Grade-II post ke liye Tier-I mein shortlist hone ke baad Tier-II mein Paper-II (Statistics) dena hota hai. Ye 100 questions ka, 200 marks ka paper hai jisme duration 2 ghante hai aur negative marking 0.50 per wrong answer hai. Paper alag shift ya alag din conduct hota hai.
Kya sabhi SSC CGL candidates ko DEST dena hoga? ▼
Haan, DEST sabhi SSC CGL candidates ke liye mandatory hai lekin qualifying nature ka hai. Sirf PwBD-OH candidates certain conditions par exempt ho sakte hain, aur wo bhi sirf specific posts ke liye jo DEST-prescribed category mein nahi hain (Tax Assistant CBDT ko exemption milti hai). Baki sabhi candidates ko DEST dena hi hoga.
AAO post ke liye Tier-II mein kaunsa extra paper dena hota hai? ▼
Assistant Audit Officer (AAO) ya Assistant Accounts Officer ke liye shortlist hue candidates ko Tier-II mein Paper-III dena hota hai. Ye General Studies (Finance and Economics) ka paper hai jisme 100 questions, 200 marks (2 per question) aur 2 ghante duration hoti hai. Negative marking 0.50 per wrong answer hai.
Tier-II ke sabhi sections independently qualify karne kyon zaroori hai? ▼
SSC ke official rules ke anusaar, Tier-II Paper-I ke sabhi sections ko separately qualify karna mandatory hai. Iska matlab agar tum ek section mein bahut achha karo aur doosre mein fail ho jao, tab bhi tumhe disqualify kiya ja sakta hai. Isliye balanced preparation aur har section ko seriously lena zaroori hai.
English Comprehension sirf English mein hota hai — Hindi option kyon nahi? ▼
Bilkul sahi. Tier-I mein English Comprehension section sirf English mein hota hai — Hindi option available nahi hai. Baaki teeno subjects (Reasoning, General Awareness, Quantitative Aptitude) bilingual hain yaani Hindi aur English dono mein set kiye jaate hain. Tier-II mein bhi English Language & Comprehension part sirf English mein hota hai.
Marks normalization kya hota hai SSC CGL mein? ▼
Agar SSC CGL exam multiple shifts mein conduct hoti hai, to alag-alag shifts ki difficulty alag ho sakti hai. Is fairness issue ko handle karne ke liye SSC normalization procedure apply karta hai (SSC Notice dated 02-06-2025). Ye procedure ensure karta hai ki sab candidates ke scores comparable hoon, chahe unka shift koi bhi ho. Normalized scores hi final merit ke liye use hote hain.
Tier-II Session-I aur Session-II ka total duration kya hai? ▼
Session-I mein Section-I (1 hour), Section-II (1 hour) aur Section-III Computer Knowledge Test (15 minutes) conduct hote hain. Isliye Session-I ka total duration 2 hours 15 minutes hai. Session-I ke baad break milta hai re-registration ke liye. Session-II mein sirf Section-IV yaani DEST hota hai jo exactly 15 minutes ka hai. Paper-I ka total exam time approximately 2 hours 30 minutes ho jaata hai.
