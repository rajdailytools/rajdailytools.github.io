Not every useful tool fits neatly into finance, health or exam preparation — some address the everyday personal questions people quietly search for online: Should I stay in this relationship? Is my daily routine actually productive? What does my Janam Kundli say? This hub brings together a practical, India-focused mix of such tools, each designed to give quick clarity without judgment.

💔 Relationship & Daily Routine Tools

The Should I Stay or Leave Relationship Tool offers a structured way to reflect on a relationship decision through guided questions rather than pure emotion. The Daily Routine Effectiveness Checker scores your typical day and offers practical suggestions to improve focus and productivity.

🔮 Janam Kundli & Mangal Dosha Tools

The Free Janam Kundli Online Maker generates a birth chart instantly using your date, time and place of birth — a commonly needed tool for Indian families before marriage or major life decisions. Key insight: Janam Kundli and Mangal Dosha results are based on traditional Vedic astrology calculations and are intended for informational and cultural reference — not as a substitute for a professional astrologer's detailed reading.

The Introvert Extrovert Test offers a quick, simple personality self-assessment, while the How Poor Will Inflation Make Me Calculator shows how inflation quietly erodes the real value of today's savings — a sobering but useful financial-awareness tool for long-term planning.

❓ FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
Is the Free Janam Kundli Maker accurate?
+
It generates a birth chart using standard Vedic astrology calculations based on the date, time and place of birth you enter. For detailed life predictions, we recommend consulting a professional astrologer.
What is Mangal Dosha and why do people check it?
+
Mangal Dosha (also called Manglik Dosha) is a traditional Vedic astrology condition believed to affect marriage compatibility. Many Indian families check this before finalising a match — our calculator gives a quick, traditional-method result.
Is the relationship tool a substitute for counselling?
+
No — the Should I Stay or Leave Relationship Tool is a self-reflection aid based on guided questions. For serious relationship concerns, speaking with a licensed counsellor or therapist is recommended.
Is my birth or personal data stored anywhere?
+
No — all calculations, including Kundli and Mangal Dosha results, are generated instantly and are not stored or shared with any third party.
How does the inflation calculator help with financial planning?
+
It shows how the real purchasing power of today's savings decreases over time due to inflation, helping you plan more realistic long-term savings and investment goals.
Are all these lifestyle tools free?
+
Yes, 100% free with no login, subscription, or hidden charges.
