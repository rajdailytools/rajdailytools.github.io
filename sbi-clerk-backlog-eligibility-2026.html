Nationality / Citizenship

As per the Official SBI Notification CRPD/CR/SPLDRIVE/2026-27/16

SBI Junior Associates (Customer Support & Sales) Backlog Recruitment 2026 ke liye applications sirf eligible Indian Citizens se invite ki gayi hain, jo Junior Associate (Customer Support & Sales) ke clerical cadre pad par appointment ke liye apply kar rahe hon.

The official notification does not specify any other nationality category (such as Nepal, Bhutan or Persons of Indian Origin) for this recruitment drive. Only Indian Citizens fulfilling the SC/ST/OBC backlog eligibility conditions mentioned in the notification may apply.

Age Limit (As on 01.04.2026)

As per the Official SBI Notification CRPD/CR/SPLDRIVE/2026-27/16

The age of the candidate will be calculated as on 01.04.2026. Candidates must satisfy the prescribed minimum and maximum age limits mentioned in the official SBI notification.

  • Minimum Age: 20 Years
  • Maximum Age: 28 Years
  • Candidates must have been born not earlier than 02.04.1998 and not later than 01.04.2006 (both days inclusive).

The Date of Birth mentioned in the online application and supported by valid documentary proof will be considered for determining eligibility. Candidates must ensure that all documents match the details entered in the application form.

Important Note: Always calculate your age using the official cut-off date 01.04.2026. Category-wise age relaxation is available only for eligible candidates as specified in the official SBI notification.

Trained Apprentice of SBI — Relaxation Conditions

Relaxation in upper age limit will be applicable to a trained Apprentice of SBI, whose engagement start date is on or after 01.01.2022 and who has completed the specified branch experience of one year successfully in SBI on or before the last date of registration, i.e. 27.08.2026, provided his/her conduct and performance is found satisfactory during the entire training period. Successful completion of Apprenticeship training refers to passing of the skill evaluation test (Trade Test) and obtaining a joint National Apprenticeship Certificate jointly issued by SBI-NSDC/BFSI-SSC.

Educational Qualification

As per the Official SBI Notification (Essential Academic Qualification, as on 31.12.2026)

Candidates applying for SBI Junior Associates (Customer Support & Sales) Backlog Recruitment 2026 must possess a Graduation degree in any discipline from a recognised University or any equivalent qualification recognised as such by the Central Government, as on 31.12.2026.

Candidates having an Integrated Dual Degree (IDD) certificate should ensure that the date of passing the IDD is on or before 31.12.2026.

Those who are in the final year/semester of their graduation may also apply provisionally, subject to the condition that, if provisionally selected, they will have to produce proof of having passed the graduation examination on or before 31.12.2026.

Date of passing: The date of passing the eligibility examination will be the date appearing on the mark sheet/certificate or provisional certificate issued by the University/Institute. In case the result of a particular examination is posted on the website of the University/Institute, a certificate issued by the appropriate authority of the University/Institute indicating the date on which the result was posted on the website will be taken as the date of passing.

Matriculate Ex-Servicemen: Matriculate Ex-servicemen who have obtained the Indian Army Special Certificate of Education or the corresponding certificate in the Navy or Air Force, after having completed not less than 15 years of service in the Armed Forces of the Union, are also eligible for the post. Such certificates should be dated on or before 31.12.2026.

Other Eligibility Conditions

Beyond age and qualification, the official SBI notification specifies the following additional eligibility conditions that candidates must satisfy:

  • Serving/Ex Clerical Cadre Employees Not Eligible: Candidates who are currently working in SBI in the Clerical cadre are not eligible to apply under this project. Candidates who were earlier employed in State Bank of India and resigned from the Bank while in clerical cadre are also not eligible to apply for the post.
  • Character & Antecedents: Candidates against whom there is/are adverse report regarding character & antecedents, moral turpitude etc. are not eligible to apply for the post.
  • One State/UT Only: Candidates can apply for vacancies of one State/UT only and can appear for the test only once under this recruitment project.
  • Local Language Proficiency: Candidates applying for a particular State/UT should be proficient (reading, writing, speaking and understanding) in the specified opted local language of that State/UT. The Local Language Proficiency Test is conducted after qualifying the online Main Examination but before joining the Bank. Candidates who fail to qualify this test will not be offered appointment. Candidates who produce a 10th or 12th standard mark sheet/certificate evidencing having studied the specified opted local language will not be required to undergo this test.
  • No Inter Circle/Inter State Transfer: There is no provision for Inter Circle Transfer/Inter State Transfer for Junior Associates recruited under this project. Intra-state transfer is permitted only between Delhi Circle & Lucknow Circle in Uttar Pradesh, and between Mumbai Metro Circle & Maharashtra Circle in Maharashtra.
  • PwBD and Ex-Servicemen — Parent Category: PwBD and Ex-servicemen/ Disabled ex-servicemen can apply under their respective parent category (SC/ST/OBC, as applicable). The notification clarifies that there is no horizontal reservation, and concessions applicable to PwBD will only be available to candidates.

OBC Category Conditions

Vacancies reserved for the OBC category are available only to OBC candidates belonging to the "Non-Creamy Layer". Candidates belonging to the OBC category but coming under the "Creamy Layer" are not eligible to apply. OBC category candidates should submit the OBC certificate on the format prescribed by the Government of India, having the "Non-Creamy Layer" clause issued on or after 01.04.2026 up to the date of taking up appointment, if selected.

Caste certificates issued by the Competent Authority, on the format prescribed by the Government of India, will have to be submitted by SC/ST candidates during document verification.

Application Fee

The application fee/intimation charge (Non-Refundable) for this recruitment is:

  • Nil for SC, ST, PwBD, Ex-Servicemen (XS) and Disabled Ex-Servicemen (DXS) candidates.
  • ₹750/- for OBC candidates.

Fee/intimation charges once paid will not be refunded on any account, nor can it be held in reserve for any other exam or selection.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common SBI Clerk Backlog Eligibility 2026 questions answered as per the Official Notification

What is the age limit for SBI Clerk Backlog Recruitment 2026?+
Candidates must be not below 20 years and not above 28 years as on 01.04.2026, i.e. they must have been born not earlier than 02.04.1998 and not later than 01.04.2006 (both days inclusive).
What is the minimum educational qualification for SBI Clerk Backlog 2026?+
Candidates must possess a Graduation degree in any discipline from a recognised University, or an equivalent qualification recognised as such by the Central Government, as on 31.12.2026.
Can final year graduation students apply for SBI Clerk Backlog 2026?+
Yes. Candidates in their final year/semester of graduation may apply provisionally, subject to producing proof of having passed the graduation examination on or before 31.12.2026.
Who can apply under this SBI backlog recruitment drive?+
This is a Special Recruitment Drive specifically for SC, ST and OBC backlog vacancies for the post of Junior Associate (Customer Support & Sales). Eligible Indian Citizens belonging to these categories, as per the vacancy table of the applied State/UT, can apply.
What age relaxation is available for OBC candidates?+
OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) candidates get an age relaxation of 3 years in the upper age limit.
What age relaxation is available for SC/ST candidates?+
SC/ST candidates get an age relaxation of 5 years in the upper age limit.
Are current or former SBI Clerical cadre employees eligible to apply?+
No. Candidates currently working in SBI in the Clerical cadre are not eligible to apply under this project. Candidates who were earlier employed in State Bank of India and resigned from the Bank while in clerical cadre are also not eligible.
Is local language proficiency compulsory for SBI Clerk Backlog 2026?+
Yes. Candidates applying for a particular State/UT must be proficient in reading, writing, speaking and understanding the specified opted local language of that State/UT. The Local Language Proficiency Test is conducted after qualifying the Main Examination but before joining. Candidates who produce a 10th or 12th standard mark sheet evidencing having studied the specified local language are exempt from this test.
What is the application fee for SBI Clerk Backlog 2026?+
There is no application fee/intimation charge for SC, ST, PwBD, Ex-Servicemen and Disabled Ex-Servicemen candidates. OBC candidates are required to pay Rs 750/-.
Can a candidate apply for more than one State/UT?+
No. Candidates can apply for vacancies of one State/UT only, and can appear for the test only once under this recruitment project.
Is there a provision for inter-circle or inter-state transfer?+
No, there is no provision for Inter Circle Transfer/Inter State Transfer for Junior Associates recruited under this drive. However, intra-state transfer is permitted only between Delhi Circle & Lucknow Circle in Uttar Pradesh, and between Mumbai Metro Circle & Maharashtra Circle in Maharashtra.
Are PwBD and Ex-Servicemen given a separate reservation category?+
No. As per the official notification, there is no horizontal reservation for PwBD, and PwBD/Ex-Servicemen candidates can apply only under their respective parent category (SC/ST/OBC), availing the applicable age relaxation.
What documents are required for age relaxation?+
Candidates seeking age relaxation are required to submit copies of the necessary certificate(s) at the time of joining. No change in the category of any candidate is permitted after registration of the online application.
Is cumulative age relaxation available under this recruitment?+
No. Cumulative age relaxation is not available either under the above relaxation categories or in combination with any other items, as per the official notification.
Where can I download the official SBI Clerk Backlog notification?+
The official notification (Advertisement No. CRPD/CR/SPLDRIVE/2026-27/16) can be downloaded from the SBI official careers page at sbi.bank.in/web/careers/current-openings.
