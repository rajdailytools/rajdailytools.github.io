When Should You Book?

Candidates with a readiness score of 75 or above may consider booking the exam. A score of 90+ indicates strong readiness. Below 60 — continue preparing and recheck in 1–2 weeks.

🔄

How Often Should You Check?

Re-run the checker every 1–2 weeks as your mock scores improve. This lets you track genuine progress and identify the optimal exam booking window.

Common Mistakes PTE Candidates Make

These errors are entirely avoidable with proper readiness awareness.

🚫
Booking the Exam Too Early

Sitting the exam before reaching a 65+ mock score typically results in wasted fees and a demoralising result that affects confidence for future attempts.

🚫
Ignoring Mock Test Results

Practising individual exercises without attempting full mock tests gives a false sense of confidence. Only full mocks reflect real exam performance.

🚫
Skipping Weak Skill Improvement

Practising only your strong skills while avoiding weaker ones is the most common reason candidates plateau and cannot improve their mock test scores.

🚫
Setting Unrealistic Targets

Expecting a 30+ point improvement in 2 weeks creates anxiety and leads to rushed preparation. Realistic target timelines produce better outcomes.

🚫
Not Tracking Preparation Progress

Without weekly mock test tracking, candidates cannot tell if their current approach is working or needs to change before their exam date.

🚫
Confusing Feeling Ready With Being Ready

Subjective confidence is not a reliable guide. A readiness score based on actual mock performance gives a far more accurate picture of exam preparedness.

What is the PTE Readiness Checker?
+
A tool that evaluates whether you are prepared to take the PTE Academic exam based on your mock test score, target score gap, study hours, preparation duration, and preparation level. It generates a readiness score out of 100 with a booking recommendation.
Does it guarantee my PTE score?
+
No. Actual results depend on preparation quality, consistency, and exam-day performance. The checker provides estimates based on preparation inputs and typical improvement patterns — it is a guidance tool, not a guarantee.
Can beginners use it?
+
Yes. Beginners can enter their diagnostic mock test score (taken without any preparation) to understand their starting point and how much preparation is needed before reaching exam-ready status.
Is it based on mock test performance?
+
Yes. Your latest mock test score is a primary input and carries the most weight in the readiness calculation. It is the single best real-world indicator of your current PTE preparation level.
Does it provide study recommendations?

Yes. The tool generates a personalized action plan with specific steps based on your readiness level. Recommendations vary for fully ready, almost ready, moderately ready, and not ready candidates.
Can I use it multiple times?

Yes. Re-run the checker weekly as your mock scores and preparation hours increase to track your readiness progress over time and identify the optimal exam booking window.
