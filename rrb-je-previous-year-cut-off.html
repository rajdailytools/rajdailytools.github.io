📈 RRB JE Previous Year Cut Off

RRB JE previous year cut-off marks give candidates a realistic idea of the score required to qualify for the next stage in a particular zone and category. Since cut-off marks depend on factors such as the number of vacancies, number of candidates who appeared, and the overall difficulty level of the paper, they tend to fluctuate from one cycle to the next and from zone to zone.

Comparing the zone-wise and category-wise cut-off marks over previous years helps candidates identify which RRB zones have historically had a relatively higher or lower cut-off for their category, and plan their preparation and choice of RRB accordingly.

🎯 RRB JE Cut Off Marks

The RRB JE cut-off marks are the minimum marks a candidate must score in CBT 1 to be shortlisted for CBT 2, based on their zone and category. Cut-off marks are different from minimum qualifying marks — while minimum qualifying marks are a fixed percentage prescribed by category, the cut-off is the actual marks of the last shortlisted candidate, which is generally higher than the minimum qualifying marks due to the number of applicants.

In the overall RRB JE selection process, CBT 1 is a screening test used to shortlist candidates for CBT 2 at 15 times the community-wise vacancies of each RRB. Only marks scored in CBT 2 are considered for the final merit list, along with document verification and the medical examination.

🧭 RRB JE Cut Off Factors

Several factors influence the RRB JE cut-off marks for a particular zone and category each year:

  • Number of vacancies notified for that zone and category
  • Total number of candidates who appeared for the exam
  • Overall difficulty level of the CBT 1 question paper
  • Number of candidates shortlisted for CBT 2 (15 times the vacancy, community-wise)
  • Category-wise reservation and minimum qualifying marks
  • Previous years' cut-off trend for that zone

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about RRB JE Previous Year Cut Off

What is the RRB JE Previous Year Cut Off?+
RRB JE Previous Year Cut Off refers to the minimum marks obtained by the last selected candidate in each RRB zone and category in the RRB JE CBT 1 exam, used to shortlist candidates for CBT 2.
Is the RRB JE cut off same for all zones?+
No. RRB JE cut off marks vary from zone to zone and category to category, depending on the number of vacancies, number of candidates and difficulty level of the exam in that zone.
Which category has the lowest RRB JE cut off?+
Generally, the ST and OBC categories have a comparatively lower cut off than UR, but this varies by zone as shown in the zone-wise table on this page.
Does scoring the cut off marks guarantee selection?+
No. Scoring the cut off marks only qualifies a candidate for the next stage of selection, i.e., CBT 2; final selection depends on the overall merit list and category-wise vacancies.
Where can candidates check the official RRB JE cut off?+
Candidates can check the official RRB JE cut off on the website of their respective Railway Recruitment Board (RRB).
