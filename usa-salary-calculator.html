❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about US salary calculations? We've got clear answers.

How do I calculate my salary in the USA? +
Divide your annual salary by 12 for monthly pay, by 52 for weekly pay, and by (hours/week × 52) for hourly. For after-tax calculations, subtract federal tax, FICA (7.65%), and state income tax from your gross salary. Our calculator does all of this automatically in one click.
What is after-tax (net) salary? +
After-tax salary — also called net or take-home pay — is what you actually receive in your bank account after all deductions. This includes federal income tax, FICA (Social Security + Medicare), state income tax, and any other withholdings. It is always lower than your gross salary.
How accurate is this calculator? +
This calculator uses 2026 standard federal tax brackets, the standard deduction, FICA rates, and state income tax rates for all 50 states + DC. It provides a solid estimate for most W-2 employees. It does not account for pre-tax deductions (401k, HSA), tax credits, local taxes, or self-employment tax. For exact figures, consult an accountant or IRS resources.
Does the state I live in affect my salary? +
Absolutely. Nine states — Alaska, Florida, Nevada, New Hampshire, South Dakota, Tennessee, Texas, Washington, and Wyoming — have no state income tax, meaning your take-home pay is significantly higher. States like California, New York, and New Jersey have some of the highest state tax rates (9–11%), which can cost you thousands of dollars per year.
How do I convert my annual salary to an hourly rate? +
Use this formula: Hourly Rate = Annual Salary ÷ (Weekly Hours × 52). For example, $80,000 ÷ (40 × 52) = $38.46/hour gross. Enter your weekly hours in our calculator and it will display both gross and net (after-tax) hourly rates automatically.
What is the average salary in the USA in 2026? +
The median annual household income in the United States is approximately $80,000–$82,000 as of 2026. The average individual salary is around $65,000–$70,000. However, this varies significantly by state, industry, education level, and experience. Tech workers in California average over $130,000, while service workers in southern states may average closer to $35,000–$45,000.
Can I use this tool to compare two job offers? +
Yes! That's one of the best use cases. Enter the first job's salary with its state and calculate the net income. Then do the same for the second offer. Comparing net monthly income gives you a much more realistic picture than just comparing gross salaries — especially if the jobs are in different states with very different tax rates.
