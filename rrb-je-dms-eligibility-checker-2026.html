Important Notes

⚠️Candidates awaiting the result of their final qualifying exam are NOT eligible to apply (Para 1.2 / 6.0(c)).
⚠️Only one application per candidate against this CEN is allowed. Multiple applications to different RRBs, or the same RRB, will lead to rejection of all applications and debarment (Para 1.3, 13.0(a)).
ℹ️Eligibility declared in the online application is provisional. Original documents are verified only at the Document Verification (DV) stage (Para 1.13, 13.4.3).
ℹ️Community/category, PwBD and Ex-Serviceman status can be corrected — for a fee — only during the modification window, 16-09-2026 to 25-09-2026 (Para 14.7, 17.0).
ℹ️Negative marking of 1/3rd mark per wrong answer applies in both stages of CBT (Para 13.1, 13.2).
✅Recruitment process: 1st Stage CBT → 2nd Stage CBT → Document Verification → Medical Examination (Para 13.0).

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the educational qualification for RRB JE 2026?+
A 3-year diploma or a degree in the engineering discipline mapped to your chosen post's department (see the Eligibility Criteria table above / Annexure A of CEN 04/2026), held on or before 13-09-2026.
What is the age limit for CEN No. 04/2026?+
18 to 33 years as on 01-01-2027 for UR/EWS candidates (DOB between 02-01-1994 and 01-01-2009), with wider DOB limits for OBC-NCL and SC/ST candidates as per Para 5.1.
Is age relaxation available?+
Yes — SC/ST get 5 years, OBC-NCL get 3 years, and further relaxation applies for PwBD and Ex-Servicemen candidates as per Para 5.2. Relaxations are not cumulative; only the maximum applicable relaxation is given.
Can a candidate with a different (non-matching) engineering branch apply?+
Only if the discipline is explicitly listed for the chosen post/department in Annexure A, or is a recognised combination of the listed basic streams. Unrelated disciplines are not accepted for that specific post — except DMS, which accepts a diploma in any engineering discipline.
Is work experience required for JE/DMS posts?+
No experience requirement is mentioned in CEN No. 04/2026 for JE or DMS posts.
Can PwBD candidates apply for any JE/DMS post?+
Only for posts identified suitable for their specific disability and sub-category, as listed in Annexure A. Candidates with Visual Impairment (VI) are not identified suitable for any post under this CEN.
Does this tool guarantee eligibility or selection?+
No. This is an informational checker based on the official notification. Eligibility remains provisional until verified by the RRB concerned at the time of Document Verification.
Where can I read the full official notification?+
On the official RRB website: rrb.indianrailways.gov.in. Beware of fake or unofficial websites.
