DMCA Protection Section 3 · Digital Millennium Copyright Act

RajDailyTools operates in full compliance with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) — the U.S. federal law that provides a framework for online copyright protection. We actively monitor the web for unauthorized use of our content and take all necessary steps to protect our intellectual property.

If any person, organization, or website is found copying, reproducing, or distributing our content without permission, we are entitled to take the following actions:

1
DMCA Takedown Notice: We will file a formal DMCA takedown notice directly with the hosting provider or platform hosting the infringing content, requesting immediate removal.
2
Search Engine Removal: We will submit copyright infringement complaints to search engines (Google, Bing) to have infringing URLs removed from search results under DMCA provisions.
3
Legal Complaint: Persistent or willful infringement may result in formal legal proceedings under Indian Copyright Act, 1957 and international IP laws.
4
Content Removal Request: We will approach the relevant platform, domain registrar, or internet service provider to have infringing content removed and account suspended.

Report a Copyright Violation Section 4 · Filing a DMCA Complaint

If you believe that your original copyrighted content has been used on the RajDailyTools website without your permission, we take such claims seriously and will investigate promptly. You have the right to file a DMCA counter-notice if you believe your content is being used under fair use or with permission.

To file a copyright violation report, please send us an email with the following information:

📋 Required Information for DMCA Report
Your Full Name: Legal name of the copyright owner or authorized representative
Proof of Ownership: Evidence showing you are the original creator or rights holder (publication date, original file, registration certificate, etc.)
Original Content URL: The URL or link where your original content is published
Infringing Content URL: The exact URL on our website where the violation has occurred
Contact Details: Your email address and, if applicable, phone number for follow-up
Statement: A statement that you have a good faith belief that the use is unauthorized and that the information provided is accurate

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

DMCA aur copyright ke baare mein sabse common sawaal aur unke jawaab.

DMCA kya hota hai?+
DMCA — Digital Millennium Copyright Act — ek US federal law hai jo 1998 mein pass hua tha. Ye online copyright infringement ke against protection provide karta hai. Iske under, content creators aur websites apne original content ki protection ke liye DMCA takedown notice file kar sakte hain — hosting providers ya platforms ko us infringing content ko remove karne ke liye. Globally, most platforms DMCA notices ka compliance karte hain.
Agar koi mera content copy kare to kya karu?+
Agar aapko lage ki aapka content kisi website par bina permission copy kiya gaya hai — RajDailyTools sahi ya koi bhi site par — toh aap rajdailytools@gmail.com par email bhejein with: aapka naam, original content ka URL, copy ka URL, aur ownership proof. Hum 48 ghante mein review karenge aur agar valid hai toh content remove kar denge. Aap Google ko bhi directly DMCA complaint file kar sakte hain Search Console mein.
RajDailyTools kis type ka content use karta hai?+
RajDailyTools par sab content original aur in-house created hai — tools, calculators, guides, articles sab kuch apne team ne banaya hai. Jab bhi publicly available factual information (jaise sarkari exam criteria, official government rules) reference ki jaati hai, woh public domain information hoti hai jo copyright protected nahi hoti. Hum kisi bhi third-party ke copyrighted content ko copy nahi karte.
DMCA complaint resolve hone mein kitna time lagta hai?+
RajDailyTools 48 ghante ke andar genuine DMCA complaints resolve karta hai. Process: Email receive hone ke 24 ghante mein acknowledgment → Review aur verification → Agar valid hai toh 48 ghante mein action (content remove/modify) → Confirmation email. Invalid ya incomplete complaints ke baare mein hum clarification maangenge. Genuine requests hamesha priority par handle ki jaati hain.
Kya main RajDailyTools ke tools apne website par use kar sakta hoon?+
Nahi — bina explicit written permission ke RajDailyTools ke tools, code, ya design ko kisi bhi doosre website ya application par reproduce karna copyright violation hai. Agar aap kisi specific use case ke liye permission chahte hain (educational, non-commercial etc.), toh rajdailytools@gmail.com par contact karein describing your use case. Hum case-by-case review karte hain. Tools ko link karna (redirect karna) permitted hai — copying nahi.
