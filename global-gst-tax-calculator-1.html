📖 Complete Guide

Everything About GST, VAT & Sales Tax

A comprehensive guide to understanding tax systems around the world.

🧾 What is GST (Goods and Services Tax)?

GST stands for Goods and Services Tax — a comprehensive, multi-stage, destination-based tax that is levied on every value addition. It is a single domestic indirect tax law for the entire country. Countries like India, Australia, Canada, Singapore, and New Zealand use the GST system.

In India, GST was introduced on July 1, 2017, replacing multiple indirect taxes. It operates on a dual structure — Central GST (CGST) and State GST (SGST) for intra-state transactions, and Integrated GST (IGST) for inter-state transactions.

India GST Slabs: 5% (essential goods), 12% (standard goods), 18% (most services and goods), 28% (luxury and sin goods). Some items like fresh vegetables, milk, and books are exempt from GST.

🇬🇧 What is VAT (Value Added Tax)?

VAT stands for Value Added Tax — a consumption tax placed on a product whenever value is added at each stage of the supply chain, from production to the point of sale. VAT is used by most countries in Europe, the Middle East, Africa, and parts of Asia.

The UK has a standard VAT rate of 20%, with a reduced rate of 5% for specific goods like children's car seats and home energy. Some goods are zero-rated (0%), such as most food, children's clothing, and books.

  • VAT is collected at every stage of production and distribution
  • Businesses can reclaim VAT paid on purchases (input tax credit)
  • End consumer bears the final VAT cost
  • EU countries have VAT ranging from 17% (Luxembourg) to 27% (Hungary)

🇺🇸 What is Sales Tax?

Sales Tax is a consumption tax imposed by the government on the sale of goods and services. Unlike GST and VAT, Sales Tax is only applied at the final point of sale to the consumer — it is not collected at every stage of the supply chain.

In the United States, there is no federal sales tax. Instead, each state (and sometimes city or county) sets its own sales tax rate. Rates can vary from 0% (in states like Montana, Oregon, Delaware) to over 10% when state and local taxes are combined.

USA Sales Tax Complexity: Because tax rates differ by state, county, and city, the USA has one of the most complex sales tax systems in the world. California can have combined rates above 10.25% in some areas.

🔍 Key Differences: GST vs VAT vs Sales Tax

Though they all serve the same purpose — generating government revenue from consumption — GST, VAT, and Sales Tax operate quite differently.

  • Collection Stage: GST and VAT are collected at every stage; Sales Tax only at the final sale
  • Input Credit: GST and VAT allow businesses to claim credits on taxes paid; Sales Tax does not
  • Complexity: Sales Tax (especially in the US) is more complex due to varying rates; GST is more unified
  • Cascading Effect: GST and VAT eliminate the cascading tax effect; Sales Tax can lead to tax on tax
  • Global Adoption: VAT is used by 160+ countries; GST is used by 50+ countries; Sales Tax is primarily US-based

🌏 Country-Wise Tax Systems: Real Examples

Let's understand how different countries apply their tax systems with real-world examples.

Example 1 — India (GST 18%): You buy a mobile phone worth ₹10,000. GST at 18% = ₹1,800. Final price = ₹11,800.

Example 2 — UK (VAT 20%): You buy a laptop worth £800. VAT at 20% = £160. Final price = £960.

Example 3 — Australia (GST 10%): You buy clothes worth AUD 200. GST at 10% = AUD 20. Final price = AUD 220.

Example 4 — USA (Sales Tax 8%): You buy electronics worth $500 in California. Sales tax ≈ $40. Final price ≈ $540.

❓ Frequently Asked

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Everything you need to know about GST, VAT, Sales Tax and this calculator.

🧾 What is GST and how does it work? +
GST (Goods and Services Tax) is a comprehensive indirect tax levied on the manufacture, sale, and consumption of goods and services. It replaced multiple cascading taxes and operates on a value-addition basis. In India, GST has four slabs: 5%, 12%, 18%, and 28%. Businesses collect GST from customers and deposit it with the government after claiming input tax credit on their purchases.
🇬🇧 What is VAT and which countries use it? +
VAT (Value Added Tax) is a consumption tax collected at each stage of the supply chain. It is used in 160+ countries including the UK (20%), Germany (19%), France (20%), UAE (5%), and South Africa (15%). Unlike Sales Tax, VAT is collected from producers, wholesalers, and retailers — but only the final consumer truly pays it since businesses can reclaim their VAT payments.
🔍 What is the difference between GST and Sales Tax? +
The main difference is the collection stage. GST is collected at every stage of the production and distribution chain (with input tax credits), while Sales Tax is only collected at the final point of sale to the consumer. GST eliminates the cascading tax effect (tax on tax), whereas Sales Tax can result in taxation on already-taxed goods. The USA primarily uses Sales Tax, while India, Australia, and Canada use GST.
✅ How accurate is this Global Tax Calculator? +
This calculator uses standard tax rates for each country as defined by their respective tax authorities. The mathematical formula is 100% accurate. However, actual tax rates may vary based on product category, exemptions, state/province differences, and government policy changes. For India's GST, we include all four official slabs. We recommend verifying rates with official sources for business or legal purposes.
✏️ Can I use a custom tax percentage? +
Yes! This calculator supports custom tax rates. If your country or region has a specific tax rate not auto-filled by our tool, you can enter any custom percentage in the "Custom Tax Rate" field. This overrides the default country rate and gives you full flexibility to calculate any tax scenario — whether it's a state-level US Sales Tax, a special GST category in India, or a custom VAT rate in any country.
🌍 Which countries are supported by this tool? +
This tool supports 150+ countries worldwide including all major economies: India, USA, UK, Australia, Canada, Germany, France, Japan, Singapore, UAE, New Zealand, South Africa, Brazil, Malaysia, and many more. Each country has its standard tax rate pre-filled. You can also use the custom tax % option for any country not listed or for regional rates that differ from the national standard.
➕➖ What is "Add Tax" vs "Remove Tax" mode? +
Add Tax (Exclusive): The amount you enter is the base price (before tax). The tool adds the tax on top to show the final total. Formula: Final = Amount × (1 + Tax%).

Remove Tax (Inclusive): The amount you enter already includes tax (like a price tag). The tool extracts the tax portion to show the original base price. Formula: Base = Amount ÷ (1 + Tax%).
