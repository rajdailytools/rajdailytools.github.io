SSC CGL Negative Marking Kya Hoti Hai?

SSC CGL exam mein negative marking ek aisi system hai jisme har galat answer par kuch marks deduct kiye jaate hain. Matlab ye nahi ki sirf sahi answers ke marks milte hain — balki galat answers ke marks kaat bhi liye jaate hain. Ye directly aapke final score ko affect karta hai.

Bahut saare aspirants ek common galati karte hain — wo sirf correct answers count karte hain aur socha karte hain ki "80 sahi kiye toh 160 marks" — lekin agar 20 galat bhi kiye toh Tier-1 mein actual score sirf 150 hoga, 10 marks less. Ye fark cutoff mein bahut matter karta hai.

📌 Featured Fact: SSC CGL 2026 mein Tier-I me har galat answer par 0.50 marks aur Tier-II Paper-I mein har galat answer par 1 mark deduct hota hai. Isliye accuracy aur smart attempts final score ko significantly impact karte hain.

SSC CGL Tier-I Negative Marking — Full Detail

Tier-1 SSC CGL ka pehla aur qualifying stage hai. Yahan 100 questions, 200 marks hote hain. Har sahi answer par +2 marks aur har galat answer par -0.50 marks deduct hote hain.

  • Total Questions: 100
  • Total Marks: 200
  • Correct Answer: +2 marks
  • Wrong Answer: -0.50 marks
  • Unattempted: 0 (No penalty)
  • Formula: Score = (Correct × 2) − (Wrong × 0.50)
Tier-I Example: Agar aapne 80 sahi kiye aur 15 galat kiye — Score = (80×2) − (15×0.50) = 160 − 7.5 = 152.5 marks. Sirf 15 galat answers ne 7.5 marks ghata diye!

SSC CGL Tier-II Paper-I — Zyada Risk Zone

Tier-2 Paper-I mein negative marking Tier-1 se double hai — har galat answer par -1 mark deduct hota hai. Ye paper Maths, Reasoning, English, General Awareness aur Computer sections cover karta hai.

  • Correct Answer: +3 marks
  • Wrong Answer: -1 mark
  • Formula: Score = (Correct × 3) − (Wrong × 1)
Tier-2 Example: 90 correct, 15 wrong = (90×3) − (15×1) = 270 − 15 = 255 marks. Lekin agar 30 wrong karein toh = 270 − 30 = 240 marks — 15 extra wrong answers ne 15 marks ghata diye!

Tier-II Statistics & Finance Papers

Tier-2 Paper-II (Statistics) aur Paper-III (Finance & Economics) dono mein +2 sahi, -0.50 galat ka marking scheme hai — Tier-1 jaise hi. Statistics paper Junior Statistical Officer ke liye aur Finance paper Assistant Audit Officer ke liye hota hai.

Accuracy Kitni Zaroori Hai?

Accuracy = Correct Answers ÷ Attempted Questions × 100. Ye formula batata hai ki aap jo attempt kar rahe ho usmein kitne percent sahi aa rahe ho. 90%+ accuracy excellent mani jaati hai aur selection ke liye strong indicator hai. 60% se neeche accuracy dangerous zone hai jahan negative marking score drastically gira sakti hai.

Common Negative Marking Mistakes

  • Blind guessing — sabse zyada score ghataata hai
  • Last 5 minutes mein panic attempts karna
  • Accuracy check kiye bina zyada questions attempt karna
  • Tier-2 Paper-I mein Tier-1 wali casual approach rakhna
  • Eliminate kiye bina 4-option questions attempt karna
  • Time pressure mein cafefully nahi padhna

People Also Ask

SSC CGL Tier 1 negative marking kitni hai?
Tier-1 mein har galat answer par 0.50 marks deduct hote hain. Sahi answer par +2 marks milte hain.
Tier-2 mein 1 mark deduction hota hai?
Haan, sirf Tier-2 Paper-I mein -1 per wrong. Statistics aur Finance papers mein -0.50 hai.
Safe attempts kitne hone chahiye?
Jo questions 90%+ confidence ke saath solve ho sakein unhe hi attempt karein. Forced attempts avoid karein.
Accuracy percentage kaise calculate karein?
Accuracy (%) = Correct ÷ (Correct + Wrong) × 100. Example: 72 correct, 90 attempted = 80%.
Guesswork karna sahi hai?
Educated guess (2 options eliminate karke) acceptable hai. Pure blind guess risky hai especially in Tier-2.
Negative marking cutoff ko kitna affect karti hai?
Bahut zyada. 20 extra wrong answers Tier-1 mein 10 marks aur Tier-2 mein 20 marks gha sakti hai — jo cutoff mein critical difference ho sakta hai.
Tier-1 aur Tier-2 mein fark kya hai?
Tier-1: +2/-0.50 (100 questions). Tier-2 Paper-I: +3/-1 (stricter). Tier-2 Paper-II/III: +2/-0.50.
FAQs

SSC CGL Negative Marking FAQs

Sabse common sawaal — short aur clear answers ke saath.

SSC CGL Tier-1 mein negative marking kitni hai?
+
SSC CGL Tier-1 mein har galat answer par 0.50 marks deduct hote hain. Sahi answer par +2 marks milte hain. Unattempted questions par koi penalty nahi hai.
SSC CGL Tier-2 Paper-I mein negative marking kya hai?
+
Tier-2 Paper-I mein negative marking -1 mark per wrong answer hai — Tier-1 se double. Sahi answer par +3 marks milte hain. Ye paper sabse risky hai guessing ke liye.
Tier-2 Statistics paper mein negative marking kya hai?
+
Tier-2 Paper-II (Statistics) mein -0.50 per wrong answer aur +2 per correct answer hai. Ye Junior Statistical Officer posts ke liye applicable hai.
Finance & Economics paper mein negative marking kya hai?
+
Tier-2 Paper-III (Finance & Economics) mein bhi -0.50 per wrong aur +2 per correct hai. Ye Assistant Audit Officer aur Assistant Accounts Officer ke liye hai.
Unattempted questions par negative marking lagti hai kya?
+
Bilkul nahi. SSC CGL mein unattempted questions par zero penalty hai. Agar kisi question ka confident answer nahi hai toh skip karna smart strategy hai.
SSC CGL Tier-1 ka complete formula kya hai?
+
Final Score = (Correct Answers × 2) − (Wrong Answers × 0.50). Total 100 questions, maximum 200 marks. Example: 85 correct, 8 wrong = 170 − 4 = 166 marks.
Accuracy percentage kaise calculate hoti hai?
+
Accuracy (%) = Correct Answers ÷ Attempted Questions × 100. Agar 72 correct aur 90 total attempted (correct + wrong) hain = 72/90 × 100 = 80% accuracy.
80% accuracy good mani jaati hai SSC CGL mein?
+
Haan, 80–89% accuracy "Very Good" category mein aati hai. Iska matlab hai aap sahi direction mein hain. Ideally 90%+ ki taraf aim karein kyunki ye selection ke liye excellent indicator hai.
Blind guessing karna sahi hai SSC CGL mein?
+
Nahi. Blind guessing bahut risky hai. Random guess ki accuracy sirf 25% hoti hai 4-option MCQ mein. Tier-2 Paper-I mein ek blind guess se 1+3 = effective 4 marks ka nuksaan ho sakta hai.
10 galat answers se Tier-1 score kitna ghata hai?
+
Tier-1 mein 10 wrong answers = 10 × 0.50 = 5 marks deduction. Agar 80 correct the toh 160 − 5 = 155. 20 wrong hote toh 160 − 10 = 150. Difference gradually badhta hai.
Tier-2 Paper-I mein 10 wrong answers ka impact kya hai?
+
Tier-2 Paper-I mein 10 wrong = 10 × 1 = 10 marks deduction — Tier-1 se double impact. Plus un 10 questions ka +3 bhi nahi mila, total loss 10 correct answers ke barabar ho sakta hai.
SSC CGL mein safe attempt strategy kya hai?
+
90%+ accuracy pe freely attempt karein. 80-90% mein balance maintain karein. 70-80% mein accuracy improve focus karein. 70% se neeche mein random guessing strictly avoid karein.
Negative marking final score ke saath selection ko kaise affect karti hai?
+
Normalized score aur merit list mein position determine karte waqt har mark count karta hai. Excessive negative marking final score ghata ke cutoff miss karwa sakti hai even if correct attempts kaafi hain.
Mock test mein negative marking calculator kaab use karein?
+
Har mock test ke immediately baad use karein. Ye aapko actual score, accuracy aur risk level dikhata hai taaki aap galti analyse karke strategy improve kar sakein next test ke liye.
Kya educated guess karna safe hai?
+
Educated guess — jab 2 options confidently eliminate kar sako — acceptable hai. 2 remaining options mein 50% chance hai jo Tier-1 mein +2 expected return vs -0.50 risk se favorable hai mathematically.
SSC CGL ke liye ideal accuracy target kya hona chahiye?
+
Preparation mein 90%+ accuracy target rakhein. Real exam mein 85%+ maintain karna strong performance mana jaata hai. 80% se neeche girne par strategy review zaroor karein.
Tier-2 Paper-I mein minimum safe accuracy kya honi chahiye?
+
Tier-2 Paper-I mein -1 negative marking ki wajah se 85%+ accuracy recommended hai. 75% se neeche jana bahut risky hai. Har wrong answer bahut costly hai is paper mein.
Over attempting kya hota hai aur ye kyun galat hai?
+
Over attempting matlab hai accuracy drop kiye bina attempt count badhana. Agar aap 75 confident attempts ke saath 90 attempt karte hain toh extra 15 attempts mein accuracy low hogi, jisse net score ghatta hai.
SSC CGL Tier-1 mein total negative marks ka limit kya hai?
+
No specific limit. Maximum nuksaan 100 questions × 0.50 = 50 marks hai agar sab galat karo. Practically 10-15 galat answers pe bhi attention dena chahiye.
Kya SSC CGL 2026 mein negative marking rules change hue hain?
+
Official SSC CGL 2026 notification ke anusaar Tier-1: -0.50, Tier-2 Paper-I: -1, Tier-2 Paper-II & III: -0.50. Hamesha official SSC website se latest notification verify karein.
