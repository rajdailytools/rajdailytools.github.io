    Introduction

    The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) invites online applications every year for various Group 'B' (Non-Gazetted) and Group 'C' posts spread across different Ministries/Departments/Organizations of the Government of India through its "Selection Posts" examination cycle. The current cycle — Advertisement No. Phase-XIV/2026/Selection Posts — was uploaded on the Commission's website on 13 April 2026. Before applying, candidates must ensure that they meet all the eligibility conditions specified in the official notification for the specific post-category they wish to apply for.

    Unlike single-post exams, Selection Posts cover a large number of individual posts, each with its own Essential Qualification (EQ), age-limit, physical/medical standards and other conditions listed against that post's code in Annexure-III of the notice. This page summarizes the eligibility framework that applies across all posts — Nationality, Age Limit bands, Age Relaxation, and the three Educational Qualification levels — exactly as stated in the official Notice of Examination (File No. HQ-RHQS015/1/2026/C-4). Where a rule is specific to an individual post (such as its exact EQ, physical standard, or experience requirement), the notice directs candidates to check the post-details in Annexure-III, and this page states that clearly rather than guessing.

    Candidates who do not satisfy any of the applicable conditions may have their candidature cancelled at the Scrutiny Stage or at Document Verification, even after qualifying in the Computer Based Examination. Checking eligibility carefully — against the exact post code you intend to apply for — is therefore essential before submitting the application.

    Nationality Criteria

    As per the official SSC notification (Para 5)

    A candidate must be one of the following:

    • A citizen of India
    • A subject of Nepal
    • A subject of Bhutan
    • A Tibetan Refugee who came over to India before 1 January 1962 with the intention of permanently settling in India
    • A person of Indian origin who has migrated from Pakistan, Burma (Myanmar), Sri Lanka, Kenya, Uganda, the United Republic of Tanzania (formerly Tanganyika and Zanzibar), Zambia, Malawi, Zaire, Ethiopia or Vietnam with the intention of permanently settling in India

    A candidate belonging to the Nepal, Bhutan, Tibetan Refugee, or Person of Indian Origin category must be in possession of a Certificate of Eligibility issued by the Government of India. A candidate for whom this certificate is necessary may be admitted to the examination, but the offer of appointment will only be given after the eligibility certificate has been issued.

    Educational Qualification

    As per the official SSC notification (Para 10, crucial date: closing date 04-05-2026)

    Selection Posts cover a wide range of post categories, so the Essential Qualification (EQ) is not the same for every post — it is fixed post-wise in Annexure-III of the notice. However, the Computer Based Examination itself is organized around three broad qualification levels:

    • Matriculation Level — for posts requiring 10th Standard pass as the minimum EQ.
    • Higher Secondary (10+2) Level — for posts requiring 12th Standard pass as the minimum EQ.
    • Graduation & Above Level — for posts requiring a Bachelor's Degree (or higher) as the minimum EQ.

    The Commission conducts three separate Computer Based Examinations, one for each of these levels (Para 15.1). Candidates must identify the exact EQ prescribed for their target post code in Annexure-III before applying — Information not specified in the official notification for a generic, post-independent qualification requirement, since it genuinely varies by post.

    The Crucial date for possession of Essential Qualifications (EQs)/Experience is the closing date, i.e. 04-05-2026; the crucial date for age-limit is 01-01-2026. Equivalence of qualifications will not be allowed unless specifically stipulated in the EQ for that post. Degrees/diplomas/certificates awarded through Open and Distance Learning mode are recognized only if approved by the Distance Education Bureau/University Grants Commission for the relevant period; programmes in Engineering, Medicine, Dental, Nursing, Pharmacy, Architecture and Physiotherapy are not permitted under this mode (except certain IGNOU B.Tech. degrees for students enrolled up to 2009-10).

    At Document Verification, candidates must produce original Mark sheets/Provisional Certificate/Degree as proof of having acquired the minimum EQ on or before the cut-off date. Mere processing of the result by the University/Institute by the cut-off date does not fulfil the EQ requirement — the result must have been actually declared by that date.

    Physical Standards

    The official notification advises candidates to go through the requirements of "physical and medical standards, etc." of the Post-category they wish to apply for and satisfy themselves that they are eligible (Para 16.2 / 20(e)). Information not specified in the official notification as a single, common physical-standard figure applicable to all Selection Posts — any post-specific physical standard (such as for posts in Police/Defence-linked departments) is listed against that individual post code in Annexure-III.

    Medical Standards

    Information not specified in the official notification as a common medical standard for all posts. Candidates are advised to check the post-details in Annexure-III for any medical-fitness requirement specific to their target post, and to satisfy themselves of eligibility before applying.

    Experience Requirement

    Where a particular post requires experience in a specific field/discipline as an Essential Qualification, this is indicated against that post code in Annexure-III (Para 10.5). Where prescribed, the applicant must fill the relevant column of the online application form and upload self-attested copies of the relevant experience certificates from the Competent Authority, when called for by the Commission/User Department after the Computer Based Examination or at Document Verification — failing which the candidature will be rejected.

    Such experience must be acquired after completing the prescribed educational qualification for that post (Para 10.4). Internship, training, and research experience gained while acquiring the educational qualification will not be counted as experience, even if it was technically completed after finishing the qualification.

    Number of Attempts

    Information not specified in the official notification. The Notice of Examination does not prescribe a limit on the number of attempts a candidate may make for the Selection Posts examination, provided the candidate meets the age-limit and other eligibility conditions for the post-category applied for on each occasion. Note, however, that only one online application is allowed per candidate for each category of post during the normal application period (Para 21.1(j)) — submitting more than one application for the same post-category leads to rejection of all such applications.

    Reservation Rules

    Reservation for Scheduled Castes (SC), Scheduled Tribes (ST), Other Backward Classes (OBC), Economically Weaker Sections (EWS), Ex-Servicemen (ESM) and Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD) is determined and communicated by the Indenting Ministries/Departments/Organizations for each individual post, as per extant Government Orders, and is not published as a single common percentage table in the main body of this notice. Candidates seeking reservation benefits must ensure they are entitled to such reservation as per the eligibility prescribed and must be in possession of the certificate in the prescribed format (Para 20(f)).

    Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD) are eligible for reservation, fee concession and age relaxation only if they have a benchmark disability; the suitability of each post for PwBD categories and the nature of admissible disabilities is indicated against that post in Annexure-III (Para 4). PwBD categories to be selected in the application form are: VH (Blindness and low vision), HH (Deaf and hard of hearing), OH (Locomotor disability, cerebral palsy, leprosy cured, dwarfism, acid attack victims, muscular dystrophy, spinal deformity/injury), Others (Autism, intellectual disability, specific learning disability, mental illness) and Others (multiple) for multiple disabilities.

    Application Fee

    Fee payable: ₹100/- (Rupees One Hundred only) per category of post. Fee can be paid online only through BHIM UPI, Net Banking, or Visa/Master/Maestro/RuPay Credit or Debit Card. Women candidates and candidates belonging to Scheduled Castes (SC), Scheduled Tribes (ST), Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD) and Ex-Servicemen (ESM) eligible for reservation are exempted from payment of fee (Para 12). Fee once paid is not refunded under any circumstances, nor adjusted against any other examination.

    Important Notes / Official Rules

    • Date of Birth entered in the application form must exactly match the Matriculation/Secondary certificate; mismatch is a ground for candidature rejection (Para 6.3, Note-3).
    • Crucial date for age-limit is 01-01-2026; crucial date for possession of Essential Qualifications/Experience is the closing date, 04-05-2026 (Para 10.2).
    • Reserved category, PwBD, ESM or age-relaxation claims must be supported by a valid certificate in the prescribed format at the time asked for by the Commission/User Department or at Document Verification.
    • Only one online application is allowed per candidate for each category of post; multiple applications for the same post-category lead to rejection of all such applications and cancellation of candidature (Para 21.1(j)).
    • Candidature is accepted only "provisionally" at the time of examination; eligibility is finally verified by the concerned User Department at the Scrutiny Stage and Document Verification (Para 16.2).
    • Window for Application Form Correction is available from 11-05-2026 to 13-05-2026 (up to 2300 hrs), with correction charges of ₹200 for the first correction and ₹500 for the second (Para 13.5).
    • Age relaxation for Central Government Civilian Employees is not applicable for Group 'B' posts (Para 3.5.3).

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Applying against the wrong age-band for a post — always confirm the exact age-limit shown in Annexure-III for your target post code before relying on any generic figure.
    • Entering a Date of Birth that does not exactly match the Matriculation/Secondary certificate.
    • Assuming a single "12th pass" or "Graduate" qualification applies to all posts — the EQ genuinely differs post to post.
    • Submitting more than one application for the same category of post — this leads to rejection of all such applications.
    • Claiming reserved-category or age-relaxation benefits without keeping the certificate ready in the prescribed format.
    • Missing the Application Form Correction window (11-05-2026 to 13-05-2026) if a mistake is found after submission.
    • Assuming the qualifying result will "process in time" — the result must be actually declared by the cut-off date (04-05-2026), not merely under process.

    Important Tips

    Points every Selection Post aspirant should keep in mind

    📖
    Check Annexure-III for Your Post
    Since age-limit, EQ and physical/medical standards vary post-wise, always open Annexure-III and confirm the exact conditions for your target post code.
    🎂
    DOB Must Match Certificate
    Always enter your Date of Birth exactly as recorded in your Matriculation Certificate to avoid rejection.
    📜
    Keep Certificates Ready
    Keep your category, PwBD or ESM certificates ready in the prescribed format for Document Verification.
    🧮
    Two Crucial Dates, Not One
    Remember age is checked as on 01-01-2026, but Essential Qualification/Experience is checked as on the closing date, 04-05-2026 — the two are not the same.
    📝
    One Application Per Post-Category
    Apply only once for each category of post; multiple applications for the same post-category will all be rejected.
    🕒
    Use the Correction Window Wisely
    You get two chances to correct your form (11–13 May 2026) — check every field carefully before the final submission to avoid correction charges.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Answers based on the official SSC Selection Post Phase-XIV/2026 notification

    SSC Selection Post Phase XIV ke liye minimum qualification kya hai?+
    Qualification post ke hisaab se alag hoti hai — Matriculation Level, Higher Secondary (10+2) Level, ya Graduation & Above Level. Exact requirement apne target post code me Annexure-III se check karein.
    SSC Selection Post ke liye age limit kitni hai?+
    Age limit post ke hisaab se alag hoti hai — notification me 11 alag age-band diye gaye hain (18–25, 18–27, 18–28, 18–30, 18–35, 20–25, 20–30, 21–27, 21–28, 22–28 aur 23–28 years), calculated as on 01-01-2026.
    Crucial date age ke liye aur qualification ke liye same hai kya?+
    Nahi. Age ka crucial date 01-01-2026 hai, jabki Essential Qualification/Experience ka crucial date closing date, 04-05-2026 hai.
    SC/ST candidates ko kitni age relaxation milti hai?+
    Notification ke anusaar SC/ST candidates ko upper age limit mein 5 years ki relaxation milti hai.
    OBC candidates ko age relaxation milta hai?+
    Haan, official notification ke anusaar OBC candidates ko 3 years ki relaxation di jaati hai.
    PwBD candidates ke liye kitna relaxation hai?+
    Category ke hisaab se: PwBD (Unreserved) 10 years, PwBD (OBC) 13 years, PwBD (SC/ST) 15 years relaxation notification me di gayi hai.
    Ex-Servicemen ko age relaxation milta hai?+
    Haan, notification ke anusaar military service rendered ki deduction ke baad 3 years ki relaxation prescribed hai.
    Nationality ke liye kaun eligible hai?+
    Indian citizen, Nepal ke subject, Bhutan ke subject, 01-01-1962 se pehle aaye Tibetan refugees aur notification mein diye gaye deshon se aane wale eligible Person of Indian Origin (Certificate of Eligibility ke saath).
    Physical ya medical standards kya hain?+
    Ye common tor par notification me nahi diye gaye — post-specific hain aur Annexure-III me us particular post code ke against mention kiye jaate hain.
    Kya experience zaroori hai?+
    Sirf un posts ke liye jahan Annexure-III me experience Essential Qualification ke roop me specify kiya gaya ho; experience qualification poori karne ke baad hi count hoti hai.
    Number of attempts par koi limit hai?+
    Notification me attempts ki koi limit specify nahi ki gayi hai. Lekin ek category of post ke liye sirf ek hi online application allowed hai.
    Application fee kitni hai aur kaun exempt hai?+
    Fee ₹100 hai. Women candidates aur SC, ST, PwBD, ESM category ke eligible candidates fee se exempt hain.
    DOB kis document se verify hoti hai?+
    Matriculation/Secondary certificate me recorded Date of Birth ke aadhar par hi age determine ki jaati hai. Baad me DOB change ki request accept nahi ki jaati.
    Kya main ek se zyada application submit kar sakta hoon?+
    Ek hi category of post ke liye sirf ek online application allowed hai. Multiple applications detect hone par sabhi reject ho jaati hain aur candidature cancel ho sakti hai.
    Application form me correction ka kya process hai?+
    Closing date ke baad Commission 11-05-2026 se 13-05-2026 tak 'Window for Application Form Correction' deta hai, jisme ₹200 (pehli baar) aur ₹500 (dusri baar) correction charges lagte hain.
    Central Government employees ko kitni age relaxation milti hai?+
    3+ years regular service wale Central Government civilian employees ko upto 40 years, aur SC/ST category me upto 45 years tak ki relaxation di jaati hai — lekin Group 'B' posts ke liye ye relaxation applicable nahi hai.
    Widows/Divorced Women ko age relaxation milta hai?+
    Haan, widows/divorced/judicially separated women (not remarried) ko upto 35 years, aur SC/ST category me upto 40 years tak ki relaxation di jaati hai.
