2. Bank Account Requirement / बैंक खाता आवश्यकताएँ

To receive financial assistance under the Delhi Lakshmi Yojana (DLY), the applicant must have an active bank account in any of the following banks participating in the Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC) ecosystem.

दिल्ली लक्ष्मी योजना (DLY) के अंतर्गत वित्तीय सहायता प्राप्त करने के लिए आवेदक का सेंट्रल बैंक डिजिटल करेंसी (CBDC) प्रणाली से जुड़े निम्नलिखित बैंकों में से किसी एक में सक्रिय बैंक खाता होना आवश्यक है।

How To Check & Apply Delhi Lakshmi Yojana

1

Visit the official Delhi Government / Delhi Lakshmi Yojana website.

2

Open the Delhi Lakshmi Yojana application or scheme section.

3

Read the eligibility conditions and required document details carefully.

4

Keep the required documents ready, including the School Leaving Certificate, Class 10 Certificate, Driving Licence, PAN Card, Aadhaar Card and Voter ID Card, as applicable.

5

Make sure you have an active bank account with one of the participating banks listed under the scheme.

6

Complete the application process by entering the required information correctly.

7

Upload or submit the required documents as instructed on the official portal.

8

Submit the application and keep the acknowledgement or application details safely for future reference.
