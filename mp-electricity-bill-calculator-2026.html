📖 Guide

MP Bijli Bill Kaise Calculate Hota Hai?

Poori jaankari — slab system se lekar tips tak — ek jagah pe.

Madhya Pradesh Mein Bijli Bill Kaise Banta Hai?

Madhya Pradesh mein bijli bill sirf "Units × Rate" nahi hota. Aapka final bill kai cheezein jodhkar banta hai: energy charges (slabs ke hisaab se), fixed charges (chahe aap ek unit bhi use karo), fuel adjustment charge (FAC), electricity duty (9%), aur kabhi-kabhi meter rent bhi. Isliye aksar log hisaab galat lagate hain.

Slab System Kya Hota Hai? (Simple Hindi Mein)

MP mein MPPKVVCL "slab system" use karta hai — matlab alag-alag ranges mein alag rate lagta hai. Jaise:

  • Pehle 50 units: ₹3.00/unit — yeh sabse sasta hota hai
  • 51 se 150 units: ₹4.50/unit — normal ghar ke liye
  • 151 se 300 units: ₹6.00/unit — medium usage
  • 300 units se zyada: ₹7.00/unit — sab se mahanga slab

Important: Slab "cumulative" hota hai, "flat" nahi. Matlab agar aapne 200 units use kiye, to aap ₹6/unit sabke liye nahi doge — sirf 151–200 wale units ke liye. Pehle 50 units ₹3/unit pe aur agle 100 units ₹4.50/unit pe hi rahenge.

MP Mein Bijli Bill Kam Kaise Karein?

  • 300 units limit dhyan mein rakho — jaise hi 300 units se upar jaoge, rate ₹6 se ₹7/unit ho jaata hai. AC, geyser control karo.
  • 5-star BEE rated AC lagao — 3-star ke mukable 20–30% bijli bachata hai.
  • LED bulbs use karo — ek 60W bulb ki jagah 9W LED lagao — 85% bachat.
  • Geyser timer use karo — geyser sabse zyada bijli khata hai, usse sirf zarurat ke waqt chalao.
  • Refrigerator sun mein mat rakho — compressor zyada chalta hai aur bill badhta hai.
  • PM Surya Ghar Yojana — rooftop solar lagao aur 300 units/month FREE pao, MP mein subsidy bhi milti hai.

MP Bijli Bill Online Kaise Bharein?

  • mpseva.gov.in portal par jaake bill bharo — Net Banking, UPI, Debit/Credit Card sab accepted hain.
  • MPEZ / MPPKVVCL official app download karo — meter reading dene ki suvidha bhi milti hai.
  • PhonePe, GPay, Paytm se bhi MPPKVVCL ka bill bhara ja sakta hai — CA Number dalo aur pay karo.
  • 1912 helpline par call karo kisi bhi billing complaint ke liye — yeh toll-free hai.

Bijli Bill Mein "Fixed Charges" Kya Hota Hai?

Fixed charges woh monthly amount hai jo aapko chahe ek unit bijli use karo ya zero, dena hi padega. Yeh aapke connected load (kW) ke hisaab se hota hai. MP mein domestic consumers ke liye yeh ₹70 se ₹130/month ke beech hota hai. Agar aap ek mahine ghar se bahar gaye aur zero units use ki, tab bhi yeh charge lagega.

❓ FAQs

Aksar Pooche Jane Waale Sawal

MP mein bijli ka rate kya hai 2026 mein?+
MP mein domestic tariff 2026 mein: 0–50 units: ₹3.00/unit | 51–150 units: ₹4.50/unit | 151–300 units: ₹6.00/unit | 300+ units: ₹7.00/unit. Iske upar fixed charges + electricity duty (9%) + fuel surcharge alag se lagta hai.
MPPKVVCL ka bill online kaise check karein?+
MPPKVVCL ka bill check karne ke liye mpseva.gov.in ya mpez.co.in par jaayein. Wahan apna CA Number (Consumer Account Number) enter karein — yeh aapke purane bill par likha hota hai. Aap PhonePe, GPay aur Paytm se bhi bill check aur pay kar sakte hain.
Fixed charge kya hota hai aur yeh kyun lagta hai?+
Fixed charge ek monthly minimum amount hai jo aapko chahe koi bijli use na karo, phir bhi dena padta hai. Yeh grid connection aur infrastructure maintain karne ke liye hota hai. MP mein domestic consumers ke liye yeh connected load ke hisaab se ₹70 se ₹130/month ke beech hota hai.
Kya yeh calculator accurate hai?+
Haan, yeh calculator MPPKVVCL ke 2026 ke officially published tariff rates par based hai. Yeh domestic, commercial aur agriculture — teeno ke liye accurate estimate deta hai. Lekin aapka exact bill thoda alag ho sakta hai kyunki fuel surcharge quarterly change ho sakta hai. Official bill ke liye mpseva portal check karein.
Kya MP mein BPL consumers ko koi subsidy milti hai?+
Haan, MP sarkar BPL (Below Poverty Line) consumers ko bijli subsidy deti hai. 0–30 units per month tak BPL consumers ke liye rate aur bhi kam hota hai. Iske liye aapko BPL ration card aur local electricity office mein registration karwana hoga. Yeh scheme state government revise karti rehti hai — latest jankari ke liye local DISCOM office contact karein.
300 units se upar jaane par bill itna kyu badh jaata hai?+
MP ke slab system mein 300 units ke baad rate ₹6.00 se ₹7.00/unit ho jaata hai. Matlab sirf 1 unit zyada hone par aapko us unit ke liye ₹1 extra pay karna padta hai. Agar aap 400 units use karte hain to woh 100 extra units (301–400) ₹7/unit ke hisaab se charge honge. Isliye 300 units limit dhyan mein rakhna bahut zaroori hai.
MP mein bijli complaint kahan karein?+
Bijli complaint ke liye: 📞 Toll Free: 1912 (24/7 available) | 🌐 mpseva.gov.in par online complaint darz karein | 📱 MPEZ official app se bhi complaint de sakte hain. Billing galti ho to nearest DISCOM office jaayein aur bill ki photo lekar jaayein.
Agriculture connection ke liye MP mein rate kya hai?+
MP mein agriculture (sinchai) connections ke liye rate bahut kam hota hai — ₹1.50/unit se ₹3.50/unit tak (pump ki capacity ke hisaab se). Yeh rate regular domestic rate se kaafi kam hai kyunki sarkar kisan ko bijli par subsidy deti hai. Pump connection ke liye apne local DISCOM office mein register karwana padta hai.
