How These Tools Work
⚙️ Behind the scenes
1
The Eligibility Checker and Age Checker run simple date-math and rule comparisons against the age, nationality, qualification, computer-literacy and language conditions from the official notification — no image or file is involved.
2
The Photo and Signature Resizers read your image using the browser's File API, draw it onto an HTML5 Canvas, and auto-crop it from the centre to the required aspect ratio.
3
The canvas is resized to the exact target pixel dimensions (200×230 for photo, 140×60 for signature).
4
The tool repeatedly re-compresses the image at different JPEG quality levels and picks the closest result to the required file-size range, showing you the final size and a clear status.
5
If the required range genuinely cannot be reached without unacceptable quality loss, the tool tells you honestly instead of marking it as compliant.

🔒 Privacy & Data Safety

Your uploaded photo and signature are processed locally in your browser. RajDailyTools does not upload or store these files on its server, does not send them to any external image-processing API, and does not save them in a database.

  • All cropping, resizing and compression happens using your browser's Canvas API.
  • Your image never leaves your device during processing.
  • Nothing is written to localStorage or sessionStorage.
  • The processed file is generated and offered to you as a direct download.

Your image is not stored by RajDailyTools. Always verify the final file against the latest official IBPS notification before uploading it to the official application portal.
