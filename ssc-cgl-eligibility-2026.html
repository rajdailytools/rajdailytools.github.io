📌 Quick Answer — SSC CGL 2026 Eligibility

People Also Ask — Common Doubts Cleared
Kya 43% wale SSC CGL de sakte hain?
Haan — most SSC CGL posts ke liye koi minimum percentage nahi hai. Sirf pass degree hona enough hai. Low percentage se eligibility issue nahi hoti.
Kya diploma holders SSC CGL ke liye eligible hain?
Nahi — sirf diploma se SSC CGL nahi de sakte. Bachelor's Degree compulsory hai. Diploma ke baad graduation complete karo phir apply karo.
Kya final year students SSC CGL bhar sakte hain?
Haan — final year students apply kar sakte hain lekin 01-08-2026 tak degree result declared hona mandatory hai. Sirf appearing enough nahi.
Kya spectacles wale SSC CGL de sakte hain?
Haan — desk jobs ke liye bilkul eligible hain. Inspector/SI posts ke liye vision 6/6+6/9 hona chahiye (glasses ke saath bhi acceptable hai).

Kya height issue se SSC CGL reject hota hai?
Sirf kuch specific inspector/SI posts ke liye height required hai — desk jobs jaise ASO, Auditor, Tax Assistant mein koi height requirement nahi hoti.
⚠️ Final Year Cutoff Date Alert
Final year students ka degree result 01-08-2026 tak declared hona chahiye — agar result baad mein aata hai toh candidature cancel ho sakti hai document verification mein. "Processing by university" enough nahi — officially declared result chahiye.
❌ Post Preference — One-way Decision
SSC notification mein clearly hai — agar aapne higher preference post choose ki aur selection hua, lekin physical/medical standard fail kiya, toh candidature reject hogi. Post nahi badli jayegi. Isliye physical standards carefully verify karo pehle.
🔵 OBC Creamy Layer — Careful
OBC candidates ko closing date (22-06-2026) tak non-creamy layer ensure karna hoga. OBC-NCL certificate required hoga document verification mein. Creamy layer mein aane wale OBC candidates reservation benefit nahi le sakte.
✅ Distance Learning Degree — Valid
Open/Distance learning degrees valid hain — agar university DEB (Distance Education Bureau) approved hai. IGNOU degree valid hai. UGC approved open universities ki degrees accepted hain. Document verification mein DEB approval letter bhi present karna hoga.
SSC CGL 2026 — Eligibility Complete Guide

SSC CGL 2026 Educational Qualification — Detail Mein Samjho

SSC CGL graduation level exam hai — isliye Bachelor's Degree sabse pehli requirement hai. Isme interesting baat ye hai ki degree mein subject, stream ya percentage pe generally koi restriction nahi hai most posts ke liye. Arts wala, Commerce wala, Science wala — sab apply kar sakte hain. B.Tech, BCA, BSc, BA, B.Com — sab valid hain.

Lekin do exceptions hain jo candidates miss karte hain: JSO post ke liye ya toh 12th mein 60% Maths chahiye, ya degree level par Statistics subject hona chahiye. Statistical Investigator Grade-II ke liye specific subjects mandatory hain — Statistics, Maths, Economics, Demography, OR/IT/CS/Computer Engineering/Data Science/AI.

Open University Note: Open/Distance degree valid hai agar DEB approved hai. IGNOU degree valid hai. B.Tech degree IGNOU se — only valid for students enrolled up to 2009-10 (Supreme Court order ke anusar). Normal universities ki B.Tech — fully valid hai SSC CGL ke liye.

SSC CGL 2026 — Post-wise Age Logic Samjho

SSC CGL mein alag-alag posts ke alag age limits hain — ye confusing lagta hai but logic simple hai. Pay Level se directly connected hai:

  • 18-27 years: Group C posts (Pay Level 4-5) — Auditor, Accountant, UDC/SSA, Tax Assistant, Postal Assistant
  • 18-30 years: Most Group B posts (Pay Level 6-8) — Inspector CBDT/CBIC, ASO IB/MEITY, AAO, SI-CBI/NIA, Inspector Posts etc.
  • 20-30 years: CSS, MEA, Railways, AFHQ ke ASO posts — ministrial cadres
  • 18-32 years: Sirf JSO post ke liye — statistical work ki demand ki wajah se extended limit

SSC CGL 2026 Physical Eligibility — Sirf Kuch Posts Ke Liye

Bahut candidates galti karte hain — SSC CGL ko physical-heavy exam samajhte hain. Reality ye hai ki SSC CGL ki majority posts desk-based hain — koi physical test nahi hota. Physical standards sirf in specific posts ke liye relevant hain: Inspector Central Excise/Examiner/Preventive Officer, Sub-Inspector CBI, Sub-Inspector NIA, Sub-Inspector/JIO NCB, Inspector/Sub-Inspector CBN, aur BRO posts.

Agar aap Tax Assistant, ASO, Auditor, Accountant, Section Head jaise posts ke liye apply kar rahe ho — height, chest, running kuch bhi nahi chahiye. Medical fitness basic honi chahiye but specific physical standards mandatory nahi hain.

SSC CGL 2026 Eyesight — Konsi Post Mein Issue?

  • Desk jobs (most posts) — spectacles fully allowed, koi restriction nahi
  • Inspector Excise/Examiner/Preventive/CBN — official notification mein vision standard specifically nahi diya, but medical fitness required hai
  • SI-CBI aur SI-NIA — clear vision criteria hai: 6/6+6/9 distant, 0.6+0.8 near (glasses ke saath bhi OK)
  • SI/JIO NCB — same: 6/6+6/9 with/without glasses
  • Color blindness — desk jobs ke liye generally issue nahi, field/investigative posts mein problem ho sakta hai
Smart Strategy: Post preference list bharte waqt pahle carefully eligibility verify karo — especially physical posts ke liye. Notification Note-I clearly kehta hai: agar selected ho gaye aur physical standard fail kiya toh candidature reject hogi, post change nahi hogi. Toh pehle verify, phir prefer.
SSC CGL Eligibility 2026 — 40 FAQs

Human-style answers — official notification se confirmed information

1. SSC CGL 2026 ke liye minimum qualification kya chahiye?+
SSC CGL 2026 ke most posts ke liye kisi recognized university se Bachelor's Degree hona zaroori hai. Final year students bhi apply kar sakte hain — bas 01-08-2026 tak degree complete honi chahiye. JSO ke liye 12th mein 60% Maths ya degree level par Statistics bhi chahiye.
2. Kya final year students SSC CGL 2026 apply kar sakte hain?+
Haan, SSC CGL 2026 notification ke anusar final year students apply kar sakte hain. Lekin degree 01-08-2026 (essential qualification cutoff date) tak complete hona mandatory hai — result officially declared hona chahiye. Sirf appearing hona enough nahi. Document verification mein certificate present karna hoga.
3. SSC CGL mein graduation percentage kitni chahiye?+
Most SSC CGL posts ke liye koi minimum percentage nahi chahiye — sirf pass degree enough hai. Sirf JSO post ke liye 12th mein Maths mein 60% marks ya degree level par Statistics subject compulsory hai. Desk jobs ke liye low percentage se koi eligibility issue nahi.
4. Mere graduation mein sirf 43% marks hain, kya main eligible hoon?+
Haan — agar aapke paas recognized graduation degree hai toh 43% marks hone par bhi aap SSC CGL ke most posts ke liye eligible ho. Percentage barrier sirf JSO ke liye hai. Tax Assistant, ASO, Auditor, Accountant jaise sab posts ke liye aap apply kar sakte ho.
5. Kya diploma holder SSC CGL de sakta hai?+
Nahi — sirf diploma se SSC CGL apply nahi kiya ja sakta. Bachelor's Degree compulsory hai. Diploma ke baad regular graduation (BA/B.Com/BSc/B.Tech etc.) complete karo — phir SSC CGL ke liye eligible honge. Diploma partially equivalent nahi maana jaata.
6. Kya B.Tech students SSC CGL ke liye eligible hain?+
Haan — B.Tech / B.E. students SSC CGL ke almost sabhi posts ke liye fully eligible hote hain. Graduation complete honi chahiye. B.Tech = Bachelor's Degree level qualification hai — completely acceptable hai SSC CGL ke liye.
7. Kya B.Tech final year student SSC CGL apply kar sakta hai?+
Haan — B.Tech final year student SSC CGL 2026 ke liye apply kar sakta hai. Condition ye hai ki degree result 01-08-2026 tak declared hona chahiye. Agar final year exam dene ke baad result iss date tak aata hai — eligible ho.
8. Kya BA final year student SSC CGL bhar sakta hai?+
Bilkul — BA final year students SSC CGL 2026 ke liye eligible hain. Degree cutoff date 01-08-2026 tak complete honi chahiye. Stream se koi farak nahi — BA equally valid hai B.Tech jaisa. Applying mein koi barrier nahi.
9. SSC CGL ke liye graduation mein backlog ya grace marks allowed hain?+
Haan — grace marks hone se generally problem nahi hoti. Final result pass hona chahiye. Backlog cleared hona chahiye — 01-08-2026 tak sab subjects pass hone chahiye. Agar koi backlog pending hai toh degree incomplete mani jaayegi.
10. Kya open university ya distance learning degree valid hai SSC CGL mein?+
Haan — open/distance learning degrees valid hain provided university UGC DEB (Distance Education Bureau) approved hai. SSC notification mein clearly mentioned hai. Document verification mein DEB approval letter bhi present karna hoga. IGNOU degree valid hai.
11. Kya IGNOU degree SSC CGL ke liye valid hai?+
Haan — IGNOU (Indira Gandhi National Open University) SSC CGL ke liye valid hai. IGNOU UGC recognized institution hai. Agar IGNOU se graduation complete hai — SSC CGL mein eligible ho. B.Tech IGNOU se sirf 2009-10 tak enrolled students ke liye valid hai (Supreme Court order).
12. SSC CGL ke liye age limit kya hai 2026 mein?+
SSC CGL 2026 mein post-wise age limit hai — age 01-08-2026 ke anusar count hogi. 18-27 years: Auditor, Accountant, UDC, Tax Assistant (Pay Level 4-5). 18-30 years: Inspector, ASO-IB/MEITY, SI-CBI/NIA, AAO (most Group B). 20-30 years: CSS/MEA/Railways/AFHQ ASO. 18-32 years: Junior Statistical Officer.
13. OBC candidates ko SSC CGL mein age relaxation milta hai?+
Haan — OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) candidates ko upper age limit mein 3 saal ki relaxation milti hai. Matlab 18-27 ke post ke liye OBC candidate 30 tak eligible. Non-Creamy Layer certificate mandatory hai — creamy layer mein aane wale OBC candidates ko relaxation nahi milti.
14. SC/ST candidates ko kitni age relaxation milti hai SSC CGL mein?+
SC/ST candidates ko upper age limit mein 5 saal ki relaxation milti hai. 18-27 ke post ke liye SC/ST 32 tak eligible. 18-30 ke post ke liye SC/ST 35 tak eligible. 18-32 JSO ke liye SC/ST 37 tak eligible. Caste certificate valid hona chahiye.
15. SSC CGL mein girls ke liye koi special eligibility hoti hai?+
Educational qualification same hoti hai — boys aur girls ke liye alag nahi. Physical posts ke liye female standards alag (less strict) hote hain. Application fee mein women candidates exempted hain. SSC notification mein specifically mentioned hai ki "women candidates encouraged to apply." BRO posts ke liye sirf male candidates eligible hain.
16. Kya girls ASO in IB post ke liye eligible hain?+
Haan — ASO (Assistant Section Officer) in Intelligence Bureau post ke liye female candidates eligible hain. IB ASO ek Group B gazetted non-ministerial post hai, age limit 18-30. Graduation degree honi chahiye. Physical test nahi hota is post ke liye.
17. Kya SSC CGL mein physical test hota hai?+
Sabhi posts mein nahi — sirf specific posts ke liye physical test hota hai: Inspector Central Excise/Examiner/Preventive Officer, SI in CBI, SI in NIA, SI/JIO in NCB (MHA), Inspector/SI in CBN, aur BRO posts. Tax Assistant, ASO, Auditor, Accountant jaise desk job posts ke liye koi physical test nahi hota.
18. Kya SSC CGL mein height requirement hoti hai?+
Sirf kuch specific inspector/SI posts ke liye. Inspector Excise/CBN: Male 157.5 cm, Female 152 cm. SI-CBI: Male 165 cm, Female 150 cm. SI-NIA: Male 170 cm, Female 150 cm. SI/JIO-NCB: Male 165 cm, Female 152 cm. Desk jobs jaise ASO, Tax Assistant, Auditor mein koi height requirement nahi.
19. Meri height 156 cm hai, kya SSC CGL mein problem hogi?+
Desk jobs ke liye bilkul nahi — 156 cm height se Tax Assistant, ASO, Auditor, Accountant sab posts eligible hain. Inspector Excise/CBN ke liye male minimum 157.5 cm hai toh 156 cm borderline issue ho sakta hai. SI-CBI ke liye minimum 165 cm chahiye. Physical posts se bachao toh koi dikkat nahi.
20. Kya spectacles wale candidates SSC CGL de sakte hain?+
Haan — spectacles wale candidates SSC CGL ke bahut saare posts ke liye eligible hote hain. Desk jobs mein koi vision restriction nahi. Inspector/SI posts ke liye — vision 6/6+6/9 hona chahiye, glasses ke saath bhi acceptable hai. SI-CBI/NIA ke liye vision with glasses bhi 6/6+6/9 hona chahiye.
21. Kya weak eyesight wale SSC CGL mein eligible hain?+
Depends on post. Desk jobs ke liye generally koi restriction nahi — spectacles/glasses allowed hain. Inspector/SI posts ke liye vision corrected standard (6/6+6/9 with glasses) meet karna hota hai. Agar glasses se bhi standard nahi milta — inspector posts ke liye disqualified ho sakte hain. Desk jobs ke liye koi issue nahi.
22. Kya color blind candidates SSC CGL de sakte hain?+
Haan — color blind candidates SSC CGL apply kar sakte hain. Most desk-based posts mein color vision issue nahi hoti. Field-based or investigative posts ke liye medical fitness requirements hoti hain — color blindness issue ho sakta hai. ASO, Tax Assistant, Auditor jaise posts ke liye generally eligible hain.
23. Color blind candidates ke liye kaunsi SSC CGL posts better hain?+
Desk-based posts color blind candidates ke liye comparatively better hain: Auditor (C&AG, CGDA), Accountant, Tax Assistant (CBDT/CBIC), ASO (CSS, MEA, Railways), SSA/UDC, Statistical Investigator (if qualification met). Inspector/SI posts mein medical fitness strictly checked hoti hai — avoid karna better hai.
24. Kya handicapped/PwBD candidates SSC CGL apply kar sakte hain?+
Haan — PwBD (Persons with Benchmark Disabilities) candidates SSC CGL ke bahut saare posts ke liye eligible hain. Age relaxation bhi milti hai — 10 years General PwBD, 13 years OBC PwBD, 15 years SC/ST PwBD. Scribe facility bhi available hai. Specific posts ke liye PwBD eligibility notification mein Annexure format mein detailed list hai.
25. JSO ke liye kya qualification chahiye?+
JSO (Junior Statistical Officer) ke liye official notification ke anusar: Bachelor's Degree in any subject + 12th mein Mathematics mein minimum 60% marks — YA — Bachelor's Degree in any subject with Statistics as one of the subjects at degree level. Age limit 18-32 years (JSO ke liye extended limit hai).
26. Kya B.Com students JSO post ke liye eligible hain?+
B.Com students JSO ke liye eligible ho sakte hain agar: Option 1 — 12th mein Maths mein 60% marks hain (phir koi bhi graduation). Option 2 — B.Com degree mein Statistics ek subject tha. Agar dono nahi — toh JSO ke liye eligible nahi honge, lekin other SSC CGL posts ke liye still eligible rahenge.
27. SSC CGL 2026 application dates kya hain?+
SSC CGL 2026 official dates: Application start: 21-05-2026. Last date for application: 22-06-2026 (23:00 hours). Last date for fee payment: 23-06-2026 (23:00 hours). Correction window: 29-06-2026 to 01-07-2026. Tier-I (tentative): August-September 2026. Tier-II (tentative): December 2026.
28. SSC CGL 2026 application fee kya hai?+
Application fee ₹100 hai. Fee exempted categories: Women candidates, SC candidates, ST candidates, PwBD candidates, ESM (Ex-Servicemen) candidates — inka koi fee nahi lagta. Fee BHIM UPI, Net Banking, Visa/Mastercard/Maestro/RuPay debit card se pay ho sakti hai. 23-06-2026 tak pay karni hogi.
29. SSC CGL 2026 mein total kitni vacancies hain?+
SSC CGL 2026 notification ke anusar approximately 12,256 vacancies hain — updated list ssc.gov.in pe milegi. Final vacancy number update hoti rehti hai. State/zone-wise vacancies SSC collect nahi karta — user departments se contact karna hoga. SSC website pe tentative vacancy link available hai.
30. SSC CGL mein normalization hoti hai kya?+
Haan — SSC CGL 2026 notification mein clearly mentioned hai ki "Marks scored in Computer Based Examination, if conducted in multiple shifts, will be normalized by using the procedure published by the Commission vide Notice dated 02-06-2025." Normalized scores se final merit aur cutoff marks determine honge.
31. Kya 12th ke baad SSC CGL diya ja sakta hai?+
Nahi — SSC CGL graduation level exam hai. 12th pass candidates SSC CGL ke liye eligible nahi hote. 12th ke baad SSC CHSL (Higher Secondary Level), SSC MTS (10th pass), ya SSC GD (12th pass) try kar sakte hain. SSC CGL ke liye graduation complete karo phir apply karo.
32. Kya MSc graduate SSC CGL ke liye eligible hai?+
Haan — MSc graduates SSC CGL ke liye fully eligible hain. MSc = postgraduate degree hai — its eligibility includes graduation level requirements. No upper qualification barrier hai SSC CGL mein. MSc in Statistics graduates JSO ke liye bhi eligible ho sakte hain agar Statistics undergraduate level par tha.
33. Kya CSE student SSC CGL de sakta hai?+
Haan — Computer Science Engineering (B.Tech CSE) students SSC CGL ke liye eligible hote hain. B.Tech = graduation level hai. CSE graduates Statistical Investigator Grade-II post ke liye bhi eligible ho sakte hain (Computer Engineering eligible subjects mein listed hai). Sab general posts ke liye bhi eligible hain.
34. Kya private college ki degree SSC CGL mein valid hoti hai?+
Haan — agar college/university recognized hai toh degree valid mani jayegi. UGC recognized universities, state government recognized universities, AICTE recognized technical institutions sab valid hain. Document verification mein original degree certificate plus institution recognition proof helpful hota hai agar koi doubt ho.
35. SSC CGL mein reservation milta hai kya?+
Haan — SSC CGL mein SC, ST, OBC, EWS, PwBD aur ESM candidates ko reservation aur age relaxation diya jaata hai. Application fee exemption bhi SC/ST/Women/PwBD/ESM ke liye hai. Reservation rosters individual user departments maintain karte hain — SSC ke paas state/zone-wise vacancy data nahi hota.
36. SSC CGL clear karne ke baad dubara exam de sakte hain?+
Haan — SSC CGL mein koi fixed attempt limit nahi hai. Jab tak age eligible hai, har year apply kar sakte hain. Ek bar join kar lene ke baad bhi technically age-eligible hain toh apply kar sakte hain — but departmental permission/NOC required ho sakta hai.
37. SSC CGL mein negative marking kya hai?+
SSC CGL 2026 Tier-I mein negative marking hai: Section-I, II aur III (Paper-I) mein 1 mark per wrong answer cut hoga. Paper-II (JSO) aur Paper-III (AAO) mein 0.50 mark per wrong answer cut hoga. Computer Knowledge Test (Section-III) mandatory but qualifying nature hai — marks cut hoti hain wrong answers par.
38. SSC CGL application correction window kya hai?+
SSC CGL 2026 correction window: 29-06-2026 to 01-07-2026 (23:00 hours). Candidates 2 baar correction kar sakte hain. First correction: ₹200 charge. Second correction: ₹500 charge. Sirf completed applications (fee paid) ke liye correction allowed hai. DOB correction generally allowed nahi hoti — Matriculation certificate DOB final authority hai.
39. Kya diabetic ya heart patient SSC CGL ke liye eligible hai?+
Desk jobs ke liye generally eligible hain — SSC CGL specific medical disqualification list most desk posts ke liye nahi deta. Agar controlled condition hai toh application mein issue nahi. Inspector/SI/physical posts ke liye medical fitness strictly checked hoti hai — consult karo doctor se pehle preference prefer karne se. Individual medical board decide karta hai.
40. SSC CGL ke liye OBC certificate kab tak valid hona chahiye?+
SSC notification 2026 mein clearly likha hai: "The Commission will not insist on candidates producing OBC certificate issued within crucial/cut-off date." Candidature provisional rahega user department verification pe. Lekin practically document verification mein valid (non-expired) OBC-NCL certificate present karna hoga. Certificate state government prescribed format mein DM/DC/SDM se issued honi chahiye.
