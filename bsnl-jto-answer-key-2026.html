Bharat Sanchar Nigam Limited (BSNL) has released the provisional Answer Key 2026 for the recruitment of Junior Telecom Officer (JTO) posts. The Computer Based Test (CBT) was conducted on 23 August 2026, and the Answer Key / Response Sheet was made available on 29 August 2026.

Candidates can access the Answer Key and Response Sheet through the official examination response portal using their allotted Roll Number and Date of Birth.

The Answer Key and Response Sheet for BSNL JTO 2026 are available on the official examination response portal: bsnl.examresponse.in.

The portal provides candidate login using the allotted Roll Number and Date of Birth.

Login ID: your allotted Roll Number  •  Password: your Date of Birth (as required by the portal)

BSNL has not published a separate downloadable master PDF for the JTO Answer Key. The Answer Key / Response Sheet is generated individually for each candidate after logging in with Roll Number and Date of Birth on the official portal.

Set/shift-wise consolidated PDF: Notify Soon
  1. Visit the official BSNL examination response portal: bsnl.examresponse.in
  2. Enter your Roll Number.
  3. Enter your Date of Birth / password as required by the portal.
  4. Complete the verification shown on the portal.
  5. Login to your candidate profile.
  6. Open the Answer Key / Response Sheet section.
  7. Download or view your Answer Key / Response Sheet.

Candidates can access their Response Sheet through the official examination response portal at bsnl.examresponse.in, using the same Roll Number and Date of Birth login used for the Answer Key.

To estimate your probable score, match your marked responses in the Response Sheet against the provisional Answer Key, then apply the marks awarded for correct answers and the negative marking for wrong answers as per the official notification.

Marking Scheme: Check Official Notification

Candidates who wish to challenge any answer in the provisional Answer Key can raise an objection online through the official examination response portal.
