Army Document Verification — Complete 2026 Guide

Army Document Verification — Why It Can Make or Break Your Selection

Every year, lakhs of young Indians train hard for the Indian Army rally — the running, the physical fitness tests, the medical examination. Many pass all of these. But a significant number get stopped at the final hurdle: document verification. It's not a lack of fitness or intelligence. It's often a missing certificate, an unattested photocopy, or an outdated format that sends a completely deserving candidate home.

The army document checklist generator on this page exists for one reason: to make sure that if you've worked hard for your fitness, your documents don't let you down. Use it before every rally, every recruitment drive, every application.

Key Rule: Always carry originals + at least 2 attested photocopies of every document. Some zones ask for 3–4 copies. Bring extra. Attestation should be done by a Gazetted Officer, not just a notary for most documents.

Complete Army Document List — What Every Candidate Needs

The Indian army bharti documents list has evolved over recent years, especially with the introduction of the Agniveer scheme in 2022. The core list remains consistent across recruitment rallies and all post types. Here's a complete breakdown of the army rally document checklist 2026:

  • 10th Marksheet & Certificate: Board examination marksheet and passing certificate. This serves as your primary age proof and educational qualification proof.
  • 12th Marksheet & Certificate: Required for Agniveer Clerk/SKT, Technical, and NDA/CDS posts. For GD and Tradesman, it's optional but beneficial.
  • Aadhaar Card: Mandatory identity proof. Must show your current address. Both original and photocopies required.
  • Domicile / Residence Certificate: Issued by the SDM/Tehsildar of your district. Proves you belong to the rally zone's state. Very commonly missing — get this done at least 2 months before the rally.
  • Character Certificate: Issued by the Village Sarpanch or Ward Councillor, countersigned by a Gazetted Officer. Must be recent (within 6 months of rally date).
  • Caste Certificate (SC/ST/OBC): If you belong to SC/ST/OBC category, this is mandatory. Must be issued by the SDM/Sub-Divisional Officer. OBC certificates must be "Non-Creamy Layer" type.
  • Police Verification Certificate: Issued by the local SHO (Station House Officer) / Thana. One of the most commonly missing documents. Takes time to get — apply at least 3–4 weeks before your rally.
  • Unmarried Certificate: Issued by a Gazetted Officer / Sarpanch certifying that you are unmarried. Mandatory for all Army posts as candidates must be unmarried at the time of recruitment.
  • Affidavit (Sworn Statement): Notarized affidavit on stamp paper declaring correct information. Format may vary by zone — check official rally advertisement for the prescribed format.
  • Bank Passbook / Account Details: Required for salary disbursement after selection. Should have candidate's name and IFSC code clearly visible.
  • Passport Size Photographs: Carry at least 10–15 recent passport-size photographs (white or light blue background, without cap or glasses unless worn by religion). Must be taken within the last 6 months.
  • Admit Card / Army Application Printout: Print your online application confirmation / admit card. Required for entry to the rally ground.
  • PAN Card: For post-joining formalities and salary account. Carry a copy.

Post-specific Documents — What Changes by Post Type

While the core list above applies to all posts, certain post types require additional documents for the army document verification process:

  • Agniveer Clerk / SKT: 12th marksheet with at least 60% marks (50% in each subject), computer certificate or typing certificate if available — gives bonus points during selection.
  • Agniveer Tradesman: ITI Certificate or trade certificate for the specific trade you're applying under (Cook, Washer Man, Store Keeper, etc.). This is a hard requirement — without it, your application is invalid for Tradesman posts.
  • Agniveer Technical: 12th with Physics, Chemistry, Maths (PCM group) with minimum 50% marks. For specific technical trades, additional technical diplomas or certificates may be required.
  • NDA / CDS: 12th passing certificate (for NDA) or graduation degree (for CDS), UPSC admit card, fitness certificate, and all standard identity documents.

Common Document Mistakes That Lead to Rejection

Based on patterns across army rallies over the years, here are the most frequent document mistakes that cause candidates to be turned away at verification — and how to avoid them before your army rally document preparation:

  • Police Verification Certificate applied too late: This takes 2–4 weeks. Most candidates apply for it only days before the rally. Apply for it immediately after the rally announcement.
  • Character Certificate outdated: If it's more than 6 months old at the time of the rally, it may be rejected. Always get a fresh one closer to the rally date.
  • Improper attestation: Photocopies attested only by a notary (not a Gazetted Officer) are sometimes rejected. Know the difference and get proper attestation.
  • Name mismatch across documents: Spelling of your name differs between Aadhaar, 10th certificate, and domicile certificate. Get these corrected before applying — mismatches are a common ground for rejection.
  • OBC certificate without NCL clause: Candidates from OBC category who bring an OBC certificate without the "Non-Creamy Layer" mention may be treated as general category. Make sure your certificate explicitly states NCL.
  • Insufficient number of copies: Some zones require 3–4 photocopies of each document. Carry at least 4 copies of everything to be safe.
  • Photographs too old or wrong format: Photographs older than 6 months, or with coloured backgrounds, caps, or goggles (except religious ones) are rejected. Always bring fresh, standard-format photos.
Pro Tip: Create a dedicated folder or envelope for your army documents. Keep originals in one section and attested copies in another. Label everything. Practice retrieving documents quickly — at busy rallies, candidates are given very little time to produce documents during verification.

Step-by-Step Document Preparation Timeline

The best approach to army document preparation is to start early and work systematically. Here's a practical timeline based on when the rally announcement is made:

  • Immediately after announcement: Apply for Police Verification Certificate at your local police station (takes 2–4 weeks).
  • Week 1–2: Get Domicile Certificate, Caste Certificate (if applicable), and Affidavit prepared. Visit SDM/Tehsildar office early as these can take 1–3 weeks.
  • Week 2–3: Get fresh Character Certificate and Unmarried Certificate from Sarpanch/Ward Councillor. Get all documents properly attested by a Gazetted Officer.
  • 1 week before rally: Get new passport photographs taken (10–15 copies). Print admit card/application. Make 4 photocopies of everything.
  • Day before rally: Arrange all documents in order. Check each item against the Army document checklist. Put everything in a waterproof folder.

NCC and Sports Certificates — Bonus Marks That Matter

If you have an NCC Certificate (A, B, or C grade), carry it. NCC 'C' Certificate holders get significant bonus marks in the merit list for Agniveer recruitment — this can be the difference between selection and the waitlist. Similarly, candidates with recognized sports achievements at district, state, or national level get bonus marks. These are not required documents, but they are game-changers in a competitive selection process.

Final Advice: Use the Army Document Checklist Generator above to generate your personalized list. Tick off each document you have. Don't go to the rally ground with even one critical document missing. Your fitness can get you to the door — but your documents decide if you walk through it.
❓ FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Army documents ke baare mein sabse common sawaalon ke seedhe jawaab

Army rally me kaun kaun se documents chahiye? (Full List)+
Army rally mein standard required documents: 10th marksheet + certificate, Aadhaar Card, Domicile Certificate, Character Certificate, Police Verification Certificate, Unmarried Certificate, Affidavit (notarized), Passport size photos (10-15), Bank passbook, Admit card/Army application printout. Caste certificate (SC/ST/OBC ke liye), 12th marksheet (Clerk/Technical ke liye), ITI Certificate (Tradesman ke liye) bhi required hote hain. Originals + minimum 2 attested photocopies har document ki le jaayein. Upar wala checklist generator use karke apni exact list generate karein.
Army document verification me kya check hota hai?+
Army document verification mein following things check hoti hain: (1) Documents ki originality — originals dekhe jaate hain. (2) Attestation — photocopies Gazetted Officer se properly attested honi chahiye. (3) Name consistency — sabhi documents mein naam exact same hona chahiye. (4) Date/expiry — character certificate, police verification etc. recent hone chahiye (6 months). (5) Format correctness — especially affidavit aur caste certificate ka format notification ke anusaar hona chahiye. (6) Number of copies — usually 2–4 attested copies required hoti hain har document ki.
Police Verification Certificate kitne time mein milta hai aur kahan se?+
Police Verification Certificate aapke local thane (police station) ke SHO se milta hai. Process mein 2 se 4 hafte lag sakte hain. Aapko apne thane mein jaake application dena hoga — generally Name, Address, DOB, Aadhaar details ke saath. Kuch states mein online apply bhi hota hai. Important: isko army rally announcement ke baad immediately apply karein. Last minute pe apply karne pe certificate nahi milega rally ke din tak. Ye sabse commonly missing document hai.
Army me affidavit kya hota hai aur kaise banwate hain?+
Army affidavit ek notarized sworn statement hota hai jisme candidate declare karta hai ki uski diya hua information correct hai, woh unmarried hai, aur koi criminal case nahi hai. Ye stamp paper pe hota hai aur notary se attest hota hai. Format usually rally notification mein diya hota hai — use download karke apne paas ki stationery shop pe print karwa lein aur notary se sign karwa lein. Cost approximately ₹50–200 hoti hai depending on state. Ek baat yaad rakhen — affidavit ka format zone se zone vary kar sakta hai.
Agniveer GD ke liye kya 12th certificate zaruri hai?+
Agniveer GD ke liye 10th pass (Matriculation) minimum qualification hai. 12th certificate mandatory nahi hai GD ke liye, lekin agar aapke paas 12th ka certificate hai toh carry zaroor karein — kuch recruitment rallies mein bonus marks milte hain ya aap 12th ke basis pe Clerk/Technical post ke liye bhi eligible ho sakte hain. Note: Agniveer GD ke liye minimum 45% marks in aggregate with 33% in each subject in Class 10th required hai (for General category).
Documents mein name mismatch ho toh kya karein?+
Name mismatch ek serious issue hai. Agar Aadhaar mein naam alag spelling mein hai aur 10th certificate mein alag, toh isko pehle hi correct karwa lein. Aadhaar correction ke liye nearest Aadhaar centre ya online SSUP portal. 10th certificate correction ke liye apne respective board office se contact karein. Agar correction time pe possible nahi, toh ek Gazetted Officer se affidavit banwayein jisme clearly mention ho ki dono names ek hi vyakti ke hain. Lekin is situation ko hone se roknaa best hai — apne documents pehle se check karein.
Army rally mein kitni photos chahiye aur kaisa format?+
Army rally ke liye minimum 10–15 passport size photographs carry karein. Requirements: Recent photographs (last 6 months mein li gayi), white ya light blue plain background, without cap, without dark goggles (religious headgear / spectacles allowed), clearly visible face, standard passport size (3.5cm × 4.5cm). Kuch forms pe coloured photos chahiye hoti hain, kuch pe black & white. Safe taraf pe dono ready rakhein. Photos ke peeth pe apna naam aur date of birth pencil se likh lein.
Agniveer Army documents list 2026 me kya kya chahiye?
Agniveer Army documents list me required hote hain: 10th marksheet, Aadhaar card, domicile certificate, caste certificate (if applicable), passport size photos, police verification certificate, aur admit card.
Indian Army document verification process kaise hota hai?
Document verification process me sabhi original documents check hote hain, photocopies verify hoti hain, aur name, date of birth aur certificate details match ki jati hain. Agar mismatch hota hai to candidate reject ho sakta hai.
Indian Army documents list PDF download kaha milega?
Army documents list PDF aap official notification ya checklist tools se generate kar sakte hain. Ye PDF aapko complete document preparation me help karta hai.
TA Army documents list me kya difference hota hai?
TA Army (Territorial Army) ke documents thode alag ho sakte hain jaise employment proof aur additional eligibility documents. Baaki basic documents same rehte hain.
Army GD ke liye kaun se documents compulsory hote hain?
Army GD ke liye 10th pass certificate, Aadhaar card, domicile, caste certificate (if applicable), photos aur admit card compulsory hote hain.
Army rally ke liye documents ka format kaisa hona chahiye?
Documents clean, readable aur properly attested hone chahiye. Photocopies clear honi chahiye aur original documents saath me hona zaruri hai.
Army document checklist generator ka kya use hai?
Ye tool aapke post, education aur category ke basis par personalized checklist banata hai jisse aap koi important document miss nahi karte.
Army me document reject hone ke main reasons kya hote hain?
Main reasons hote hain: document mismatch, incomplete documents, wrong format, attestation missing, aur outdated certificates.
Army documents me name mismatch ho to kya karein?
Name mismatch hone par affidavit ya gazette correction karwana padta hai. Sab documents me same name hona compulsory hai.
Army rally ke liye documents kitne din pehle ready karne chahiye?
Documents rally se kam se kam 1–2 mahine pehle ready kar lene chahiye, kyunki kuch certificates jaise police verification me time lagta hai.
