Important Posting Alerts

Ye cheezein jaanna zaroori hai SSC GD posting ke baare mein — myths aur reality

🚨
Home Posting Guaranteed Nahi Hoti
SSC GD mein home state posting ka koi guaranteed rule nahi hai. Posting department operational requirements ke hisaab se hoti hai — aapki preference secondary hoti hai.
⚠️
Border Posting ka Risk Real Hai
BSF, ITBP aur AR mein mostly border area postings hoti hain. Agar ye forces choose ki hain toh border area mein jaane ki poori tayyari rakhni chahiye mentally aur physically.
🔄
Transfer Policy Force-wise Alag Hai
CISF mein transfer cycle CRPF ya BSF se alag hota hai. Kuch forces mein 3 saal, kuch mein 5–7 saal baad transfer request possible hai. Joining se pehle force-specific transfer rules samjho.
👩
Female Candidates — Separate Posting Norms
Female SSC GD candidates ke liye comparatively suitable postings consider ki jaati hain. CISF aur SSB mein female-friendly postings ki zyada possibilities hain. Extreme hard area postings generally avoid ki jaati hain.
🏙️
Metro Posting Ka Chance Limited Hai
CISF ki airport security postings metro cities mein hoti hain — lekin vacancies limited hain. BSF, CRPF, ITBP ke liye metro posting expect karna unrealistic hai as a primary expectation.
📋
Force Preference Fill Karna Important Hai
SSC GD process mein force preference form fill karna padta hai. Preference guarantee nahi karti but allocation mein consideration zaroor milti hai. Informed choice karo apni situation ke hisaab se.

Suresh UP se SSC GD qualify kiya tha — topper tha apne district mein. Socha tha ki UP mein hi kuch posting milegi ya nearby. Force preference mein usne BSF choose kiya kyunki uske bade bhaiya ne bola tha — "BSF mein chance hai north India posting ka."

Lekin joining ke baad pehli posting aayi — Tripura. Northeast. Suresh ke liye ye almost ek naya planet tha. Pehle 6 mahine bahut mushkil raha — ghar se door, language barrier, alag culture.

Suresh ki story ek lesson hai — posting kabhie bhi expect ke hisaab se nahi hoti. Lekin agar mentally tayyar ho, toh kuch saal ki hardship ke baad situation better ho sakti hai. Ye tool usi expectation management ke liye hai — taaki aspiration realistic rahe.

SSC GD Posting Process Asal Mein Kaise Kaam Karta Hai?

SSC GD mein selection ke baad posting process kuch steps mein complete hota hai. Pehle SSC merit list aur force-wise vacancy ke basis par candidates allocate hote hain. Phir document verification aur medical examination hoti hai. Successfully clear karne ke baad force-specific training shuru hoti hai jo 11 months se ek saal tak ki hoti hai. Training complete hone ke baad final posting orders jari hote hain — aur ye posting training center se completely alag jagah bhi ho sakti hai.

Force preference form SSC GD ke process mein ek important step hai. Is form mein candidate apni preferred force indicate karta hai. Merit-wise higher candidates ko preference consideration milne ke chances thode zyada hote hain, lekin ye final allocation nahi hai — ye sirf ek input hai system ke liye. Actual allocation vacancy-driven hota hai.

Kaunsa State Kaunse Force Mein Zyada Post Karta Hai?

Ye ek commonly poocha jaane wala sawaal hai aur iska jawab samajhna important hai. Force deployment geography ke hisaab se hoti hai:

  • BSF: Pakistan aur Bangladesh borders pe concentrated hai — Rajasthan, Punjab, Jammu, West Bengal, Assam aur Tripura mein major presence. In states ke candidates ko BSF mein relatively closer to home chances ho sakte hain.
  • ITBP: India-China border pe hai — Uttarakhand, Himachal Pradesh, Ladakh, Arunachal Pradesh. Pahadi states ke candidates ke liye slightly better chances hain ITBP mein.
  • SSB: Nepal aur Bhutan borders par — UP, Bihar, West Bengal, Uttarakhand, Sikkim. In states se candidates ke liye SSB proximity advantage de sakta hai.
  • CISF: Industrial units aur airports puri India mein hain — Delhi, Mumbai, Hyderabad, Bangalore airports, aur PSU sites. Ye force geographically most diverse hai posting locations ke liye.
  • CRPF: Internal security operations — puri India mein mobile force. Naxal-affected areas (Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, MP, Telangana) mein heavy presence. Regular transfers hoti hain.
  • Assam Rifles: Primarily Northeast India — Manipur, Nagaland, Mizoram, Arunachal mein major deployment. Northeast ke candidates ke liye slightly better proximity chances.

Transfer Policy — Kab aur Kaise Hoti Hai?

Transfer SSC GD jobs ka ek inherent part hai. Har force ki apni transfer policy hoti hai:

  • Normal Routine Transfer: Generally 3–7 saal ki service ke baad administrative transfer hoti hai. Ye force ke operational needs pe depend karta hai.
  • Mutual Transfer: Do candidates apas mein consent se transfer kar sakte hain agar dono ke supervising officers agree karein. Isme dono ki posting locations match honi chahiye.
  • Compassionate Posting: Serious family medical emergency ya special circumstances mein compassionate ground par posting request ki ja sakti hai. Ye guarantee nahi hai lekin consideration milti hai.
  • Promotion-based Transfer: Jab Head Constable ya ASI mein promote ho jaate hain, toh transfer bhi possible hota hai naye responsibilities ke saath.

Transfer request karte waqt vacancy aur department approval dono zaroori hain. Koi bhi transfer force ke operational requirements ke bina possible nahi hota.

CISF Kyun Best Option Hai Urban / Home Posting Ke Liye?

CISF (Central Industrial Security Force) SSC GD candidates ke beech sabse zyada preferred force hai home posting ki wajah se. Iske kai reasons hain — CISF ke under airports (Delhi, Mumbai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Chennai, Bangalore etc.), nuclear power plants, petroleum refineries, metro rail, aur other PSU sites hain jo puri India mein distribute hain. Iska matlab hai ki ek CISF constable ka posting kisi bhi major city ke kareeb ho sakti hai.

CISF postings indoor security work involve karti hain — no border patrolling, no jungle operations, no extreme weather conditions. Salary samaan hoti hai doosre forces se, lekin lifestyle comparatively stable hoti hai. Female candidates ke liye bhi CISF sabse zyada recommended force hai safe aur reasonable working conditions ki wajah se.

SSC GD Posting Myths vs Reality

  • Myth: "High marks se home posting pakki ho jaati hai." Reality: Marks allocation mein ek factor hai, lekin home posting guarantee nahi karta. Operational requirements primary factor hai.
  • Myth: "Force preference form bharne se preferred force pakki ho jaati hai." Reality: Preference consider ki jaati hai, guarantee nahi. Higher merit candidates ko comparatively better allocation chances hain.
  • Myth: "BSF mein toh sirf border pe hi kaam karna padega, kuch else nahi." Reality: BSF mein operations pe posting hoti hai, lekin HQ postings, training assignments aur administrative roles bhi hote hain.
  • Myth: "Female candidates ko easy postings milti hain automatically." Reality: Considerations hain, automatic guarantee nahi. Proper process follow karna padta hai.
  • Myth: "Transfer request karne se posting badal jaati hai." Reality: Transfer mutual consent aur vacancy-dependent process hai — request approval time-consuming ho sakta hai.
