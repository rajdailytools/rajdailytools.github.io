Complete Guide

Govt Exam Height Criteria Explained 2026

Height requirement ke baare mein poori jankari — category relaxation, region-based rules, aur common mistakes.

Govt Exams Mein Height Criteria Kya Hota Hai?

Government exams — especially police, paramilitary, and defence forces — mein physical fitness standards ka ek important part height requirement hota hai. Ye ensure karta hai ki candidates operational duties perform karne ke liye physically capable hain.

Height criteria exam-specific, gender-specific, category-specific, aur region-specific hoti hai. General category ke male candidates ke liye typically highest criteria hoti hai, aur relaxation reserved categories aur specific regions ke candidates ko milti hai.

Category Relaxation Kya Hota Hai?

SC/ST category ke candidates ko 5 cm ki height relaxation milti hai in most exams. Matlab agar SSC GD ke liye general category mein 170 cm chahiye, toh SC/ST candidates ke liye 165 cm sufficient ho sakta hai.

  • SC/ST: Typically 5 cm relaxation in most exams
  • OBC/EWS: Generally no height relaxation (same as General)
  • Women candidates: Already have separate (lower) height criteria
  • PH (Physically Handicapped): Separate norms as per exam
Note: Category relaxation sirf height mein hoti hai — qualifying criteria (marks, age) may have separate relaxation norms. Check official notification for complete relaxation list.

Region-Based Height Relaxation

Certain regions ke candidates ko special height relaxation milti hai because of geographical and physiological factors:

  • Gorkha / Garhwali / Kumaoni: 5 cm relaxation in SSC GD, BSF, CRPF, CISF
  • North East / Assam: 5 cm relaxation — separate criteria in Indian Army also
  • Dogra: Region-based relaxation in some paramilitary exams
  • ST Area / Tribal Belt: 5 cm relaxation generally applicable
  • Andaman & Nicobar / Lakshadweep: Separate criteria in some exams
Important: Region relaxation ke liye typically domicile certificate ya relevant regional proof require hota hai. Certificate ready rakhein pehle se.

SSC GD vs Police vs Army — Key Differences

SSC GD: 170 cm (Male General), 157 cm (Female) — one of the more competitive height requirements. Category/region relaxation available.

Indian Army GD: Region-specific — 170 cm general, 160-165 cm for NE/Hill states. More varied criteria based on regiment.

Navy/Air Force: Lower height requirement (157-163 cm) — more candidates qualify here.

State Police: Varies significantly by state — UP Police 168 cm, MP Police 167 cm, Bihar Police 165 cm for males.

❌ State Police Ignore Karna
State police exams mein criteria often lower hoti hai — candidates better fit hone ke bawajood apply nahi karte.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Government exam height criteria ke baare mein common sawaal.

SSC GD ke liye height kitni chahiye male aur female ke liye?+
SSC GD Constable ke liye Male (General): 170 cm aur Female (General): 157 cm height required hai. SC/ST candidates ke liye 5 cm ki relaxation milti hai — Male SC/ST: 165 cm, Female SC/ST: 152 cm. Gorkha, Garhwali, Kumaoni, NE, Dogra aur ST area ke candidates ko bhi 5 cm relaxation milti hai.
Indian Army mein height kitni honi chahiye?+
Indian Army GD ke liye height state/region ke hisaab se vary karti hai. General: 170 cm (most states). Gorkha/NE/Hill states: 160–165 cm. Assam region: 157–162 cm. Female candidates (Officer entry): 152 cm. Tribal areas: region-specific criteria apply. Har unit ya regiment ke hisaab se slight variation possible hai.
Ladkiyon ke liye police mein height criteria kya hai?+
Female candidates ke liye police height criteria: SSC GD: 157 cm, Delhi Police: 157 cm, CRPF/BSF/CISF: 157 cm, UP Police: 152 cm, MP Police: 152 cm, Bihar Police: 155 cm. Navy/Coast Guard: 152 cm. State police mein criteria vary karta hai — kuch states mein 150 cm bhi acceptable hai. Hamesha specific state notification check karein.
ST category ko height mein kitna relaxation milta hai?+
ST (Scheduled Tribe) category ke candidates ko majority of central government exams mein 5 cm ki height relaxation milti hai. Iska matlab — SSC GD mein general 170 cm ki jagah ST candidates ke liye 165 cm sufficient hai. Iske alawa, agar ST candidate hill/tribal area se hai, toh additional relaxation bhi possible hai. OBC category ke liye height mein relaxation generally nahi milti.
Assam aur North East ke candidates ke liye alag height rules hote hain?+
Haan, bilkul. Assam aur North East candidates ke liye 5 cm ki height relaxation milti hai SSC GD, CRPF, BSF, CISF aur kuch aur exams mein. Indian Army mein toh NE region ke liye separate height criteria hi hoti hai — generally 157–165 cm depending on state. State domicile certificate ya relevant proof submit karna hota hai is relaxation ke liye.
CRPF aur BSF ke liye height kya honi chahiye?+
CRPF: Male General 165 cm, Female 157 cm. SC/ST: 5 cm relaxation. BSF: Male General 170 cm, Female 157 cm. SC/ST: 5 cm, Gorkha/NE/ST area: 5 cm. CISF: Male General 170 cm, Female 157 cm. Same relaxation rules. Exact criteria notification mein confirm karein — every recruitment cycle mein slight changes ho sakte hain.
Agar height 1-2 cm se kam reh jaaye toh kya karein?+
Agar height criteria se 1-2 cm kam hai toh: 1) Category relaxation check karein — SC/ST candidate hain toh 5 cm relaxation milti hai; 2) Regional relaxation check karein — Gorkha/NE/Hill area ke hain toh relaxation milti hai; 3) Other exams check karein — Navy, Air Force, RPF, Forest Guard mein lower criteria hoti hai; 4) State police check karein — kuch states mein height requirement lower hoti hai. Is tool se other options explore karein.
RPF aur Forest Guard ke liye height kitni chahiye?+
RPF (Railway Protection Force) Constable: Male 165 cm, Female 157 cm. SC/ST: 5 cm relaxation. Forest Guard: Male approximately 163 cm, Female 150 cm (state-specific variation hoti hai). Forest Guard criteria state forest department ke hisaab se decide hoti hai — UP, MP, Rajasthan etc. mein alag criteria ho sakti hai. Official state forest department notification zaroor check karein.
Navy aur Air Force mein height criteria SSC GD se alag hai?+
Haan — Navy aur Air Force mein height criteria SSC GD se kam hoti hai. Indian Navy Sailor (AA/SSR): 157 cm Male, 152 cm Female. Indian Air Force (Group X/Y Airman): 152.5 cm Male. Indian Coast Guard Navik: 157 cm Male. Inka matlab hai ki jo candidates SSC GD ke liye height se thode kam hain, woh Navy ya Air Force ke liye eligible ho sakte hain. Is tool se check kar sakte hain.
Height measurement mein koi relaxation milti hai ya exact hona chahiye?+
Government exams mein height exact honi chahiye ya minimum criteria se upar. Koi "rounding off" nahi hota — agar 170 cm chahiye aur aap 169.9 cm hain toh ineligible maana ja sakta hai. Physical examination mein measuring tape ya medical equipment se accurate measurement hoti hai. Subah fresh hone par height generally thodi zyada hoti hai — physical test ke liye subah ka time prefer karein.
