📖 Complete Guide

CRPF Tradesman 2026 — Complete Guide

CRPF Tradesman Recruitment 2026 — Overview

The Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF), India's largest Central Armed Police Force, has released its recruitment notification for Constable (Technical, Tradesman & Pioneer Wing) posts in 2026. With 9195 total vacancies, this is one of the biggest government job opportunities of 2026 for candidates who have passed Class 10th. The online application window opened on 20 April 2026 and closes on 19 May 2026.

This is a golden opportunity for youth across India — especially from rural and semi-urban areas — to serve the nation and build a stable career in paramilitary service. The CRPF Tradesman Age Calculator 2026 on this page helps you instantly verify your eligibility so you don't waste time on confusion.

Quick Fact: Age for CRPF Tradesman 2026 is calculated as on 01 January 2026. General category: 18–23 years. OBC: up to 26 years. SC/ST: up to 28 years. Driver post: 21–27 years (General). Always verify from official notification at rect.crpf.gov.in

Who Can Apply for CRPF Tradesman 2026?

To be eligible for CRPF Constable Tradesman Recruitment 2026, candidates must meet the following criteria:

  • Passed Class 10th examination from any recognized board in India
  • For Driver Post: Valid driving license required along with Class 10th
  • For Motor Mechanic: Class 10th + 2 Years ITI Certificate
  • For Pioneer Wing: Class 10th + 1 Year experience in related field
  • For Other Posts: Class 10th + proficiency and work experience in the related trade
  • Age: 18–23 years for most posts (as on 01 January 2026), 21–27 for Driver
  • Meet physical standards: Height 170 cm (Male/UR), 162.5 cm (Male/ST), 157 cm (Female/UR), 150 cm (Female/ST)

Age Confusion — The Biggest Problem for Candidates

One of the most common reasons candidates miss out on government job opportunities is age calculation confusion. Many candidates don't know:

  • The exact reference date for age calculation (01 January 2026 for CRPF 2026)
  • How much relaxation their category gets
  • Whether the Driver post has different age limits than other posts
  • Whether Ex-Serviceman and other special category relaxations apply to them

This is exactly why the CRPF Age Calculator 2026 on this page was built — to eliminate this confusion in one click. Enter your DOB and category, and get an instant, accurate, clear answer.

CRPF Selection Process 2026

The selection for CRPF Constable Tradesman 2026 happens in four stages:

  • CBT Exam (Computer Based Test) — Written examination to test general knowledge, reasoning, and trade knowledge
  • PET & PST (Physical Efficiency Test & Physical Standard Test) — Height, chest measurement, and physical fitness tasks
  • Document Verification — Verification of all certificates, identity documents, and category certificates
  • Medical Examination — Complete health assessment to ensure fitness for active duty

Physical Standards for CRPF Tradesman 2026

Meeting the physical standards is essential. Age eligibility alone is not enough. Here are the official physical requirements:

  • Height (Male — UR/SC/OBC/EWS): 170 cm minimum
  • Height (Male — ST candidates): 162.5 cm minimum
  • Height (Female — UR/SC/OBC/EWS): 157 cm minimum
  • Height (Female — ST candidates): 150 cm minimum
  • Chest (Male — UR/SC/OBC/EWS): 80–85 cm (unexpanded–expanded)
  • Chest (Male — ST candidates): 76–81 cm
  • No chest measurement for female candidates

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Calculating age from today's date instead of 01 January 2026
  • Not applying OBC/SC/ST relaxation when you're eligible for it
  • Confusing Driver post age limit (21–27) with general tradesman limit (18–23)
  • Applying without having the required ITI certificate for Motor Mechanic post
  • Paying application fee when SC/ST candidates are exempted (fee is ₹0 for SC/ST)
  • Missing the last date — 19 May 2026. Apply well before the deadline
Official Notice: Always read the complete official CRPF notification from rect.crpf.gov.in before filling the application form. This tool is for reference and quick checking only. Details may be updated — verify the latest age limits from the official notification.
❓ FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about CRPF Tradesman 2026 age eligibility.

What is the CRPF Tradesman 2026 age limit?+
For most posts: 18 to 23 years as on 01 January 2026. For Driver post: 21 to 27 years. With OBC relaxation: up to 26 years (other posts) / 30 years (Driver). With SC/ST relaxation: up to 28 years (other posts) / 32 years (Driver). Always verify the exact limits from the official CRPF notification on rect.crpf.gov.in.
How is age calculated for CRPF Tradesman 2026?+
Age is calculated as on 01 January 2026 as per the official CRPF notification. The formula is: Age = 01 January 2026 − Your Date of Birth. For example, if your DOB is 01 January 2003, your age on 01 January 2026 is exactly 23 years — which is the maximum age for General category. Our calculator does this automatically and accurately.
Is age relaxation available for CRPF Tradesman 2026?+
Yes — OBC candidates get 3 years relaxation (max age 26 for other posts, 30 for Driver). SC/ST candidates get 5 years relaxation (max age 28 for other posts, 32 for Driver). Ex-Servicemen get relaxation equal to total military service + 3 years. No relaxation for General/EWS category.
What is the last date for CRPF Tradesman 2026 application?+
The last date to submit the online application form for CRPF Constable Tradesman Recruitment 2026 is 19 May 2026. The last date for fee payment is also 19 May 2026. Applications started on 20 April 2026. Do not wait until the last date — apply early to avoid technical issues.
Who can apply for CRPF Tradesman 2026?+
Indian citizens who have passed Class 10th from a recognized board can apply. Additional requirements: Driver post needs a valid driving license, Motor Mechanic needs 2-year ITI certificate, Pioneer Wing needs 1-year relevant experience. Age must be 18–23 years (General) or with applicable relaxation. Male and female both can apply — the notification has posts for both.
Is this CRPF Age Calculator tool accurate?+
This tool provides accurate age calculation based on the official age rules published in the CRPF Constable Tradesman Recruitment 2026 notification. It calculates age precisely as on 01 January 2026 and applies correct category-wise relaxation. However, always verify your eligibility from the official CRPF notification before submitting your application. This tool is for quick reference only.
Where can I check the official CRPF Tradesman notification?+
The official CRPF Constable Tradesman Recruitment 2026 notification is available on the official CRPF recruitment website: rect.crpf.gov.in. You can also check the official CRPF website crpf.gov.in. For online application, use the direct link provided on the official notification page. Always use official government websites for applications and payment.
