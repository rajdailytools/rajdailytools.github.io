Official Specification

RRB JE / DMS Signature Specifications 2026

Reproduced in plain language from the official RRB CEN No. 04/2026 notification (Para 14.5.1).

📄
Paper & Ink: Your signature must be written on plain white paper using a black ink pen — no other paper colour or ink colour is accepted.
✍️
Handwriting style: The signature must be in running (cursive/joined) handwriting. Block letters, capital letters, or disjointed letters are not allowed.
📐
Physical size: The signature should fit within a box of 50 mm × 20 mm on paper.
🖥️
Preferred pixels: The scanned/uploaded image should be 140 × 60 pixels.
🖼️
Format: The final file must be saved as JPG/JPEG — no other image format is accepted.
⚖️
File size: The final file must be between 30 KB and 49 KB.
🙋
Applicant's own signature: The signature uploaded must belong to the applicant themselves.
⚠️
Rejection warning: Any deviation from the specification above may lead to rejection of the application, as stated in the official notification.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the RRB JE signature size for CEN 04/2026?+
The signature should fit within a physical box of 50 mm × 20 mm, scanned or photographed as an image of 140 × 60 pixels, saved as JPG/JPEG between 30 KB and 49 KB.
What is the preferred pixel size?+
140 × 60 pixels is the preferred pixel dimension mentioned in the official notification, corresponding to the 50 × 20 mm physical box size.
What is the required signature file size?+
The final signature file must be between 30 KB and 49 KB, as per the official CEN No. 04/2026 notification.
Which image format is required?+
JPG/JPEG is the only accepted format for the signature upload.
