🏦 Loans, Credit Cards & Interest Tools

The EMI Calculator, Compound Interest Calculator, and Credit Card Interest Calculator help you understand exactly what a loan or credit card balance will cost over time. Key insight: Paying only the minimum due on a credit card can mean your bill takes years to clear and costs 3-4x the original amount in interest. Run the numbers in our Minimum Payment Calculator before you decide.
