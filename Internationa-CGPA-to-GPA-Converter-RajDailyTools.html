CGPA to GPA — Everything Explained

Everything you need to know about converting Indian CGPA to international GPA for university applications abroad.

🎓 CGPA vs GPA — What's the Difference?

CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) is the academic grading standard used by most Indian universities and IITs, NITs, and central universities. It runs on a 10-point scale, where 10 is the highest possible score.

GPA (Grade Point Average) is the international standard used in the USA, UK, Canada, and many other countries. The most common scale globally is the 4.0 GPA scale, where 4.0 represents perfect marks.

🔢 Standard Conversion Formula

The most widely accepted and straightforward formula is:

GPA = (CGPA / 10) × Target Scale
Example: CGPA 8.5 → USA GPA = (8.5 / 10) × 4.0 = 3.4 out of 4.0

🌍 Country-wise GPA Systems

  • USA & Canada: 4.0 scale is standard. Most universities require a minimum 3.0 GPA for graduate admissions.
  • UK: Uses degree classifications (First Class, Upper Second etc.) but equivalent 4.0 GPA is used for international applications.
  • Australia: Uses a 7.0 GPA scale at most universities. (7 = High Distinction)
  • Germany: Uses a 1.0–5.0 inverted scale where 1.0 is the highest (best) and 5.0 is failing. This converter uses the standard approach — always verify with the German institution.

⚠️ Why Conversion Matters for Abroad Applications

When you apply to universities abroad — especially the USA, UK, Canada, or Australia — admission offices need to understand your academic performance in their grading language. Simply writing "8.5 CGPA" on a US application form may confuse the evaluator. Converting it to 3.4 GPA immediately communicates your academic standing in a universally understood format.

Pro Tip: Many US universities also accept WES (World Education Services) or NACES-member evaluations. If your target university requires a formal transcript evaluation, use this tool for estimation — then get an official evaluation from WES or ECE.

📋 Common Mistakes Students Make

  • Using the wrong target scale (4.0 vs 7.0 — Australia uses 7.0, not 4.0)
  • Not verifying with the specific university's equivalency policy — policies vary
  • Confusing CGPA with percentage — they are different metrics
  • Forgetting to mention the scale on the application (always write "8.5/10")
  • Using outdated conversion charts — always use updated, formula-based conversions
❓ FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about CGPA to GPA conversion.

What is the standard formula for CGPA to GPA (USA) conversion?+
The most widely accepted formula is: GPA = (CGPA / 10) × 4.0. For example, a CGPA of 8.0 converts to (8.0/10) × 4.0 = 3.2 GPA on the USA 4.0 scale. Some universities may use slightly different conversion charts — always check with the specific institution.
Is 7.5 CGPA good for studying abroad?+
Yes — 7.5 CGPA converts to 3.0 GPA on a 4.0 scale, which is generally the minimum requirement for most graduate programs in the USA. For top universities (MIT, Stanford, Harvard), you typically need 3.5+ GPA (8.75+ CGPA). For UK universities, 7.5 CGPA qualifies as a solid 2:1 degree equivalent.
How does Australia's 7.0 GPA scale work?+
Australian universities use a 7-point GPA scale: 7 = High Distinction, 6 = Distinction, 5 = Credit, 4 = Pass, and below 4 = Fail. The conversion formula is: GPA = (CGPA / 10) × 7.0. So a CGPA of 8.5 converts to approximately 5.95 / 7.0 in the Australian system — a solid Distinction level.
Is this tool's conversion accepted by universities?+
This tool provides an estimated conversion for reference purposes. For official university applications, many institutions require a formal transcript evaluation from recognized organizations like WES (World Education Services), ICAS, or ECE. Use this tool to estimate and plan — then get a formal evaluation if required.
How does Germany's GPA system work differently?+
Germany uses an inverted 1.0–5.0 scale where 1.0 is the highest (best) grade and 5.0 means fail — opposite of the Indian system. The rough conversion is: German GPA = 1 + 3 × (Max CGPA − Your CGPA) / (Max CGPA − Min CGPA). This tool uses a simplified standard approach — always verify the specific formula with the German university you are applying to.
What CGPA is equivalent to a First Class degree in the UK?+
In UK degree equivalency: First Class Honours = 70%+ = approximately 8.5+ CGPA. Upper Second Class (2:1) = 60–69% = approximately 7.0–8.4 CGPA. Lower Second Class (2:2) = 50–59% = approximately 6.0–6.9 CGPA. These are general guidelines — different Indian universities have different equivalency policies.
