Important Eligibility Alerts

Ye cheezein SSC CGL 2026 mein sabse common mistakes hain — avoid karo

🚨
DOB Change Nahi Hogi — Kabhi Bhi
SSC CGL mein jo DOB application form mein fill ki jaaye, wo exactly Matriculation certificate se match karni chahiye. Application submit karne ke baad DOB change request accepted nahi hogi. Ek baar commit karo — galti nahi.
⚠️
Final Year Students — Result Declared Hona Chahiye
Final year apply kar sakte hain, lekin degree result 01.08.2026 tak declared hona zaroori hai. Sirf "result processing" kaafi nahi — officially declared hona chahiye. Document verification mein degree na hone par candidature cancel hogi.
📋
OBC Certificate — Non-Creamy Layer Mandatory
OBC candidates must ensure they are NOT in creamy layer as on application closing date (22 June 2026). EWS certificate FY 2026-27 valid hona chahiye based on FY 2025-26 income. Purana certificate rejected ho sakta hai.
✅
Attempts Limit Nahi Hai SSC CGL Mein
SSC CGL mein koi fixed attempts limit nahi hai. Jab tak age limit within hai, candidate apply kar sakta hai. Age cutoff date 01 August 2026 hai — usi se eligibility calculate hogi.
💡
Correction Window — ₹200/₹500 Charge
Application correction window 29 June – 01 July 2026 hai. First correction: ₹200, Second correction: ₹500. Correction charges sabke liye applicable hain — category ke bawajood. Fee refundable nahi hai.
⚖️
Physical Standards — Kuch Posts Ke Liye Required
Sub Inspector (CBI, NIA), Inspector (Central Excise, Preventive Officer, Examiner), NCB posts ke liye physical aur medical standards required hain. Post preference dene se pehle physical eligibility confirm karo — baad mein change nahi hoga.

Priya Ka OBC Relaxation Confusion — Aur Iska Correct Answer

Priya UP se thi, OBC category se. SSC CGL ke liye apply karna chahti thi lekin uski age 29 saal thi aur usne suna tha ki SSC CGL ki age limit 27 saal hai. Woh samajh rahi thi ki ab woh eligible nahi hai.

Lekin actually — woh eligible thi. Kyunki jis post ke liye woh apply karna chahti thi (Inspector Income Tax) ki age limit 18-30 years hai. Aur OBC category se usse 3 saal ki relaxation milegi. Matlab effective upper age limit uske liye 33 years ho jaati hai.

Aur jo 18-27 years wali posts hain — Tax Assistant, Auditor etc. — unmein bhi OBC ke liye upper age 30 years ho jaati hai. Priya ke paas abhi bhi 1 saal bacha tha eligibility window mein.

Aisi hi confusion avoid karne ke liye ye exact calculator banaya gaya hai — jo official notification ke exact rules ke saath automatically relaxation apply karta hai aur sahi answer deta hai.

SSC CGL Age Limit 2026 — Complete Guide

SSC CGL 2026 Official Age Limit — Exact Data from Notification

SSC CGL 2026 ke official notification (F.No. HQ-C11018/1/2026-C-1, uploaded 21 May 2026) ke Para 5 mein clearly stated hai ki age calculation ki cutoff date 01 August 2026 hai. Matlab aapki age 01-08-2026 ko kitni hogi — wohi measure ki jaayegi, application date ko nahi.

Para 5.1 mein four different age categories specified hain based on post type. Agar post ke liye 18-27 years age limit hai, toh candidate 02-08-1999 ko ya baad mein born hona chahiye aur 01-08-2008 se pehle born hona chahiye. Agar 20-30 years hai, toh 02-08-1996 se 01-08-2006 ke beech born. Age limit 18-30 ke liye, 02-08-1996 se 01-08-2008 ke beech born. Aur JSO (18-32) ke liye, 02-08-1994 se 01-08-2008 ke beech born hona chahiye.

Category-wise Age Relaxation — Para 5.2 Official Details

SSC official notification ke Para 5.2 mein category-wise relaxation detail mein given hai. Ye sirf upper age limit par apply hoti hai. Minimum age mein koi relaxation nahi milti.

  • OBC (Code 02): 3 years relaxation upper age limit se zyada. Matlab agar post ki max age 27 hai, OBC ke liye 30 ho jaati hai.
  • SC/ST (Code 01): 5 years relaxation. 27 years limit wali post ke liye SC/ST candidates 32 tak eligible hain.
  • PwBD — General (Code 03): 10 years. PwBD + OBC (Code 04): 13 years. PwBD + SC/ST (Code 05): 15 years.
  • Ex-Servicemen (Code 06): Actual age mein se military service subtract karo, phir 3 saal relaxation add hoti hai as on 01.08.2026.
  • Central Govt. Civilian Employees (Code 10): Group C posts ke liye — 3 saal regular continuous service ke baad — age up to 40 years.
  • Widows/Divorced Women (Code 12): Up to 35 years. SC/ST widows (Code 13): Up to 40 years.

Educational Qualification — Kya Chahiye Officially?

Para 8.4 mein clearly stated hai ki most posts ke liye Bachelor's Degree from a recognized University or equivalent required hai. Koi minimum percentage requirement nahi hai most posts ke liye. Arts, Commerce, Science, B.Tech, BCA, BBA — koi bhi stream valid hai jab tak degree recognized university se ho.

Sirf Junior Statistical Officer (JSO) ke liye special requirement hai — Para 8.2 mein: 60% marks in Mathematics at 12th standard level, OR Statistics as one of the subjects at degree level. Ye bahut important hai — JSO ki unique requirements hain.

Statistical Investigator Grade-II ke liye Para 8.3 mein specified: degree must be in Statistics, Mathematics, Economics, Demography, Population Studies, Operation Research, IT, Computer Science, CS Engineering, Computer Technology, Computer Application, Data Science, or Artificial Intelligence.

Final Year Students — Official Rules (Para 8.5)

Para 8.5 mein directly stated hai: "The candidates who have appeared in their final year of their graduation can also apply, however they must possess Essential qualification as on 01.08.2026."

Matlab final year students apply kar sakte hain. Lekin ek critical requirement hai — degree result officially declared hona chahiye 01 August 2026 tak. Para 8.8 mein aur clearer hai: "the result of the qualifying examination was declared on or before the cut-off date". Sirf result processing ya examination given hona kaafi nahi — declaration hona zaroori hai.

Distance Learning Degrees — Valid Hain Ya Nahi?

Para 8.6 mein mentioned hai ki MHRD Notification dated 10-06-2015 ke anusaar, Open and Distance Learning degrees valid hain provided they are approved by Distance Education Bureau, UGC. Ye automatic recognition hai universities established by Parliament/State Legislature ya deemed universities ke liye. Lekin candidates ko document verification mein UGC DEB approval certificate produce karna padega relevant period ke liye.

Application Fee — Who Pays, Who is Exempt?

Para 10.1 mein clearly stated: Fee ₹100 (Rs one hundred only). Para 10.2 mein exemption list: Women candidates, SC, ST, PwBD, and eligible Ex-Servicemen are exempted. Note karo ki EWS aur OBC candidates fee exempt nahi hain — unhe ₹100 pay karna hoga.

Common Mistakes During SSC CGL Application

  • Wrong DOB: Bahut se candidates Aadhaar card se DOB fill karte hain jo Matriculation certificate se match nahi karta. SSC strictly Matric certificate follow karta hai.
  • OBC Creamy Layer Error: Kuch OBC candidates creamy layer mein hote hain aur phir bhi OBC category mein apply karte hain — document verification mein cancellation hoti hai.
  • EWS Certificate Old: EWS certificate FY 2026-27 ka hona chahiye (based on FY 2025-26 income). Purana certificate invalid hai.
  • Post Preference Without Physical Check: CBI Sub Inspector, NIA SI jaise posts ke physical standards check kiye bina preference dena — physical fail hone par woh post milega hi nahi aur koi other post ke liye option nahi rehga.
  • Final Year Result Not Declared: Final year mein apply karke graduation result 01.08.2026 tak complete nahi karna — candidature cancel.
