FAQs

SSC CGL Eligibility Doubts

Quick answers to 50 most common eligibility queries.

1. What is the critical date for CGL 2026 eligibility?+
The critical date is 01-08-2026. All age and qualification requirements must be met by this date.
2. Can final year students apply?+
Yes, but your final degree results must be declared on or before 01-08-2026.
3. What is the age limit for IT Inspector?+
The age limit is 18-30 years. OBC gets 3 years and SC/ST gets 5 years relaxation.
4. What is the qualification for JSO?+
Bachelor's Degree in any subject with 60% in 12th Math OR Bachelor's with Statistics as a subject.
5. Is any specific degree needed for Income Tax Inspector?+
No, any Bachelor's degree from a recognized university is sufficient.
6. What is the age limit for Assistant Section Officer (CSS)?+
For CSS ASO, the age limit is 20-30 years.
7. What is the minimum age for SI in CBI?+
The minimum age is 20 years as on 01-08-2026.
8. How much relaxation do PwBD General candidates get?+
PwBD (Unreserved) candidates get 10 years of age relaxation.
9. Is a B.Tech degree eligible for Statistical Investigator?+
Yes, if Statistics was one of the subjects during the degree.
10. Can I apply for JSO if I had 59% in 12th Math?+
No, exactly 60% or more is required if applying via the 12th Math route.
11. What is the upper age limit for SC/ST in JSO?+
The limit is 32 + 5 = 37 years.
12. Is degree from IGNOU/Distance learning valid?+
Yes, if the university is recognized by UGC/DEB.
13. Is there any attempt limit for SSC CGL?+
No, there is no limit on attempts as long as you meet the age criteria.
14. What is the age limit for Tax Assistant?+
The age limit is 18-27 years.
15. Is BCA degree eligible for CGL?+
Yes, BCA is a recognized Bachelor's degree.
16. What is the age limit for AAO?+
18-30 years for General category.
17. Is CA qualification mandatory for AAO?+
No, it is a desirable qualification. A simple Bachelor's degree is enough to apply.
18. What is the age relaxation for OBC in Tax Assistant posts?+
OBC limit is 27 + 3 = 30 years.
19. Can I apply for JSO with B.Com?+
Yes, if you had 60% in 12th Mathematics.
20. What is the age limit for Sub Inspector in NIA?+
Up to 30 years for the General category.
21. Is B.Sc Mathematics eligible for Statistical Investigator?+
Yes, as per the 2026 notification Para 8.3.1.
22. Can I apply for ASO MEA if I am 19 years old?+
No, the minimum age for ASO posts is 20 years.
23. Is height a factor for Auditor posts?+
No, physical standards apply only to specific Inspector and SI posts.
24. What is the age limit for Senior Secretariat Assistant?+
The limit is 18-27 years.
25. Can a B.Tech (CS) apply for Statistical Investigator?+
Yes, if Statistics was a subject in the curriculum.
26. Is the degree certificate needed during application?+
No, you just need to enter details. Original is checked during Document Verification.
27. What if my result comes on 02-08-2026?+
You will be ineligible as the cut-off is strictly 01-08-2026.
28. Is there age relaxation for central govt employees?+
Yes, up to 40 years (General) for Group C posts if they have 3 years of service.
29. What is the age limit for SI in Narcotics?+
18-30 years for General category.
30. Can an Ex-Serviceman get age relaxation?+
Yes, 3 years after deduction of the military service rendered.
31. Is B.E. (Electronics) eligible for CGL?+
Yes, any B.E. or B.Tech degree is considered a Bachelor's degree.
32. What is the age limit for Divisional Accountant?+
18-30 years for General category.
33. Can candidates with benchmark disabilities apply for Excise Inspector?+
Yes, specific PwBD categories are identified as suitable for these posts.
34. Is 12th math required for IT Inspector?+
No, 12th math is only a requirement for the JSO post.
35. Can a B.Sc Physics student apply for JSO?+
Only if you have 60% in 12th Math or Statistics as a subject in B.Sc.
36. What is the upper age for OBC in JSO?+
32 + 3 = 35 years.
37. Is BBA degree valid for CGL?+
Yes, BBA is a recognized graduation degree.
38. Is there any physical test for Auditors?+
No physical test is required for Auditor posts.
39. Can a married person apply?+
Yes, provided they do not have more than one living spouse (Para 22).
40. What is the age limit for Junior Accountant?+
18-27 years for the General category.
41. Is there age relaxation for widows?+
Yes, up to 35 years for General and 40 years for SC/ST (Group C posts).
42. Can I apply if I graduated from a private university?+
Yes, as long as the university is UGC-recognized.
43. What is the age limit for ASO in Railways?+
18-30 years for General category.
44. Is 60% in graduation required for CGL?+
No, a simple pass certificate is enough for most posts.
45. What is the age limit for SI in NIA?+
18-30 years.
46. Can a B.Arch student apply?+
Yes, B.Arch is a valid graduation degree.
47. Is there physical criteria for AAO?+
No, there is no physical criteria for the AAO post.
48. What is the age limit for Section Head?+
18-30 years for the General category.
49. Is B.Sc IT eligible for Statistical Investigator?+
Yes, as per Para 8.3.1 of the 2026 notification.
50. Can I change my category after application?+
No, changes are generally not allowed after the correction window closes.
