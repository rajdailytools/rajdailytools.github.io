Exam / परीक्षा
Primary & Secondary School Teacher Eligibility Test 2026 (MSPSTET 2026) — Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB), Bhopal. This special eligibility examination is being conducted for in-service teachers teaching at Primary and Secondary levels.
Step 1 — Teaching Level अध्यापन स्तर चुनें
Primary Level: The examination is for teachers teaching at the Primary level who have not qualified the required TET earlier, including eligible teachers of government and non-government schools as specified in the rulebook.
Step 2 — Current Teaching Status वर्तमान शिक्षक स्थिति
Step 3 — School Type विद्यालय का प्रकार
Step 4 — Current Teaching Post वर्तमान पद
Step 5 — Previous TET Status पूर्व TET स्थिति
Step 6 — Category श्रेणी
Step 7 — Expected Qualifying Marks न्यूनतम उत्तीर्ण प्रतिशत
As specified in the MSPSTET 2026 rulebook, the minimum qualifying percentage is 60% for the general category and 50% for Scheduled Caste / Scheduled Tribe / Other Backward Class / Persons with Disability / Economically Weaker Section categories.
Important: MSPSTET 2026 is being conducted as a special eligibility examination for in-service teachers. The qualification/result of this examination will not be treated as eligibility for direct recruitment to government teacher posts in future.
