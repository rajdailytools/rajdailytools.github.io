Important Highlights

💳
Fee is ₹100 only: Women candidates and SC/ST/PwBD/ESM applicants eligible for reservation are fully exempted from the application fee (Para 12.1–12.3).
🖥️
3 separate CBEs: The Computer Based Examination is conducted separately for Matriculation, Higher Secondary (10+2), and Graduation & above levels (Para 15.1).
➖
Negative marking: 0.50 marks deducted for every wrong answer, across all parts of the CBE (Para 15.2).
📸
Live photo capture only: Candidates do NOT upload a pre-existing photograph — the application module captures a real-time photo during form-filling (Para 11.7–11.9).
✍️
Signature spec: JPEG/JPG format, 10–20 KB, approx. 6.0 cm (Width) × 2.0 cm (Height) (Para 11.10).

Frequently Asked Questions

Top 10 questions about SSC Selection Post Phase XIV/2026, answered from the official notification

What is SSC Selection Post Phase XIV/2026?+
It is a recruitment notification (Advertisement No. Phase-XIV/2026/Selection Posts) issued by the Staff Selection Commission for various Selection Posts across Ministries/Departments, as indicated in Annexure-III of the official notice.
What is the last date to apply for SSC Selection Post Phase XIV/2026?+
The last date for submission of online applications is 04 May 2026 (up to 2300 Hrs). Online fee payment is accepted till 05 May 2026 (up to 2300 Hrs) (Para 11.3, 12.4).
What is the application fee for SSC Selection Post Phase XIV/2026?+
The application fee is ₹100 (Rupees One Hundred only), payable online via BHIM UPI, Net Banking, or Visa/Master/Maestro/RuPay Card (Para 12.1–12.2).
Who is exempted from paying the application fee?+
Women candidates and candidates belonging to Scheduled Castes (SC), Scheduled Tribes (ST), Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD), and Ex-Servicemen (ESM) eligible for reservation are exempted from the fee (Para 12.3).
When will the SSC Selection Post Phase XIV/2026 CBE be conducted?+
The Computer Based Examination (CBE) is tentatively scheduled for June 2026. The exact date will be announced by SSC through the Admission Certificate.
What is the selection process for SSC Selection Post Phase XIV/2026?+
Selection happens in stages: Computer Based Examination (CBE) → scrutiny of documents → Document Verification (DV) by the concerned User Department → Final Result / Select List and nomination.
What is the age limit for SSC Selection Post Phase XIV/2026?+
Age limit is calculated as on 01.01.2026 and varies by post — the notification specifies 11 different age bands ranging from 18–25 years to 23–28 years, with additional relaxation for reserved categories (Para 6).
Is there negative marking in the CBE?+
Yes. 0.50 marks are deducted for every wrong answer in the Computer Based Examination (Para 15.2).
Do I need to upload a photograph while applying?+
No pre-existing photo upload is required. The application module captures a real-time photograph via webcam while filling the form — without cap, mask, or spectacles, in good light with a plain background (Para 11.7–11.9).
What are the signature upload specifications?+
Signature must be scanned in JPEG/JPG format, sized 10–20 KB, with image dimensions of approximately 6.0 cm (Width) × 2.0 cm (Height) (Para 11.10).
