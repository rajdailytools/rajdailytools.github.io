Eye Test Ke Baare Me Myths vs Facts

Jo galat suna hai aur jo sach hai — dono clearly jaano

❌ Myth
"Chashma hai toh SSC GD ke liye apply hi mat karo."
✅ Fact — Low power spectacles candidates jo corrected vision acceptable range me laate hain, wo medical me eligible ho sakte hain. Blindly apply nahi chhodna chahiye.
❌ Myth
"Thodi si color weakness toh chalta hai, koi nahi dekhta."
✅ Fact — Color vision test (Ishihara test) medical exam ka part hota hai. Serious color deficiency eligibility impact kar sakti hai. Honestly assess karo.
❌ Myth
"LASIK karwa lo toh automatically select ho jaoge."
✅ Fact — LASIK ke baad stabilization time chahiye. Recent surgery generally deferred hoti hai. Final call medical board ka hota hai. LASIK se guaranteed selection nahi milta.
❌ Myth
"Eye test toh formality hai, seriously nahi lete."
✅ Fact — SSC GD CAPF medical examination me eye test ek critical component hai. Failures isi me sabse zyada hoti hain. Seriously prepare karo aur pehle se check karwa lo.
❌ Myth
"Night blindness toh sirf raat ko problem deta hai — din ka exam hai."
✅ Fact — CAPF personnel ko 24-hour duty karni padti hai. Night blindness medical examination me specifically test hoti hai aur generally disqualifying condition hai.
❌ Myth
"6/9 better eye se bhi ho jaata hai."
✅ Fact — Expected standard is 6/6 for better eye. 6/9 better eye me borderline ya rejection case ho sakta hai. Proper eye care aur timely check important hai.
Important Medical Alerts

Ye warnings seriously lo — medical rejection avoid karne ke liye

✅
Medical Test Se Pehle Eye Check Karwao
Official medical se 2-3 mahine pehle kisi achhe ophthalmologist se complete eye checkup karwa lo. Isse koi issue early identify hoga aur preparation hogi.
⚠️
Spectacles Ka Power Accurately Jaano
Agar chashma lagate ho toh exact dioptre (D) power jaano. ±2D se zyada myopia medical me serious risk factor hai. Low power wale candidates hopefully corrected vision se pass hote hain.
🚨
Color Blindness Hide Mat Karo
Ishihara test me color blindness clearly identify hoti hai. Hide karne ki koshish mat karo — ye na sirf rejection karata hai balki character issues bhi create kar sakta hai record me.
⚕️
LASIK Ke Baad Stabilization Zaruri Hai
Surgery ke baad kam se kam 6-12 months stabilization time chahiye. Recent LASIK candidates generally deferred hote hain. Surgery ka documentation sambhal ke rakho medical ke liye.
🌙
Night Blindness Ek Serious Condition Hai
CAPF personnel ko night operations karne padte hain. Night blindness generally disqualifying condition hai — agar ye problem hai toh pehle ophthalmologist se treat karwao.
💡
Medical Rejection Par Appeal Ka Option
Agar medical me reject hote ho toh review medical board ka provision hota hai. Apni papers ready rakho, second opinion lo aur official procedure follow karo — ek rejection final nahi hota.
SSC GD Eye Medical Complete Guide

SSC GD Eye Sight Standards Kya Hain?

SSC GD ke through BSF, CISF, CRPF, ITBP, SSB aur Assam Rifles me recruit hone wale candidates ko CAPF medical standards ke anusar eye examination clear karna padta hai. Expected standards me better eye 6/6 distant vision aur worse eye 6/9 distant vision generally require hoti hai — dono uncorrected (without spectacles) basis par.

Iske alawa near vision (N6 each eye), color vision (minimum CP2 standard), aur night vision bhi medical examination ke important parts hain.

SSC GD Me Spectacles Allowed Hai Ya Nahi?

Ye sabse common aur confusing sawaal hai. Seedha answer: high myopia (above ±4D) generally CAPF medical me reject karti hai. Low power spectacles candidates ke case me corrected vision acceptable range me aa sakti hai — lekin ye clearly stated nahi hai ki corrected vision pe test hoga ya uncorrected. Generally CAPF physical ke liye uncorrected standards apply hote hain.

Contact lenses bhi spectacles jaise hi treat hote hain — high dependence pe risk bana rehta hai. Best approach hai natural vision upar lana ya ophthalmologist se realistic assessment lena.

CP1 aur CP2 Vision Category Kya Hai?

CP stands for Colour Perception. CAPF me ye categories hoti hain:

  • CP1: Normal color vision — sabse best category, koi restriction nahi
  • CP2: Mild color deficiency — generally acceptable minimum standard
  • CP3: Moderate deficiency — risk factor, borderline case
  • CP4 / Total: Severe color blindness — generally disqualifying

SSC GD Eye Medical Test Me Kya Hota Hai?

  • Snellen chart reading — distance vision both eyes
  • Jaeger card reading — near vision check
  • Ishihara plate test — color vision assessment
  • Cover-uncover test — squint detection
  • Night vision evaluation (practical or questionnaire)
  • Slit lamp examination — anterior eye health
  • Ophthalmoscopy — posterior eye (fundus) check

Eye Test Ke Liye Smart Preparation Tips

  • Medical date se 2-3 mahine pehle comprehensive eye checkup karwao
  • Vitamin A rich diet lo — gajar, paalak, anda, fish — ye natural vision support karte hain
  • Screen time reduce karo, especially raat ko — eye strain kamta hai
  • Adequate sleep lo — aankhon ki recovery aur performance ke liye jaruri hai
  • Active eye infection hone par medical board se deferred date request kar sakte ho
  • Sahi glasses ya lenses prescription use karo — purani prescription se test mat do

Medical Rejection Ke Baad Kya Kare?

Agar SSC GD medical test me eye sight ki wajah se reject hote ho toh immediately panic mat karo. Review medical board ka provision hota hai where second opinion se your case reviewed hota hai. Required documents — previous medical reports, ophthalmologist certificate, LASIK documentation if applicable — ready rakho.

Agar genuinely high myopia ya serious eye condition hai toh honest assessment karo aur accordingly aage ka career plan consider karo. Bahut se other government exam opportunities exist karti hain jahan medical standards different hote hain.
