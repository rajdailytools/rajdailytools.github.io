Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about RajDailyTools popular tools — accuracy, access, updates, and more.

What are the most popular tools on RajDailyTools?+
Our most popular tools include the Agniveer Age Calculator, Army Running Time Calculator (1600m), SSC CGL Eligibility Checker, Army BMI Calculator, SSC CGL Rank Predictor, and Word Counter Tool. These tools get thousands of daily visitors from students and aspirants across India.
Are all popular tools completely free?+
Yes — every single tool on this page is 100% free. No subscription, no premium tier, no hidden payment. We believe powerful tools must be accessible to every student in India regardless of their background or financial situation. That promise will never change.
How accurate are the Army running and eligibility calculators?+
All Army tools are built using verified data from official Indian Army recruitment notifications. Running time marks, height-weight standards, BMI ranges, and chest measurements are cross-checked against official norms and updated whenever new notifications are released. Our founder's physics background means every formula is tested rigorously.
Do I need to create an account to use these tools?+
Absolutely not. There is zero login, zero registration, and zero sign-up required for any tool on RajDailyTools. Open the tool, enter your details, and get your result instantly. We will never force account creation — it is against what we stand for.
Do these tools work on mobile phones?+
Yes. All tools are built mobile-first and work perfectly on any smartphone browser. No app download is needed. Just open your phone's browser, search for the tool you need, and use it directly. Buttons, forms, and results are all optimized for mobile touch interaction.
Are SSC CGL tools updated for 2026 notifications?+
Yes. Our SSC CGL tools — including age limit calculator, eligibility checker, and rank predictor — are reviewed and updated as soon as SSC releases new notifications. We monitor SSC official releases regularly so the data you get always reflects the latest 2026 norms and cutoffs.
Can I check NDA eligibility on RajDailyTools?+
Yes. We have both an NDA Eligibility Checker and an NDA Age Limit Calculator. These tools check your age, educational qualification, gender, and nationality against the official UPSC NDA requirements. Results are instant and based on the latest NDA notification data.
Is my personal data stored when I use these tools?+
No. The vast majority of tools run entirely inside your browser — data you enter is processed locally and never sent to any server. We do not collect, store, or sell your personal information. Your privacy is treated as a core principle, not an afterthought.
Which government exam tools are available?+
We have tools for MP Police eligibility, CRPF Tradesman age calculation, NCL Recruitment eligibility, Patna HC Typist age verification, KV Admission documents list, and a general Government Exam Height Eligibility Checker covering Police, CRPF, BSF, and Army standards. More government exam tools are added every week.
How often are popular tools updated?+
Tools are updated whenever official rules, age limits, or exam patterns change. We track government notifications, UPSC updates, SSC releases, and Army recruitment calendar announcements to ensure every tool reflects the most current and accurate information available.
