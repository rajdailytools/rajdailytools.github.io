Patna HC Typist Recruitment 2026 — Complete Information

⚖️ Patna High Court Computer Operator-cum-Typist Recruitment 2026

The High Court of Judicature at Patna (Bihar) has officially released a recruitment notification for the post of Computer Operator-cum-Typist under Advertisement Number PHC/02/2026. This recruitment offers 48 posts to eligible candidates who meet the age, educational, and typing speed requirements set by the court. Applications opened on 17 April 2026 and the last date to apply online is 16 May 2026.

This is a highly sought-after government job opportunity in Bihar, offering job security, good salary, and the prestige of working in one of India's oldest High Courts. For candidates who have been preparing for state government jobs, the Patna HC Typist eligibility 2026 criteria is relatively straightforward — but the age limit confusion often stops many eligible candidates from applying.

Quick Fact: The Patna HC Typist post offers a government job in the judicial sector with stability, respect, and career growth. The selection process has three stages — Written Exam, Trade/Skill Test, and Interview.

📅 Age Limit — The Most Confusing Part Explained Clearly

The Patna HC Typist age limit 2026 is calculated as on 01 January 2026. The minimum age for all categories is 18 years. The maximum age varies by category — this is where most candidates get confused.

  • Male — UR (Unreserved / General): Maximum 37 years
  • Female — UR / EWS / OBC: Maximum 40 years
  • Male — BC / OBC: Maximum 40 years
  • Female — BC / EBC: Maximum 40 years
  • SC / ST (Both Male and Female): Maximum 42 years

This tool calculates your exact age as on 01 January 2026 and instantly tells you whether you fall within the eligible age range for your specific category. No more confusion — just clear, instant answers.

🎓 Educational Qualification — What You Need

For the Patna High Court Computer Operator-cum-Typist post, candidates must fulfill all three educational criteria:

  • A Graduation Degree in any discipline from a recognized university (final year students are not eligible)
  • A Minimum 6-Month Diploma or Certificate in Computer Application from a recognized institute — this is non-negotiable
  • A Typing Speed of 40 WPM in English AND 30 WPM in Hindi — both are required, not optional

⌨️ Importance of Typing Speed in Government Jobs

The typing speed requirement for government jobs is one of the most critical eligibility criteria that candidates often underestimate. For Patna HC Typist, you need 40 Words Per Minute (WPM) in English and 30 WPM in Hindi — which sounds manageable but requires consistent daily practice to achieve reliably under exam conditions.

Typing is tested under a trade/skill test stage where you'll be evaluated on a computer under timed conditions. Errors reduce your effective WPM score. The recommendation from successful government job aspirants: practice typing for at least 30–45 minutes daily for 3–6 months before the exam. Use standard typing software that simulates exam conditions.

Typing Tip: 40 WPM in English = approximately 200 characters per minute with accuracy. Start with touch typing fundamentals — home row keys first. Free tools like TypingTest.com can help you track progress toward the required speed.

🏛️ About the Typist Job Role in a High Court

A Computer Operator-cum-Typist in the Patna High Court performs critical administrative and clerical functions that support the judicial process. The role typically involves typing court orders, judgments, and legal documents accurately; managing computer-based records; preparing certificates and official correspondence; data entry for case management systems; and maintaining digital records of court proceedings.

The role demands accuracy, speed, confidentiality, and familiarity with legal document formats. It is a Class III Government Post with a structured pay scale, allowances, and career advancement opportunities within the judicial system.

🤔 Common Eligibility Confusions — Solved

Q: I'm 38 years old and belong to General category (Male). Am I eligible?
A: No. The maximum age for UR Male is 37 years as on 01 Jan 2026. Being 38 years makes you over the limit.

Q: I'm 39 years old, Female, OBC. Am I eligible?
A: Yes! Female candidates under UR/EWS/OBC have a maximum age of 40 years. At 39, you are within the limit.

Q: I'm SC category, Male, 41 years old. Can I apply?
A: Yes! SC/ST candidates (both male and female) have a maximum age of 42 years. At 41, you are eligible.
