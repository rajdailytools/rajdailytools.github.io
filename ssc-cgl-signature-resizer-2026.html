SSC CGL Signature — Complete Guide 2026

SSC CGL Signature Size Limit 2026 — Official Data

SSC CGL 2026 notification ke Para 9.6 mein clearly stated hai: "Candidates are required to upload the scanned signature in JPEG/JPG format (10 to 20 KB). Image dimension of the signature should be about 6.0 cm (width) x 2.0 cm (height)." Ye rules sirf recommendation nahi hain — inhe strictly follow karna padta hai, warna application summarily reject ho sakti hai.

File size ka range intentionally 10 KB se 20 KB rakha gaya hai. Agar file 20 KB se badi hai toh SSC portal upload reject kar deta hai. Agar file 10 KB se chhoti hai toh image quality itni low ho sakti hai ki signature blur ya miniature nazar aaye — jo equally rejection cause karta hai. Ideal target 13–17 KB ke beech hai.

SSC CGL Signature Format — Why JPEG Only?

JPEG format image compression ke liye designed hai — isi wajah se SSC CGL jaisi high-traffic portals pe JPEG standard hota hai. PNG files generally zyada badi hoti hain (30–100 KB easily) aur unhe JPEG mein convert karna zaroori hota hai. iPhone users ke liye HEIC format common hai — wo bhi JPEG mein convert karna padega.

  • JPEG (.jpg): Official accepted format — SSC CGL 2026 ke liye required
  • PNG: Not accepted — usually too large, different compression method
  • PDF: Not accepted — ye document format hai, image nahi
  • HEIC/HEIF (iPhone): Not accepted — convert to JPG required
  • BMP/TIFF: Not accepted — uncompressed formats, too large

Signature Banate Waqt Ye Galtiyan Mat Karo

Bahut candidates signature technically sahi banate hain but visually galat hoti hai — aur wo bhi rejection cause karta hai. Kuch common mistakes jo aspirants karte hain:

  • Too light ink: Light grey ink se signature nahi karti — black ya dark blue ink use karo
  • Pencil use: Pencil signature acceptable nahi — pen se karo
  • Colored background paper: White paper pe hi karo — colored paper pe ki gayi signature background issue create karta hai
  • Shadow in photo: Mobile se photo lete waqt aapka shadow pad jaata hai — light source signature ke same side pe hona chahiye
  • Too small signature: Bahut chhoti signature compress hone ke baad aur chhoti ho jaati hai — thodi badi rakho
  • Overwriting: Ek baar mein clearly karo — baar baar kaatna ya likhna clarity reduce karta hai

Correction Window Mein Signature Change Ho Sakta Hai?

Haan. SSC CGL 2026 notification Para 11 ke anusaar, correction window 29 June – 01 July 2026 tak hai. Is window mein candidates apni submitted application mein corrections kar sakte hain. Agar signature upload galat hua hai — blur tha, wrong format tha, ya size off tha — toh correction window mein sahi signature re-upload kar sakte hain. First correction ₹200 aur second correction ₹500 charge karta hai. Isliye pehli baar mein sahi karna best approach hai.

Aadhaar Authentication — Signature Rules Se Exemption

Para 9.3 aur 9.6 mein SSC ne clearly mention kiya hai ki jo candidates Aadhaar Based Authentication se apply karte hain unki applications photograph aur signature requirements ke grounds par reject nahi ki jaayengi. Ye ek significant provision hai. Agar aapke paas Aadhaar hai, toh Aadhaar authentication option choose karna recommended hai — ye signature upload errors ke risk ko significantly reduce karta hai. Para 9.4 mein bhi live photograph capture ka provision hai Aadhaar users ke liye.
