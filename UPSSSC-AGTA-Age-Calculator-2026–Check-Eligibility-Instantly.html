Complete Guide

UPSSSC AGTA 2026 Age Eligibility Explained

Poori jankari AGTA age limit ke baare mein — category relaxation, calculation method, common mistakes aur important tips.

UPSSSC AGTA 2026 Kya Hai?

UPSSSC (Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission) ne Agriculture Technical Assistant (AGTA) ke 2759 posts ke liye recruitment notification jaari kiya hai. Ye posts agriculture department mein technical assistance ke liye hain aur state government ke under aati hain.

Is recruitment mein apply karne ke liye candidates ka Agricultural Science ya related field mein graduation/diploma hona zaroori hai, saath hi age eligibility bhi check honi chahiye as on 01 July 2026.

Age Limit 01 July 2026 Ko Kyun Calculate Hoti Hai?

UPSSSC AGTA 2026 notification ke mutabiq, candidates ki age 01 July 2026 ko reference date maan kar calculate ki jaati hai. Matlab agar koi candidate 01 July 2026 ko 21 saal ka ho chuka hai aur 40 saal se zyada nahi hua hai (General category), toh woh eligible hai.

Example: Agar DOB hai 15 March 1985, toh 01 July 2026 ko age hogi: 41 years 3 months 17 days. General/EWS ke liye NOT eligible (40 exceed), OBC ke liye ELIGIBLE (43 limit ke andar).

Category Relaxation Kaise Apply Hoti Hai?

Age relaxation reserved categories ke candidates ko maximum age limit mein extra years ke roop mein milti hai. Minimum age (21 years) sabke liye same rehti hai.

  • General / EWS: Min 21 – Max 40 years (no relaxation)
  • OBC (NCL): Min 21 – Max 43 years (+3 years relaxation)
  • SC / ST: Min 21 – Max 45 years (+5 years relaxation)
  • PwD (Divyangjan): Min 21 – Max 55 years (+15 years relaxation)
  • Ex-Servicemen: As per UP Government rules (actual military service deducted from age)
Important: Category relaxation ke liye valid caste/category certificate hona zaroori hai. Certificate ready rakhein form fill karne se pehle — issued by competent authority only.

Exact Age Calculation Method

Age calculate karne ke liye reference date 01 July 2026 use hoti hai:

  • Reference date se Date of Birth minus karein
  • Exact years, months aur days calculate honge
  • Agar 01 July 2026 ko birthday hai — woh day bhi count hota hai
  • Age years mein dekha jaata hai — 40 years 0 months = still eligible for General
  • 40 years 1 month ya zyada = NOT eligible for General category

Common Mistakes Jo Candidates Karte Hain

❌ Galat Reference Date Use Karna
Age current date se calculate karna — WRONG. Hamesha 01 July 2026 ko reference date use karein as per official notification.
❌ Category Relaxation Ignore Karna
OBC/SC/ST candidates apni relaxation miss kar dete hain aur sochte hain ineligible hain — jabki woh eligible hote hain.
❌ Age Without Days Calculate Karna
Sirf years count karna — borderline cases mein months aur days bhi matter karte hain. Exact calculation zaroor karein.
❌ Official Notification Nahi Padhna
Sirf online tools par depend karna — hamesha upsssc.gov.in se official notification download karein aur verify karein.

AGTA 2026 Mein Apply Karne Ka Sahi Tarika

Age eligible hone ke baad ye steps follow karein:

  • Official website upsssc.gov.in par jaayein
  • AGTA 2026 notification download karein aur poora padhe
  • OTR (One Time Registration) complete karein agar nahi hua
  • Educational qualification documents ready rakhein
  • Category certificate valid aur updated hona chahiye
  • Application fee online mode se pay karein
  • Form submit karne ke baad confirmation printout zaroor lein

Frequently Asked Questions

UPSSSC AGTA 2026 age eligibility ke baare mein common sawaal.

UPSSSC AGTA 2026 mein age limit kya hai?+
UPSSSC AGTA 2026 ke liye minimum age 21 years aur maximum age 40 years hai — as on 01 July 2026. Reserved categories ke liye relaxation: OBC +3 years (max 43), SC/ST +5 years (max 45), PwD +15 years (max 55). Ex-Servicemen ke liye UP Govt ke rules apply honge. Hamesha official notification se verify karein.
Age calculation mein reference date kya hai — AGTA 2026?+
UPSSSC AGTA 2026 ke liye age ki calculation 01 July 2026 ko reference date maankar ki jaati hai. Matlab agar aapki DOB 02 July 1986 hai, toh 01 July 2026 ko aapki age 39 years 364 days hogi — jo 40 years se kam hai, toh General/EWS ke liye eligible honge. Agar DOB 30 June 1986 hai, toh age exactly 40 years 1 day hogi — NOT eligible for General (but OBC/SC/ST eligible).
OBC candidates ke liye AGTA mein kitni age relaxation milti hai?+
OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) candidates ko UPSSSC AGTA 2026 mein 3 years ki age relaxation milti hai. Matlab maximum age limit 40+3 = 43 years ho jaati hai as on 01 July 2026. Is relaxation ke liye valid OBC-NCL certificate required hai — jo competent authority se issued ho. Creamy layer candidates ko OBC relaxation nahi milti.
SC/ST candidates ke liye kya alag age limit hai AGTA mein?+
Haan — SC aur ST candidates ko 5 years ki age relaxation milti hai. Iska matlab maximum age limit 40+5 = 45 years ho jaati hai as on 01 July 2026. Valid SC/ST caste certificate hona zaroori hai. Is tool se apni DOB enter karke instantly check kar sakte hain ki aap eligible hain ya nahi.
